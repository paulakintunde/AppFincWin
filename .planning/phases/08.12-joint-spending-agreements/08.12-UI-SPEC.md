# Phase 08.12 (Joint spending agreements) - UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for every screen-building plan in Phase 08.12. Behaviour is locked by `08.12-CONTEXT.md` (D-00a..D-19) and `08.12-OWNER-ANSWERS.md` (rounds 1-6). The base design is not redrawn: tokens, spacing, rows, tiles and icons are exactly as in CLAUDE.md "Design fidelity". Nothing new is added except the few status keys and small components listed below, all composed from existing parts. Format and voice follow `08-UI-SPEC.md`, `08.1-UI-SPEC.md` and `10-UI-SPEC.md`.

Sources: 08.12-CONTEXT, 08.12-OWNER-ANSWERS, the 08.12 research files (arrangements red lines, free access, top-up and credit, market gaps, group name), `HouseholdAnswerNote`, `ZeroShareLine`, `SpendRuleScreen`, `SharedAccountsScreen`, `NoticesScreen`, `noticeText.ts`, `ApprovePlanScreen`, `statusMap.ts`, `iconData.ts`, `useDecideServerSwitch.ts`, CLAUDE.md.

**Copy status:** every string is draft except those marked **[exact]** (the owner's words in CONTEXT or OWNER-ANSWERS). Drafts go to the one wording sign-off phone page in wave 5 (D-15). Voice: declarative, grade-10 English, short words first, calm. Banned in every 08.12 string: "advice", "advise", "recommend", "should", "you must", "we suggest", "owes", "debt" (for a person), "allowed", "approved by" (control words), emoji, exclamation marks. FincWin records what the group agreed. It holds no money and blocks no payment. Push and notice text names facts (who, which item, what price) and never shows a balance.

**i18n:** every string goes in `src/i18n/locales/**` under these key groups: `household.pool.*` (held back, as-of figure, pool notice), `household.rule.*` (free access, Used by both, personal money, limit, review date), `household.ask.*` (OK requests, Talk it over, notices, push), `decide.shortMoney.*` (gap, cover, paid from, instalments), `household.safety.*` (Money and safety page), `household.setup.*` (Who's in it), `appLife.status.*` (badge words). The 08.1 banned-word guard runs on all of them, plus the extra words above.

## Typography

No new sizes or weights. Roles from `textRole`: 26/700 `heading`, 16/600 `sheetTitle`, 15/500 `body`, 15/600 `bodyStrong`, 13/600 `label`. Where this spec says `meta` it is `metaRole(pairing)` (12.5), the role `HouseholdAnswerNote` already uses for answer lines. **Inherited, not new:** these are the existing roles of the owner-locked §2 type scale (CLAUDE.md: "The §2 type scale is complete"). This phase adds no size, weight or role. Counted plainly, the roles span five sizes (26, 16, 15, 13, 12.5) and three weights (500, 600, 700). Within 08.12's new surfaces: `heading` (700) is used only for the "Who's in it?" step and the Money and safety page title, where existing screens already use it; everything else uses 600/500. Cutting a size would break consistency with the shipped screens, so the scale stays as is. Amounts, dates, times and counts use tabular figures and never truncate. Money in sentences is formatted by the money formatter, never typed into copy.

## Layout and Screen Contracts

All screens: `Screen`, scrollable, safe-area aware. Pushed screens have a back chevron (44 x 44, "Back"), title and optional right action. Sheets use `Sheet`; Close top right; primary `Pill` pinned. One visual anchor per screen, named below.

### 1. Held back, as-of figure, pool notice (D-01..D-04, D-18)

**Visual anchor:** the verdict card; the held-back line sits directly under its number.

**1a. Held-back line in Just me.** On every Just me answer, Home and Overview where the member counts their money (opt-in on) and the rule pools money (`shares`), `PoolLine` shows one `label` line in `inkMuted`, `tight` (8) below the verdict number: "$400 is held back for the household pool." **[exact base]** The verdict card uses the smaller amount. When nothing is held back, the line is absent. With the rule `shared` (free access) there is no held-back line; the answer shows the free-access line instead (Section 2). Accessible label: "$400 is held back for the household pool. Double tap to see why." The line is the whole row (44 tall minimum). It opens a `DetailSheet`:
- Title "Why $400 is held back".
- Body "Your household counts some of your money as shared. So your Just me answer leaves out the part your household can use, and the same money is never answered Fits twice."
- `LabelValueRow` "Held back: $400", "Your answer counts: $1,150".
- Text link "Change how we share" opens `SpendRuleScreen`.
- Primary "Got it".

**1b. Partner figure as of a time (D-02, D-03).** On a Household answer the existing `HouseholdAnswerNote` gets one `meta` line per member whose money is in the pool: "Ben's money as of 2:10 pm". **[exact base]** Rules: time uses the member's local clock format; "as of yesterday, 9:40 pm" past 24 hours; over 7 days the line reads "Ben's money as of Nov 20" and a second `meta` line "Not updated lately." appears. With three or more people the pool line names whose money is in: "Counting Ben's and Sam's money. Kai has not switched on." The figure is rounded down by the engine (`FIGURE_STEP`), so the screen shows no cents. Never shown: Ben's balance, accounts or open checks. The line is `meta`, `inkMuted`, wrapping to two lines at large text.

**1c. One-time pool notice (D-18).** A Notices item (kind `pool_fix`) for members with pooling on, once, on the first launch after the fix ships. Title **[exact base]** "Your Just me answers now leave out the share your household can use." Body "The same money is never answered Fits twice. See what is held back." Action `Pill`-small "See how it works" (opens the Section 1a sheet) and text link "Dismiss". Removing the card is permanent. No push. No count badge on the tab.

States: loading (figure not yet fetched): the as-of line is absent and the answer shows the partner-figure-missing line from the existing engine copy; offline: the figure keeps its original time, no extra warning; the figure is the same cached value the answer used. No spinner.

### 2. Free access: "Both can use all of it" (D-00b, FA-01..15)

**Visual anchor:** the selected sharing-choice row in `SpendRuleScreen`.

`SpendRuleScreen` lists five `RadioDot` rows (64), in this order. The first four keep today's words and order; the fifth is new and last:
1. existing choices (unchanged, see `spendRuleView.ts`)
5. **"Both can use all of it"** **[exact]**, sub `label` "Each of you can spend any of the shared money."

When the group is a one-earner household (engine reports `zeroShare`), free access is moved to the top of the list and carries a `meta` line "Suggested because one of you brings no money." No auto-switch (D-00b): the group picks. The `ZeroShareLine` copy is replaced in 08.12 by this suggestion, as OWNER-ANSWERS round 2 item 2 states.

Answer lines under free access (in `HouseholdAnswerNote`, `meta`, `inkMuted`):
- Just me answer: "This money is shared. Ben can also use it." The partner's name is the only fact shown about them (FA-03: no balances, no open checks).
- Household answer: "Counted once. Both of you can use it."
- Both people can get "Fits" for the same pot only when this line is present (JA-01).

A `freeAccess` `StatusBadge` ("Free access") shows on the rule row header only, not on every answer.

Selecting a new choice follows Section 5d (before-and-after notice with Undo). The `ApplyToExistingSheet` stays as is.

### 3. Used by both, Personal money, spend limit (D-00b, round 2 items 6, 9-12, 15)

**3a. Used by both (SharedAccountsScreen).** Each account row gets a trailing `ToggleRow`-style switch labelled "Used by both" (the account owner only; others see the `usedByBoth` badge without a switch). Sub-line when on: "Counted like a joint account. {name} can use it." Sub-line when off: "Only you count this money." (The partner's slice is $0.) The mark appears wherever the account is listed, as the `usedByBoth` `StatusBadge` ("Used by both") next to the account name, never as a colour alone. Joint accounts carry the same badge by default and cannot be switched off here. The limit applies to marked accounts (Section 3c, D-00b item 15). Changing the mark follows Section 5d.

**3b. Personal money.** New `SettingsGroup` "Personal money" in `SpendRuleScreen`: `ToggleRow` "Give each person personal money" (default off for existing households; suggested on in the one-earner setup). When on:
- `LabelValueRow` per member: name and amount with a `MoneyInput` (64 each). Default: the same amount for everyone. A top row "Same for everyone" `Stepper`-style with one `MoneyInput`; editing one person's row switches to per-person amounts, with `meta` "Amounts can differ. Everyone sees the same list."
- A `meta` line "Personal money is paid to each person and is theirs. It is not counted under the spend limit."
- A `SafetyLink`.
- Change follows Section 5d.

Missed payment (round 2 item 12, D-00b): when a Personal money payment is not recorded by its date, only the person who should receive it gets a Notices item: "Your personal money of $200 for November has not arrived." Body "It was due Nov 1." (owner 2026-11-28, UI question 4: the fact only, with no prompt to act) It has no action link (owner answer 4). Nobody else gets a notice, a push or a badge. The payer sees no "late" mark. There is no reminder repeat.

**3c. Spend limit.** New `SettingsGroup` "Spend limit" in `SpendRuleScreen`, below the rule choices. Visual anchor of this group: the `ToggleRow`.
- `ToggleRow` "Spend limit" with tile `pinLock`; sub "Ask each other before a big purchase from shared money."
- `MoneyInput` "Ask first over" (shown when on), `Stepper`-free; one number, same for everyone.
- `meta` "One limit for everyone. It counts each item paid from shared money, including Used by both accounts, and the whole price of an instalment plan. It never counts personal money."
- A **start step**: turning the limit on does not start it. It opens `AskCard` state "Waiting" for every member: "{name} would like to start a spend limit of $300." Each member taps "OK" or "Not now". It starts when all have said OK. Until then the toggle shows `StatusBadge` `waitingApproval`. A "Not now" leaves no record and the proposer is told once (Section 5b).
- **Any one member can turn it off** at any time: the same toggle, instant, followed by the Section 5d notice with Undo. No confirm sheet. Copy sub-line when on: "Anyone in the household can turn this off."
- A `SafetyLink` below.
- States: off (default); waiting for everyone; on (`limitOn` badge); offline (the toggle is disabled and the group shows `SyncStatusLine` "Needs a connection").

### 4. Decide answer: "Fits. Needs {name}'s OK" (D-06..D-12)

**Visual anchor:** the verdict card; the `AskCard` sits directly under it.

When the item passes the spend limit (price at or over the limit, paid from shared money, verdict not "Doesn't fit"), the verdict word is unchanged; the answer adds one `AskCard` and the headline reads **[exact base]** "Fits. Needs Sam's OK". With more than one other member sharing the money the headline reads "Fits. Needs an OK from the household." Verdict colour stays `fits`. The `AskCard` always carries the `waitingApproval` or `okGiven` badge so Fits is never read as final.

| State | AskCard content | Actions |
|---|---|---|
| Not asked yet | Title "Needs Sam's OK", sub "Over the household limit of $300." | Primary "Ask Sam" (accent pill, 52). Secondary text link "Talk it over" is **not** here (see Section 5c) |
| Waiting | Badge `waitingApproval` ("Waiting"), title "Asked Sam", sub "Sent Nov 28. Open for 7 days." | Text link "Take back" (cancels; leaves nothing behind) |
| OK given | Badge `okGiven` ("OK given"), title "Sam said OK", sub "Good for 30 days at $1,200 or less." | None. The Record as bought button is now enabled |
| Not now | A one-time toast and card text: "Sam said not now." After it is read, the card returns to "Not asked yet" and no history is kept. The card shows no reason and no count of earlier tries | Primary "Ask {name}" again is available at once |
| Lapsed | Badge `okLapsed` ("No answer yet"), title "No answer yet. Ask again?" **[exact base]**, sub "The request closed after 7 days." | Primary "Ask again" |
| OK expired | Treated as Not asked yet after 30 days; the sub-line reads "The OK ended Dec 28." | Primary "Ask Sam" |
| Price went up | Card returns to Not asked yet: sub "The price is higher than the OK covered. Ask again." | Primary "Ask Sam" |
| Verdict changed after OK | The verdict card shows the new verdict; the OK card stays and its sub-line reads "Sam's OK still stands." **[exact base]** An OK never recolours or rewords a "Doesn't fit" verdict as Fits | None |
| Offline | The Ask button is disabled with sub "Needs a connection to ask." The existing `SyncStatusLine` pattern. The answer itself still works | |

Rules: only members who share the money can be asked; "any one other member" can OK and the first OK counts (D-06). The OK states are visible only to the asker and to members who share the money. The headline "Needs Sam's OK" is declarative: it states a household agreement, not an instruction. The asker never sees the OK-giver's balance, answer or open checks.

Record as bought is disabled (with `pinLock` glyph and the sub-line "Needs an OK first") until OK given, but it only reflects the agreement. FincWin blocks no payment: a text line under the disabled button reads "FincWin does not move money. This records what you agreed." and a link "Record it anyway" opens a `Sheet` "Record without an OK?" with body "Your household agreed to ask first. Others will see this was recorded without an OK." (the one place the agreement is visible when skipped; no shame wording) and primary "Record it". The link stays (owner 2026-11-28, UI question 1).

More-than-half line (round 2 item 7): when one item uses more than half of the shared money, add `meta` `inkMuted` under the verdict: "This uses more than half of the shared $1,000." **[exact]** The verdict does not change and no badge is added. Shown only on checks paid from shared money.

### 5. Requests, notices, push, Talk it over, sharing change

**Visual anchor:** the request item in `NoticesScreen` (a standard notice row with an `AskCard`-style action pair).

**5a. Request notice (D-10).** A Notices item on every sharing member's phone, kind `ask_ok`: `IconTile` 36 `household`, title "Ben would like to buy a Couch for $1,200", sub "Paid from shared money. Asked Nov 28." Two actions: primary-small `Pill` "OK" and text link "Not now". Once any member taps OK, the item is replaced on the others' phones by a plain line "Sam said OK." and then leaves Notices on read. The item never shows anyone's balances, how much shared money is left or what the others' checks say. Tapping the body opens the check (item, price and the more-than-half line only).

**5b. Push copy (D-10).** Only the asker, the item and the price. Title "Ben asked for an OK". Body "A Couch for $1,200 from shared money." No balance, no verdict, no "you should". When push is off, the Notices item is the only channel and the app badge on the You/Household tab counts it. Outcome pushes (one only, to the asker): "Sam said OK." and "Sam said not now." No push on lapse. Quiet hours from Phase 10 apply (`quietHeld` already exists).

**5c. Talk it over (D-00d, D-12).** On any Household or Mixed Decide answer, a text link "Talk it over" sits below the answer's actions, shown only to the person who made the check. It opens a `Sheet`:
- Title "Share this check".
- Body "Your household will see this check as you see it. The answer does not change."
- A `LabelValueRow` list: item, price, verdict word (with its symbol).
- Primary "Share this check", secondary text link "Keep it private" (closes the sheet; nothing is shared), and a `SafetyLink`.
After sharing: toast "Shared with your household." Members get a Notices item `shared_check`: title "Ben shared a check", sub "A Couch for $1,200. It fits." (verdict word and symbol, never colour alone), one action text link "See it" opening a **read-only** view of the answer. There is **no OK and no Not now** on a shared check (D-12). The sharer sees a card "Shared with the household" with a text link "Stop sharing". Stopping removes it from everyone's Notices and leaves no record. The shared check shows the same verdict the sharer saw; it is a snapshot with a date line `meta` "As of Nov 28, 2:10 pm."

**5d. Sharing-choice before-and-after notice with Undo (round 2 item 8).** Any change to the sharing choice, Used by both, Personal money, the limit or its amount creates one household notice, kind `rule_changed`, on every member's phone, including the person who made the change: title "Ben changed how you share", sub "Before: Split by what each brings. After: Both can use all of it." Two lines in `LabelValueRow` form so "Before" and "After" are labelled. Action text link "Undo" (every member can undo for 24 hours; after that the link is gone and the notice remains as a plain line). Undo restores the old value and sends one more notice "Sam undid the change." The person who made the change also gets a `ToastView` "Changed. Undo" with the `undo` glyph, 6 seconds. Notice words never say "allowed" or "not allowed". Extends the existing `spend_rule_changed` and `joint_purpose_changed` kinds in `noticeText.ts`.

### 6. Short money: the gap, Cover choices (D-00c, TB-/IN- cases)

**Visual anchor:** the gap line, directly under the verdict card.

When the shared money cannot cover the item, the answer works from the shared money only and adds, under the verdict, one `bodyStrong` line **[exact base]** "The shared money is $300 short." with the `short` `StatusBadge` ("Short", symbol `stateShort`). Below it `CoverChoiceRow`:
1. "Cover it from my own money" (`ChoiceCard`, sub `label` "Uses your own money for the $300.")
2. "Ask Sam to cover $300" (sub `label` "Sam sees a request. Nothing is added until Sam taps OK.")

With three or more members who share, choice 2 opens a `Sheet` "Who can cover?" listing each member as a `RadioDot` row with "Ask the household" first (sends to everyone; first OK counts).

## Accessibility (acceptance rules)

- Every interactive element has an accessible label that includes state: "Spend limit, switch on, waiting for everyone", "Used by both, switch off, Savings account", "Ask Sam, button", "OK, button", "Sam said OK, good for 30 days at $1,200 or less", "Cover it from my own money, button", "Leave this page, button".
- The held-back line reads "$400 is held back for the household pool. Double tap to see why." The as-of line reads the time in words ("Ben's money as of 2:10 pm"). Badges read their word then their state ("Waiting", "OK given", "No answer yet").
- Verdict plus ask is read in one order: verdict word, price, then "Needs Sam's OK", then the `AskCard` state. The `okGiven` and `waitingApproval` badges carry symbol and word, never colour alone.
- New request notices announce once via `accessibilityLiveRegion="polite"` and do not steal focus. The Not now message is announced once, then removed.
- Focus on opening a sheet moves to its title; on close, returns to the element that opened it. Sheets close on the back gesture and Android back.
- The Money and safety page: `LeaveFastBar` is the first focusable element and is always reachable; on activation the screen reader focus goes to the Home tab with no extra announcement. No content reads as an alert.
- All targets at least 44 x 44. Switch rows are 64 with the switch as its own 44 target and the body as a second target.
- **Text fit at small widths and large font scale:** the Pixel text-fit matrix (402/320 dp; font scale to 200%) applies to every screen. Rows grow to two lines before truncating; names and the long sentences ("Ben's money as of 2:10 pm", "This uses more than half of the shared $1,000.") wrap, never truncate. Amounts, times, dates and counts use tabular figures and never truncate. `CoverChoiceRow` and `AskCard` stack their text and action vertically at 320 dp. The pinned primary `Pill` keeps 52 and the page scrolls above it. Pinned `LeaveFastBar` stays one line ("Leave this page") and never wraps.
- **Reduce Motion:** `RiseIn` and the toast slide are replaced by an instant show. No new animation is added in this phase. The `AskCard` state changes cross-fade only when Reduce Motion is off, and swap instantly when it is on.
- Haptics (names from `src/ui/haptics.ts`): `hapticTick` on switch and choice moments (free access, limit, Used by both, Personal money); `hapticTap` on sending an Ask and on tapping OK; `hapticMilestone` on none (no confetti); `hapticWarn` never; `hapticFailed` only for a save that really failed. "Not now" and "Take back" give no haptic.
- TalkBack pass on Android (and VoiceOver on the iPhone XR check, D-19): Just me held-back sheet, free access choice, Used by both, Personal money, limit start, Decide Ask states, Notices items, short-money choices, Paid from, plan fields, Talk it over, review date, Who's in it, Money and safety. Two-person device check on Pixel 9 and iPhone XR (D-19).
