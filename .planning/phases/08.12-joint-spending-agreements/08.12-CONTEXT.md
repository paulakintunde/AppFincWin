# Phase 08.12: Joint spending agreements - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-11-28
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Groups can record how they agree to spend shared money, and Decide shows the agreement in its answers. The phase covers:

- the pool fix (08.1 review A-CR-02): the same money is never answered "Fits" twice;
- free access ("Both can use all of it"), accounts marked "Used by both" and "Personal money";
- one-earner households;
- the spend limit, with OK requests and "Talk it over";
- what happens when the shared money is short: cover from your own money, personal credit, or instalments;
- an optional review date, the "Money and safety" help page, and the "Who's in it?" setup question.

FincWin only records what the group agreed. It holds no money and blocks no payment.

Most decisions were made in six earlier rounds (2026-11-21) and are recorded in `08.12-OWNER-ANSWERS.md`. That file is **locked input**: read it in full, and do not ask its questions again. This file adds the decisions from the 2026-11-28 discussion and sets the build order.

Out of this phase: Rooms (Phase 08.13; but 08.12's new tables are keyed by room from the start), the full money-meeting screen, savings clubs, splitting bills by days, event budgets, the Family support and Giving pot (see Deferred), and price-rise notices (Phase 10).

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Locked earlier (summary; the full text is in 08.12-OWNER-ANSWERS.md)
- **D-00a:** Round 1. Setup types stay the same. A payment from one member to another counts as money out (not spending) for the payer and money in for the receiver, counted once.
- **D-00b:** Round 2 (FA 1-15). Free access is a fifth choice, "Both can use all of it" (rule `shared`, joint split `whole`). The defaults approved in 08.1 do not change. In one-earner homes, free access is suggested first. The pot goes down when a buy or a planned item is recorded ("Paid from" pick). Your own money counts first. The spend limit uses the safe version: one limit for everyone; it starts only when all agree; any one person can turn it off; it applies per item paid from shared money; "Not now" leaves no record. A line appears when one item uses more than half of the shared money. Any member can change the sharing choice; everyone gets a before-and-after notice with Undo. Every member gets "Personal money", the same amount by default. "Used by both" accounts can be marked. A missed personal-money payment is told only to the person who should receive it. The non-earner's small income stays their own money. The limit also covers "Used by both" accounts.
- **D-00c:** Round 3 (short money: top-up, credit, instalments). All answers are option A. The answer names the shortfall and offers "Cover it from my own money" or "Ask {name} to cover". Another member's cover counts only after they tap OK. The asker sees only "{name} covers $X". A top-up is recorded as a transfer. Nothing is owed unless the payer adds "Paid back from shared money". The limit applies to the whole plan price. Late fees are shown but not added. A plan in one person's name paid from shared money is allowed and names the person. A plan "in both names" counts only after the other person confirms it. The safety page gets one credit-pressure line, and that line needs a compliance check because it must not use "should".
- **D-00d:** Round 4. "Talk it over" is in this phase: only the person who made the check can share it, and the verdict does not change. This phase also adds the optional review date.
- **D-00e:** Round 5. Rooms are Phase 08.13. **The new tables are keyed by room from the start.**
- **D-00f:** Round 6. Keep "Household" as the product word, and show the group's own name first. The setup question "Who's in it?" only suggests a name. Use the region's word: "flatmates" in UK/IE/AU/NZ, "roommates" in US/CA.
- **D-00g:** Owner rule (2026-11-22). Money pooled to the household is held back from the member's own Just me answers, so the same money is never answered "Fits" twice. Test this as a property across scopes.

### The pool fix (A-CR-02): which reading of the hold-back
- **D-01:** **Always hold back the share.** When a member has "count my money" switched on and the rule pools money (`shares`), their Just me answers, home and overview always leave out the share their partners can count. This applies the personal-pool delta in every scope, not only `scope === 'household'` (`src/engine/household/plan.ts`, `pool.ts`). There is no set amount, and no "hold back only what was used".
- **D-02:** **Fresher partner figures.** Each phone still publishes at most once an hour when the app opens. It also publishes right after the member records money in or out, still rounded **down** to `FIGURE_STEP`. The Household answer says how old the figure is, e.g. "Ben's money as of 2:10 pm". The planner sets a sensible minimum gap between publishes to protect the server.
- **D-03:** **Partial opt-ins (3 or more people).** The pool holds the money of only the people who switched on. Each person's money is counted once, and the answer names whose money is in the pool. This fixes today's over-count on Household checks when only some people opted in.
- **D-04:** **One plain line in Just me.** For example: "$400 is held back for the household pool." The verdict uses the smaller amount. The words go to the sign-off.
- **D-05:** The property test from the review is required. It runs A's Household view and B's Just me view (and three-person mixes) together and asserts that the sum fits inside the pool. It also covers free access, where both people may answer "Fits" only when the answer says the money is shared (JA-01 restated).

### How an OK request works
- **D-06:** **Whose OK:** any one other member who shares the money. Everyone who shares the money gets the request, and the first OK counts.
- **D-07:** **How long a request stays open:** 7 days. After that it lapses quietly, and the asker sees "No answer yet. Ask again?"
- **D-08:** **Not now:** the asker is told once ("Sam said not now"). After that, nothing is kept in any history, list or export.
- **D-09:** **How long an OK lasts:** 30 days, for this item at the same price or lower. A higher price needs a new OK.
- **D-10:** **How the partner is told:** a push notice (when push is allowed) plus an item in the household Notices screen with OK / Not now. The push names only the asker, the item and the price. It never shows anyone's balances.
- **D-11:** **OK then the verdict changes:** the OK stays, and the verdict wins. The answer shows the new verdict and says "{name}'s OK still stands". An OK never makes "Doesn't fit" look like it fits.
- **D-12:** The same request flow handles the spend limit, "Ask {name} to cover" (D-00c) and "Talk it over" (D-00d). For "Talk it over" there is no OK and no Not now, only a shared view of the check, unless the planner finds a reason to differ.

### Size, order and leftovers
- **D-13:** **One phase, built in waves.** There is no roadmap split, which avoids the 08.1/08.12 number bug. Wave order:
  1. The pool fix and fresher figures (D-01..D-05).
  2. Free access, "Used by both", "Personal money" and the one-earner setups.
  3. The spend limit, OK requests and "Talk it over".
  4. Short money: top-up, personal credit, instalments.
  5. The "Money and safety" page, the review date, "Who's in it?" and the region word, the server switch, the wording sign-off, the case approval and the release.
- **D-14:** The **"Who's in it?" setup question and the region word go in 08.12** (wave 5), not 08.13.
- **D-15:** **The wording sign-off is one phone page near the end**, as in 08.1: OK or new words per line. It includes the compliance check on the safety page's credit line (no "should").

### Release safety
- **D-16:** **A server switch** (same pattern as `app_config.decide_off`) can turn off limits, requests, "Personal money", "Talk it over" and short-money cover without a new build. It is on for testers. **The pool fix has no switch**, because it is a safety fix.
- **D-17:** **Changed right-answer cases** (mainly Just me in pooled households) go on a phone page that shows each case's old and new answer. The owner approves each case one by one, and each gets a `changeApproved` date (`check:right-answers`).
- **D-18:** **Existing poolers get one notice when the pool fix ships.** It is one household notice, sent only to members with pooling on, e.g. "Your Just me answers now leave out the share your household can use." It links to the setting.
- **D-19:** **Two-person device check:** a Pixel 9 and the iPhone XR, with two test accounts in one household, one on each phone. It covers real push on both platforms. The preview update goes to testers first, and the store build comes after.

### Planner first checks (carried from OWNER-ANSWERS)
- **P1:** Do the shared account's future pay-ins and bills reach the 12-month path? Cases IN-04, IN-06 and IN-07 depend on it. Re-work the near-the-line cases (TB-02, TB-03c, IN-01, IN-07) with the standard floor.
- **FA-03:** Do planned joint lines reach `monthOut` today?
- The right-answer cases are OE-01..12, FA-01..08, plus the top-up, credit and instalment cases from RESEARCH-TOPUP-CREDIT, all restated under the locked answers.
- Check the safeguards against the economic-abuse red lines in `08.12-RESEARCH-ARRANGEMENTS.md`. The limit and "Personal money" must never become a control tool.

### Answers to the research questions (owner, 2026-11-28, during plan-phase)
- **D-20:** **Partial pool (3 or more people).** The pool is made of groups of people who all switched on for each other. Each person is in one group only, filled in join order. The answer names whose money is in the pool. (Refines D-03.)
- **D-21:** **Old or missing partner figure.** Just me still holds money back, using the last figure and saying how old it is ("Ben's money as of Tuesday").
- **D-22:** **"Not now" note.** A note with no item and no price may wait for the asker for up to 2 days so they are told once. It is deleted when read, or after 2 days at most. This is acceptable under D-08.
- **D-23:** **"Record it anyway" line.** It is shown once, on that one item only, with the word "recorded". There is never a count, a list or a history of going ahead without an OK. The words go to the sign-off (D-15).
- **D-24:** **OK request push.** A request is always sent, even when partner alerts are off, because it is a direct question (like settle-up). Quiet hours still apply.
- **D-25:** **12-month path (P1).** Future shared pay-ins, shared bills and plan payments go into each person's 12 months at their share of the shared money, and each dollar is counted once across everyone.
- **D-26:** **Paying a shared item from your own account** creates nothing owed unless the payer adds "Paid back from shared money", the same as top-ups (D-00c). The copy never says "owes".

- **D-27:** **"Record a payment" sheet for Personal money** is approved (plan 13). The payer records the payment once. It lands as money out for the payer and as a private money-in line for the receiver, in the account the receiver picked. Its wording goes to the sign-off.
- **D-28:** **Shared-account visibility is approved, for shared accounts only.** Members of the household may see the amounts and dates of future lines on joint and "Used by both" accounts (`joint_account_ahead()`). Marking an account "Used by both" shows its balance to the partner, and the marking screen says so ("Your partner will see this account's balance"). Private accounts and Personal money stay private.

- **D-29:** **Home headline stays as recorded.** Home's headline (`position.left`, this month's recorded money in minus money out) is unchanged. The D-01 hold-back applies to Home's "left after plans" line and to Decide home's "Your month" figure (plan 04, Task 3).
- **D-30:** **The "recorded without an OK" mark is in the person's own data download** as a yes/no column on their own items only, with no count and no list (plan 11). Plan 19's gate expects exactly one occurrence in the export manifest.
