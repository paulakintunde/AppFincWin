Personal copies: for every non-deleted shared line in p_household with a split row for p_user: insert into H2 a personal transaction (`created_by p_user, is_shared false, copied_from = line id, local_date, name, note, category_id` = p_user's transaction_member_categories override, else the line's category when p_user owns it, else null). If p_user paid it from their own (moving) account: the copy goes on that account with the FULL original amount and currency and `copy_share` = their share (their account balance stays right). Record copy ids.

Do NOT re-create or overload hh_move_rows (adding a defaulted 5th parameter via `create or replace` would create a second overload and make every 4-argument call ambiguous); leave 08-09's `hh_move_rows(uuid,uuid,uuid,boolean)` untouched. Keep the 08-09 allow-list design exactly: iterate `public.hh_move_tables()` only, never every `household_id` table, and do not add household_former_members, member_departures or any settlement/split table to it (they stay in p_household so the remaining members' balances are unchanged, SC6/HH-13).
