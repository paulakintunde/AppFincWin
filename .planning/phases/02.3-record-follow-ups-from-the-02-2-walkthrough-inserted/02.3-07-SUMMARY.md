# Phase 02.3 Plan 07: Remembered accounts and Google flows Summary

Device-side ACC-14: the phone remembers up to three signed-in identities (name, email, provider, last used; no token, no financial data) outside the `fincwin:` wipe prefix, and the Google service gains "Continue as", "Use a different account" and forget flows that AuthProvider exposes and feeds on every sign-in.

## What was built
- `rememberedAccounts.ts`: AsyncStorage key `fincwin-identity:recent-accounts:v1` (not SecureStore, so uninstall removes it). Max 3, most recent first, deduplicated by provider + email case-insensitively. `parseRemembered` validates stored JSON, drops malformed records, truncates names to 200 characters and caps the list at 3. A test proves the list survives `wipeDeviceData()` while other `fincwin:` keys are wiped.
- `google.ts`: `continueWithGoogle` accepts silent sign-in only when the returned Google email matches the tile's email (case-insensitive); otherwise it signs out of the Google SDK and opens the chooser hinted at that email (`loginHint` on iOS, `accountName` on Android), re-running `configureGoogle()` in a `finally`. `signInWithDifferentGoogleAccount` always signs out first. `forgetGoogleOnAccountDeletion` revokes access and signs out.
- `AuthProvider.tsx`: exposes `continueAs` and `signInWithDifferentAccount`; calls `rememberAccount(identityFromUser(user))` on `SIGNED_IN`. `signOut.ts` untouched.
- Docs: ACC-14 section in `docs/ops/auth-providers.md`; draft privacy paragraph in `docs/compliance/privacy-policy-remembered-accounts.md`.
