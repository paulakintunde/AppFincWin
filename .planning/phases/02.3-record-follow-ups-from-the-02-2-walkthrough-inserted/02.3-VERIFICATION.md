## Review findings weighed against must-haves
- **MD-06 (not a gap):** UI path cannot produce the defect. `listForm.itemFromForm` (lines 17-21) and `transactionForm` (lines 317-323) require a parsed received amount whenever currencies differ, so a cross-currency transfer cannot enter the list without one. Only a corrupted or hand-edited stored draft reaches `?? item.amountMinor` in `bulkAdd.ts:82`, and `parseItem` (draft.ts:75-80) does not reject it. This is a validation-hardening defect, not a defeat of REC-30 as delivered. Recommended follow-up: reject in `parseItem` and throw in `buildEntryListWrite`.
- **MD-04 (warning, non-blocking):** `existingChecked` (useStatementImport.ts:462) is true when the range read failed but any unrelated cached transactions array exists. Offline with an unrelated month cached, Reconcile lists every line as missing and could re-import existing lines. This undercuts REC-26's intent in an offline edge case only; online path is correct and device-verified. Recommend fix `existingChecked = rangeRead.ok`.
- **MD-05 (warning, non-blocking):** a permanent RPC rejection after insert leaves rows without an undo step while the copy says nothing changed. Rare, since the RPC validates what the client just built. Recommend follow-up.
- MD-01, MD-02, MD-03: edge-case correctness (archived learned category, silent duplicate rule offer, extras window narrower than period). None defeats a requirement; track as follow-ups.
- LW-01: Remembered-account PII is not purged on in-app account deletion (no caller for `forgetGoogleOnAccountDeletion`; Apple not covered). Deletion flow is a Phase 10 item; must be carried into Phase 10 acceptance.
