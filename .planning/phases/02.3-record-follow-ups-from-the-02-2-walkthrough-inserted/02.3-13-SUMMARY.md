# Phase 02.3 Plan 13: Entry-list save path Summary

`useBulkAdd().save(items, ctx)` inserts every listed entry (rows, transfer pairs, series anchors) in one request and records one `addedMany` undo step through `record_entry_list`.

## What was built
- `recordEntryList` RPC wrapper: validates sizes (1..200 rows, <=61 series) before the call and the response shape (bad-response otherwise).
- `buildEntryListWrite` (pure): single-save sign rule, payment type validated per direction, transfers via `buildTransferLegs` in the Transfer category with status paid, repeating items yield a series anchored to their row. More than one request's rows is refused.
- Queued mutation under WRITE_SCOPE: one insert, then the RPC (also on replay). Failure removes optimistic rows, invalidates transactions, logs ids and counts only, and shows the refusal toast.
- `optimisticTransactionRow` exported from batchInsert and now keeps `transfer_id` (Pitfall 4).
- Registered in `mutations/index.ts`.

## Deviations from Plan
- Row cap is min(IMPORT_CHUNK_MAX, ENTRY_LIST_MAX_ROWS) = 200, so an oversized list is refused before mutate rather than after rows were inserted and the RPC then rejected it. Stricter than the plan's 500 wording, within it.
