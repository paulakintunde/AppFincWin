  <action>
RED (commit `test(01-04): ...`) then GREEN (commit `feat(01-04): ...`).
Implement with BigInt to avoid overflow of total*weight: `S = sum of weights`; for each i: `p = |total| * w_i` (BigInt); `floor_i = p / S`; `rem_i = p % S`. `leftover = |total| - sum(floor_i)` (always < number of weights). Order indices by `rem_i` descending, ties by index ascending; add 1 to the first `leftover` of them. Convert back with `minorUnits(Number(x))` and negate every share when total < 0. No float division anywhere. Docstring: MON-03, deterministic tie-break keyed on member join order, reused by Phase 8 settlements. `src/engine/split/index.ts` re-exports allocate.
