## Finding for the owner

A refund of a payment-plan purchase is classed as a refund (everyday credit) in the purchase month even though the day_one row itself is a debt_payment, not everyday. A credit larger than that month's everyday spending is floored at 0 and can make the month look under-logged. Not changed here (out of scope); worth a look in plan 31.
