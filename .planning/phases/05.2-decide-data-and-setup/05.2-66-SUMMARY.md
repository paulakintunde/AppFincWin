# Phase 05.2 Plan 66: Not my pay (data side) Summary

A "Not my pay" answer is stored as a namespaced recurring_dismissals row, detection skips marked payers, and marking/clearing are undoable with their own labels. No migration.

## What changed

- `notPay.ts` (pure): `NOT_PAY_PREFIX`, `notPayKey` (prefix, cut to 200 chars deterministically), `isNotPayKey`, `notPayMatcher(rows)` (live, direction 'in', not-pay rows only; matching via `notPayKey(candidateKey)` so cut keys still match).
- `useIncomeContext`: reads `useRecurringDismissals`, drops incoming candidates the matcher marks (engine unchanged), `found` now carries `key`, new `notPay: {row, payer}[]` (latest matching history name, else the key's name part).
- `dismissals.ts`: optional `labelKey` on add (`paymentDismissed` | `payNotMine`) and remove (`paymentRestored` | `payNotMineCleared`) vars and hook inputs; used in buildStep and in failed-write `attempted.labelKey`. Defaults unchanged from plan 65. (The failed-write record on remove now says `paymentRestored` rather than the previous `paymentDismissed`; ids-only record, no behaviour impact.)
- `UNDO_LABEL_KEYS` + en.ts: `payNotMine` 'Not my pay · {{name}}' / 'Marked not my pay'; `payNotMineCleared` 'Shown as pay again · {{name}}' / 'Shown as pay again'.
