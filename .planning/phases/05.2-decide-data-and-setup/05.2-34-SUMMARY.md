# Phase 05.2 Plan 34: Decide settings Summary

Decide settings: 16 to 17 rows in five section cards, each with a value and an effect line, any answer editable alone through the stepper's edit-one-step mode, the two sheets wired in, and Reset (setup answers only) with one Undo.

## Behaviour
- Rows in UI-SPEC order, grouped under the five section names. Hidden: Regular pay without a regular pay type, Steady amount outside the steady method, Unusual months under two full months, What each account is for with no money accounts. Unanswered: "Not set"; a needed unanswered row also says "Needed".
- Row tap: Emergency fund -> `EmergencyFundSheet`; Unspent money -> `UnspentSheet` (its "Set them" opens `/decide/setup?only=accountUse`); every other row -> `/decide/setup?only=<step>`. "What each account is for" is the account-use step in edit mode.
- Edit-one-step (stepper `only`): header shows the step name, no rail, no Skip, body mounted with mode `edit`, primary "Save answer" (disabled until the step is changed and valid; a needed step cannot be cleared), "Keep as is", Back with edits shows the shipped "Discard changes?" confirm. Save shows "{Step title} saved" with Undo and returns to Settings.
- Reset: ConfirmSheet "Reset Decide setup?" ("Keep it" / "Reset", danger), `useResetDecideSetup().reset()`, toast "Decide setup reset" with the Undo step; nothing shown when there was nothing to clear. Account use, records, payments, loans, envelopes are untouched (owner decision).
- "Rough" chip and "Finish setup" (opens `/decide/setup?startAt=<first unanswered needed>`) while status is rough.
- Route `/decide/settings` redirects to the Decide tab unless `useDecideAccess().v3`.
