# Phase 05.2 Plan 65: Undo labels and held-Next reasons Summary

All 05.2 undo labels now read as sentence-case labels (for example "Marked not regular · Bookshop"). Clearing "Not regular" records its own label. Single-choice steps no longer say "At least one is needed".

## What changed
- Task 1: rewrote the 05.2 block of `undo.label` in `en.ts` (header comment corrected: shown on their own and after "Undone · "), added `paymentRestored` and the `labelUnnamed` fallbacks (setup answer saved, marked regular, marked not regular, not regular cleared).
- Task 2: `paymentRestored` added to `UNDO_LABEL_KEYS`. `addDismissal`/`removeDismissal` take an optional `labelName`; hooks take `dismiss({..., name})` and `restore(row, name)`. The remove step uses `paymentRestored`. RegularStep passes the payment name; the cleared toast and `DECIDE_TOAST_LABEL` map to `paymentRestored`. No exhaustive `Record<UndoLabelKey,...>` failed typecheck, so no other entries were needed.
- Task 3: new keys `decide.setup.shell.chooseOneReason` and `eachAccountReason`; `CHOOSE_ONE_REASON_KEY` / `EACH_ACCOUNT_REASON_KEY` exported from `shared.tsx`. Calls per step: Everyday, Unspent, Method (single choice) use choose-one; AccountUse uses each-account; Paid (multi-select) and Pay (completeness of amount, frequency and date, not a choice) keep `neededReason`.
- Tests updated/added: undoLabel.test.ts (labels, composition after "Undone · ", sentence case), decideCopy.test.ts (expected map), dismissals.test.tsx (names, `paymentRestored`), regularLoansSteps, UndoToastHost.decide52, spendingSteps, unspent, setupStepperTextFit (everyday and accountUse cases).

## Deviations from Plan
1. [Rule 1 - copy guard] The plan's exact strings 'Choose one to continue.' and 'Choose a use for each account to continue.' open with "Choose", which the existing DCU-09 guard (`decideCopy.test.ts`, INSTRUCTION_LEAD) bans, and the project voice is declarative. Used 'One choice is needed to continue.' and 'A use for each account is needed to continue.' instead (same meaning, same style as the existing "At least one is needed to continue.").
2. The stepper text-fit test covers everyday and accountUse only; the unspent step needs a full V3 inputs mock, so it is covered by `unspent.test.tsx` (reasonKey assertion) instead.
