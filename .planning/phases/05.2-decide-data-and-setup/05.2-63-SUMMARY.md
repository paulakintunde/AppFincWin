# Phase 05.2 Plan 63: Only money accounts are V3 accounts Summary

`buildV3Inputs` now leaves credit and loan accounts out of `inputs.accounts`, so the unspent step and the account-use step count the same accounts (F-05).

## What changed

In the `accounts` flatMap of `buildV3Inputs.ts`, accounts with `kind` `credit` or `loan` return `[]`. The comment says it mirrors `holdsMoney` (`setup/accountUseSuggest.ts`). It is not imported (depcruise).

## Engine readers of `inputs.accounts` (plan-checker grep of src/engine/decide/v3)

- `funds/build.ts:39` `spendingOf`: `use === null` goes to `unstated`; only everyday and mixed add to total, gross and fenced, so a card with a null use added nothing to money. `ids` (line 41) decides whether an envelope or the emergency fund is "unlinked".
- `funds/build.ts:195`: `hasMixed`, which looks at mixed uses only.
- `funds/emergency.ts:43` `liveEmergencyOf`: finds the emergency fund's account in the list.
- `month/refine.ts:209`: filters to uses of everyday or mixed; null never counted.

No reader uses cards for debts or card balances. Engine behaviour was not changed.

Edge case recorded: an envelope or the emergency fund linked to a credit or loan account (an odd link; the account-use step never offers one) would now read as unlinked rather than linked to a card. Not reachable through the account-use step, and no test or demo data does it.
