# Phase 05.2 Plan 67: Not my pay UI Summary

The pay step's found-payer note now has a "Not my pay" button, and payers marked that way are listed with a "Show again" button. Each action writes through the 05.2-66 data path and shows an Undo toast.

## What changed
- PayStep: "Not my pay" calls `dismiss({ payeeKey: notPayKey(found.key), direction: 'in', name, labelKey: 'payNotMine' })` then `showToast` with `undoLabelText('payNotMine')` and the stepId. Each `ctx.notPay` item shows "X isn't counted as your pay." plus "Show again", which calls `restore(row, payer, 'payNotMineCleared')` and toasts with Undo. Neither control touches the cards. Both are hidden when householdId or userId is missing.
- en.ts: `income.pay.notMine`, `notMineA11y`, `notMineLine`, `showAgain`, `showAgainA11y`. The copy is declarative.
- Tests: new payStepNotMine.test.tsx (5 cases). incomeTextFit gains fit/44 dp/a11y-label cases at the whole matrix (320 and 402 dp, font scale 1.0 and 2.0) with a 60-character payer, plus a copy-guard case. Existing income and stepper-fit mocks got `notPay` and a dismissals mock.
