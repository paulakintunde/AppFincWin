<objective>
Make Undo on a toast actually undo (N-04, high). On the Pixel, tapping Undo on the Not regular, Reset, income tag and Stopped toasts dismissed the toast, but the undo_log rows stayed status 'available' with resolved_at null. History "Undo to here" (the rollbackUndo mutation) works on the same steps. Writes made after the tap still land, so the queue is not stuck.

Because History works on the same steps, the server RPCs and the stored steps are fine. The fault is on the client, between the toast's Undo press and `rpc('apply_undo_step')`. Task 1 finds the cause with a failing test. Task 2 fixes it and adds a regression test for each caller family.
Output: a diagnosis, the fix, tests.
</objective>
