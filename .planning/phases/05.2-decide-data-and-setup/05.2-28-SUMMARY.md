# Phase 05.2 Plan 28: Unspent money sum, sheet and step Summary

The unspent money worked sum is now a view of engine output. A card, a sheet (five states) and a needed step sit on top of it. Every confirm is a new anchor with an Undo toast; Clear sets both columns back to null with an Undo toast and no dialog.

## Commits
- 2a2715c0: engine `unspentSum` (the sum behind `funds.suggested`), tests
- 488eed8d: `unspentView` pure model and tests
- a1fbf635: `UnspentSumCard`, `UnspentSheet`, `UnspentStep`, `useUnspent`, registry action, tests

## Behaviour
- Lines: Bank balance, mixed set-aside, bills still due, everyday still expected, envelopes, emergency fund, held back for plans. Mixed, envelopes, emergency and held-back are omitted at 0; bills and everyday are always shown. The lines net to the total.
- States: Not confirmed, Confirmed fresh (rolled-forward line and note), Drifted ("Use {sum}" / "Keep {rolled}" / own-figure link), Stale (days chip), Own figure (Save figure disabled until >= 0 and changed; Keep as is writes nothing). Drifted wins over stale. State comes from the engine's own `drifted` and `stale` flags.
- Step: SegmentedToggle with neither selected; own figure reveals a MoneyInput; needed (reason key until one choice); saves `unspent_anchor` and `unspent_anchor_at` (engine as-of date) with labelKey `unspentConfirmed`.
- Toasts carry no amounts; Undo is wired through the toast `undoLabel` param (`unspentConfirmed` / `unspentCleared`).

## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Rule 3 - Blocking] Engine addition (plan said "stop and report if no engine field").** The card's lines (gross bank balance, mixed set-aside, envelopes, emergency separately) had no engine field: `Funds` only holds the net `spending` and an aggregate `ringFenced`. Rather than recompute them in the UI, I made `spendingOf` in `funds/build.ts` name its parts, split `billsAndErest` and `heldBackOf` out of `suggestedUnspent` / `unspentState` (same values), and exported a new `unspentSum(inputs, month)` through the barrel with an `UnspentSum` type. No existing engine output changed (all 1169 engine tests, goldens included, pass). New tests: Sam, mixed account, unlinked envelopes and fund, overshoot, held-back, and a 100-run property that the lines net to `funds.suggested`. This touches `src/engine`, outside the plan's file list; please review.
2. **[Signature] `unspentSumLines(sum)` and `unspentStateOf(evaluation, sum, today)`** instead of `(e)` / `(e, today)`: the sum needs `V3Inputs` (accounts, envelopes), which `Evaluation` does not carry. `useUnspent` builds the sum from `useDecideV3().inputs`.
3. **[Design call] Held-back money (D-38).** The engine subtracts held-back bridges from the confirmed figure (`available`), so a held-back line in the sum would be counted twice if the total were saved as is. The total shown is `suggested - heldBack`; every displayed figure is in those display units and `toStoredAnchor` adds the held-back amount back on save, so the engine reads back exactly what the user saw. With no bridges (the usual case) this is a no-op.
4. **[Overshoot netting]** When envelopes, the fund and set-aside exceed the gross balance, the engine floors the spending balance at 0. The named deductions are cut (envelopes first, then the fund, then set-aside) so the lines still add up.
5. **[Registry] `editAccountUse()` added to `setupSheetHost`** (plan 24's pattern) for "Set them"; with nothing registered it does nothing. The stepper/Settings host must register it when the unspent step or sheet is wired in (plan 32 / Settings).
6. **[Extra file] `useUnspent.ts`** holds the shared read and the two writes for the card, sheet and step. Not in the plan's file list.
7. **[A11y] The step's radiogroup label is the intro line**, not "Your unspent money", so it does not collide with the own-figure field label.
8. **[Not wired]** The sheet and step are not yet registered in the step table, setup page or Settings; that belongs to the shell and Settings plans. `onSetThem` is a prop on the sheet.
9. **[Stale threshold]** Stale uses the engine flag (more than 30 days), not "30 days or more" from the UI-SPEC text; the engine owns I35.
