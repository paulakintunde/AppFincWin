Make the 05.2 undo labels read properly in toasts and in History (N-01), and fix the held-Next reason on steps where only one choice can be made (minor copy finding).

Root cause of N-01: the 05.2 labels were written as lowercase phrases meant to follow "Undid: " (the header comment in en.ts says so). But `undo.reverted` is 'Undone · {{label}}', and RegularStep and History show the labels on their own. So the Not-regular toast read only "regular payment", and History rows read "income type", "setup answer", "unspent money". The fix rewrites them in the same style as the older labels ("Stopped · {{name}}", "Pot added"). That reads correctly on its own and after "Undone · ".
Output: copy, a new label for clearing Not regular, names on the dismissal steps, reason keys, tests.
</objective>
