# Phase 05.2 Plan 69: Regular confirm fixes Summary

Marking a found bill Regular now stores a money-out series that owns its logged rows, and a replayed confirm still has a complete Undo.

## What changed
- CR-01: `useConfirmRegularCandidate.confirm` takes `direction` and writes `-abs` for out, `+abs` for in. `runConfirm` throws `DbError(..., '23514')` before any RPC if the sign does not match a stated direction. Older persisted variables with no direction skip the check.
- CR-02: `confirm` takes `linkTransactionIds`; `runConfirm` calls `createRecurringSeries` with `anchorTransactionId: null` and those ids (RegularStep passes `candidate.rowIds`). The linked rows reach the undo step through the existing change set.
- WR-01: on `already-applied`, `liveSeriesRows` reads the series' live transactions; rows not in the link list become `inserted`, rows in it become `linked` at their live version. Undo is complete on replay.
