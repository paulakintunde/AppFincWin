<objective>
Make the new decide_settings money columns mean home-currency money, on write and on read (CR-03), and fix the month_marks false change (IN-02, trivial, same file).

Re-verified in code (the claim holds):
- `useStamped` (decideSetup.ts ~229) takes `patch.currency ?? current?.currency ?? null`, never the home currency; with no settings row it stamps nothing.
- `buildV3Inputs` (~511) checks only `reserve` against `settings.currency !== home`; `everyday_override`, `unspent_anchor`, `steady_amount`, `rough_*`, `unexplained_amount` reach the engine as home minor units whatever they were typed in.
- `change_home_currency` (20261010000400) touches profiles and category caps only.

Split of the fix:
- This plan (client, no migration): stamp home on write; drop mismatched settings money on read; invalidate the caches after a home change.
- Plan 05.2-74 (migration, owner-approved push): inside `change_home_currency`, stamp `p_from` on settings rows that hold money with a null currency (so rows saved before this fix read as the old currency after a switch, not as the new one), and soft-delete the user's live `expected_income` and `held_bridge` answers. decide_answers has no currency column. Adding one would mean rewriting the whole apply_patches allow-list function, so a switch clears these two money answers instead, and they are asked again (05.1 D-59: asked again when the figure behind it changes). Those answers have no 05.2 screen (D-13: their sheets are 05.3), so no user has any yet.

Not changed: the emergency-fund save (src/data/mutations/accountUse.ts) keeps its own stamp. Its reserve is already guarded on read, and in account mode its figure is the chosen account's balance in that account's currency.
</objective>
