## Mixed-account rule built
Owner answer to 05.2-37 (e), quoted: "in a mixed account the emergency fund is the stated amount capped at the account's live balance, floored at 0; any other linked account uses its whole live balance, floored at 0; a fund with no link or an unlisted account stays as stored." Built: when the account's resulting use is mixed (already mixed, or the "also holds spending" answer is Yes) the sheet and step show "Amount kept for emergencies", prefilled with the balance (floored at 0, capped at MAX_INPUT_MINOR) until the user types; the typed value is saved. The cap at the live balance is applied by the engine (no change to liveEmergencyOf). A dedicated account keeps the read-only "follows this account" line.
