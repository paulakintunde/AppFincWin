<objective>
Make the setup step body impossible to lose (F-01) and show the rail complete on the Summary (F-15).

Purpose: on the Pixel the season step went blank about 4 s after the "Your regular pay saved" toast and stayed blank (also once on the pay step after an Esc key), while the step key and facts were unchanged. The current `RiseIn` (SetupStepper.tsx ~81) drives `opacity` from a shared value via useAnimatedStyle; after a native overlay or keyboard closes, a React commit can leave the view at its initial animated opacity of 0. The rail's `SegmentFill` uses the same pattern. The fix removes every lasting animated opacity from the setup shell.
Output: a shared RiseIn on layout animations, a rail with static fills, tests.
</objective>
