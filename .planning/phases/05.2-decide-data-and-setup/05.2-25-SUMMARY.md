# Phase 05.2 Plan 25: Spending and Answers setup steps Summary

Five step bodies (everyday, unusual, share, detail, reminders) on the setup step contract, each writing through `useSaveSetupStep` on `save()` and writing nothing when unchanged.

## Behaviour
- **Everyday**: 3+ full past months show the typical-month hero (display figure, nowrap, 1.3x cap) and a Looks right / Change it toggle, neither selected. The figure is the records' median (up to 6 months, marked months skipped, half rounds up), so it stays the records' figure even when an override exists. Looks right writes nothing, or clears `everyday_override` / `everyday_override_at` when one was stored. A typed figure writes both (date = today). Under 3 months: question and field only. A stored override opens as Change it with its figure.
- **Unusual**: up to six tiles from the last full months, tap cycles Normal, Leave it out, Half-logged, announces "{Month}: {status}", columns 3/2/1 by width and scale. `month_marks` keeps only non-normal marks, preserves marks for months not on screen, caps at 12 keys (newest kept), and writes null when empty.
- **Share**: Just me writes 100; sharing writes the chosen 30/50/60/70 chip or a custom 1..99; sharing with no percentage chosen writes nothing.
- **Detail**: short or full, footer line.
- **Reminders**: two ToggleRows start Off; turning one on asks permission, refusal returns it to Off and shows "Notifications are off ..."; only changed flags are written. No amounts anywhere in the copy.
