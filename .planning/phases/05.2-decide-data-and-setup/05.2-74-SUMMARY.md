## Cache-lag case (plan-checker addition)
Covered: src/data/mutations/homeCurrency.ts invalidates `decideSettings(userId)` and `['decide-answers', userId]` on `applied`, and src/data/mutations/__tests__/homeCurrency.test.tsx (plan 71, lines ~67 and ~78) asserts both on applied and neither otherwise. So answers refetch after the switch.

Residual risk (documented, not fixed): a decide-answers write queued offline or in flight across a switch can replay after the server clear and land as a new live expected_income or held_bridge answer entered in the old currency. The server clears only answers live at switch time. Impact is bounded to one user's own answer for one month or check; the user can re-answer it. A fix would need a currency column on decide_answers, which is out of scope here.
