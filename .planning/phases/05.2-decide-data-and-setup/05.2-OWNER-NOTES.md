## Refund re-dating (from the 05.1-18 finding)

**Problem.** In v3, a refund logged in a later month than its purchase (05.1-18 `funds/unspent.ts` roll-forward) restores the full refund in the later month. When the purchase sat inside that earlier month's planned everyday spending, this can lift unspent above its pre-purchase figure and overstate the money available. Today it only shows as `drifted`; no golden hits it.

**Owner rule (accounting practice).** A refund belongs to the month of the purchase it refunds.

Example: a $10,000 purchase in May, refunded in July.

- **Full refund (10,000 back):** July nets to 0, because the refund credit is offset. May carries a refund credit of 10,000 against the purchase, so May also nets to 0.
- **Partial refund (9,980 back, short because of rates or charges):** July still nets to 0. In May, the 10,000 purchase and the 9,980 refund cancel, and the 20 shortfall stays in May as an expense.

**Decisions (owner, 2026-10-26):**

1. **Engine view only.** Records and account balances stay exactly as logged; no rows are written. Decide's maths (unspent, month figures, typical month and its medians) reads the later-month refund as belonging to the purchase month, with any shortfall as an expense of the purchase month.
2. **User links it.** A credit counts as a refund only when the user marks it "refund of…" and picks the original purchase. There is no automatic matching, so a wrong guess can never move money between months.
3. **The shortfall goes to the original purchase's category**, so that category shows the true net cost.
4. **Built in 05.2**, with the data needed for the link (refund-of link and tag). 05.1 closes with today's behaviour, documented as known.

**Open for discussion in 05.2:**

- A refund larger than the purchase (a rate gain): is the surplus income in the purchase month?
- Re-dating changes past months' everyday totals, and so the typical-month median. Recompute history, or apply only from now on?
- Several partial refunds against one purchase.
- A refund of a purchase made on a payment plan (05.1-07 schedules): does it shorten the plan, or count as a credit?
- How a linked refund shows in Activity (tag, and a link back to the purchase).

## Variable and seasonal income: data needed by the 05.1 engine (owner, 2026-10-26)

05.1 gap closure (05.1-CONTEXT D-33 to D-39) adds engine inputs that 05.2 must store and collect:

- **Income pattern (D-34).** Setup asks "Does your income change by season?" and stores `'steady' | 'variable' | 'seasonal'` on the income profile. When the engine finds big income swings that the user hasn't stated, the app asks the user to confirm their pattern.
- **Reconfirmed expected income (D-35).** When history can't tell (fewer than 12 months of records, or a season with no history), the app asks "What do you expect to have coming in by then?". The answer is saved and reused across checks, and only asked again when it's out of date.
- **Held-back bridges (D-38).** When the user presses "Go ahead" on a plan that relies on unspent money to get through the lean months, the bridge amount is stored. Every later check passes it to the engine as held back, and it is released when the plan is paid off or cancelled.
