rollForward builds `all` (paid lines from `months`, sorted by date then id). It uses `all` for: (a) `loggedAtAnchor`, the everyday base in the anchor month up to the anchor day; (b) the settle candidates after the anchor in the anchor month; (c) the main after-anchor loop (`l.date <= anchor.at || l.date > asOf` skipped; refunds reduce anchorMonthEveryday or laterEveryday).
