# Phase 2.2 Plan 15: Sample data RPCs Summary

Server-side sample figures: security-definer seed, clear and exists RPCs, so no client ever holds an `is_sample` write grant.

## What was built
- `seed_sample_data`: membership check (42501), `p_today` within 2 days (22023), refuses with `already-seeded` while samples exist. Inserts 3 sample accounts (Everyday account, Credit card, Cash), the sample category "Weekend trips", 19 current-month lines (including one refund and exactly one foreign-currency line on the credit card: EUR, or USD for a EUR home), and one monthly "Streaming" series materialised through `materialise_series` so occurrences inherit `is_sample`. Amounts are integer minor units: `round(major * magnitude) * 10^exponent`, with a per-currency magnitude table. All inserts are normal inserts, so guards and FX stamping run (`system_restamp` left off).
- `clear_sample_data`: hard-deletes transactions still flagged (soft-deleted included); deletes sample series, accounts and caller-owned categories that nothing references and converts (`is_sample = false`) those still referenced. No `undo_log` row.
- `sample_data_exists`: security invoker, drives the banner.
- Execute revoked from public/anon, granted to authenticated.

## Deviations from Plan
None in behaviour. Two small notes: the foreign line category is Transport; test 48 uses the "Weekend away" line (on the credit card) as the edited line, so both Everyday account and Credit card are converted to real and only Cash is deleted.
