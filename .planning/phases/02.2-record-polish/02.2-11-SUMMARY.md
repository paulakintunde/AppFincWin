# Phase 2.2 Plan 11: Record polish reads Summary

Two security-invoker read RPCs (pending split, paid-before) returning text sums, plus a security-definer `change_home_currency` that converts all of the caller's category caps at stored rates and switches the profile currency in one transaction.

## Tasks

1. Migration `20261010000400_record_polish_reads.sql` - commit c0eff9e
2. pgTAP `46_record_polish_reads.test.sql` (15 assertions) - commit 0fab507

## Behaviour notes

- `change_home_currency` raises 42501 (no user), 22023 (p_today more than 2 days from current_date), 40001 (stored home currency differs from p_from), P0001 with hint `rates-unavailable` (a needed rate is null or older than 7 days before p_today, only checked when caps exist). The profile row is locked `for update`; the `fincwin.system_restamp` GUC is set during the cap update so `is_sample` is preserved, then reset.
- `account_paid_before` also returns `paid_home_sum` and `unconverted` for the all-accounts running figure.
- Expected pgTAP cap values (50000 GBP minor to USD at 0.85/1.10 gives 64706, whole unit 64700; 10 gives 13, clamped to 100) were worked out by hand from the formula, not run through the engine with node.
