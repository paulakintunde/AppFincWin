# Phase 05.5 Plan 10: One calendar and one bill detector Summary

`engine/time/calendar.ts` is the one date calendar, and `engine/recurring/detectRecurring` plus `twoDate.ts` is the one bill detector; Decide's `detectCandidates` is now a thin adapter.

## What changed

- **Calendar.** `addDays`, `addMonthsClamped`, `daysInMonth`, `daysBetween`, `parseDate` now live in `time/calendar.ts` (moved from v3 dates, which re-exports them and keeps its Cadence layer). Private copies of `daysBetween` removed from `recurring/detect`, `recurring/payMatch`, `statement/duplicates`, `statement/extras`, `statement/refundMatch`; `activity/weeks`, `activity/calendar` and `statement/pdf/dates` use the calendar's `daysInMonth`. `recurring/schedule.ts` is untouched (check:recurring-mirror up to date).
- **Detector.** `twoDateClusters` moved to `recurring/twoDate.ts` (exported from the recurring barrel). `detectCandidates` groups rows (per account), runs `twoDateClusters`, then calls `detectRecurring` per group or per two-date cluster, and adds only the dismissed/linked filters, month-end anchor, signed-to-direction mapping and ordering. `RECURRING_WINDOWS`, `RECURRING_MIN_REPEATS`, `AMOUNT_TOLERANCE_DIVISOR` removed from v3 constants.

## Parity result

- Calendar parity property (cadence dates vs `occurrencesBetween`, every freq, 2020-2035, 200 runs) and `daysInMonth` 1990-2100: no counterexample.
- Detector parity property vs the frozen old detector: equal wherever both old windows agreed. One failing draw during development was the known window difference, not a bug: a pay on the 3rd and 17th for 2 months (rows 3 Jan, 17 Jan, 3 Feb, 17 Feb, gaps 14,17,14) was offered as fortnightly by the old detector and is not by the new one (17 is outside Activity's fortnightly window, and 2 months is below the 3-row two-date minimum). The generator now draws 3 to 5 months for two-date pay; the difference is pinned in the boundary table.

## Changed answers for the D-07 page (05.5-16)

Where the two old detectors disagreed, Activity's values won. A series is now offered or dropped differently only when a gap falls in these bands (pinned in `detectorParity.property.test.ts`):

| Cadence | Old Decide window | New (Activity) window | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weekly | 5 to 9 days | 6 to 8 | Gaps of 5 or 9 days no longer offered |
| Fortnightly | 11 to 17 | 12 to 16 | Gaps of 11 or 17 no longer offered (includes 3rd/17th pay seen only as 4 rows) |
| Monthly | 28 to 33 | 27 to 33 | A 27-day gap is now offered (also in two-date clusters) |
| Quarterly | 85 to 95 | 85 to 97 | Gaps of 96 or 97 now offered |
| Yearly | 360 to 370 | 360 to 370 | None |

Min repeats and the 10% amount tolerance were identical. No existing fixture in `month/model` tests changed its result; the only test edits were moving the twoDateClusters tests and dropping the v3 windows-disjoint test (replaced by an equivalent on `INTERVAL_WINDOWS`).
