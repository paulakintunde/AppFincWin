## What it does

- Reads logged months (full-month rule, used months, role of every line), series (bills, pay, other income, Savings), cash, accounts, debts (recorded loans plus debts derived from cards and loans, card minimums with stored or market terms), goals / safety net / envelopes through the Grow connection points, typed estimates, and the stored settings answers.
- Settlement rows (`isSettlement: true`) add nothing to any month line (D-06). Accounts no rate converts are still sent, flagged `noRate` (D-114).
- Category limit lines: one `category_monthly_cap` line per category with `categoryCaps[id]` (categories.monthly_cap), id `cap_<categoryId>`, always on. Rules-document `category_cap` entries are ignored (D-11).
- The old "rough" quick-estimates kind is `typed` here; no quoted tier word appears in either file.
- Input type: `InputsSource` = today's `SnapshotSource` plus optional `isSettlement` on rows and optional `categoryCaps` (record of category id to cap). Today's sources pass unchanged.

## Parity results

Cases run through both paths (14): demo with and without settings, household (USD, EUR, foreign rows, no-rate account), recorded loan with series and due day, card with market / stored / no terms plus a pending charge, typed estimates only, goals with safety net and Savings series, rules-document caps with category caps, category caps only. Every difference is named; no entry is stale. Nothing else differed (cash, months, series, debts, accounts, envelopes, thresholds, settings answers all equal).
