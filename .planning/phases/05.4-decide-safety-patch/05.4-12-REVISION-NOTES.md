**D-45 finding (QE-07, recorded as `plannerNote`).** Mostly already happens. Server: the daily `materialise_recurring` job (migration 20260926000400) writes every occurrence of a recurring bill or pay series through the end of next month (`recurring_horizon`) as a `pending` row, labelled "Expected" in the app, and it stays pending until confirmed paid or received. Engine: `v3/month/thisMonth.ts` counts each live bill occurrence as due until a paid line settles it (`settle.ts`: a pending line never settles; a passing due date settles nothing), and `incomeProfile.ts` counts pay series at their dates (dropped only after the missed-pay grace days) and other pending income only when marked expected. Gaps for the planner: everyday spending that is not a recurring series does not roll over as rows (the engine uses the typical month instead), and `fixture.ts` does not model pending rows, so QE-07 cannot assert the rollover yet.
