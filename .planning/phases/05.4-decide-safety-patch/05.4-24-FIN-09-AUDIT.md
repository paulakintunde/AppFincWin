- **What the purchase takes this month:** on an answer with a funding split, the split's month part and the protected net money it
  uses (SN-03: $300 - $500 = -$200); money from earlier months (spare money still in the account, extra savings in savings) and the
  purchase's own pot are named, not taken from this month (BR-17: "This month ends at $500 - $500 = $0"; D-44 earlier_money_from
  { amount, where }). With no split (Not yet, Short each month, an ask): the whole day-one cost (OD-04: $750 - $600 = $150). A plan
  not judged by cash also loses its payments dated after today and before next month (BR-06's 28 November payment).
- **afterBuy** { lead, month, usualMonth, before, monthName }: before is Decide Home's figure; usualMonth is the usual month less
  the monthly cost, for plans and one-offs with a running cost; a plan leads with the usual month (owner, 2026-11-01), a one-off and a
  D-68 plan judged by cash with this month.
- **Home and the answer (D-24, I64):** `homeFigure` is the stricter (lower) of the readings' month figures (D-42); every answer's
