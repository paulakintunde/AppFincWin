# Phase 05.4 Plan 09: Cross-check calculator Summary

An independent bigint calculator (about 70 lines) is compared with the engine on random fixtures, with lint, depcruise and a verify-gates probe enforcing that it imports nothing from the engine.

## What was built

- `plain.ts`: own bigint maths, written from the engine's documented ladder and the table. Rules: no price is needs_input; month left is money in minus bills paid, bills due, everyday logged and everyday still to come (usual pace over the days left, never less than the unlogged part of a usual month, zero on the last day); cash check first (money in the spending accounts must carry bills due and the everyday still to come until payday, plus the cost) gives not_yet; then month room gives fits_month; then earlier unspent gives fits_unspent; else not_yet. Plan mode has a baseline (level payment from basis-point APR) but is skipped by the comparison until FIN-06/FIN-07 land.
- `rules.ts`: TIGHT_SHARE_BP, FLOOR_DIVISOR, EXPENSIVE_APR_BP, EXPENSIVE_INTEREST_BP restated from the table.
- `pendingRules.ts`: 12 entries (FIN-03, FIN-01, FIN-11, FIN-04, FIN-06/FIN-07, FIN-05, FIN-08, FIN-09, FIN-15, FIN-27, BO-05, ENG-14). FIN-09 is code-only (never skips).
- `crosscheck.test.ts`: `CROSSCHECK_RUNS` (default 1000), `CROSSCHECK_SEED` (printed on failure), skip counts per fix ID, fast-check prints the shrunk fixture.
- `table.test.ts`: every approved case with no pending rule is run through the calculator (zero approved cases exist yet, so it checks 0 today and starts working as cases are approved).
- Guards: ESLint block (restricts the four calculator files, plus the verify-gates probe, to the table schema only), depcruise rule `crosscheck-independent`, verify-gates Probe 6 (both tools must fail on a calculator file importing the engine).
- CI: ci.yml gains cross-check (1,000), `check:stryker-disables`, `check:right-answers` (with `git fetch origin main` first so the check cannot skip silently) and a `mutation-changed` PR job (incremental Stryker over changed engine files only, cache pinned by SHA). nightly.yml: cron 03:17 plus workflow_dispatch, `CROSSCHECK_RUNS: 20000`, `FC_RUNS=10 npx stryker run --force --ignoreStatic`, `timeout-minutes: 340`, uploads `reports/mutation/` as an artifact. All actions are pinned to 40-character SHAs (cache v4.3.0, upload-artifact v4.6.2, checkout and setup-node as in ci.yml).

## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 3 - Blocker] depcruise excluded all `__tests__` folders, so `crosscheck-independent` could never fire.**
Changed `options.exclude` in `.dependency-cruiser.cjs` to keep every other test file excluded but cruise the crosscheck folder (the pattern must leave the parent `__tests__` directory unpruned). Module count rose from 900 to 907, no new violations.

**2. [Rule 1 - Finding] The adapter and engine disagree with the plan's baseline formula in four ways; the calculator follows the engine's documented ladder and the disagreements the adapter cannot express are pending rules rather than silent narrowing.**
- The plan's month-left formula omitted spending already logged; the engine's money-in minus money-out model (and its documented erest floor) is used.
- A purchase also needs a cash check and an earlier-unspent step (fits_unspent), which the plan's one-line ladder lacked.
- Fixtures that need real bill dates or a bills list (a bill part-paid and part-due; an everyday line near a bill amount that the engine reads as that bill's payment; bills-due changing the cash verdict) are skipped under FIN-11.
- A month logged below half the usual pace (records_look_behind) is skipped under ENG-14; a usual month already short (pay below bills plus everyday) and plan mode are skipped under FIN-06/FIN-07, because the engine's 90-day cash look-ahead decides them.

Each skip is counted and printed. A typical 1,000-case run compares about 600 and skips the rest (the calculator and engine agree on all compared cases across seeds 1-3 at 3,000 runs each).

**Total deviations:** 2. No scope creep; the engine was not changed.
