# D-55 Research: three labelled account figures in the app

**Written:** 2026-11-03. **Question:** should the app (Decide answer, account rows, Home) show "Balance on <date>", "Available now" and "Expected at month end", as the review pages now do?

## Plain-English summary

**Recommendation: adopt a reduced form.** Keep all three figures on the review pages. In the app, show the three together only inside "How this was worked out" on Decide. Everywhere else show one figure at a time, each with its own label and date.

Why, in short:

1. **Three figures at once is too many for a first look.** The apps people already know show one number up front and keep the rest a tap away. Banks show two (the balance and the "available" balance), and even those two confuse people.
2. **"Available" is a bank word with a different meaning.** At a bank, available balance is the balance adjusted for pending card payments and held deposits. FincWin's version also subtracts bills due before pay and adds unused overdraft. The two numbers will disagree in the bank's own app, and the user will think one is wrong. Use a different word.
3. **The engine cannot do it per account yet.** Bills are not tied to an account in the engine. "Available now" and "Expected at month end" can be worked out for the whole picture (and for the main paying account), not honestly for each account row. That arrives with the 05.5 month picture and the Bills connection point.
4. **The safety net rule is good as it stands.** Show it as its own line, never added in. That rule applies everywhere.

## 1. Is the split clear? What others show

| App or source | What they show | Takeaway |
|---|---|---|
| Banks and the CFPB | CFPB Circular 2022-06 calls the balance not reduced by pending holds the "ledger balance". "Available balance" is the ledger balance plus deposits made available but not yet cleared, less pending debits. The CFPB notes the two can differ, and that a debit can be covered when authorised but overdraw the account when it settles. | Two figures is the normal ceiling for consumers, and the gap already causes overdraft fees. The bank's "available" does not subtract future bills. [CFPB Circular 2022-06](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/compliance/circulars/consumer-financial-protection-circular-2022-06-unanticipated-overdraft-fee-assessment-practices/) |
| YNAB | The account shows a "Working Balance" (cleared plus uncleared). The cleared balance is used only to reconcile, and sits behind an expand icon. "Available" in YNAB is a category figure, not an account figure. | One main number, the second on tap. Same word "available", different meaning again. [YNAB reconciling help](https://support.ynab.com/en_us/finding-your-cleared-balance-to-reconcile-an-overview-BkH27l_A9.md) |
| Copilot | Dashboard headline "Free to Spend" (for example "$1,380 left"): what can still be spent this month without going over budget. Recurring bills are kept off that chart. | One headline, based on the month plan, not the balance. [Copilot dashboard help](https://intercom.help/copilotmoney/en/articles/6045480-dashboard-tab-overview) |
| Monarch | "Left to budget": planned income minus planned expenses for the month. Monarch says budgets run on expected income and planned spending, not current account balances. | Also a month figure, not a balance. [Monarch budget help](https://help.monarch.com/hc/en-us/articles/360048883631-Understanding-Your-Budget-in-Monarch) |
| Cleo, Emma | I found no official help page giving a formula for a "safe to spend" figure. Reviews say Cleo answers "how much can I spend before payday" on request. | Not citable. Treat Cleo and Emma as unverified. |

**Reading:** no comparable app puts a balance, a "now" figure and a month-end figure side by side as equals. The common pattern is one headline plus detail on tap. The three-way split is more complete than any of them, which is a strength on a review page and a risk on a small phone screen. Unverified: how ordinary users read the three labels. Only a reading test can settle that (round 1 of the planned reading tests is the place).

## 2. Words

| Figure | Review-page wording | Recommended app wording | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Balance | Balance on 3 Nov | **In the account on 3 Nov** | Plain, dated, no jargon. Says what a bank app's balance says. |
| Available now | Available now | **After bills due before payday** (headline form: "Free until payday: $X") | Avoids the bank's word "available". Names the rule in the label, so no footnote is needed. |
| Expected at month end | Expected at month end | **Left at month end (expected)** | Keeps the D-45 word "Expected". It is the same figure as D-22's "this month after its plans", so it must carry that name or a near copy of it. |
| Safety net | Saved in it, kept apart | **Safety net: $X saved. Not counted above.** | Short, declarative. |

**Conflicts with existing words:**
- "Left this month after its plans" (D-22) and "Left at month end" are the same number. Use one phrase everywhere. Recommend keeping D-22's phrase and adding the date ("November, after its plans").
- "Spare money": I did not check where the app uses this. If it means the same as the middle figure, retire one of them. Flag for the planner to grep.
- "Safety net": no conflict, provided it is never inside any of the three figures. Note that a bank's "available" often includes a savings overdraft or linked account; FincWin's must not.

## 4. Accuracy risks and honest labels

| Risk | What goes wrong | Honest label |
|---|---|---|
| Pending card payments not posted | Balance is higher than the true free money. The CFPB circular shows banks already see this gap. | "In the account on 3 Nov. $X pending, not yet taken." Never show a balance without its date. |
| Sync lag (v1.1) | The date on the balance could be stale. | Show the date the balance was last updated, not today's date. Reuse the `recordsAsOf` idea (ENG-14). |
| Expected pay does not arrive | Month-end figure is too high. | "Expected" on every month-end figure (D-45), and list the pay it counts on with its date. Existing `pay_missed`/`pay_not_seen` notices apply. |
| Overdraft | "Available" that includes overdraft looks like free money. | Show the overdraft as a separate line: "includes $X of overdraft you have not used." Never merge into the headline unless the owner chooses so. Past the limit is "Not yet" (D-27). |
| Multi-account | Bills not linked to accounts, so a per-account figure would be a guess. | Show the "after bills" figure only for the whole everyday picture until bills have accounts. Say which accounts are counted. |
| Household money | A partner's money counts only with their opt-in (BO-07 point 6). | Label scope: "Your money" or "Household (shared lines only)". |
| Safety net | Added in, it hides a real cushion being spent. | Always a separate line. |

## 5. Compliance

CLAUDE.md requires declarative voice with no "advice", "recommendation" or "you should". I found no regulator guidance that bans the words "available" or "safe to spend", so treat the following as judgement, not law.

- **"Safe to spend"**: the highest risk. "Safe" is a promise about an outcome and reads as a recommendation. **Do not use.**
- **"Available now"**: reads like a bank statement of fact, and a user may treat it as a promise that a payment will clear (the CFPB circular shows the real gap between authorisation and settlement). Medium risk. Replace it with the rule-naming label above.
- **"Free to spend / Free until payday"**: Copilot uses a close form. It is acceptable only as a label on a stated calculation ("after bills due before 14 Nov"). Not as an instruction.
- **Safe declarative forms:** "After bills due before payday: $X", "Left at month end (expected): $X", "In the account on 3 Nov: $X". Each states a sum, not a permission.
- **Footer line on any three-figure block:** "These sums use your logged records. They are not a bank balance." Wording is the planner's to settle.

## 6. Recommendation and owner decisions

**Adopt a reduced form:**
1. Review pages: all three, as D-55 says.
2. Decide answer: lead with the D-22 figure; show all three, plus the safety net line, under "How this was worked out".
3. Account rows: balance with date and pending note only.
4. Home: one figure, with the three on tap.
5. No per-account "after bills" or "month end" until bills have an account link (05.5 Bills point).
6. 05.5 builds one month-picture function that returns all three, pinned by test to the D-22/D-24 figure.
7. Reading-test the three labels in round 1 before 08.1 copy locks.

**Owner decisions (plain English):**

1. **How much to show in the app?**
   - A. All three everywhere.
   - B. **One figure up front, all three on tap (recommended).**
   - C. Keep it to the review pages only.
   - Recommended B: it keeps the app calm and still shows the full picture to anyone who asks.

2. **What to call the middle figure?** Banks already use "available" for something different.
   - A. "Available now".
   - B. **"After bills due before payday" (recommended).**
   - C. "Free until payday".
   - Recommended B: it names the rule, and makes no promise.

3. **Should unused overdraft be counted in the middle figure?**
   - A. Yes, as D-55 says.
   - B. **No: leave it out of the figure and show it as a separate line (recommended).**
   - Recommended B: using an overdraft costs money (D-26), so folding it into the "free" figure hides that. The review pages could keep A, and the app use B. The owner should note this changes D-55's definition for the app only.
