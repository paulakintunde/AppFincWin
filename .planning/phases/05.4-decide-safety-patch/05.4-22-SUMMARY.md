# Phase 05.4 Plan 22: borrowing costed and compared with saving (FIN-05, ENG-08, UX-13, D-48), at the D-14 owner checkpoint

**A plan that fits and costs 20% APR or more, or interest of 10% or more of the amount borrowed (a home loan skips the 10% line,
D-39c), is now "Fits, but borrowing costs more" when paying the same price at once fits, and "Only by borrowing" when it does not.
An expensive plan's answer shows "Saving for it: ready <month>. Borrowing: costs <amount> more." (or what paying cash leaves), and
every plan answer shows the D-48 lines: the interest as money that could stay with you, that a safety net covers surprises while
you repay, and that more income or lower costs makes repaying easier. A Not yet plan waits for its whole cost inside the 90 days
(BR-12) or for its first payment (BR-13). 36 of 310 corpus answers changed (36 stricter, 0 looser), plus 2 ready dates the answer
key does not carry; all 38 CORRECT by hand. Not approved: Task 3 (D-14) waits for the owner.**
