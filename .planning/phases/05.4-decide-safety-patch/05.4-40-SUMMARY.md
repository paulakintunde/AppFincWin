## Open questions for the owner (engine findings, not changed)

**D96-SALE: a sale that arrives after day 90 can still count toward a purchase dated after day 90.** D-96 says a purchase more than 90 days ahead is counted on day 90, "stricter, never looser". `fullCost/replaces.ts` (line 238) nets a sale against the purchase when the sale arrives on or before the *typed* purchase date, even though the purchase itself is held to day 90 on the path. The same person can get a looser answer by moving the date one day later.

Failing input (from the crosscheck generator, seed 0x0d96): asOf 2026-10-15, pay $1,000, bills $318.50, everyday $591.50, $852.13 in the account, a $1,500 one-off, a $1,230 sale arriving 2027-01-19.
- Bought 2027-01-18: **Not yet** (not_covered).
- Bought 2027-01-19: **Fits, but tight**.

Both dates are held to day 90 (2027-01-12), so under D-96 both should be Not yet. The property leaves out checks with a sale until the owner decides. No engine change was made.

**I9-ROUNDING: a cheaper price can give a stricter plan answer, because of rounding at the cent.** The I9 property ("a cheaper price is never worse", monotone.test.ts) found this once its run count went from 60 to 200. The suite uses a random seed, so the failure comes and goes; this run's was seed 1841994184.

Replayed input: golden person Sam, a plan of 11 payments every 2 months at 11% APR, first payment today.
- Price $1.01: interest $0.08 (7.9% of the amount): **Fits**.
- Price $1.00: interest $0.10 (exactly 10%): **Fits, but borrowing costs more** (fits_by_borrowing).

The 10%-of-the-amount interest test (D-07) flips on a 1-cent rounding difference. On real prices the effect is tiny, but the property is right that the cheaper check answered more strictly. Options for the owner:
- (a) accept it and limit I9 to prices where rounding cannot flip the 10% test (for example $100 and above);
- (b) change the engine to read the test on the unrounded interest (a D-14 change).

Either way monotone.test.ts I9 stays red at 200 runs until the owner decides. No engine change was made.
