## Conflicts with later decisions, and inside the plan (the later decision wins)

1. **One-off money left (the plan against its own I58).** The plan reads fits_month from this month's figure and fits_unspent
   from spare money. That breaks I58: a dearer price that tips into fits_unspent could stop being tight. Built: spare money (this
   month's own figure when unknown) + extra savings above the net's size - what the purchase takes, for both codes. The answer is
   the same on every approved case. It differs on audit-p10 and golden #21 (stricter) and on gen:37 (extra savings count; not tight). Owner question 1.
2. **D-68 (later) against FIN-06 / I57.** A short plan judged by cash may fit an already-short month (refinement a: the D-65 tier).
   I57 makes an exception for answers with short_plan_from_cash, and the one-off tight rule applies to them for the whole cost.
3. **D-63 / D-65 (later) against "a losing variant keeps its fact".** The month-short headlines outrank tight, and tight_floor is
   left out under them (its copy follows the tight body).
4. **D-71 (later) and the variant order.** D-71 put costly debt above extra savings. The plan puts tight above costly debt, and it is
   built that way (Dana). Owner question 3.
5. **QE-08.** The plan expected it to keep its FIN-09 tag; it passes, so the runner requires it out of knownRed.
6. **verdictRowFor.** The plan picks the one-off row "when pay.mode is once"; the function has no check, so it takes `once`. A plan
   whose deposit comes from earlier money keeps the plan words on fits_unspent (row fits_unspent.tight_plan, not in the plan).
7. **Copy placeholders.** The plan's `{left}` is written `{{left}}` (the i18n convention).
