# Phase 05.4 Plan 17: overdrafts Summary

**Balances are now signed throughout v3. An account below $0 but inside its overdraft limit gives "Uses your overdraft", with how far below, the days below $0 and a daily-sum cost labelled an estimate. If the next pay does not clear it, the answer is "Uses your overdraft past payday" and the warning comes first. Going past the limit, or below $0 with no limit recorded, is Not yet, and the no-limit answer asks whether the account has an overdraft. The owner approved the 5 changed answers (D-14).**

## What changed

- **Signed balances (ENG-02).** These are signed now:
  - the spending balance (`funds/build.ts`);
  - the ladder's earlier money;
  - the ready-month gap (`readyMonth`);
  - the test fixture's unspent anchor.

  A negative month funds nothing, but its shortfall is never taken off twice, because it is already inside unspent.
- **Floor and cash check (D-27, D-28).** The floor is minus the sum of limits on counted checking and current accounts. `cashCheck` returns `overdraft` when the low point is below 0 but at or above the floor; exactly at the floor counts as inside (I-limit). Past the floor it is payday / not_covered, as before.
- **Uses your overdraft (D-26, D-36).**
  - Facts: `overdraft_use` (deepest point, days below 0, cost), `overdraft_without_purchase` and `overdraft_purchase_adds`. "Adds" is the cost with the purchase minus the cost without it, each rounded first.
  - The path without the purchase comes from the same prepared open path, never a second prepare.
  - `not_cleared`: the balance on the next pay date after asOf is still below 0, or no pay lands inside the 90 days. It adds `overdraft_not_cleared` (first), `overdraft_cost_90d` and `wait_clears` (the judge's ready month).
- **Cost (D-36).** Cost = divideHalfUp(sum of each day's amount below 0 × rate, 100 × scale × 365). It uses integer bigint maths; no floats.
- **Repeat use (D-37).** Without the purchase, the balance is below 0 on the day before at least 2 of the next 3 paydays. Read from the existing path; nothing new is stored.
- **No limit (D-38).** Facts: `no_overdraft_limit` (a question, with the actions Add limit / No overdraft opening the account's settings), `clearing_first` when the account is already below 0, and `ready_without_interest` when there is a ready date and no rate is set.
- **OD-08.** `other_account_overdrawn` (below, rate, monthlyCost) names a counted account that is not the payer and is below 0. It is a fact only.
- **Copy (en.ts).** All wording is declarative:
  - Verdict "Uses your overdraft" / "Uses your overdraft past payday".
  - 11 fact strings, plus the `_no_rate`, `_no_pay` and `_above` variants.
  - `factAction.addLimit` / `noOverdraft`.
  - The banned-word, instruction-verb and copy-contract tests pass. "Add limit" is on the control-label list.

## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Rule 1 - Correctness] Not-cleared wording.** The plan's "staying below {zero} until {month} costs about {cost}" would present the 90-day cost as if it ran to the clear month. It now reads "At {{rate}}%, the next 90 days below {{zero}} cost about {{cost}}. This is an estimate." The verdict titles ("This goes into your overdraft until payday." / "...and payday does not clear it.") keep the chip phrase on one line each, as the grep acceptance needs. Commit 2bdf9e25.
2. **[Rule 3 - Interpolation] Double-brace placeholders and code-named fact keys** (`decide.v3.fact.overdraft_use`), following the en.ts convention, as 05.4-16 did. Rate and cost are left out when no rate is set, and date when no pay lands in the 90 days. Each case has its own copy variant.
3. **[Rule 1 - I47] The overdraft covers only the real dip.** Letting the overdraft cover any ladder shortfall would make a lower balance give a better answer when the user has said money is spoken for. A unit test pins that case as Not yet.
4. **[Rule 3 - I44] Separate arbitrary.** `OverdraftTweak` / `overdraftInputsArb` replaces new fields on `Tweak`, so the existing draws and digests are unchanged.
5. **[Rule 3 - Harness] Savings with no use.** `fixture.ts` maps a savings account with no use stated to `untouched`. Otherwise OD-07 asked the account-use question about an empty savings account.
6. **[Rule 1] Signed earlier money.** The ladder's earlier money is signed, which also tightens the emergency rung when unspent is below this month's room. No corpus answer changed because of this.
7. **I18 and the cash check.** I18 now treats only payday / not_covered as a failed cash check; an overdraft dip does not count as failed.
8. **[Rule 2] Cross-check coverage.** The cross-check now draws overdraft fixtures (lowered balances, limits, rates; about 57 compared overdraft answers per 3,000). `duesDecideCash` (the FIN-11 skip) also skips any dip when bills are still due and a limit is set. Without that, the engine (which does not see this month's overdue bill) and the plain calculator disagree on the dip size.
9. **OD-04 stays in knownRed** (re-tagged FIN-09), against the plan's acceptance line; see the knownRed table.
10. **TDD order.** Tests were written with the implementation, not as a failing commit first.
