## The rule as built

- **Floor:** the user's own line when set (`V3Inputs.tightLine`), else a tenth of the usual month's bills plus everyday
  spending, rounded up to the cent. Card and loan minimums and regular saving are not spending (DB-02).
- **Plan (FIN-07):** tight when the monthly cost is more than 80% of the usual room, or the room left after it is under the floor.
- **One-off (D-40):** tight when the money left is under the floor (never the 80% share). Money left = spare money after this
  month's plans (this month's own figure when spare money is not known) + extra savings above the safety net's size - what the
  purchase takes from them. A D-68 plan judged by cash reads the one-off rule for its whole cost.
- **Order:** tight leads every fit variant except the D-63 / D-65 month-short headlines (D-59; above low month and costly debt as
  the plan says). Never on Dips into your safety net or Uses your overdraft (D-50 (2)). A losing variant keeps its fact.
- **Short month (FIN-06):** a usual month at or below $0, or a plan whose first payment falls this month while spare money after
  this month's plans is below $0, is Short each month for any earner, before any other monthly rule.
