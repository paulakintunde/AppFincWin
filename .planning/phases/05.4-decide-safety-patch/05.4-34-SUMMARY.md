# Phase 05.4 Plan 34: Dev harness, Maestro flows and on-device engine timing Summary

This plan adds a dev-only harness route at `fincwin://decide/dev-harness`, with two parts: an engine timer and a Record-as-bought harness. It also adds three Maestro flows with run notes. The engine was timed on the Pixel 9: judgeCheck p95 was 2.81 to 2.85 ms against the 8 ms budget. The Back flow is green on the device. The two flows that write data are not run yet, because the phone is signed in to a real account and not the sample account (see Open items).

## What was built

- **Gate (`harnessEnabled.ts`).**
  - The route opens when `__DEV__` is true, or when the build was made with `EXPO_PUBLIC_PERF=1`.
  - The Record-as-bought harness opens only when `__DEV__` is true.
  - On device, a release-mode bundle with PERF=1 showed only the engine timer (checked).
- **Route.**
  - `app/(app)/decide/dev-harness.tsx` sits inside its own `Stack.Protected guard={harnessEnabled()}`, outside the DECIDE_OPEN guard.
  - The screen also redirects to Home (`APP_HOME_HREF`) when the gate is off.
  - Nothing links to it: the grep check returns 0.
- **Engine timer (`EnginePerfHarness`, `heavyAccount.ts`).**
  - The heavy account has the same shape as the one in prepare.test.ts (12 x 120 lines, 20 series, 6 loans, 4 accounts). It is built app-side, without test helpers.
  - The checks are the shadow sample set: $60, $500, $2,000, and $9,000 paid at once or on a 48-month plan.
  - Each run does `prepare` once, 20 warm-up calls, then 200 timed calls.
  - Timing uses `performance.now()`, with nearest-rank p50/p95. `judge:start`/`judge:end` perf marks are also sent.
- **Record-as-bought harness (`BoughtWriteHarness`, `sampleBought.ts`).**
  - The tap handler creates an open check named "Maestro test laptop" and waits until it is saved: it watches the query cache, never an effect, with a 30 s limit.
  - It then records the sample through `useRecordBought`. The sample's Purchase and Result come from the real v3 engine.
  - The sample is 120,000 minor units, with a 20,000 deposit, 4 monthly payments at 0% and a 1,500 monthly running cost. It writes a day-one row, a payment series, a running-cost series and a debt. This is checked in USD, GBP and EUR.
  - An undo toast carries the step.
- **Maestro flows.** `back-stays-short.yaml` (read-only), `save-offline.yaml` and `record-as-bought.yaml`, plus shared subflows in `.maestro/common/`. The flows that write undo their writes through History > Undo to here. `docs/ops/maestro.md` has the install, dev client, run and timing steps.
