# Phase 05.4 Plan 55: D-69 generated prices tied to pay, plans 3 to 60 months Summary

**Every generated price is now a share of the person's usual month's money in (90% between 1% and 300%, 5% below, 5% above up to 1,500%), and every plan runs 3 to 60 months with its payment count set by the term and how often it is paid, in both the shadow generator and the crosscheck. No engine file and no reviewed data file changed.**

## The distribution (binding, D-69)

- **Price** = max($1.00, base x share), rounded down to the cent. The base is the same one the costs use: pay, or a freelancer's stated steady amount (70% of average pay). The share is drawn in basis points:
  - 1% to 9.99%: 10% of draws
  - 10% to 49.99%: 30%
  - 50% to 149.99%: 30%
  - 150% to 300%: 20%
  - 0.1% to 0.99% (kept low extreme): 5%
  - above 300%, up to 1,500% (kept high extreme): 5%
- **Plan term**: 75% take a usual term (3, 6, 12, 18, 24, 36, 48 or 60 months), 25% any whole number of months from 3 to 60.
- **Payment count**: every week term x 52 / 12, every 2 weeks term x 26 / 12 (both rounded), monthly the term, every 3 months term / 3 rounded down (at least 1). The old `count` draw (2..24) is gone.
- The draws sit inside the existing `fc.noBias` record, so the weights hold.
- Unchanged from 05.4-51: costs 50..125% of pay in the same bands, deposits 0/5/10/20% of the price.
