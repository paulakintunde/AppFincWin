**How to read the money figures (D-55).** Every balance in the inputs is the balance on the check date. The test adapter (`golden/adapter.ts`, `statedAccounts`) puts the person's total minus the safety-net fund in the main account, never below $0. The engine reads `accounts[].balance` and `balance` as the figures on `asOf`. On the page:
- "Balance on <date>" is what is in the account that day.
- "Available now" is the balance, less pots, plus any unused overdraft, less bills due before the next pay.
- "Expected at month end" is the balance less pots, plus pay still to come this month, less the bills, everyday spending and saving still to come.
- The safety net row shows "saved in it", kept apart from available now.
- The money saved above the net's size counts as spare (D-35). The engine adds it when it opens the 90-day path.
