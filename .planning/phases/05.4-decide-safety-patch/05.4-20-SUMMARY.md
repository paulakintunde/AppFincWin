# Phase 05.4 Plan 20: card and loan minimums on the 90-day path, counted once (FIN-10), owner-approved

unpaid part this month), and a minimum that is also one of the bills is counted once (the DB-07 double count is gone: Pat's
usual month goes from -$700 to $800). Debt, housing and joint-loan fact lines show by default and never change the answer;
spending money saved in earlier months while a debt at 20% APR or more remains is the amber "Fits, but you carry costly debt";
on quick estimates or thin records Decide asks about the loans, cards and overdraft not recorded. 10 of 310 corpus answers
changed (1 stricter, 9 same code with a new headline), all 10 CORRECT by hand. The owner approved all 10 (D-14) and added D-71..D-73; D-72 moves one answer (gen:145, FIN-10b), also approved.**
