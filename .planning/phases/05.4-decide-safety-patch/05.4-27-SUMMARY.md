## Rules as built (plain words)

- **A stopped bill** (D-104): the recorded bill's own dues after the stop date are given back on the 90-day path (a due on or before
  the stop date is still paid: SO-05's $80 on 1 December). The usual month gets back what it counted (a monthly bill its amount, a
  yearly bill left out of the usual month nothing). A yearly bill that stops before its due date is not held back any more.
- **A stopped everyday cost** (D-103): its monthly share (amount / months, never more than the usual everyday spending, or its
  category's) comes back at the end of each month that starts after the stop date; this month never changes (SO-06: $100, tight,
  usual month $1,050).
- **A sale** (D-105): not counted before today. It pays its debt first, up to what is owed on the day it arrives; only the rest
  reaches the account. A paid-off debt's payment, interest line and costly-debt caution stop after the sale; a part-paid debt keeps
  its full payment (D-102, SO-04: $750, fact "$2,000 is still owed"). A sale arriving by the purchase date lowers what the purchase
  needs on day one (SO-01: $500 of her own money, $500 left); one arriving later is on the path from its date and the ready date
  counts it from its month; a payday before it is not the ready date (SO-02: ready December 2026).
- **The usual month after buying** gets back what stops, and so does the low month; a usual month already short before the purchase
  is judged as it is (never looser).
- **judgeBoth**: cash = the same check paid once (no deposit, no plan fees); plan = the check itself, only when it is a plan.
- **saveForItReadyDate** (D-108): money in kept savings set aside for it counts like the item's own envelope, up to what those
  accounts hold; money already counted is never added again. Fits now = the purchase date; Not yet = its ready date (a month reads
  as its last day, D-80); anything else = null. SO-07: 31 January 2027; SO-08: 1 December 2026.
