## Errors found while building

1. **Engine error, fixed (Rule 1).** The crosscheck (with debts now drawn) found that a costly debt whose balance is so small
   its monthly interest rounds to 0 (9 or 11 minor units at 29.9%) was not counted as "a debt at 20% or more remains". Fixed in
   debts.ts before the reviewed diff; it changes no corpus answer.
2. **Engine error, not fixed (out of scope, owner question 4).** On quick estimates, the everyday spending still to come is
   worked out from the estimates' whole spending (bills + everyday), while the bills are also counted: QE-13 Zoe has £1,080 of
   "everyday still to come" instead of £80, so she gets "Not yet" instead of "Fits, £600 left"; QE-01 asks for more records
   for the same reason. Present before this plan (the old engine gives the same answers). QE-13 stays knownRed, re-tagged ENG-11.
3. **Test-data note.** The shadow generator can give a debt a minimum above its balance (gen:136: $707.08 on $587.32), and the
   engine takes the minimum every month without stopping at the balance (owner question 3). gen:136 stays Not yet either way.
