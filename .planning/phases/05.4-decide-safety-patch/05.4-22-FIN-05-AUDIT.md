## The rule as built

- **Expensive (D-07, D-39c):** an APR of 20% or more, OR (not for a home loan) total interest of 10% or more of the amount borrowed.
  FincWin's lines, not law. Nothing borrowed is never expensive. The rate is the typed rate, else what a quote implies.
- **A plan that fits and is expensive is `fits_by_borrowing`:** variant `borrowing_costs_more` ("Fits, but borrowing costs more")
  when the same purchase paid once fits this month or from spare money; variant null ("Only by borrowing") otherwise (cash not yet,
  cash only with the overdraft or the safety net, or cash that asks for a figure first). A cheap plan keeps its answer (BR-03; the
  0% plans BR-07..BR-09, BR-11, BR-14; BR-15's home loan at 5.5%). The code never depends on the cash route, so more bills or less
  money never make an answer looser (I10, I54, I55).
- **save_vs_borrow** shows on every expensive plan answer whose cash route does not ask (QE-12: on Short each month too), except a
  home loan cash cannot pay. kind `cash_fits` { left, more }, `ready` { readyBy, more }, `with_net`, `with_overdraft` or `no_date`.
  `left` is the money really left (D-75: spare money after the price plus extra savings still above the net). `more` is the
  interest plus the plan's fees.
- **D-48 lines on every plan answer, whatever its code** (not on an ask; never hidden by the fact-lines switch, D-50 (3)):
  interest_is_a_cost { interest, total } when there is interest (never twice: the depletion line counts),
  net_covers_surprises, income_or_costs_first.
- **Ready date of a Not yet plan (FIN-05, BR-12, BR-13, BR-19, BR-20):** a plan whose payments all land inside the 90 days waits for
  its whole cost; any other plan waits for what it takes on day one and its first payment. The later of that and the date as judged
  leads; spare money that already covers it adds nothing (a payday wait stays the payday).
- **Order:** save_vs_borrow sits right after the depletion and overdraft lines (they lead, I-D54); a D-63 / D-65 month-short
  headline keeps the lead as the variant on `fits_by_borrowing` (three new rows sharing the month-short copy).
- **Home loans (D-39c, BR-15):** PayV2.housing skips the 10% line; the new payment joins the housing_cost fact line.
