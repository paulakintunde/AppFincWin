## Notes for the rule plans and owner review (05.4-12)

- **OD-10 rule (owner question).** "Uses your overdraft" here needs payday to bring the account back above $0. 05.4-17's rule as written ("90-day low point below $0 but above the floor") would call OD-10 a fit if a big enough limit were set. OD-10 has no limit set, so it is Not yet under both rules. The difference only shows with a limit.
- **OD-09 / QE-11 vs the audit.** For A7 the audit said "Not yet". These cases say "Short each month", following settled disagreement 5. The owner should confirm.
- **"Not set" vs "protected by default".** SN-01 (no record) holds nothing back. A real user who has saved nothing gets protection on with the starter (SN-11), so the whole starter is held back and many purchases become Not yet. This is the literal Decision 2 / D-05 rule. Show it to the owner next to SN-01.
- **Reason names.** Cases use `quick_estimates`, `few_months`, `pay_gap`, `records_synced` and `debt_due_day_assumed`, as in this plan. 05.4-15 calls the first one `quick_estimates_used` (a ReasonCode). 05.4-16 sorts `result.reasons` by ConfidenceReason. The rule plans must make `result.reasons` carry these names, or `pick.ts` must map them.
- **Fixture adapter gaps** (already pending under their fix IDs): variable income with a low month (QE-03..05 use `pay.lastThree` as the nearest field), bill due dates and the bills list, savings accounts used as a bridge, and the safety-net account.
- **New fact codes these cases expect:** `covered_from_savings` (QE-12, from 05.4-23) and `housing_cost` (DB-07, from 05.4-20). They are named in later plans, not in `types.ts` yet.
