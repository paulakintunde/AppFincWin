## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Rule 3] The fixture schema could not describe three of the cases.**
   - A4 needs payments every two weeks, and A18 needs a 0% deal that charges back the held-back interest. The plan fixture now has two optional fields: `every: 'month' | 'fortnight'` and `deferredInterest`. `fixture.ts` maps them to a two-weekly cadence (with termMonths left null) and to `deferredInterestTerm`.
   - RC-09 needs a running-cost line the user has switched off, so FixtureRunningCost gets an optional `off` field. It is not mapped yet, because running costs are not mapped at all yet (ENG-14 / BO-05).
   - If a field is missing, the case behaves exactly as before, so batch 1 is unchanged. The full decide suite passes.
2. **[Rule 1] The TX-01 spike's numbers did not agree with each other.** It said "$3,100 spare", but $600 of everyday spending was still to come, which leaves $2,500 free. Its expected answer was `not_yet` with no ready date, even though pay on 1 November ($1,200) covers the shortfall. It is now `not_yet`, variant `payday`, ready 2026-11-01, with the situation and `why` rewritten. ENG-29 was added to its fixIds.
3. **Scope additions.** The plan listed 36 cases. Seven extra audit cases bring this batch to 41: BR-11 to BR-14 and RC-10 are new, and BR-01 and BR-10 were given A3 and A2. The table total is 85, at the top of the widened 65 to 85 range. No "fails on purpose" checks are left.
4. **Plan case BR-02 adjusted.** At 20% over 12 months the interest would be 11.2%, so both D-07 limits would apply at once. BR-02 uses 6 months instead (interest 5.91%), so that only the APR limit applies. BR-03 uses the same term at 19.9%, which gives a clean pair of cases either side of the limit.

## Owner questions (for 05.4-12 / 05.4-14 review)

- **A16 (RC-10).** FIN-11 as written holds back £700, which makes a £1,450 buy Not yet until December. The auditor said the old Fits was fine because Hana saves £700 a month, and that the rule matters for people who save very little each month. Which should win?
- **Mortgages and D-07.** Any long mortgage costs more than 10% of the amount borrowed in interest, so under D-07 as written every home loan would get "Only by borrowing". RC-06 avoids this by using a cash purchase. A home-loan rule is FIN-30, in a later phase.
- **Borrowing plus tight (BR-01).** The answer is fits_by_borrowing with no tight variant. Should both conditions show?
- **Buy now pay later (BR-12).** Judging it like cash gives May 2027. The audit's engine gave November 2026. Should a later ready date look at when the user could start the four payments?
