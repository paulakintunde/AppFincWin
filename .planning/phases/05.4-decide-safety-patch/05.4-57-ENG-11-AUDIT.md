# 05.4-57 ENG-11 hand audit (D-14)

Fix ID: **ENG-11** (quick estimates: count the bills once). Two named causes:

- **ENG11-QE-BILLS**: with quick estimates and no usable history month, the everyday figure was the estimates' whole money out
  (bills + everyday), and the bills were then counted again as bills. Now everyday = max(0, money out - usual bills).
- **ENG11-QE-ESSENTIAL** (found while planning): when the quick estimates state the essential part of everyday spending, the
  D-51 starter safety net uses that part. Absent or null keeps the old rule (all of the everyday estimate counts as essential).

## Root cause

The app's quick-estimates spending is `rough_spending`, labelled on screen "Money out each month" (`src/i18n/locales/en.ts`,
`decide.rough.out`, line 2832: `out: 'Money out each month'`): bills included. `typicalOf` took that whole figure as the
everyday spending E, and `thisMonth` / the 90-day path then counted the bill series on top.

Zoe by hand (QE-13, fixture as approved; November 2026 has 30 days, check date 22 November, so 8 days are still to come):

- Quick estimates: pay £2,500, money out £1,300 (= £1,000 bills + £300 everyday). The fixture also records the £1,000 of bills
  as a monthly bill series (recurringBills 100000), paid this month (billsPaid 100000, billsDue 0). Everyday so far £220.
- Before: E = 130000. Still to come = max(halfUp(130000 x 8 / 30) = 34667, 130000 - 22000 = 108000) = 108000 (£1,080).
  Money out = 100000 + 0 + 22000 + 108000 = 230000; November left = 250000 - 230000 = 20000 (£200). £600 does not fit.
- After: E = max(0, 130000 - 100000) = 30000. Still to come = max(halfUp(30000 x 8 / 30) = 8000, 30000 - 22000 = 8000) = 8000
  (£80). Money out = 100000 + 22000 + 8000 = 130000; November left = 120000 (£1,200); £1,200 - £600 = £600 left.
- The 90-day path carried the same double count: from December each month had £1,300 of daily everyday on top of the £1,000
  bill series (£2,300 out instead of £1,300).
- Starter safety net (D-51): before, bills 100000 + essential 30000 (no history month, so the marked category cannot be read and
  all of the everyday estimate counts; that 30000 was itself 130000 - 100000, the one place the bills were taken out) = 130000,
  so her £1,200 net was "small". With the stated essential part (£200): 100000 + 20000 = 120000, full.
- QE-01 (same Zoe, £25 card minimum on 28 November, £300 net, £1,150 price): before, November left 250000 - 100000 - 22000 -
  108000 - 2500 = 17500; under-logged: 22000 x 30 x 100 = 66,000,000 < 50 x 130000 x 22 = 143,000,000, so "needs a figure"
  with under_logged. After: 50 x 30000 x 22 = 33,000,000 < 66,000,000, not behind; November left 117500; £1,175 - £1,150 =
  £25 left. Its starter under D-51 is £1,000 + £25 + £200 = £1,225.
