# 05.4-57 ENG-11 hand audit (D-14)

Fix ID: **ENG-11** (quick estimates: count the bills once). Two named causes:

- **ENG11-QE-BILLS**: with quick estimates and no usable history month, the everyday figure was the estimates' whole money out
  (bills + everyday), and the bills were then counted again as bills. Now everyday = max(0, money out - usual bills).
- **ENG11-QE-ESSENTIAL** (found while planning): when the quick estimates state the essential part of everyday spending, the
  D-51 starter safety net uses that part. Absent or null keeps the old rule (all of the everyday estimate counts as essential).

## Errors found while building

1. **The usual month from quick estimates does not take out recorded card or loan minimums, or a regular saving
   (ENG11-QE-SETASIDE). Not fixed here; deferred.** The first crosscheck run that drew quick-estimates people (seed 0, 1,000 runs)
   disagreed on one person: pay $1,000, estimates money out $910 (bills $318.50 + everyday $591.50), two loans (minimums $30 on a
   $0 balance and $60.01 on $59.70 at 20%). The plain calculator's usual month is $1,000 - $318.50 - $591.50 - $90.01 = -$0.01,
   short, so it names the D-65 tier "Fits from extra savings, but your month is already short". The engine's usual month on
   estimates is income - money out - typed debt - typed saving - typed invest = $1,000 - $910 - $0 = $90 (the recorded minimums
   are not taken out), so it is not short and the answer shows "Fits, but you carry costly debt". With the same person on 3
   months of records both sides say the D-65 tier. ENG-11 did not cause it (that formula was left as it was, as the plan says),
   but drawing quick-estimates people found it. Deciding how typed debt and saving combine with recorded minimums and saving is
   an owner choice (owner question 3), so the crosscheck draws quick-estimates people without a card, loan or regular saving
   for now, said in fixtureArb.ts. In the app today a quick-estimates user's snapshot carries no bills, series or debts
   (buildV3Inputs.ts header), so no app user meets it yet; it bites once 05.5 lets records sit beside estimates (D-43).
2. **The plan's "no bill series gives the same £80" is not right, and the plain rule is restated to say so.** With no bill series
   recorded, Zoe's £1,300 is all everyday spending (counted once, as everyday): still to come is £1,080, and November still
   leaves £1,200 (£2,500 - £220 - £1,080), the same money left, split differently. The plan's plain rule
   (`usualEveryday = quickEstimates.everyday`) would have dropped her £1,000 of bills altogether in that case (£2,200 left), so
   plain.ts reads "money out (bills + everyday) less the usual bills she has, never below 0", which is QE-01's "the rest of her
   £300 everyday estimate" whenever the bills are recorded. table.test pins both.
3. **No app user is affected by ENG11-QE-BILLS today.** Same reason as 1: the app's quick-estimates snapshot carries no bill
   series, so the bills it would subtract are £0. The fix matters for the right-answer cases now and for 05.5 (records beside
   estimates).

## Owner questions

1. **The essential part (ENG11-QE-ESSENTIAL).** The starter safety net now uses the part of everyday spending the quick estimates
   say is essential (Zoe: £1,000 bills + £200 = £1,200). The app's quick-estimates sheet does not ask for that part yet, so app
   users keep the old, larger starter (all everyday spending counts as essential) until the 08.1 screens ask it. Keep this?
2. **QE-01's starter is £1,225, its wording says £1,200.** D-51 (decided a day after QE-01 was approved) puts card and loan
   minimums in the starter: £1,000 + £25 + £200 = £1,225. The case's wording says "£300 of its £1,200"; the case pins no size, so
   the runner does not fail on it. D-51 wins as built; should the case wording be updated to £1,225 (through D-03)?
3. **Recorded minimums and saving on quick estimates (ENG11-QE-SETASIDE, deferred).** Should the usual month from quick
   estimates also take out the card and loan minimums and the regular saving that are recorded (and, if so, instead of or on top
   of any debt and saving typed in the estimates)? Not built; the crosscheck leaves those people out until it is decided.
