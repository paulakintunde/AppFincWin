# Phase 05.4 Plan 25: sales tax by region (FIN-27, ENG-29, ARC-25, CMP-F24), owner-approved

**Every check now adds sales tax: the rate the person typed, else their state's or province's usual rate (an estimate, named in a
fact line), nothing in the UK and EU (prices include VAT), and nothing with a plain "Tax not included. Set your region to add it."
when the region is unknown, including the US or Canada with no state or province. The "Price already includes tax" switch adds
none. Tax is part of the up-front cost and of what a plan finances. No default APR exists anywhere. 0 of 310 corpus answers
changed (the corpus has no regions); TX-04, TX-05 and TX-09 now pass. Owner approved FIN-27 on 2026-11-04 (D-90..D-93).**
