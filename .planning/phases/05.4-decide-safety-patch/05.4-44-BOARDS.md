## 1. Board-gap method (Task 1, read only)

For each kit task 1-16:

1. Open the kit task. Write down its question as read aloud and the boards it shows (ids such as C6, C17).
2. Open each board on canvas version 1793593885-664c. Write down the chip, the title, the lead figure, every line under it, and the bottom of the answer.
3. Compare it with section 3 of this file. Mark each element:
   - **matches**: same words, give or take figure values.
   - **words differ**: quote the board's words and the en.ts key that replaces them.
   - **answer differs**: under today's rules the engine gives a different chip for this case shape (section 3 says when and why).
   - **missing**: an en.ts line section 3 requires is not on the board.
   - **board only**: on the board, but with no en.ts key (layout, buttons, cards that are 08.1 wording). Leave it unless it breaks a rule in section 2.
4. Check section 2's rules on every board the kit shows.
5. Hand the executor a list of the form `Task N | board | element | status | board words -> en.ts key`. It holds no reader data.

The 16-task kit may number or word tasks differently from the 14-task text kit this file was built from. If it does, map the tasks by answer kind, and note the mapping at the top of the list.

## 2. Rules every round-8 board meets

- Chip and title come from `decide.v3.verdict.<row>` exactly. One chip per answer.
- The lead figure is the `decide.v3.afterBuy` line (D-57: one lead figure). The three account figures sit under "How this was worked out", not on the face.
- Under every answer, in this order: the basis sentence (`decide.v3.basis.*`); at most one caveat (`decide.v3.caveat.*`, D-119 order); then `legal.decideAnswer`:
  - "Worked out only from what’s recorded here. The choice is yours."
  - link "What this can’t see"
  - "FincWin is a planning tool, not a lender."
- At most one next step, stated as a fact (`decide.v3.nextStep.*`).
- None of these words anywhere on a board: "advice", "recommend", "you should", "suggestion", "overspent", "room", "safe", "Yes", "free" (as in "free help"), "untouched" when there is no net, "leaves little", "Rough / Fair / Firm".
- No filled buttons on answers. Every rate that was not typed is labelled `decide.v3.tax.exampleRate` "Example rate only. It is not your rate and is not saved." or `decide.v3.plan.estimatedRate`.
- The safety net is **locked by default** (D-61). A locked amount is never shown as usable. "Dips into your safety net" appears only after the unlock step.
- Money help reads `decide.v3.fact.free_help` "{{name}} gives money help at no cost." The name comes from FREE_HELP: GB MoneyHelper, US NFCC, CA Credit Counselling Canada. It shows on every answer whose usual month is short (D-123).
- Figures: keep each board's persona figures. Where section 3 says the answer changes, recompute with the rule given there and write the arithmetic in the board's mockup note, so the owner can check it. The right-answer table decides the maths, not the board (VC-02).
