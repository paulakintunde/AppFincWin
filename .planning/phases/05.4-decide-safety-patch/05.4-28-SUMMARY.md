# Phase 05.4 Plan 28: price limits, accounts with no exchange rate, the unsaved note, The cheaper one (ENG-15), owner-approved

**What this plan does:**
- Decide asks for a price when it is empty or 0, and calls any price over about US$100,000,000 "too large to check". This works in every currency and in both pay modes.
- An account with no exchange rate now counts nowhere and is named in a note. It is no longer dropped silently.
- Unsaved changes are a note on top of the answer and never touch the sums.
- The options list gains "The cheaper one" and is ordered by need or want.

**0 of 310 saved answers changed.** The 2,000 recorded results (I44) are unchanged. The owner approved ENG-15 on 2026-11-05 (0 changed answers).

## Rules as built (plain words)

- **Price limits (D-109, ENG-15):**
  - The limit reads the typed price, before tax and fees.
  - No price, 0 or below asks for a price. Above the limit asks with `price_too_large` ("That price is too large to check.").
  - This holds when paying at once and on a plan; an incomplete plan keeps its own asks too.
  - Limits: $100,000,000; £80,000,000; €90,000,000; C$100,000,000; ¥20,000,000,000; ₩100,000,000,000.
  - Rupiah and any currency not in the table use the database cap.
  - `exceeds_range` stays for other overflows (fees past the safe range).
- **No exchange rate (D-114, ENG-17):**
  - Such an account becomes, inside prepare, an untouched account holding 0. A safety-net fund in it follows its live 0, and an envelope in it has nothing saved.
  - It stays listed, so nothing in it reads as "linked to no account" and triggers a question.
  - Each answer names it: `account_no_rate { account }`, "No exchange rate for {{account}}, so it is left out."
- **Unsaved changes (D-113, APP-02):**
  - The note is first on every answer: "Some changes are not saved yet. This answer counts them once they save."
  - The confidence reason `unsaved_changes` and the next step `save_changes` (with its en.ts line) are gone.
  - The answer is otherwise identical (I7, I74).
- **The cheaper one (D-111, D-112, UX-28):**
  - Shown when 0 < cheaper price < price.
  - Carries price, perYear and pickedPerYear, each = price / years of use. Years are read to 2 decimals and rounding is half up. Both are null when years are empty or round to 0.
  - It is optional on `Futures` (absent when not offered).
- **Order (D-110, D-41):**
  - `orderOptions` filters a fixed rank table.
  - Need = necessity, for_work.
  - Want = every other purpose, including "Invest in me" and custom purposes.
  - No purpose = today's order with the cheaper one last.
  - The I-D41 explorer clause (futures.test.ts): any two purposes give the same options and figures.

## I74 reading of D-115 (please confirm at D-14)

"Takes this month below $0" is checked as: the month was at or above $0 before buying and is below $0 after.
- A plain Fits never does that.
- Another fit may do it only with a caution.
- A month already below $0 before buying counts as "already that low".

Finding while proving it: 16 of 4,000 random checks are plain "Fits" plans in a month that started overdrawn (an overdraft carried in, D-86). The plan's payment later this month lowers the already-negative month a little more, for example -$805.00 to -$805.16. The answer leads with the usual month and carries the move-money and carried-overdraft facts. No saved answer has this shape, and no fit took a month from at or above $0 to below $0 without a caution. If the owner wants a caution here, that is a new rule for a later plan.

**Owner, 2026-11-05 (D-14): "Later plan".** Deferred rule, recorded in 05.4-CONTEXT.md's deferred list: **a plan that makes an already-overdrawn month more negative carries a caution.** Found in 16 of 4,000 random checks (for example -$805.00 to -$805.16); no saved answer has this shape.
