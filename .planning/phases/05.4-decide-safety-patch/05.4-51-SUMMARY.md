## Task 1: realistic generated people; no deposit at or above the price

The distribution is stated in the generate.ts header. In minor units:

- **Pay:** 150,000..1,200,000. A freelancer's months swing by 80..120%.
- **costShare** (bills + everyday + debt minimums as a percent of pay; for a freelancer, of the stated steady amount): 50..90 at weight 15, 91..105 at weight 4, 106..125 at weight 1.
- **Bills:** billShare is 35..60% of the costs, split by 2..6 weights of 1..10 on days 1..28. The last bill takes the remainder.
- **Debts and everyday:** debt minimums are 1..6% of pay each. Everyday = max(10% of pay, costs - bills - minimums), swinging 85..115% a month.
- **Other draws:**
  - one-offs: 10..60% of pay
  - September close: 0..300% of pay
  - emergency fund: 0..600% of pay, or none
  - envelopes: saving 0..10% of pay a month
- **Plan deposit:** 0/0/0/5/10/20% of the price.
- `historyArb = noBias(shareHistoryArb).map(resolveShares)`. `buildPerson` and the near-line generator keep the resolved shape.

  - the 200 corpus histories: 160 / 31 / 9
- **Already-short usual month in the 200: 25** (13 salaried, 12 freelance), inside 10..60.
- **Plans:** 387 generated plans, 212 with a deposit, every deposit below its price.
