On the same day and the same data, a check's answer said "November remaining after this −$3,101.25", with the notice "November is heading about $3,101.25 overspent". The check was $35, so the price does not explain a gap of about $28,700.

- **To find out first:** which basis each screen uses (typical month vs. this month so far vs. the "Judge on" choice from 05-D-107), and whether one side counts November's expected income and the other doesn't. The owner had just answered "November is up to date as it is" before the check.
- **Done when:** a test pins that home's month figure and the answer's "remaining after this" use one month picture and differ by exactly the purchase (plus any stated reason, such as a first payment date).

- **Why it matters:** it implies a safety the user doesn't have. The fix plan's safety-net decision (Decision 2) changes this wording anyway. Until then, the line should not show when no safety net exists, or should say there is none.
- **Done when:** with no safety net set, no answer claims one is untouched.
