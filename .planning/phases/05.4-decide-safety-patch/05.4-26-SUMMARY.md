# Phase 05.4 Plan 26: running costs, closing costs and the purchase date (BO-05 part A, ENG-29, D-42), owner-approved

**A check can now carry Variant C's running costs (amount, every 1, 3 or 12 months, a start date and an optional end date, each
line switchable), home closing costs as a % of the price, and the day it is to be bought. Each running cost falls on the 90-day
cash path on its own dates from its start; the usual month after buying loses each line's monthly share, so "Short each month" and
"tight" read the running costs as a payment. Closing costs are paid up front. A purchase date moves the up-front money to that day,
and a purchase in a later month also has the usual leftover of the whole months before it. Every answer states its purchase date.
All ten running-cost cases pass; 0 of 310 corpus answers changed. The owner approved BO-05-A on 2026-11-04.**

## Rules as built

- **Running costs** (runningCosts.ts): a line counts only when switched on with a positive amount and a cadence of 1, 3 or 12
  months. Outflows fall from its start date (the day clamped to short months, worked out from the start each time) to its end date,
  inside the path; a start or end that is not a real date counts from today with no end (never fewer costs). The monthly share is
  amount / months, half up, per line; a line that has not started still counts (a usual month once it starts, RC-03); a line ended
  before this month does not (RC-05). The buyer's household share is applied once.
- **Closing costs** (closingCosts.ts): price x % / 100, half up; up front with a one-off and with a plan's deposit; never financed.
- **Purchase date** (purchaseDate.ts): the day-one flow moves to it (held to day 90); the schedule keeps its own dates. In a later
  month the ladder adds laterRoom (positive usual leftover x whole months between) and the tight money-left figure adds it too;
  this month's figure loses nothing and unspent_before_after is not shown.
- **Usual month line:** afterBuy.usualMonth is set for a one-off that lists running-cost lines, even all off (RC-09).
