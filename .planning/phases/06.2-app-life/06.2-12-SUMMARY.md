# Phase 06.2 Plan 12: Activity calendar (L03) Summary

The Activity calendar now matches board L03: each day's dot grows with what was spent that day (4 / 6 / 9 for under 50, 50 to 200, over 200 in home major units) in the existing dot colour, a day with an unpaid bill shows the 16 slate clock (`Glyph stateDue` in `stateColors.notYet.color`), a legend under the grid names the three sizes (whole-unit home-currency amounts) and the clock, and tapping a day opens `CalendarDaySheet` (the app's own Sheet: 0.26 s rise, no bounce, drag to dismiss, Reduce Motion) titled with the long date ("Friday, Oct 9"), showing the day net and the day's rows through the caller's `renderRow` (or the default ActivityRow). The inline list under the grid is gone. Each cell's spoken label adds the band ("spent under £50", "spent £50 to £200", "spent over £200") and "bill due". Cells keep the 44 minimum and the view keeps its prop API.
