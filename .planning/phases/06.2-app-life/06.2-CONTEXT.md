# Phase 06.2: App life - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-11-14
**Status:** Waiting on owner answers (see "Open questions for the owner"), then planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

The app stops feeling bland without turning into a game. The owner's aim is **"calm and confident"**: it should feel alive but serious about money. The owner's complaints that started this phase (06.1 device check, 2026-11-12) were: icons missing, no motion or feedback, and low contrast between the background and the cards.

This phase delivers, across the screens that exist on main:
- FincWin's own icon set (SVG, in the tab-icon style) on every row, replacing the letter tiles and the CSS-drawn arrows.
- One shared status cue map (colour + shape + word).
- Tidy sizes: six spacing steps, two row heights, two icon tile sizes.
- Quiet motion at key moments and haptics that each mean one thing, with a Haptics switch in You.
- A very pale theme-tinted page background behind white cards, contrast-checked.
- Empty screens with an icon tile and one button; the save moment (fade in, toast with Undo, light tap).
- Animated icons at real milestones (Lottie, subject to the open question on installing the player).

Source of the design: the owner-approved plan page "Bringing life to the app, calmly" (2026-11-05) and boards L01-L08b. The phase starts now (owner, 2026-11-14), right after 06.1 merged to main (9472c6f3), with room for an audit and fixes afterwards ("start building, room for audit and fixes later").

Not in this phase: new features, new data, engine changes, Insights / Household / Welcome screens that are not built yet (they adopt the kit when their phases build them), Decide answer-board redesign (kept as built in 06.1).

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Direction
- **D-01:** The aim is "calm and confident". Quiet motion at key moments only, an icon on every row, nothing game-like. No confetti, bursts or rewards for spending or investing; no looping or bouncing animation (including tab icons); no emoji; no mascot.
- **D-02:** Keep the original Overview (Home) structure and card order. Improve within each card (icons, cues, spacing, motion); do not move, merge or remove cards. Where the plan page suggests Home layout changes (range choice and hide-amounts in the header row, health score as one row, trend line 92 to about 64 tall, quick actions as four 48 tiles in one row), they apply only inside the existing cards and without changing the card order.

### Icons
- **D-03:** FincWin draws its own icon set in the tab-icon style: solid shapes in one colour with a lighter second layer. Not Phosphor or any other library (option A on the plan page). Board L07 holds the set (55 icons); the plan page's list of about 45 is the minimum.
- **D-04:** Icons are static SVG (react-native-svg, already installed) in rows, tiles, chips and badges. Animated icons at moments are Lottie (see open question Q1 on installing the player).
- **D-05:** Three icon sizes: 16 (next to words in chips and badges), 20 (inside a 36 row tile), 24 (inside a 48 big tile for quick actions and empty screens). Tile corner 11.
- **D-06:** Every icon has a word beside it. Icon-only is allowed only for search, close, back, more and add (each still has an accessibility label). One icon per row, plus at most one small status icon. No icons on section headers.
- **D-07:** `IconTile` replaces today's letter tile (`src/ui/CategoryGlyph.tsx` shows a first letter). The letter stays as a fallback for any category whose icon is not yet drawn. Custom categories pick an icon from our own set, never emoji.

### Sizes (changes the prototype pixel rule)
- **D-08:** Six spacing steps, named: hair 4 (icon to its word), tight 8 (chips in a row, tiles in a grid), row 12 (inside a card, icon to text), card 16 (card padding), edge 22 (screen edge, as now), section 28 (between cards and groups). Gaps between groups are at least twice the gaps inside them.
- **D-09:** Rows have two heights only: 52 (one line) and 64 (two lines). Layout left to right: 36 tile, 12 gap, title and second line, amount or status, then a real arrow icon. Tap areas stay at least 44 on iPhone and 48 on Android.
- **D-10:** Icon tiles are 36 (row) and 48 (big). The current 32/34/38/42/52 tile sizes and the 13/15/18/21 gaps go.
- **D-11:** This overrides the BUILD-PROMPT §2 / 00-UI-SPEC rule "exact prototype pixel values, do not round these" in `src/theme/layout.ts`. When the phase lands, update that comment and CLAUDE.md to state the new scale.

### Cues
- **D-12:** Every status shows colour, shape and word together (for example ✓ Fits, ◐ Tight, ▲ Over, clock Not yet, padlock Safety net). Nothing relies on colour alone. One `StatusBadge` map (24 tall) is shared by Decide, goals, caps, bills and import.
- **D-13:** Decide state colours (the seven state tokens from 06.1 D-16) appear only in badges and amounts. Tiles keep their category colour.
- **D-14:** Spending is not red. Red means over a cap, short, or debt. Money in gets a "+". Ups and downs get an arrow and a word ("up 12%").

### Motion
- **D-15:** Durations: press, switch, chip 0.1 s; fade or value swap 0.15-0.2 s; card or sheet appears 0.22-0.26 s; bar or ring fill 0.55 s, first view per session only; animated icon 1-1.3 s, plays once. At most two things move at once and one animated icon at a time. Nothing waits for an animation: content is readable and tappable at once.
- **D-16:** No count-up or spinning numbers on Decide. The answer card fades in over 0.22 s and its badge icon plays once; on a changed input the changed words swap and the icon replays only if the verdict changed. Nothing moves while the user types.
- **D-17:** Reduce Motion on: movement becomes a quick fade and animated icons show their last frame (extends `useReduceMotion` / `useMotion` in `src/theme/motion.ts`).
- **D-18:** The This-month bar on Home animates by stretch (scaleX with a left origin), not by width (`src/features/home/ThisMonthCard.tsx` animates `width` today).
- **D-19:** No confetti anywhere, and nothing at 25/50/75% goal progress. Milestone animated icons never celebrate spending, a "fits" answer, streaks or deleting an account.

### Haptics
- **D-20:** One meaning each. Light tap: saved, answer shown, swipe done. Warning: over a cap, short. Success: goal reached, debt cleared, import done, and nothing else; never for a purchase that "fits". Error: a save that really failed. Tick (selection): choosing a date, switch or answer. No haptic on tab change. Use only `notificationAsync` / `impactAsync` / `selectionAsync` (expo-haptics SDK 57), through `src/ui/haptics.ts` renamed by meaning.
- **D-21:** A Haptics on/off switch goes in You (default: see Q4). All haptic calls go through one helper that reads it.

### Colour and background
- **D-22:** The page background becomes a very pale tint of the user's chosen theme colour (green, navy, rust, slate); cards stay white; the contrast tests check text on the background and the card edge against the background for every theme colour. Owner decision from the 06.1 device check (2026-11-12). This overrides CLAUDE.md's "the §2 token set is the complete palette ... no additional colours" rule for this one family of tokens; update CLAUDE.md when the phase lands. Exact tint rule: see Q3.

### Screens and parts
- **D-23:** Decide keeps the 06.1 answer boards, state colours, padlock and coloured switches. Only Decide home recent checks change: plain rows with the state icon as the tile (board L08/L08b). Light tap for every answer, plus warning when short.
- **D-24:** Shared parts to build (plan page "Shared parts to build"): Glyph set (16/20/24), IconTile (36/48, corner 11, letter fallback), Row change (52/64, left slot for tile/avatar/ring, amount slot, status slot, real arrow), StatusBadge, SectionHeader (28 tall, count or "See all", no icon), StatTile (about 88 tall, two across), ProgressRing (36 as a row tile, 124 large), EmptyState change (48 tile + one heading + one sentence + one button), motion kit (new timing steps, once-per-session check, play-once Lottie wrapper, haptics by meaning).

### Claude's Discretion
- Wave order inside the agreed screen scope (see Q2), plan split, and which existing components are refactored versus wrapped.
- The once-per-session store (in memory versus a small persisted flag) as long as fills run once per app session.
- Which milestone animated icons ship in this phase: only those whose feature exists on main (for example goal reached, debt cleared, safety net built, import done, first entry, saved offline, bill paid if a paid action exists). Household joined, Welcome and Export ready wait for Phases 8, 9 and 10.
- Toast detail (48 tall, icon, one line, Undo, thin time-left bar, one at a time) and sheet header details from the plan page, fitted to the existing `ToastView` and `SheetHeader`.

</decisions>

## Open questions for the owner

Plain-English questions for things not yet decided. Each has a recommendation; plans are written once these are answered.

**Q1. Add the animation player (Lottie) now?**
The moving icons (goal reached, padlock closing and so on) need a new piece of the app called lottie-react-native. It is native code, so the app on the phones cannot get it by a quick update: we need a new test build and a new preview build.
- A. Add it now, in the first wave, so the next preview build carries it. Until a phone has the new build, the app shows the icon's still last frame instead of crashing. *(Recommended)*
- B. Skip Lottie. Draw the moving icons with the animation tools the app already has (Reanimated and SVG). No new build, but each animation is hand-coded and harder to change.
- C. Wait. Ship everything else first; add Lottie and the moving icons at the end of the phase.

**Q2. Which screens come first?**
- A. First: the shared parts (icons, tiles, rows, status badge, motion, haptics, background tint) plus Activity and Home. Then You, Accounts, Categories, empty screens and toasts. Then Grow, Decide recent checks and Import. *(Recommended: biggest change first, and Activity shows icons on every row)*
- B. Everything built at once in one big wave.
- C. Activity only first, check it on the phone, then decide the rest.

**Q3. How pale should the background tint be?**
- A. A fixed recipe: the theme colour mixed into white at about 5%, worked out by code for each of the four theme colours.
- B. Four hand-picked pale colours (one per theme colour), saved as named colours and checked by the contrast tests, with the card edge visibly clearer than today. *(Recommended: easiest to tune by eye and safe for tests)*
- C. Keep today's warm off-white and add only a hint of the theme colour.

**Q4. Should the Haptics switch in You start on or off?**
- A. On. People who do not want it turn it off; phones with vibration turned off in system settings stay quiet anyway. *(Recommended)*
- B. Off, so nobody gets surprised, and people turn it on.

**Q5. Where do the icon drawings come from?**
The board L07 has 55 icons, but the saved copy here only has the viewer, not the drawings.
- A. Export the L07 board files (the SVG source) so the build copies them exactly. *(Recommended)*
- B. Let the build redraw them from a screenshot of L07, then you check them on a board.
