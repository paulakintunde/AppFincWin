### `header/HeaderIconButton.tsx` (component, request-response)

**Analog:** `shell/header/BackButton.tsx`

**Core pattern** (lines 15-28): 36px circle, `hitSlop` computed from `touchMin`, `fill1`. Deviation required by UI-SPEC: pressed state is opacity `PRESSED_OPACITY`, not a `line3` background swap.
```tsx
<Pressable
  accessibilityRole="button"
  accessibilityLabel={t('a11y.back')}
  hitSlop={(space.touchMin - dim) / 2}
  onPress={onPress}
  style={({ pressed }) => ({
    width: dim, height: dim, borderRadius: dim / 2,
    alignItems: 'center', justifyContent: 'center',
    backgroundColor: pressed ? colors.line3 : colors.fill1,   // replace with fill1 + opacity: pressed ? PRESSED_OPACITY : 1
  })}
>
```
Imports to copy: `useT` from `@/i18n`, `useTheme` from `@/theme/ThemeProvider`, `size, space` from `@/theme/layout`; `PRESSED_OPACITY` lives in `theme/layout.ts` (line 19), not `tokens.ts`. Use `size.backCompact` (36) for the circle. Badge: absolutely positioned `accent` circle, `tabLabel` text in `surface` with `scale="capped"`, wrapper `importantForAccessibility="no-hide-descendants"`, label carries the count. Hit areas butt together (hitSlop 4 each side).
