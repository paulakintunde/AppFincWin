**D-60 (user, 2026-10-15, after the expansion replan; extends D-23 to income).** Confirmed weekly and fortnightly pay series are smoothed for the income averages and steadiness: in each averaged month, the paid income lines of such a series are replaced by its monthly equivalent (latest amount × 52/12 or × 26/12, D-26, via `monthlyEquivalent`). This reverses the replan's reading that only bills are smoothed (04-REPLAN-NOTES first section, interpretation 3, and expansion default 9 — both superseded by D-60). Three readings taken where D-60 is silent, recorded for the user: (1) a month that holds no paid line of the series gets nothing added — D-60 replaces a month's occurrences, so it never invents pay for a month before the job started or after it ended (bills differ: D-23 adds a bill's equivalent to every month); (2) smoothing changes the month figures themselves, so incomeWorst and incomeBest move with the averages and the CV; (3) (checker W2) only months the series spans in full are smoothed — the series had started by the month's first regular payday (`anchorDate < addDays(monthStart, interval)`, interval 7 weekly or 14 fortnightly: had the rhythm been running, no payday of the month would fall before the anchor) and it does not end in or before the month (`endDate`, or with `occurrenceCount` the last scheduled date `addDays(anchorDate, interval × (occurrenceCount − 1))`, has a month later than this one, or is null). A partial first or last month keeps its logged lines, so a job that starts on 23 January with one payday that month counts January at that one payday, not a full month's equivalent; the cautious basis then still sees the short month as the worst. Named test: deviations/d60-pay-smoothing.test.ts.

**2026-10-15 expansion (D-47, amends D-29).** Lines the user marks `oneOff` (a new Record row field the snapshot builder passes; 05 D-29) are left out of the month's figures and counted. Only lines that would otherwise count are counted as left out: a pending or skipped line (D-28), a transfer leg, or a line of a smoothed series (replaced by its monthly equivalent, D-23) is ignored exactly as before, whatever its flag. Unmarked spikes still stay in. Named test: deviations/d47-one-off-lines.test.ts.
