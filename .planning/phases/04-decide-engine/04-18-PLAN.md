<objective>
Build the position layer that turns one user's context into the position a check is judged against: other open checks stacked first (D-41, as amended so every check sees all the others), then the worst of the next 12 months under the user's future changes and the purchase's own lost income (D-45, D-53). Rewire `assess` to take the other open checks and judge the combined position.

New plan, created 2026-10-15 for the engine depth expansion. It modifies assess.ts after 04-07 (wave 6). Everything downstream that takes a context — bisection, options, alternatives, setbacks, the wait plan — runs against the combined position when the caller passes the combined verdict's `context` (D-41: "bisection and options run against the combined position").

Interpretations taken here (recorded for the user in the planning report):
- A stacked monthly invest check joins investing and the pausable amount, as a recorded monthly investment does (D-32).
- A stacked check's own negative purchase-linked income change applies to the combined position; a positive one never does (D-53).
- The stacked plans' monthly payments count in every one of the 12 future months, even when a short plan would have ended — the cautious reading.
- Spending changes (D-45) move the month test, not a month's costs or the cushion, which stay on the logged average (D-02).

Purpose: DEC-06 (the verdict for the position the user is really in) and DEC-09 (bisection on that position). Output: position.ts at 100% branch coverage, the assess rewire, and the D-45 and D-53 named tests.
</objective>
