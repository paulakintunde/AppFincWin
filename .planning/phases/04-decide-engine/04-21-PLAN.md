- (b) excludes the day-one cost (that is view (a)); negative running costs contribute 0.
- (c) uses the investing part of the D-35 pause (investingAtRisk), held for the whole horizon.
- D-63 holding fee (user, 2026-10-15): net rate = entered rate − fee, at PERCENT_SCALE, allowed to go below 0 — the closest reading of "reduces the D-52 projections": flooring at 0 would leave the 0% line untouched by a fee the user actually pays. - (d) without the purchase, cash grows by the month's surplus plus saving (leftBefore + monthlySaving: saving stays cash, investing goes to investments); the existing debts are held constant in both paths (the snapshot has no schedule for them, and they cancel in the comparison); the item itself is not an asset (D-55: its loss of value is non-cash).
