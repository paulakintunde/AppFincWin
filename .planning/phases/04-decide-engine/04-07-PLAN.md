<objective>
Port the prototype's `dassess` as a two-layer API: `prepareContext(snapshot)` resolves everything that does not depend on the check (money basis, the two shares, limits with D-01…D-05 defaults, bills due within 90 days, pausable amount, highest unsecured APR), `assessPlan(context, check, plan)` computes the five ledgers, the pause rule, ordered warnings and basis facts, and `assess(snapshot, check)` composes them into a Verdict.

Revised 2026-10-15 against 04-CONTEXT D-23…D-35. What changed from the 2026-09-27 version: the single `resolveShare(scope)` applied to both existing spending and the purchase is replaced by two shares (D-34); the state rule treats saving and investing as pausable (D-35); cash is reduced by bills due within 90 days (D-24); invest checks drop `inv` and the debt lines (D-33) and can carry the D-31 basis fact; recorded monthly investments behave like existing investing (D-32); currencies are checked (D-16 clarified); statelessness is tested (D-30).

Also folds in D-36 (added 2026-10-15): `purchaseSize(context, price)` sizes my part of the price as if paid in cash against leftBefore and the reserve (after the D-24 deduction), independent of the payment method; every verdict, including a missing one whose context resolves, carries `size`. In household scope the comparison uses myPart(price, purchaseShare), the reading consistent with D-34.

- A new Task 4 builds the D-38 month table and ledgers.
