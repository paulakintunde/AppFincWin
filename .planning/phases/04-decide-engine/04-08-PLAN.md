<objective>
Implement the D-11 first read: from a cash balance, rough monthly income and rough monthly spending, return a cash-level verdict (`fits` / `adjust` / `no` / `missing`) marked as resting on the user's figures, with a rough cushion so a first read that leaves almost nothing is flagged.

Revised 2026-10-15 against 04-CONTEXT D-11 as amended (and D-16 clarified). What changed from the 2026-09-27 version: the first read now computes a rough cushion of 3 × rough monthly spending and returns `adjust` with a `cash` warning when cash after the purchase is at or above 0 but below it; results carry `month`/`cash` warnings; the 'never adjust' rule and its grep gate are removed; rough figures carry a currency. The named deviation test for amended D-11 (including the agreement with the full verdict) lives in 04-11, which runs after both this plan and 04-07.

Also folds in D-36 (added 2026-10-15): every first read, missing ones included, carries `size` computed from the rough figures — rough leftBefore = monthlyIncome − monthlySpending, the rough cushion stands in for the reserve, and the price is sized as if paid in cash.

Phase 5 alignment (2026-10-15, 04-PHASE5-ALIGNMENT.md): no contract change. Onboarding's first check uses the same inputs as the quick check (05 D-23): Cash or Credit (a loan with no deposit at a user-typed APR from 0%), 12–60 months, and sales tax and fees as on-the-day adjustments. One extra test proves that path through firstRead.
