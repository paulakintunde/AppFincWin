## Readiness rules

- past_month_missing: previous month absent or not full, no everyday line, income due but no income line, or a confirmed bill occurrence unsettled (D-51 settle).
- activity_not_current: records-look-behind arithmetic (newest everyday entry older than NEEDS_INPUT_ENTRY_AGE_DAYS and pace under UNDER_LOGGED_PACE_PERCENT, pace part skipped when month.e is null), or a bill due more than NEEDS_INPUT_ENTRY_AGE_DAYS ago this month unsettled.
- envelopes_unconfirmed: any envelope with no accountId.
- D-57: no history month and rough figures present: ready, missing [].
- D-58: no income readiness code.
- No new threshold added.
