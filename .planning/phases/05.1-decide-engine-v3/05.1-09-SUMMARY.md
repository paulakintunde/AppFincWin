## Decisions
- Custom cadences carry no id in `CustomOptions`, so `resolveCadence` takes a list index; missing or invalid falls back to monthly every 1 with `custom_option_missing`.
- `resolveIncomeKind` adds `income_kind_unmarked` when a kind is present but nothing marks it as counting.
