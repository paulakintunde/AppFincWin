D-48 rule (implement exactly, cite D-48):
- Applies only when `month.incomePicture?.volatile === true`, M > 0, and D-36 step 1 fails (lean or low-month leftover below M).
- Values: `payment` = schedule.payment (my part) or M for running costs; `periods` = schedule.count or planMonths.length; `monthly` = M; `months` = planMonths.length; `leanFrom`/`leanTo` = first and last lean month (gig: both the low month); `leanLeft` = the leftover used in step 1.

- false → monthly code 'not_yet', variant 'not_covered', readyBy as 05.1-46 step 3, reason volatile_cover_declined, notice income_volatile.
