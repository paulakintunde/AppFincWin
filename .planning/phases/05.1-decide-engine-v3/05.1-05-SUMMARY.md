# Phase 05.1 Plan 05: Recurring candidates Summary

v3 candidate detection at every cadence, with 1st/15th pay detected as two monthly series sharing one payGroupKey (24 a year, never 26).

## What was built
- `detectCandidates(rows, ctx)`: groups by offerKey plus account, drops dismissed keys and groups containing linked rows, tries the two-date split first, then the single-cadence windows (weekly, fortnightly, monthly, quarterly, yearly) with min repeats and the 10% lower-median tolerance. Output sorted deterministically.
- `twoDateClusters(dates)`: splits days of month into two clusters (centres at least 10 apart, within 3 days, at least 3 rows each, each cluster's gaps in the monthly window). Tries plain days first, then a month-end wrap (28-31 within 3 of month-end counts as day 1).
- `recurring/` is untouched; isolation test passes.
