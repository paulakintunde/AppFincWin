# Phase 5.1 Plan 44: Questions, pay cap and emergency fund in the cash check Summary

The engine now asks (pattern, expected income, unexplained money, account use, pay arrival) instead of guessing, in the owner's priority order; a variable or seasonal earner's 90-day pay is capped at expected income and says so; and an emergency-marked purchase whose purpose may use the fund sees the fund in the cash check again.

## What was built

- **needsInput** (D-34, D-35, D-50, D-54, D-58, D-62, D-59): adds `income_pattern_unconfirmed` (picture.confirmNeeded), `expected_income_needed` (any `dependsOnMonths` month with `expectedFor === null` for a variable or seasonal picture), `unexplained_money_question` (funds.unexplained above thresholds.unspentDrift), `account_use_question` (unstated accounts or an unlinked fund), `pay_arrival_question` (an unseen pay with `arrived === null`).
