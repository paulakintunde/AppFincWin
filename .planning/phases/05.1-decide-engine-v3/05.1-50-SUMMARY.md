# Phase 5.1 Plan 50: D-48 volatile income question Summary

When income is volatile and the lean months do not cover a monthly payment, the engine asks "Can you cover X a month for N months, even in your low months?"; a yes fits on the user's word at rough confidence, a no is not_yet with the Instead futures attached.

## What was built

- **judge.ts (D-48):** in `leanMonthly`, after step 1 fails and only when `incomePicture.volatile`: no answer gives monthly `needs_input` with missing `volatile_cover_question` and the `income_volatile` notice (payment = my part of the schedule payment or M, periods = schedule count or plan months, monthly, months = plan months, leanFrom/leanTo = first/last lean month or the gig low month, leanLeft). Yes gives `fits_month`, fact `rests_on_your_answer {monthly, months}`, confidence capped at rough with `user_said_cover`. No gives `not_yet` / `not_covered`, reason `volatile_cover_declined`, readyBy as step 3. The notice is shown in all three outcomes. The fund is never read. `MonthlyPart` gained `notices`, `rests`, `capRough`, `missing`.
- **purchase.ts:** `volatileCover: check.volatileCover ?? null`.
- **evaluate.ts:** `Evaluation.futures: Futures | null`, set via `insteadFutures` only on `volatile_cover_declined`.
- **copyContract:** `NOT_YET_EMITTED = []`; corpus gets the ask, yes and no cases from a hand-built volatile earner (`volatileInputs` in helpers/fixtures.ts: seasonal, 200,000 May to Sep, 40,000 otherwise).

## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] `user_said_cover` missing from the confidence REASON_ORDER**
- **Found during:** Task 1. `capConfidence` filters reasons through `REASON_ORDER`, which did not list `user_said_cover`, so the reason would have been silently dropped.
- **Fix:** added `user_said_cover` to `REASON_ORDER` in `month/confidence.ts`.
- **Commit:** 20834d1d

**2. Plan text vs behaviour:** for a running cost with no schedule, "periods" and "months" are the 12 plan months (not 7); tests assert this.
