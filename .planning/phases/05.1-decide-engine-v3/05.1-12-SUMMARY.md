# Phase 05.1 Plan 12: This-month model Summary

`erestOf` and `thisMonth` project the month to its end with an Erest floor (pace over days left, or the unlogged part of a typical month, whichever is larger), so everyday spending never stops at day d; bills count on their real schedule.

## Commits
- ac06e2ec: feat(05.1-12): this-month model with Erest floor and bills on real schedule

## What was built
- `erestOf(e, logged, day, N)`: bigint, divideHalfUp, RangeError for day outside 1..N.
- `thisMonth(input)`: paid bills, everyday (net of refunds, floored at 0), bills due from live confirmed bill series in [asOf, next month) minus occurrences settled by a same-series paid line within 3 days (each line matches once), set-aside passed through, monthLeft = moneyIn - moneyOut (may be negative), largestLine (ties by category id), `everydayUnknown` when neither e nor rough expenses exist.
- Tests (16): C15 Sam Oct 3 (erest 137500, out 357500, left 42500), I2 and I21 properties (150 runs), D-02 yearly and instalment bills, no double count, unconfirmed bill is everyday, refunds, largestLine, e unknown.

## Deviations from Plan
None. Pending lines and lines outside the current month are ignored; debt_payment/saving/investing are not added (set-aside carries them, I19).
