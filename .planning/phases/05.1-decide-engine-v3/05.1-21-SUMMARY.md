## What was built
- `buildPurchase(check, {asOf, thresholds, custom})`: whole = myPart(price + tax + one-off fees) sets the band alone (I32). Plan: financed = max(0, full whole - deposit - tradeIn); schedule on the full lender amount; scored payment is the larger of computed and quoted (I41), a quote with no rate becomes the payment (`from_quote`, reason `quote_used_no_rate`, `implied_rate` fact). dayOne = myPart(deposit + setup fee + any payment dated today + one-off extras); M = myPart(schedule.monthlyEquivalent + full running costs). Missing plan facts return `purchase: null` with codes; overflow gives `exceeds_range`; unresolvable share gives `share_invalid` (no SOLE_SHARE fallback).
- Typed extras (library and OwnQuestion alike) count by cadence/cash: monthly to M, yearly as divideHalfUp(amount, 12), one-off to dayOne, non-cash and unknown ids never.
- `buildFunds(inputs, month, envelopeId)` exactly as the plan specifies.

## Orchestrator risks
1. First payment today double count: `Purchase.dayOne` includes a payment dated today (per plan), and `cash/path.purchaseFlows` also emits it. Added `purchaseCashFlows(purchase, asOf, days)` in purchase.ts, which subtracts that payment (myPart) from dayOne before calling `purchaseFlows`. Tests: the total on asOf equals dayOne, whole-flow sum equals deposit + all payments, household share, and a one-period-on plan. The judge plan (next) must call `purchaseCashFlows`, not `purchaseFlows`, for a Purchase. cash/path.ts was not edited.
2. Debt: nothing in buildPurchase or buildFunds touches `debt_payment` series or debts; the cash path alone takes them out (I19). The new plan's own payments are the only debt-like flows added.
3. Refund in a later month than its purchase: reachable in `rollForward` (unspent can exceed its pre-purchase figure when the purchase sat inside planned everyday spending). buildFunds does not alter it: the user's figure is kept (D-30) and the gap shows through `drifted` against `suggested`. Documented in build.ts; not separately bounded by a test.
4. `everydayUnknown`: not read; buildFunds passes `month.e` (null) through to rollForward.

## Contract gaps / decisions (types.ts untouched)
- No new exported types beyond module-local `PurchaseBuild`. `purchaseCashFlows` is a new runtime export of judge/purchase.ts only, not added to v3/index.ts, so barrel.test.ts is unchanged.
- A quote with no rate whose payments cannot cover the financed amount (impliedApr null) is reported as missing `plan_rate_missing`; there is no dedicated MissingCode.
- A RangeError anywhere in the build is reported as `exceeds_range`, which also covers invalid schedule inputs (e.g. count beyond MAX_PLAN_PERIODS).
- Library one-off questions `trade_in` and `deposit` typed in `extras` add to dayOne like any one-off, per "exactly like library questions"; the dedicated `pay.deposit`/`pay.tradeIn` fields are what reduce financed. A UI should not ask both.
- planCard.runningMonthly is the full (pre-share) running cost, matching the card being on the full lender amount.
