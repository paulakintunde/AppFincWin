# Phase 05.1 Plan 47: Per-family I28 gate Summary

The I28 back-test now gates each persona family separately over honest gig and seasonal histories, and every family passes (D-47). Nothing was tuned.

## What changed

- Gig and seasonal: 24 to 30 months (`n + 12`), origins from month 12, the pay series set to the latest logged pay, and the stated pattern in the profile (gig: freelance, variable, pattern 'variable'; seasonal: salary, pattern 'seasonal'). The 0.6 / 0.62 lean-pay constants and `payFigure` are gone. The income draws and the seasonal shape are unchanged.
- SEED 0x0128, HISTORIES_PER_FAMILY, LADDER_PCT, specArb and every non-variable family are unchanged.
- D-52 and D-50 (all families, identically): each origin carries the held-out month's lines dated on or before the cut-off as the current, not-full month, and the unspent anchor is the engine's own suggested figure at the cut-off.
- D-54: persons go through `toV3Inputs` (main account everyday, or mixed with envelopes linked; fund in a dedicated emergency account). The same rule for every family.
- D-48: a 12-month monthly-plan probe at 50% of max(1000, typicalLeft) runs at every origin. It never enters fitsV3 or falseV3.
- Counts gained evaluations, notReady, unexplainedAsked, accountAsked, payAsked, probes, asked. A not-ready result is never a fit.
- Gate: `I28_MIN_LEGACY_FITS = 50` and `I28_FAMILY_CEILING_PERCENT = 25` (both cited to D-47), per-family `it.each(FAMILIES)`, plus D-52 and D-48 tests. The pooled gate and low-month calibration are unchanged.
