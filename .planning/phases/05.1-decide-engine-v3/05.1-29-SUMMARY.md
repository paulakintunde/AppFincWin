## Things to know

- **Seasonal family is above legacy per family** (v3 41.5% vs legacy 23.8%), and gig/seasonal legacy sees few fits (35 and 80). The plan's gate is overall, so it passes, but a per-family gate would fail on seasonal. v3 also fits many more rungs of the ladder, so the per-fit rates are not like-for-like. Flagging for the owner of the engine/shadow work; I did not change the engine or loosen the threshold.
- The low-month calibration sits at 28.1%. The engine's low month varies everyday spending only (income is the stated typical), so income swings (gig/seasonal) and under-logging (partial: 58/153) push held-out months below it.
- **Generator tuning (allowed by the plan, documented):** variable earners (gig, seasonal) state a lean-month pay series (60% / 62% of mean income) because v3 reads typical income from pay series only; the first attempt with 80% gave 41.6% below-low. The partial logger under-logs 25% of months (30-70% logged). No engine change. No reseeding.
- **Runtime:** about 70 s for the suite alone (single file; 95 s on an earlier cold run), above the plan's 60 s target. About 61 s of that is the v3 evaluation and 20 s the legacy engine. It is dominated by 3,000+ v3 evaluations; cutting histories or origins would drop it if CI needs it.

## Deviations from Plan

- Calibration compares against `buildMonthModel(inputs).low` directly rather than a full `evaluate` probe (same figure, cheaper).
- Gig/seasonal carry a pay series (the stated lean figure) with varying income lines, so the legacy engine can answer; the plan did not specify.
