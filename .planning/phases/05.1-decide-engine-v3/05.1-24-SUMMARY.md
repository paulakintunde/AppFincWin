# Phase 5.1 Plan 24: The one judge Summary

One pure judge decides all six codes: cash check, one-off ladder, monthly part and strictest-wins precedence, with an exact funding split, facts, notices, capped confidence and legacyState.

## What was built
- `judge/judge.ts` exports `judge`, `oneOffPart`, `monthlyPart`, `JudgeInput`, `OneOffPart`.
- Ladder order: needs_input (via `needsInput`), cash payday/not covered, month, own envelope, earlier unspent, emergency (only `mayUseEmergency`), else not_yet with `readyBy` (month = ceilDiv(P - max(0, unspent), typicalLeft); date for payday; null when typicalLeft <= 0).
- Split order: month, envelope, unspent_earlier, emergency; always sums to P for fits_* and emergency_use.
- Facts in fixed order: month_left_after, unspent_before_after {before, after}, emergency_used / emergency_untouched, envelope_used, purchase facts, then `evaluateLines` facts (key `lineId`). Test confirms `seenOf` reads both.
- Notices: month notices, emergency_not_set, unspent_stale {days}, unspent_drifted {suggested}, from_your_figures. Confidence capped: stale to fair, drift and rough figures to rough.
- Properties (fast-check): D-10 lines never change code/variant/split, notices never change code, split sums to P, I31, I18 monotone (needs_input excluded as a different question), I23.

## Integration contracts
1. Judge takes `cash` already computed; the caller must build flows with `purchaseCashFlows` (not `purchaseFlows`).
2. Fact keys honoured (see above); every code/fact/notice emitted exists in `verdictTable.ts`.
3. Scenario 02 (unspentAfter 120000) holds: 240000 - 120000. D-18 `d18-return-refund` is a re-run after the purchase was logged and refunded, so its figure is the rolled unspent, not a judge subtraction. The two do not conflict; I resolved it by keeping the judge subtraction and leaving rollForward to restore the figure.
4. Known edges untouched: 05.1-06 `isCounting` (endDate vs status), 05.1-18 later-month refund can lift unspent above pre-purchase figure (shows as `drifted`).

## Contract gaps
- `LineContext.isHousing` is derived as `purchase.itemType` of `rent_bill` or `property`; types.ts has no explicit housing flag on Purchase.
- `monthlyPart` with `typicalLeft === null` returns `monthly_short` (cautious); unreachable in practice because `needsInput` asks for everyday figures first.
- When needsInput is empty but the purchase is null, the judge reports `no_price`.
- Not added to `v3/index.ts` (not in plan files), so `V3_RUNTIME_EXPORTS` is unchanged.
- unspent_before_after and month_left_after are emitted only when a split exists (not for not_yet / needs_input), so `seenOf.unspentAfter` is null for those.
