## Mapping notes
- Debt-payment lines are omitted (debts' minimum carries them); saved/invested and other transfer roles become transfer legs; fixed saving series feed monthlySaving; isHousing bills and housing debts feed housingMonthly.
- Not carried over (no old-engine field): emergency fund (old cushion is derived), unspent anchor, rough figures, income profile, limit lines, purchase emergency and envelope flags.
- Zero-APR plans become instalments with the quoted payment or Math.round(total / count); APR above 0 on a monthly plan becomes a loan. Deposit/trade-in/fees on an instalment are unsupported (not hit by any current scenario).
- Household shares map to a household scope with the same rule for existing spending.
- The adapter does not read `v` on the legacy-v1 corpus (it does not touch that file).
