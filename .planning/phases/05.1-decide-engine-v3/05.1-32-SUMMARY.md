### Owner decisions that become gap-closure work (blocks the phase close)

1. **Variable income.**
   - Problem: the I28 back-test passes only on the overall rate. Seasonal false fits are 41.5% (342/825), against 23.8% for the old engine; gig is 30.2% (225/746).
   - Required: make the engine stricter for variable-income profiles. For example, judge the monthly part against the low month rather than the typical month for gig and seasonal earners. Add a **per-family I28 gate**, so a single family cannot hide in the average.
   - Also look again at the generator's lean-month pay assumption (60% and 62% of mean income), which was tuned to land the low-month band.
2. **'Ended' series with no end date.**
   - Problem: the series keeps counting forever. 05.1-30 confirmed the snapshot source can produce this state.
   - Required: treat the series as ended as of today. Past payments stay in history; no future occurrences count. This means changing `month/series.ts` `isCounting` and updating the pinned case in `evaluate.test.ts`.
3. **I1, partial thinning.**
   - Problem: thinning only some months can improve the answer. For Sam at $1.06 with July and August kept full, the answer moves from fits_unspent to fits_month.
   - Required: make the judge stricter, so partially thin history never improves the code or the confidence. The `it.failing` test in `invariants/answer.test.ts` (seed 88886437) then becomes a normal passing test.
4. **I31, drift and the emergency fund.**
   - Problem: the fund amount changes the `unspent_drifted` notice and can switch drift on or off, which caps confidence. This happens because the suggested unspent figure subtracts the fund.
   - Required: drift ignores the fund, by comparing against the suggested figure with the fund added back. I31 then asserts every figure is identical except the fund's own lines.
