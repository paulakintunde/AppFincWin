# Phase 05.1 Plan 46: Monthly part for variable and seasonal earners Summary

The judge now judges the monthly part of a gig or seasonal earner's purchase against the lean months or the low month, bridges any shortfall from available unspent money, and walks a seasonal earner's ready-by from last year's pattern. Steady earners' answers are unchanged.

## What was built

- Task 1 and the judge half of Task 2 (commit b8caeb2e): `monthlyPart(month, M, ctx?)` returns `{ code, lowClause, bridge, why, readyBy, reasons }`. Without `ctx`, or for a steady picture, it is the old rule. Otherwise `leanMonthly`:
  - plan months = `dependsOnMonths`, dropped to the first payment's month when there is a schedule (falls back to all if that empties it);
  - seasonal: records path (12+ full months) takes lean months from `isLeanMonth` and carries `leanRun`; confirmed path takes months whose leftover is below the payment; leanLeft is the minimum leftover over those months;
  - gig/variable: leanLeft is `month.low.left`, months to carry = min(plan months, window months with leftover below the payment);
  - step 1 leanLeft covers M: `fits_month` (reason `lean_months_used` for seasonal); step 2 bridge = shortfall x months, paid from available unspent after the one-off part: `fits_unspent` with `lean_bridge`; step 3 `not_yet` (variant `not_covered`) with a ready-by; unspent unknown or expected income unknown: `needs_input` (`unspent_not_confirmed`).
  - `lean_months_judged` (seasonal) or `low_month_judged` (gig) rides with every outcome; steady emits neither.
  - `judge` reads `funds.unspent.available ?? value` (D-38), emits `unspent_held_back` right after `unspent_before_after`, picks the later ready-by when both parts are not yet (ties go to the one-off part), and sorts `missing` by `MISSING_PRIORITY` (D-59).
  - Notices: `envelope_location_assumed` (D-49), `unexplained_money` (D-50), `account_use_unknown` (D-54, only when `unstatedAccounts.length > 0`), `fund_account_unknown` (D-62, only when the fund is unlinked and no account is unstated, so the account question comes first), `pay_not_seen` and `pay_counted_missed` (D-58, the latter capping confidence at rough with reason `pay_missed`).
  - `confidence.ts`: `pay_missed` added to the reason order; without it `capConfidence` dropped the reason.
- Task 2 corpus (commit 63238768): `NOT_YET_EMITTED` now holds exactly `income_volatile` and `rests_on_your_answer`. New corpus fixtures emit lean_bridge, lean_months_judged, low_month_judged, unspent_held_back, account_use_unknown, fund_account_unknown, unexplained_money, pay_not_seen and pay_counted_missed. `envelope_location_assumed` was already emitted by the existing envelope fixture.
- Task 3 (commit bcb27e88): `month/seasonWalk.ts` `seasonalReadyMonth(picture, fromYm, gap)` adds each following month's leftover from the same calendar month last year (never below zero); null when a whole year saves nothing or the walk passes `SIMULATION_MONTH_CAP`. `judge.readyMonth` and `instead/futures.buyLater` use it for a seasonal earner. `missingOrder.test.ts` holds the fixed-seed (0x0559, 200 runs) property over `evaluate`.
