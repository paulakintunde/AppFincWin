# Phase 05.1 Plan 35: Spending balance from stated account uses Summary

The engine now judges against a spending balance built from the uses the user stated (D-54, D-55, D-62): the fund never touches the suggested figure, drift or the cash path, I31 holds strictly, and three golden answers moved because the fund no longer cushions the cash check.

## What changed

- `funds/build.ts` `spendingOf`: everyday accounts count less envelope money inside them; mixed accounts count less put-aside (linked envelopes + linked fund + stated `extraSetAside`, floored at 0 per account); dedicated and untouched accounts never count; accounts with no use go to `Funds.unstatedAccounts`. Envelopes linked to no account are subtracted and counted in `envelopesAssumedInside`. D-62: with `inputs.accounts`, a fund linked to no stated account is subtracted and sets `fundUnlinked`. Without `inputs.accounts`, `inputs.balance` is the spending balance (less unlinked envelopes). Total floored at 0. `Funds` now always carries `spending`, `ringFenced`, `envelopesAssumedInside`, `unstatedAccounts`, `fundUnlinked` (`spending` made required in `types.ts`; two judge test helpers gained `spending: mu(0)`).
- `funds/unspent.ts`: `suggestedUnspent` takes `spending` and drops `emergency` and `envelopesSaved`.
- **New I17 form:** `suggested + bills still due + Erest = spending balance` (profile (d) keeps its horizon bills and no Erest).
- `evaluate.ts`: cash path opens at `funds.spending + funds.ownEnvelope`.
- Adapter: `statedAccounts(person)` builds a main account (mixed when envelopes exist, envelopes linked) holding total - fund, and a dedicated emergency account with the fund linked to it. Used by golden, back-test and shadow generators (all go through `toV3Inputs`). Fixtures that carry their own `accounts` are used as given.
- **Scenario 14 conversion:** the `{ balance: 300000 }` override is now `withMainBalance(person, 300000)`, i.e. the main account holds 300,000 and the fund stays in its dedicated account, same rule as the adapter. In `shadow/compare.ts` this applies to the v3 side only (`v3Person`); the old engine still reads the unchanged persona.
- Tests: build.test.ts (D-54, D-55, D-62, D-43, M-06, I17), unspent.test.ts (D-43), golden.test.ts (D-43a x3, fixture balance >= fund + envelopes, D-54 Sam stated accounts), answer.test.ts (strict I31 with seeds 0x0543 and 0x0544, `D-62 exception`).
