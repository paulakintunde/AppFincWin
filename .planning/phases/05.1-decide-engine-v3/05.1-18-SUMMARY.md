# Phase 05.1 Plan 18: Unspent money Summary

Suggested sum, D-22 roll-forward with an exact six-line breakdown, and stale/drifted flags in `funds/unspent.ts`.

## What was built
- `suggestedUnspent`: balance − billsDue − Erest − envelopes − emergency; profile (d) subtracts bill occurrences over the 1–3 month horizon and no Erest.
- `rollForward`: paid lines after anchor.at up to asOf, bucketed by `classifyLine`. Bills matching an occurrence still due at the anchor (same series, ±3 days, anchor month) are planned and skipped. In the anchor month only everyday spending above `erestOf(e, loggedAtAnchor, anchorDay, N)` counts (e null means nothing is planned). Envelope deposits/withdrawals go to investedSaved (signed). Breakdown identity `rolled − anchor = in − everyday − bills − loans − investedSaved + other` is property-tested.
- `unspentState`: stale when > 30 days; drifted when |value − suggested| × 10 > |suggested| or > driftThreshold (0 flags any difference). Value is never replaced (D-30). Null roll with an anchor yields the anchor value with a zero breakdown.

## Golden-fixture note
Scenario 02 (whole price off unspent, unspentAfter 120000) and D-18 `d18-return-refund` (unspentAfter equals the pre-purchase figure) are judge-level. This module supports the refund case: a refund line restores the figure (tested, same-month). A purchase that has not yet been logged is the judge's to subtract. A refund logged in a later month than its purchase restores the full refund, which can exceed the purchase's own effect if the purchase fell inside the planned Erest.
