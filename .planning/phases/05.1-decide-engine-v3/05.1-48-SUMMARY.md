## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 3 - Blocking] Month range and pay date for ines.** The plan said 14 months 2025-08..2026-09 with asOf 2026-11-03, which leaves October 2026 missing and gives a history that does not reach the previous month. ines carries 2025-09..2026-10 (14 full months) plus the current month, and her pay is on the 5th so that on the 3rd the pay is still due: this makes the expected-income raise (income_from_expected, last_year) visible and avoids a pay_arrival question. gabe and vic keep 2025-08..2026-09 with asOf 2026-10-03.

**2. [Rule 3 - Blocking] Extra loader fields.** To make the plan's cases expressible without touching adapter.ts the loader also accepts: `unspentPatch` (a case at another asOf or with a changed balance needs a matching confirmed figure, otherwise D-50 fires), `fundAccountId` (adapter.ts does not link a fund to an account for persons that state their accounts), `extraMonths` and `monthsPatch.drop` (the December case needs December as the current month with November absent), and the expectation keys `readyBy`, `split`, `noticesAbsent`, `reasons`, `questionKey` and `funds.spending`. Unknown keys still throw.

**3. Loader in its own file.** The loader lives in `golden/variableIncomeLoader.ts` so the golden and property tests can import it without re-registering the fixtures test's `describe` blocks. `variableIncome.fixtures.test.ts` exports `loadVariableCases()` as the plan requires.

**4. ines-bridge-short** uses `balanceDelta -264,000` with `unspentPatch 40,000` for "same plan, smaller unspent", so suggested equals the confirmed figure and no unexplained-money question fires.

**5. dee-fund-unlinked spending** is 340,000, not "checking minus 300,000": the holiday envelope (60,000) is still linked to checking and stays subtracted (D-49). The plan's one-line summary left that implicit.
