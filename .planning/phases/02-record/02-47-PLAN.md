<open_questions_answered>
- "Opening date" for an account (planner choice): `accounts` has no opening-date column (20260924000300) and the opening balance is not stamped. The opening date is the device-local date the account was created (`rc.today`), captured at save time and carried in the mutation vars, so an account queued offline still asks for its creation date's rate when it flushes. The fetched rate is what `AccountBalanceBlock` then uses (latest stored rate, decision item 5).
- Persistence of a failed account check (plan-checker warning 12): AsyncStorage key `fincwin:fx-account-rate-checks` (the `fincwin:` prefix is what `wipeDeviceData()` clears, src/services/storage/wipe.ts `STORAGE_PREFIX`), value = JSON array of `{ userId, accountId, currency, homeCurrency, openingDate }`, at most 20 entries (oldest dropped). Retried by the sweep; removed on success (requestRatesForDate returned a non-null list); dropped when `userId` is not the current session user.
- Sweep bounds (plan-checker warning 10): `SWEEP_MIN_INTERVAL_MS = 15 min`, `SWEEP_ROW_LIMIT = 200` rows read, `SWEEP_MAX_CALLS = 10` resolve-rate calls per run shared through a `SweepBudget` by every step (02-49's home check consumes it first, then account checks, then pending dates). 4 runs/hour x 10 = 40 calls/hour max, leaving headroom under the server's 60/hour for interactive saves. Rows are read OLDEST date first; a date whose rows come back still pending (or whose call returned null) is put in an in-memory backoff map for `DATE_BACKOFF_MS = 6 h` (at least as long as the server's per-reason negative cache: 30 min after a provider outage, 6 h otherwise) and skipped, so a permanently failing old date cannot starve newer ones. Rows dated after device-local tomorrow are never read (the server keeps them pending until their day).
</open_questions_answered>
