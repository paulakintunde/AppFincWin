<open_questions_answered>
- "Opening date" for an account (planner choice): `accounts` has no opening-date column (20260924000300), and the opening balance is not stamped. The opening date is the device-local date the account was created (`rc.today`). The account sheet captures it at save time and carries it in the mutation vars, so an account queued offline still asks for its creation date's rate when it flushes. The fetched rate is what `AccountBalanceBlock` then uses (latest stored rate, decision item 5).
- Pending-line retries (planner choice, required because fx-monitor goes away): an online/foreground sweep. Bounds: at most once per 15 minutes, at most 200 rows read, then grouped by date through `followUpPendingByDate` (<= 20 calls). It reads only rows the caller's RLS shows. Rows dated after tomorrow are skipped, because the server keeps them pending until their day anyway.
</open_questions_answered>
