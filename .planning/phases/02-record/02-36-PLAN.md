Resolution order (RESEARCH §A2, D-53):
1. Target kind fixes balance family: checking|savings|cash|investment|other -> 'held'; credit -> 'owed', or 'available' when draft.balanceLabel === 'available' or labels include 'available-balance-label'; loan -> 'owed'. No balances at all in the draft (no row balances, no statedOpening/Closing) -> 'none'.
2. Labels as independent evidence for s: 'debit-credit-columns' or 'direction-column' or 'dr-cr-markers' decide s outright (every row's marker is dr/cr, so s does not change stored values; decidedBy 'labels'). Payment-like description on a card (PAYMENT – THANK YOU, PAYMENT RECEIVED, DIRECT DEBIT PAYMENT, PAYMENT THANK YOU) is money in: its raw sign reveals s ('payment-row-sign'). On a deposit account SALARY, PAYROLL, INTEREST PAID are money in ('income-row-sign'). OFX TRNTYPE CREDIT/DEBIT agreeing with the sign on most rows is 'trntype-agrees'; disagreeing on most rows is strong evidence for the inverted s.
3. Numbers: reconcile under s = +1 and s = −1 with the fixed balance meaning. Exactly one with file 'all-verified' or strictly more verified links than the other -> decided ('reconciliation'). Both or neither -> step 4.
4. Weak priors (majority sign is spending; card owed balances mostly >= 0 and rising with purchases) may ORDER the candidates, never decide.
5. Otherwise 'ambiguous' with the two candidates (same balanceMeans, both s values), the prior-preferred first.
