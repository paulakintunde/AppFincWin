# Phase 05.3 Plan 63: dueDrops and D-27 in this month Summary

One pure rule, `dueDrops`, now says which bill dues stop counting (paid by someone else once, always paid by someone else, or skipped). `thisMonth` applies it after settling paid lines, so a recorded payment still wins. `MonthModel` names the dropped paid-by-other dues and every live always-other series for the answer screen.

## What was built

- **dueDrops.ts** (`src/engine/decide/v3/month/`). Exports `dueDrops`, `DueDrops` and `DueDropReason`.
  - `paid_by_other` drops one occurrence (`seriesId|date`). It is ignored if its date is after asOf.
  - `always_other` drops every occurrence of the series, in any month (`alwaysOther` set).
  - A skip resolves to its series' occurrence within `matchWindowDays(cadence, MATCH_DAYS)` of the skip date. A skip for an unknown or unconfirmed series, or with no occurrence in its window, is ignored. Neighbouring windows never overlap, so there is at most one match and no tie to break.
  - `has()` gives `paid_by_other`, then `always_other`, then `skipped`, else null.
  - The other waiver kinds (`didnt_happen`, `no_pay`, `income_elsewhere`, `nothing_to_add`, `month_complete`, `start_from`) drop nothing.
- **thisMonth.ts**. `ThisMonthInput.drops?: DueDrops` (absent = none). `settle()` runs over all dues as before. Then each unsettled due with a non-null `has()` is left out of `billsDue`, and so out of `moneyOut`, and `monthLeft` rises by the same amount. Unsettled `paid_by_other` and `always_other` dues go into `ThisMonthResult.paidByOther` (`name = series.name ?? null`, `amount = |due|`), sorted by date and then series id. Skips are not listed. The arithmetic is BigInt.
- **model.ts**. `buildMonthModel` builds `dueDrops(inputs)` once and passes it to thisMonth. 05.3-64 reuses this value for the typical month. `alwaysOther` comes from `drops.alwaysOther` matched to live bill series (`liveSeriesOf(..., 'bill')`), sorted by seriesId. It is filled whether or not the series falls due this month and whether or not it was recorded paid.
- **types.ts**. `MonthModel.paidByOther?: readonly PaidByOtherDue[]`, `MonthModel.alwaysOther?: readonly AlwaysOtherSeries[]`, plus the two new interfaces.

## W2: a figure change on real accounts (for owner sign-off)

Before this plan, a skipped bill occurrence still counted as "still to come". Now it does not. Real accounts with skipped bills will see this month's still-to-come (`billsDue`), money out (`moneyOut`) and money left (`monthLeft`) change by the skipped amount. 05.3-64 adds this to the shadow approved-changes review in `05.3-ENGINE-GATE.md`, so the owner's sign-off in 05.3-46 covers it.

Paid-by-other and always-other answers also change these figures (D-27, D-31). They are owner decisions and are named on the answer through `paidByOther` and `alwaysOther`.

## Deviations from Plan

### Interpretations

**1. paidByOther and alwaysOther are optional, and set only when non-empty**
- **Why:** the plan says to make them optional if required fields would force edits to test builders outside files_modified. They would: `judge.test.ts`, `lines.test.ts` and `needsInput.test.ts` build full `MonthModel` literals. Setting `[]` on every model would also break "byte-identical when there are no waivers and no skips" for any exact comparison.
- **Effect:** absent means `[]`. Consumers (05.3-59) read `month.paidByOther ?? []` and `month.alwaysOther ?? []`. `ThisMonthResult.paidByOther` (internal) is always set.

**2. Skip tie-break not coded**
- The interface says "earlier date on a tie". `matchWindowDays` makes windows of neighbouring occurrences disjoint, so two occurrences can never both be within a skip's window. The code takes the single match. Coding a tie-break would leave an unreachable branch and break the 100% branch gate.

**3. "Waivers dated after asOf"**
- Only `paid_by_other` carries a date among the figure-changing kinds, so the rule applies to its `date`. `always_other` has no date and always applies.
