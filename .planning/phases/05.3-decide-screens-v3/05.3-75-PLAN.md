<objective>
No everyday spending is "still to come" on the last day of the month.

Root cause (gap 10, cited): `erestOf` (src/engine/decide/v3/month/thisMonth.ts lines 18-31) returns
`max(divideHalfUp(E x (N - d), N), max(0, E - logged))`. On day d = N the pace term is 0 but the floor `E - logged` is not, so on
Oct 31 with less than a typical month logged the month shows "Everyday still to come (0 days) -$4,450.55" and subtracts it from
remaining. The rough-figures branch (thisMonth.ts, `input.roughExpenses - everyday`) has the same shape.

Output: the last-day rule in erestOf and the rough branch, with tests. 100% engine coverage.
</objective>
