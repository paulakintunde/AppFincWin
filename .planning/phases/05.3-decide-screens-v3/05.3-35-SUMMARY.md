# Phase 05.3 Plan 35: Activity refund nudge Summary

Activity shows a quiet "Refund of your {payee} purchase?" chip on likely refunds; one tap opens the 05.2 refund picker preselected, and nothing links until the user presses Link refund.

## Commits
- 927aa5f0: refundHintFor and picker preselectId
- (second commit): RefundHintChip, row and screen wiring

## Decisions
- Payee match is the normalised payer key with a bare "refund/refunds/refunded" word dropped, so "IKEA Refund" matches "IKEA" (plan example); otherwise exact key.
- Hints are computed over the rows loaded for the shown month (plan wording), so a refund whose purchase sits in an earlier month that is not loaded gets no chip. The picker itself still searches the full 12 months. Widening this needs a 12-month fetch on the screen; left for the owner to decide.
- The chip is hidden when the row already carries a RefundTag, when v3 is off, and while selectable. Copy uses existing `decide.v3.ui.refund.chip` / `chipA11y` (no new keys).
