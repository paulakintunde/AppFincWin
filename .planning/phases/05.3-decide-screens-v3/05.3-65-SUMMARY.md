# Phase 05.3 Plan 65: typical month without always-other series Summary

"Someone else always pays this" (D-31) now also leaves the bill out of the typical month and the past-month series figures. The engine gate has one entry that lists every D-27 / D-31 figure change from 05.3-63, 64 and 65, ready for the owner's sign-off at the 05.3-46 walkthrough.

## What was built

- **month/typical.ts**. `TypicalInput.excludedSeries?: ReadonlySet<string>` (absent means none). The live bill series are filtered by it before the loop. So recurring bills, housing, the per-category bills and the yearly-bill notice all leave the series out. typicalLeft and the low month rise by its monthly figure.
- **month/model.ts**. `buildMonthModel` passes `drops.alwaysOther` (from 05.3-63's dueDrops). `buildIncomeMonths` uses `typical.recurringBills`, so each past month's "left", the lean-month figure, and judge's seasonal month-by-month check (`judge.ts` leanMonthly, which reads `month.recurringBills`) move with it.
- **Records stay records.** Paid Netflix lines in past months are still bill lines, not everyday spending, so E and each month's everyday figure do not move. This month's recorded payment still counts in billsPaid.

## Tests

- `typical.test.ts` (4 new): Netflix out of recurringBills and its category, with typicalLeft +1199; housing out of housingMonthly; a quarterly series raises no notice; absent, empty or unknown-id sets are byte-identical.
- `typicalAlwaysOther.test.ts` (9 new): through buildMonthModel, a seasonal earner over 14 months (recurringBills -1199; typicalLeft, low and all 12 past-month lefts and leanLeft +1199; everyday unchanged); records win; housing; a steady earner; one-off paid_by_other and skip leave the typical figures unchanged; "Ask again" (no waiver) gives back the exact model; the golden person `sam` is byte-identical with empty lists. Agreement through `evaluate()`: always_other(Netflix 1199) lowers recurringBills by 1199 and billsDue by 1199, lowers the cash shortfall by 3 x 1199, raises funds.suggested by 1199, and leaves the confirmed unspent figure unchanged (D-32).
