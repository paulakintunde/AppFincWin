# Phase 05.3 Plan 68: Client wired to server-recorded outcome undo, home-currency plan pot

Plan it / Save the plan / Not buying now send their undo step inside `record_decide_outcome` (4-arg), and the plan pot is created in the home currency, linked to the everyday account.

## What changed
- `recordDecideOutcome(client, checkId, decision, step?)`: with a step adds `p_undo_step`; without, the 3-key call as before (persisted writes from older builds still replay). Returns `undoStepId`, `checkVersion`, `goalVersion` (null unless valid). `DecideWaitGoal` gains `account_id`.
- `decideOutcome.ts`: `recordOutcomeUndo` and its imports removed; mutationFn passes the step (name capped at 80, `{}` when empty); onSuccess invalidates `queryKeys.undoLog`; `useWaitAndSave().save` takes `accountId`, sets `goal.account_id` and the optimistic row's `account_id`.
- `useBoughtContext`: `currency` is `rc.homeCurrency` (was the everyday account's currency), so the pot and bought rows are labelled in the currency their amounts are in.
- `V3RecordProvider.planIt`: passes `accountId: ctx.cashAccountId`; with no everyday account `goalTarget` is null (plan saves, no unlinked pot).
