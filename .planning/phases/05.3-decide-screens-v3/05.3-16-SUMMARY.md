Three pure view-models for the Check screen: band chip ranges from HomeThresholds.bandEdges, D-10 purpose and item-type option lists, and card visibility driven by Purchase.layers.

## Decisions
- `plan` card shows when L3 is in layers or pay mode is plan; `wholePlanLink` needs L5 and pay mode plan (UI-SPEC: periodic only). `staged` is `stagedRequired(band)`.
- bandChip returns raw low/high minor units; the screen formats them and calls compactAmount with the currency exponent.
- Band edges list shorter than 14 is treated like source 'missing' (band number only).
- Option helpers take `{id, label}` for custom entries, since the engine's CustomOptions carries ids only.
