**Gathered:** 2026-10-27 (ahead of 05.2 planning; 05.2 decisions are inputs)

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

This phase builds the Calm Ledger mockup ("Can I Afford It — Decide mockup", https://claude.ai/artifact/CerLacxA19Prxex3JAcFzW) in the current theme, on the v3 engine (05.1) and the 05.2 data. The theme is the Bold pairing, green accent, 26 px cards and the five-tab bar.

It covers:
- home;
- the layered check (15 bands, item-type questions, payment plan);
- the answer ladder;
- the whole plan;
- "What could I do instead?";
- "How this was worked out";
- "Help me check this";
- the record sheets;
- the in-check question screens and the refine check-in cards, handed over from 05.2.

Then the new Decide is switched on for everyone. Deleting the old engine and screens is a separate follow-up after a 2-week hold (D-01).

The setup stepper, Decide settings, the unspent and emergency sheets, and all storage are 05.2.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Carried forward (locked, do not re-ask)
- **Screens:** the screen list, their states, what is reused and what is removed, the quality rules and the navigation map, from `00-UPGRADE-PLAN.md` §6.1–6.5.
- **Look:** the theme from `00-UPGRADE-PLAN.md` §9 decision 7. No new colours, gradients or shadows (CLAUDE.md design fidelity).
- **Answers and questions:** the six answer codes and their precedence (§5.3). D-59 shows one question at a time, most important first. D-57 marks rough-figure answers "rough, from your figures".
- **Switch-on order (05.1 D-15):** named test accounts first, then everyone by over-the-air update. The switch is read in one place, `decideEngine()`, and the per-account switch is built in 05.2 (05.2 D-02).
- **Wording:** declarative only. No "you should", "safe", "you can" or "approve". Every forecast says "could" or "based on your records".
- **Fit and TalkBack:** 320 and 402 px at font scale 1.0 and 2.0, plus TalkBack, with no numbers breaking across lines (`heroAmountText`).

### Switch-on and removal
- **D-01:** The phase ends at switch-on for everyone.
  - The old engine and old screens stay in the code, unused, for 2 weeks (05.1 D-16).
  - Deleting them is a small follow-up change after that: the engine, the screens, and the `decide_cleanup` migration that drops `basis_override` and reviews the `rough_*` columns.
  - Coverage stays at 100% through the deletion.
  - ROADMAP criterion 4 is updated to match.
- **D-02:** Everyone gets the new Decide only after the owner signs off a device walkthrough on the Pixel, done on the test accounts. There is no fixed waiting time.
- **D-03:** Old screens are deleted together with the old engine in the D-01 follow-up, never one by one.
- **D-04:** Switching back takes users to the **"Coming soon" screen**, not the old Decide. Users never saw the old Decide, and it has known wrong "fits". After switch-on, the old code exists only for comparison tests until it is deleted.

### Reading test
- **D-05:** **Skip the 5-person reading test** (owner, 2026-10-27). The 320/402 px, font-scale and TalkBack checks, plus the owner's device walkthrough, stand in for it. ROADMAP criterion 3 is removed. This closes `00-OWNER-DECISIONS.md` D3 with answer (b).

### Not ready, and questions during a check
- **D-06:** While Decide is not ready (05.1 D-52: last month not fully recorded, or this month not up to date), **home shows a checklist card**:
  - it reads "Before Decide can check a purchase:", with one row per missing item (for example "Record September", "Bring October up to date");
  - each row has a button to Activity or Grow;
  - envelopes, settings and saved plans still open, and only "Check a purchase" waits.
- **D-07:** An in-check question **takes the place of the answer card**.
  - Example: "One thing first: did your pay of $2,000 arrive?"
  - The user answers in that same spot, and the answer then appears there. There is no extra sheet.
  - This covers every in-check question:
    - account use (D-54);
    - did your pay arrive (D-58);
    - unspent money;
    - income pattern (D-34);
    - expected income (D-35);
    - what extra money is for (D-50);
    - can you cover it in your low months (D-48).
  - Each answer is saved through the 05.2 mutations, with Undo.
- **D-08:** When several questions are needed, only the first shows, with "and N more" under it. Each answer is saved straight away, so leaving halfway loses nothing.

### Leftover open items
- **D-09:** **No screenshot block** on Decide screens, the same as the rest of the app. Store screenshots and the automatic device checks keep working. This closes the §6.4 privacy open question. Amounts still never appear in notifications.
- **D-10:** The most optional purpose choice reads **"A luxury"**. The full list: an investment in me · for work or income · a necessity · nice to have · a treat · a luxury. This closes plan v3 §14 item 3.
- **D-11:** When a plan's rate is too high for the engine to solve (the "9999" text from 05.1-40 deviation 5), the screen shows a fact: "The payments mean a rate of over 999% a year."
  - It never shows "9999%".
  - The real payments and the total cost sit next to it.
  - If the engine's solvable maximum is below 999%, the planner uses that maximum as the figure instead.
- **D-12:** **Fix the seasonal what-if slider in this phase.** `instead/explorer.ts` must pass the income picture to `buyLater`, so the explorer's ready-by date follows the season (05.1 D-60) and never disagrees with the answer above it (ROADMAP criterion 1). This is an engine change:
  - it keeps 100% branch coverage;
  - the shadow comparison and the I28 back-test re-run;
  - any answer that newly fits needs a written reason the owner approves, a blocking checkpoint like 05.1 D-14.

### Refund linked after a check (owner, 2026-10-28, from 05.2-31 review)
- **D-13:** A refund linked after a check never changes that check's stored answer.
  - The saved check keeps its answer and shows the line "With the refund, this could fit now. Check again." when the engine, re-run with the refund, would give a better answer.
  - Only a new check gives a new answer (05.2 D-21: nothing is applied without the user choosing).
  - Covers saved plans, past decisions and drafts. 05.3 builds the line and the "Check again" action.
- **D-14:** "Was this a refund?" nudge in Activity (owner, 2026-10-28).
  - When money comes in from a payee the user bought from in the last 12 months and it is not linked, show a quiet chip: "Refund of your {payee} purchase?".
  - One tap opens the 05.2 refund picker preselected on that purchase; linking is never automatic (05.2 D-21).
  - Hidden behind the per-account switch until switch-on, like the rest of 05.2's Activity parts.
- **D-15:** History "Undo to here" must not treat an already-undone later step as a change elsewhere (05.2 finding N-05, owner 2026-10-29).
  - Seen on the Pixel: a rollback refused "Loan added" because the same loan's later "Paid off" step had been undone, with the message "changed on another device".
  - Fix in the server undo function (the version check skips steps whose status is undone), with pgTAP; the owner approves the migration push.

### Claude's Discretion

- How the screens are split into plans and waves.
