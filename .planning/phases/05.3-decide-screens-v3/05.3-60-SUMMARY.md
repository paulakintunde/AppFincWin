# Phase 05.3 Plan 60: Home month card figure rows (D-26) Summary

The home month card's three side-by-side figures (Typical month, Unspent money, Emergency fund) are now three full-width rows, one under the other. Each row has a caption, its value or "Not set", a detail line from the same Evaluation, and the existing ChevronGlyph. Each row still opens the same place as before (D-30). Nothing in a row clamps or shrinks at 320 or 402 dp, at 1.0x or 2.0x.

## What changed

- **homeFigures** (pure, 100% coverage) gains `typicalMonths` (monthsUsed.length), `unspentAt` (anchor.at, or null) and `emergencyUnlinked` (fundUnlinked === true).
- **Copy** (`decide.v3.ui.home`): `typicalDetail_one/_other`, `typicalDetailNone`, `unspentDetail`, `unspentDetailNone`, `emergencyDetail`, `emergencyDetailUnlinked`, `emergencyDetailNone`. The three `*A11y` labels now read caption, value, detail and destination, for example "Typical month, $360. From 6 months of records. Opens how it was worked out."
- **Row.tsx**: `ChevronGlyph` is exported. Nothing else changed.
- **MonthBarCard**: `FigureRow` replaces `TapFigure` and the taps/tapsStack layout. Each row is a Pressable button (minHeight touchMin, line1 top divider) with a flex-1 text block: caption (label/meta, inkMuted), value (sheetTitle, ink or inkMuted) with the stale or drifted chip in a wrapping line, and detail (body s13_5, inkDim). The chevron (inkFaint) sits last and is hidden from accessibility. `useShouldStack` stays for the head row. The bar, legend and month figure did not change.
- **Fit allowlist**: MonthBarCard's figure clamps drop from 2 to 1. Only the month figure scales to fit now.
