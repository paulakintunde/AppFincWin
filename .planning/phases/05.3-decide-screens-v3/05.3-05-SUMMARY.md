## What changed

## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Basis difference found by the agreement test (finding 6): gap of zero.**
The judge's `readyMonth` never returns month 0: a "not yet / not covered" answer where unspent already covers the price (a cash-path dip) is dated next month at the earliest. `buyLater` returned "ready now" (months 0), contradicting the answer. Fix: `buyLater` option `waitsAtLeastAMonth` and `explore` option `answerWaits`; the caller sets it when the answer is not_yet. Price/unspent basis: the caller passes the judge's `purchase.dayOne` and `max(0, funds.unspent.available ?? value)`. Documented in comments citing D-12 and finding 6. The judge was not changed.

**2. Agreement property scope.** Plans with monthly payments (`purchase.monthly > 0`) are excluded: their ready-by comes from the judge's monthly part (the plan start), a different question from "buy the whole price later" (ines-bridge-short, ines-held-back, vic-no differ by one month). The property covers one-off purchases. The UI call in the explorer screen must only use the explorer's date as the answer-matching figure for one-off purchases, or the screen plan should decide what the explorer shows for a plan purchase.

**3. Seasonal coverage source.** None of the seeded shadow histories (generateHistories, near-line) is a seasonal earner, so the property also runs the variable-income goldens (loadAllVariableCases), which include the seasonal earner Ines; the test fails if no seasonal case is checked.
