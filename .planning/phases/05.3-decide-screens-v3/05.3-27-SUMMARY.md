# Phase 05.3 Plan 27: Record sheets Summary

One Sheet carries Record as bought (confirm, then past-tense step B with Undo), Plan it and Not buying, all writing through useRecordMutations.

## Commits
- 93b7ec1c: recordLines (pure, 100% coverage), useBoughtContext, `noAccount` copy key.
- 96c8326b: RecordSheet, fitAllow, RecordSheet and matrix tests.

## What was built
- Bought step A shows the exact lines to be written; "Not yet" closes with nothing written; confirm is disabled with "An everyday account is needed to record this." when no everyday account exists or the dry run refuses. Confirm calls `recordBought` once (a ref guards a second press), moves to step B, fires `hapticSuccess` once and announces "Recorded as bought." politely. Undo calls `useUndo().undo` with the returned step id (label `decisionWritten`) and closes; Back to Decide, backdrop and drag all just close.
- Plan it writes once on open (`planIt`, reminder on) and shows "Saved to your plans" with the reminder line (`reminderOn`, `reminderOff`, or `reminderOnDate` for the payday variant); Not buying writes once on open (`notBuying`) and shows its note.
- useBoughtContext: first live (not archived, not deleted) account with use `everyday`, else the first live cash or checking account, else null. Currency is that account's. Debt category is the builtin `Debt`. `aprText` is an optional third argument.
- Matrix test: 320 and 402 wide at 1.0x and 2.0x; buttons stack under rule S, targets reach 44, text fits.

## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Plan scope] No `toast` flag added to decideOutcome.ts.** `useWaitAndSave` and `usePass` only show a toast on a definitive failure (decide.toast.outcomeFailed), never on success, and `useRecordBought` likewise. So no duplicate success toast exists to suppress; decideOutcome.ts and V3RecordProvider.tsx are unchanged. The test asserts `showToast` is never called by the sheet.

**2. [Plan scope] Plan it and Not buying step B have no Undo button.** Both hooks create no undo step (stated in decideOutcome.ts), so an Undo would have nothing to reverse. Only "Back to Decide" is shown. UI-SPEC lists Undo for these two variants; the owner may want a follow-up that adds an undo step for outcomes.

**3. [Model] The plan line key is `linePlan` with a `count` param** (not `linePlan_other`), so the catalogue chooses `_one` or `_other`. `every` carries the key suffix (`everyWeek`, `everyTwoWeeks`, `everyMonth`), which the sheet translates. A plan purchase also drops the item line (plan line, deposit line, then unspent).

**4. Plan it / Not buying with no everyday account** write with a household context (cashAccountId null), since neither needs a cash account.
