## D-27 / D-31 figure changes (2026-10-30)

Plans 05.3-63, 05.3-64 and 05.3-65 change figures on real accounts. The shadow fixtures carry no skipped bills and no readiness answers, so the shadow gate cannot see these changes. They are listed here so the owner's sign-off at the 05.3-46 walkthrough covers them.

Who sees a change: only accounts with a skipped bill, a "Someone else paid it" answer, or a "Someone else always pays this" answer. An account with none of these sees exactly the same figures as before (the tests check this byte for byte).

### What changes

1. **A skipped bill** (05.3-63, 05.3-64). Before, a skipped bill still counted as money going out. Now that one occurrence:
   - is no longer "still to come" this month, so this month's money out goes down and money left goes up by the bill's amount;
   - leaves the cash check (the 90-day cash path);
   - leaves the suggested Unspent money figure.
2. **"Someone else paid it"** (D-27; 05.3-63, 05.3-64). That one occurrence leaves the same figures as a skip: still to come, money out, money left, the cash check and the suggested Unspent figure. Other months of the same bill still count. An answer dated after today is ignored.
3. **"Someone else always pays this"** (D-31; 05.3-63, 05.3-64, 05.3-65). Every occurrence of the bill, from the answer onward, leaves:
   - this month's still to come, money out and money left;
   - the cash check, up to the 90-day horizon (for example, a monthly bill drops out three times);
   - the suggested Unspent figure (for a balance-based earner, every occurrence in their bills horizon);
   - the typical month: recurring bills, typical month left, the low month, housing and the per-category figures. A quarterly or yearly bill also stops asking for its typical-month choice;
   - the past-month series figures (each month's "left" for a variable or seasonal earner, the lean-month figure, and the month-by-month check the answer uses for a seasonal earner).
   "Ask again" removes the answer and gives back exactly the previous figures.
4. **Recorded payments always count.** A bill the user logged as paid stays counted in every figure, whatever the answer. The answers only remove bills still to come and the typical month's scheduled figure, never a payment the user recorded. Everyday spending in past months is unchanged.
5. **A confirmed Unspent figure is never raised** (D-32). Only the suggested figure moves. If an answer frees money after the user confirmed Unspent, it shows as a difference to look at until they confirm again.

The answer screen names the bills involved (05.3-59), so the user can see why a figure moved.

### 05.3-75 last day of the month

On the last day of a month, Decide no longer counts everyday spending as still to come. Before, it subtracted whatever of a typical month was not yet recorded (on Oct 31 on the test account: $4,450.55). Remaining this month can rise by that amount on the last day, and some answers on that day can move to a better answer. On the day before, nothing changes, so remaining can jump on the last day. Months that look under-recorded still lower the answer's confidence and show the 'looks under-logged' note.

No golden, fixture, back-test or shadow expectation changed, because none of them is dated on the last day of a month with an unrecorded everyday remainder.
