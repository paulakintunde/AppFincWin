That member gets 42501 when they name the owner in created_by, and their own waiver is saved as theirs. Malformed keys, an amount, a missing date, a check_id and an unknown kind are each refused with 23514. expected_income still saves.
- pgTAP 70 now plans 35 tests, up from 34. It seeds B's `income-elsewhere` waiver, raises the seeded count from 10 to 11, and asserts the waiver is gone after B is deleted while A's 4 answers remain.
