## What changed
- **L-03:** `purchaseCashFlows` removed the `dayOne < 0 -> 0` clamp. The first payment due today is taken out of the day-one flow and re-added by `purchaseFlows` as the same `myPart`, so the two cancel and the start-day total equals `Purchase.dayOne`. The mismatch was real when a trade-in credit exceeds what is due (dayOne floored to 0, yet the payment was still emitted). `buildPurchase` still floors `dayOne` at 0, now documented in a comment. A rounding-only 1-unit drift (the plan's 1/3 example) did not reproduce, since the subtract and re-add already cancelled; the property test (300 runs, share weights 1..9, upfront 0..10M, 1..60 payments, optional trade-in) now covers it.
- **L-09 withPayment:** when a quote beats the computed payment and the rate is known (source `quoted`), a payoff walk (same rule as amortise: half-up interest, last payment absorbs the rest, annuity-due for first payment today or deferred) recomputes `lastPaying`, `finalPayment`, fees and `totalPaid`. Example: 120,000 over 12 with a 12,000 quote pays in 10 periods, total 120,000, not 144,000. A `from_quote` schedule (no rate) keeps its periods, since its implied rate already fits the count. A quote at or below the computed payment is unchanged.
- **Bands:** O11 table test added; bands.ts untouched.
