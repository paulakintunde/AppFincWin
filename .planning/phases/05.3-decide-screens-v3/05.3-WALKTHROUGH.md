# 05.3 owner walkthrough on the Pixel (D-02)

This is the script for your walkthrough of the new Decide on the Pixel 9, signed in as the test account. When you sign it off, the new Decide is switched on for everyone (05.3-46 Task 3), and then published only after your separate OK (Task 4).

**Switching on also turns on the 05.2 Activity parts for everyone at the same moment:** the income tag chips and sheets, the "How are you paid?" card, refund tags and the refund chip, Decide setup and settings, and "New check" on the + button and in You > Add. Part K checks them.

## Before you start

- Claude gets the phone ready: it restarts the app server and opens the development app on the Pixel. You do not run any commands.
- You need: the Pixel 9, signed in as the test account (profile letter "G"), on Wi-Fi.
- **How to report a problem:** write the step number and what you saw, for example "Step 23: the date says 2026-10-29". Each problem becomes a small fix plan, and this walkthrough is repeated for the steps it touches.
- **How to set large text (used in Part L):** on the phone open Settings > Display > Font size and move the slider to the largest size. To go back, move it to the second mark from the left (the size the phone had before, 1.15x), or to wherever you normally keep it.
- **How to turn TalkBack on and off (used in Part L):** Settings > Accessibility > TalkBack > Use TalkBack. With TalkBack on, swipe right to move to the next item, and double-tap to press it.

## The rule for saves: save, then Undo

Every save you make during the walkthrough is undone in the app straight away, with the Undo button or the Undo on the toast (the small message at the bottom). The steps that save are marked **SAVE + UNDO**, and the table "Saves and undos" near the end lists them all.

Three kinds of clean-up are different, and each one is spelled out at its step:

- **Step 0 is mostly undone, with four answers that stay.** In Step 0 you first try every answer on the not-ready card and undo each one straight away. Then you give four real answers, and those stay, because they are what makes Decide ready. You already approved keeping them (see "Approved test data that stays" near the end).
- **New checks** save as you type, so they are cleaned up by deleting them from "Unfinished checks". When you delete one, **do not** press Undo on the delete toast, or the check comes back.
- **Two saves are cleaned up by Delete, not Undo** (steps 53 and 58), because the screen that held the Undo has closed. This is written at those steps.

If an Undo button is missing, or Undo does not put things back, stop and write down the step number.

## Step 0: answer the not-ready card on the test account

The test account is "not ready" because a few things from last month are still marked Expected, and the current month's everyday spending stops on Oct 14. Decide no longer just blocks. It names each item and asks you a question about it, with one-tap answers (D-24, D-29). The database change these answers need is already in production (05.3-62, pushed 2026-10-30), so they save.

In this step you first try every answer and undo it straight away. Then you give four real answers, which stay. Then you check how the answers show on a "Check it" answer, and how "Someone else always pays this" changes the figures (D-27, D-31).

**Which month is which.** "Last month" is the month before today. "The current month" is the one the card calls **"{Month} so far"**. On Oct 31, 2026, last month is September and the current month is October, and the steps below are written for that day.

**If you do this on Nov 1 or later:** Decide only asks about last month and the current month. From Nov 1, last month is October, so the September items below (the pay, Netflix and Lunch) no longer show at all, and the Oct 14 spending item moves under **"November so far"**. Stop at step 0.1, write down every item the card lists, and tell Claude before you go on. The chip steps, the Netflix figure step and the approved test data below all need the September items.

Dates on the card use your phone's date format. The steps write them as "Sep 3, 2026".

### "Someone else always pays this" changes the figures (D-27, D-31)

This is the one walkthrough step where an answer is meant to change your figures. Use the current month (October on Oct 31). Write every figure down exactly, cents included.

0.20. **First values.** Write down:
   - a. On Decide home, the month card's **"still to come"** figure (in the legend under the bar).
   - b. On Decide home, the **Typical month** row's figure.
   - c. On Decide home, the **Unspent money** row's figure.
   - d. On the "Coffee beans" answer (open it from Unfinished checks and press "Check it"): the facts card line **"{current month} remaining after this"**, the amounts in the strip **"WHERE THE $30 COMES FROM"**, and any cash line, such as **"Cash before payday"** or **"cash is low until {date}"**. If there is no cash line, write "no cash line". This is the cash check: it subtracts every bill on its date over the next 90 days.
   - e. Tap **"See how this was worked out"**. Write down whether Netflix is listed under **"Regular payments we count"**, and the line **"Typical month: the middle of these, {amount}."** Go back.
   - f. In Activity, the current month: is Netflix (due around the 3rd) **marked paid**, still **Expected**, or not there? And the next Netflix date after today (on Oct 31, that is Nov 3, 2026).

0.21. **Answer "always pays".** On the Coffee beans answer, tap the note line to open the sheet, and press **"Ask again"** on the Netflix row. Do **not** press Undo. The asking card comes back in place of the answer, with only Netflix on it. On the Netflix item, press the link **"Someone else always pays this"**. Toast: **"Someone else always pays · Netflix"**. The answer should come back.

0.22. **The note and the sheet.** The note should still read **"Rests on 3 of your answers."** (or, alone, **"Netflix counted as always paid by someone else."**). Open the sheet. The Netflix row should read **"Netflix: counted as always paid by someone else"**, with the grey line **"Not counted against your months, including your typical month."** under it. That line must show **even if** the current month's Netflix is recorded paid (step 0.20 f).

0.23. **Second values.** Write a to f from step 0.20 again. The expected changes:
   - **Typical month** (b) rises by exactly **$11.99**.
   - **Still to come** (a) drops by **$11.99** only if the current month's Netflix is not yet paid (step 0.20 f). If it was already marked paid, still to come does **not** change, because a recorded payment always counts. **"{current month} remaining after this"** (d) moves by the same amount as still to come, the other way.
   - **The cash check** (d) stops taking out $11.99 on each Netflix date from the next one (write the date down) to the end of its 90 days: three times for a monthly bill. On a "Fits this month" answer this may show no figure; write down any cash line that appears or changes.
   - **Unspent money** (c) does **not** change. A confirmed Unspent figure is never raised by an answer (D-32).
   - Netflix should no longer be listed under "Regular payments we count" (e). Write down what you see.
   If any figure moves differently from its expected change, write down which figure and by how much.

0.24. **Ask again puts the figures back (SAVE + UNDO, then a reset).**
   - Open the sheet and press **"Ask again"** on the Netflix row. Toast: **"Asked again · Netflix"**. Netflix comes back on the asking card. Press **Undo** on the toast straight away: Netflix leaves the card, the answer comes back, and the sheet's Netflix row says "always paid by someone else" again.

   (While Netflix is on the card, Decide is not ready, so the answer has no figures to read. That is why this step puts back the step 0.16 answer before reading them.)

0.25. **Leave the answer that stays.** Open the sheet, press **"Ask again"** on the Netflix row (no Undo), then press **"Someone else always pays this"** on the Netflix item. Netflix's answer is now "Someone else always pays this", and the figures should match your second values (step 0.23). This answer stays on the test account (see "Approved test data that stays"). If you would rather it did not stay, say so now: Claude will list what to press instead.

0.26. **Sign off the figure changes.** Claude shows you the section "D-27 / D-31 figure changes" in 05.3-ENGINE-GATE.md. It lists, in plain words, every figure that "It didn’t happen" (a skipped bill), "Someone else paid it" and "Someone else always pays this" now change on real accounts, including the ones you just saw. Write **approved** or **not approved**, with any notes, on the line "Figure changes (05.3-ENGINE-GATE)" at the end of this script.

0.27. **Clean-up.** Go back to Decide home. Under Unfinished checks, delete **"Coffee beans"** (swipe it left, or use the ⋯ menu, then **Delete**). Do **not** press Undo on the delete toast. Part B makes a new "Coffee beans". Leave "Claude" as it is.
