# 05.3 owner walkthrough on the Pixel (D-02)

This is the script for your walkthrough of the new Decide on the Pixel 9, signed in as the test account. When you sign it off, the new Decide is switched on for everyone (05.3-46 Task 3), and then published only after your separate OK (Task 4).

**Switching on also turns on the 05.2 Activity parts for everyone at the same moment:** the income tag chips and sheets, the "How are you paid?" card, refund tags and the refund chip, Decide setup and settings, and "New check" on the + button and in You > Add. Part K checks them.

## Before you start

- Claude gets the phone ready: it restarts the app server and opens the development app on the Pixel. You do not run any commands.
- You need: the Pixel 9, signed in as the test account (profile letter "G"), on Wi-Fi.
- **How to report a problem:** write the step number and what you saw, for example "Step 23: the date says 2026-10-29". Each problem becomes a small fix plan, and this walkthrough is repeated for the steps it touches.
- **How to set large text (used in Part L):** on the phone open Settings > Display > Font size and move the slider to the largest size. To go back, move it to the second mark from the left (the size the phone had before, 1.15x), or to wherever you normally keep it.
- **How to turn TalkBack on and off (used in Part L):** Settings > Accessibility > TalkBack > Use TalkBack. With TalkBack on, swipe right to move to the next item, and double-tap to press it.

## The rule for saves: save, then Undo

Every save you make during the walkthrough is undone in the app straight away, with the Undo button or the Undo on the toast (the small message at the bottom). The steps that save are marked **SAVE + UNDO**, and the table "Saves and undos" near the end lists them all.

Three kinds of clean-up are different, and each one is spelled out at its step:

- **Step 0 is not undone.** The months you record in Step 0 are your own records for the test account and they stay. They are what makes Decide ready.
- **New checks** save as you type, so they are cleaned up by deleting them from "Unfinished checks". When you delete one, **do not** press Undo on the delete toast, or the check comes back.
- **Two saves are cleaned up by Delete, not Undo** (steps 53 and 57), because the screen that held the Undo has closed. This is written at those steps.

If an Undo button is missing, or Undo does not put things back, stop and write down the step number.

## Step 0: get the test account ready

The test account is "not ready" today, because September and October are not recorded. In this step you record them yourself, on the phone. Nothing is written for you.

0.1. Open the Decide tab. You should see the card **"Before Decide can check a purchase:"** with two rows: **"Record September"** and **"Bring October up to date"**, each with an **"Open Activity"** button. (This is the not-ready state.)

0.2. Under **Unfinished checks**, tap **"Claude"**. Press **"Check it"**. The answer screen should show the same not-ready card in place of an answer, with a grey line above it: **"Last saved answer: More needed, Oct 30, 2026"** (or the date you last pressed Check it). It must never show a verdict here. If a **"Help me check this"** button shows, tap it, look at the five money fields ("Income this month" and the others), and leave with the back arrow **without saving**: this screen has no Undo. Go back to Decide.

0.3. On the "Record September" row, tap **"Open Activity"**. Activity should open on September.

0.4. Record September in full: its income (pay), its regular bills, and its everyday spending, the way you normally would. Include one purchase from a named shop that you will remember, for example **"Hardware store, 40.00, Sept 12"**. Part J uses it. These records stay.

0.5. Go back to the Decide tab **without closing the app**. The "Record September" row should be gone. (Known risk, 05.3-39: the screen may not refresh when you come back to the tab. If the row is still there, write "Step 0.5: row did not go", then pull down or switch tabs and come back, and note whether it goes then.)

0.6. On the "Bring October up to date" row, tap **"Open Activity"**, and record October's income, bills and spending up to today (Oct 30), or at least up to the last few days. These records stay.

0.7. Go back to the Decide tab. The not-ready card should go away, and the green card **"Thinking of buying something?"** with the white button **"Check a purchase"** should take its place. If you have TalkBack on, it says "Decide can check a purchase now." If the card is still there, write down which row is left and what it says.

0.8. If a third row **"Link your envelopes"** shows up, tap **"Open envelopes"**, link the envelope to its account, and come back. That link stays too (it is setup, not a test save).

## Part B: Check a purchase at each band

11. **Band 3.** Change the price to **300**. The chip should say Band 3, and a **purpose** question should appear.

19. **Band 9.** Tap "Check a purchase". Item "Cabin", kind **Home**, price **75,000**. The chip should say Band 9, and a **major asset** card should appear.

## Part C: Answer states

22. Open **"Coffee beans"** (30) from Unfinished checks and press **"Check it"**. You should get an answer, most likely **"Fits this month"**. Check:
    - the coloured answer card (the hero) with its chip, title and sentence;
    - the strip **"WHERE THE $30 COMES FROM"** with a bar and labels;
    - the facts card (for example "October remaining after this");
    - the grey line starting "Based on your records up to today…";
    - the link **"See how this was worked out"**;
    - the fixed bar at the bottom: **"Record as bought"**, the second button, and **"Not buying it"**.
    At band 1-2 there is no "What could I do instead?" link. That is expected.

23. **Live answer.** Tap the title at the top ("Coffee beans · $30") to go back to the check, and change the price to **300**. Press "Check it" again. The answer should update. Try a few prices to reach as many of the answer states below as your money allows. For each, write **seen** or **not reached**:
    - **Fits this month**
    - **Fits from unspent money** (a price above what is left this month, but within your unspent money)
    - **Not yet** (a price above your unspent money). The second button should read **"Plan it"**, and a line should say "could be covered by {Month}"
    - **Cash tight until payday** (second button "Remind me {date}"). This one is hard to reach on purpose; skip it if it does not show
    - **Overspent each month** (Part E's "Used car" may show this if the monthly payment is more than a typical month leaves)
    - **Fits, a low month is tight** (periodic purchases only)
    - **Uses emergency fund** (kind **Repair**, a price above your unspent money, if an emergency fund is set)
    - **More needed** (shows a grey hero, a list of what is missing, and the **"Help me check this"** button)
    Leave "Coffee beans" at a price that gives **Not yet** if you reached it, or at **300** if not. Part F uses it.

24. **What could I do instead?** On a band 3 or higher answer, tap **"What could I do instead?"**. Nothing on this screen is saved. Check the four cards: **Buy it now**, **Keep it**, **A fixed-rate deposit**, **Buy it later**.
    - In the deposit card, type a rate, for example **4.25**, and switch between **1y / 3y / 5y**. The sentence should update.
    - In "Buy it later", move the slider. The "Then: by {Month}" line should change.
    - With the slider at **0**, the "by {Month}" month should be **the same month** as the "could be covered by {Month}" line on the answer (only for a Not yet answer).
    Go back.

## Part D: questions in place of the answer

When Decide needs one more answer from you, a card **"ONE THING FIRST"** takes the place of the answer, with **"and {n} more"** under it if more questions are waiting, and the line "Your answer is saved as you go." at the bottom.

25. Look out for this card on any answer in Parts C, E and F. These are the questions it can ask. For each one that shows on this account, answer it and then **press Undo on the toast straight away** (**SAVE + UNDO**). Write down which ones you saw:
    - "What is {account} for…?" (four choices: Everyday, A mix, Kept for one thing, Savings I don't touch). "A mix" and "Kept for one thing" open a second view inside the same card. Toast "Account use saved"
    - "Did your pay of {amount}, due on {date}, arrive?" Toast "Pay arrival saved"
    - "Your unspent money hasn't been confirmed yet." ("That's right" / "Use my own figure"). Toast "Unspent money confirmed"
    - "What is the {amount} for…?" (spare, an envelope or goal, set aside for a bill, someone else's money). Toast "Saved"
    - "Your recorded income changes a lot… steady, variable or seasonal?" Toast "Income pattern saved"
    - "…What you expect to have coming in by then is needed." Toast "Expected income saved"
    - "Can you cover {monthly} a month for {months} months, even in your low months?" Toast "Answer saved"

26. For each question you answered: **exactly one toast** should show per save (not two), and after Undo, the question card should come back. After the last question, the answer should appear in its place.

27. If no question card showed at all on this account, write "Step 25: none reached".
