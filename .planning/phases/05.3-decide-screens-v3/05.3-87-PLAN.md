Two UAT items on Decide home lists and envelopes:
1. Step 45 (minor), copy part: "Saved-plan delete copy says 'It leaves Past decisions'; draft delete copy says 'Open checks' where the list is 'Unfinished checks'."
2. Step 61 (minor), one of four items: "the New pot sheet has no account picker".

Root cause 1: `SavedPlanRow` (src/features/decide/v3/lists/SavedPlanRow.tsx) reuses the old-screen copy. Unfinished rows pass `confirm={{ title: t('decide.delete.checkTitle'), body: t('decide.delete.checkBody') }}` ("It leaves Open checks..."); saved rows pass `del.confirmCopy(row, 0)` from `useDeleteDecision`, which returns `decide.delete.waitingBody` ("It leaves Past decisions with its pot and reminder...") for status 'waiting'. Saved plans are waiting checks listed under "Saved plans" (HomeScreen: `rows.filter(r => r.status === 'waiting')`). The old keys stay for the old screens (deleted in the D-01 follow-up); v3 gets its own keys.

Root cause 2: `PotSheet` (src/features/decide/pots/PotSheet.tsx) shows "Held in" only when `v3 && mixedAccounts.length > 0` (05.2 D-05: "only ... at least one account set to 'A mix'"), and the picker lists only 'mixed' accounts. Readiness (src/engine/decide/v3/readiness.ts `envelopeItems`, D-54) requires every envelope to name its account and "Never waived; its only answer is Open envelopes". A user with no 'A mix' account can therefore never link a new envelope, and Decide stays not ready. Per D-54 and the owner's UAT note, the picker would show for any money account (cards and loans excluded, as in buildV3Inputs' account list). That overrides the locked 05.2 D-05 visibility rule, so the executor asks the owner first (checkpoint Task 2) and applies Task 3 only on a yes.

Saved-plan Undo (step 45, restore part): owned by group A (05.3-66 / 05.3-68), not planned here. 05.3-95 re-checks step 45 Undo on the device after both groups land.
