# Phase 06.1 Plan 03: Month picture and labelled 90-day cash list Summary

This plan adds two pure engine exports. `monthPicture` gives the four D-57 figures: balance now, after bills before payday, month end (always `homeFigure`), and the unused overdraft on its own line. It also gives the usual and low month bars, and `pastMonthBars` gives the bars for earlier months. `cashTimeline` lists the next 90 days of money in and out with the purchase, each line labelled. It reads the same flows the cash check adds up, so its lowest point matches the engine's own `cash.lowest`.

**Owner questions:** none. Everything here falls under the CONTEXT discretion and the RESEARCH "Month Picture" section.

## What was built

- **`month/picture.ts`**
  - `monthPicture(prepared)` reads every figure from the reading `homeFigure` reads.
  - `balanceOn` is `funds.spending`, the everyday money prepare already builds. Accounts with no exchange rate count 0.
  - `afterBillsBeforePayday` takes off the open path's own bill and debt flows up to and including the next payday. With no payday, it goes to the last day of the month.
  - `monthEnd` is `homeFigure(prepared)`.
  - `unusedOverdraft` is `unusedOverdraftOf(accounts)`: each counted overdraft limit less the part already used, never below 0. It is never added into the other three figures.
  - `usual` and `low` are drawn as comes in / goes out / left, with goes out = comes in − left so the three always add up. The low month reads its own recorded income for a variable earner and the typical income for a steady one, matching how `typicalOf` worked out that month's "left".
  - `pastMonthBars` covers the months before this one, oldest first, from recorded lines only:
    - comes in uses the engine's own income rule, `recordedIncomeByMonth`
    - goes out = spending + debt payments + saving + other money out − refunds
    - moves between own accounts, envelope moves and pending lines are left out
    - "left" keeps its sign
- **`cash/timeline.ts`**
  - `cashTimeline(prepared, check, days = 90)` returns the start balance, the rows and the lowest point.
  - Rows are sorted by date, then by kind in `FLOW_KINDS` order, then in the order the path takes them. Each row has `balanceAfter` with the spread everyday spending taken off up to that day.
  - Everyday spending never appears as a line. Lines of 0 are dropped.
  - `days` outside 1..90 throws `RangeError`.
- **Shared flows (refactor, no result changed)**
  - `openCashFlows` labels every flow on the open path, and `openCashPath` adds up exactly those.
  - New labelled versions: `purchaseFlowsLabelled`, `purchaseCashFlowsLabelled`, `replacesFlowsLabelled` (sale vs stopped cost, using the new optional `saleOnPath`) and `savingTransferFlows`. The old functions now just drop the labels from these.
  - `prepare.ts` gains:
    - `openInputOf`, now used by `readingOf`
    - `openFlowsOf`
    - `purchaseSideOf`, now used by `judgeReading`
    - `pickOf`, now used by `stricterOf`
    - `timelineOf`, which picks the same reading `judgeCheck` keeps (D-42)

## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Rule 2 - Correctness] After bills before payday includes unpaid bills due today.** The plan said "after asOf". A bill due today that is not yet paid is still to go: one already paid is settled and never on the path. Leaving it out would show money that is already spoken for.
2. **[Rule 3 - Blocking] The refactor touched more files than the plan listed:** `judge/purchase.ts`, `fullCost/replaces.ts`, `moneySource.ts`, `overdraft.ts` and `__tests__/barrel.test.ts`. "Refactor, do not duplicate" needed labelled versions where the flows are made, and the plan allowed exporting private helpers from their own modules. Each change only adds things, and the old functions return exactly what they returned before.
3. **Interpretations (plan discretion):**
   - `pastMonthBars` covers months before the current one; the current month is the picture itself.
   - A pay expected from history, with no pay series, is labelled `income`.
   - Payments the sale ends and costs that stop are both labelled `stopped_cost`.
