# Phase 06.1 Plan 68: Answers you give show at once and can be taken back (H-7) Summary

A readiness answer on the answer screen now changes its row in the same frame: the row reads "Saved, updating…", its choices turn off, and the next open item becomes "First:". The row leaves when the engine drops it, and it comes back open if the answer is undone. While the verdict is being worked out again, it dims and says "Updating…". "None" (no other debts) and "Change my answer" now show a toast with Undo. A plan name typed in the plan sheet is saved with the pay change in one write. Records has a new list, "Answers you gave". It shows every readiness answer and every goal-to-account link, each with Remove (Undo), and goal links also have Change. The account-use radios on Records show your pick straight away and are announced as checked. A goal row on the answer now says it is a Grow goal, and Why? explains the reason.

## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 3] New files `answer/useAnsweredRows.ts` and `setup/items/answersGiven.ts`** (not in files_modified). The optimistic set and the line wording are separate units, so each can be tested and AnswerScreen and RecordsSetup stay readable.

**2. [Rule 3] `StatusCards.tsx` and `itemFields.tsx` changed** (not in files_modified):
- **StatusCards:** the Not ready card (06.1-66) shows only the first row. For the answered row to stay visible as "Saved, updating…", the card now shows answered rows above the first open row.
- **itemFields:** RadioRow now adds `checked` to `accessibilityState` (DS-14). Pay, Debts and Net radios get this too, and their suites pass.

**3. [Rule 1] The heading count no longer exists.** 06.1-66 (OQ-66-1) removed it. "The count drops" became two things: the next item takes "First:", and the Give it more to go on count leaves out answered rows.

**4. [Rule 1] Undo detection.** The toast host has no callback after a server Undo. A row counts as "taken back" when its answer was seen in the live answers (or its line was seen as skipped) and is now gone. This also catches a write that was rolled back (T-06.1-68-02).

**5. `answer.notReady.item.envelopes` and `envelopesNoName` were removed.** They were replaced by `answer.readiness.goalNoAccount` and `goalNoAccountNoName`, because every Decide envelope comes from a Grow goal (buildV3Inputs). The setup banner's "Next …" line (useSetupText) uses the same sentence, so it now says "(a Grow goal)" too.

**6. Verdict area testID.** It is `answer-verdict-area`. The plain `answer-verdict` id is already the VerdictCard's.

**7. Records' own "What's still open" list is not optimistic.** The plan scoped H-7's optimistic rows to the answer screen. Records lists every item, and an answer there is listed under "Answers you gave" once the cache has it. If the owner wants it, adding the same hook there is a small follow-up.

  - Undo on the toast brings the row back open.

  - Change on a goal opens Which account? (watch G-78-2, the backdrop).
- Text fit at 320 dp / 2.0x:
  - "Answers you gave" rows with Change and Remove side by side.
  - The goal sentence with "(a Grow goal)".
