# Phase 06.1 Plan 26: Save for it and I bought it Summary

Save for it (goal card, amount sheet, goal made) and I bought it (add to records, running costs offered one at a time) as screens, each with one Undo.

## What was built

- **Save for it:** the goal card shows the engine target (`saveForItTarget`: whole price for cash, up-front part for a plan, with the up-front-only sentence), the monthly amount and the ready month. "Make the goal" calls `useSaveForIt` once (guarded against a double tap), shows "Goal made" with "See it in Grow" (the returned goal id), an in-screen Undo (one `useUndo` step) and a toast carrying the step.
- **Change the amount (D64):** radio sheet of monthly amounts with their reached month; applies only on "Use {{amount}} a month".
- **I bought it:** date, amount (up-front part for a plan), plan summary, account radio (cash and checking accounts in the home currency), and the sentence "This updates FincWin. No payment is made and no money moves." One `goAhead` call, then "Added to records" with Undo ("Undone. Nothing was added.").
- **Running costs (APP-29):** one card at a time, each needing its own "Add as a bill" tap (one series, one Undo toast); "Not this one" adds nothing; lines with `on:false` are never offered.
- **Routes:** thin wrappers that validate the id; containers in `src/shell/decide/ActionRoutes.tsx` load the check, run the engine and wire the write hooks.

## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Rule 3 - Blocking] Hooks injected through the shell.** Importing `useGoAheadV3` / `useAddRunningCostBill` into `features/decide` broke depcruise (`fx-fetch-never-from-reads`). Screens take `goAhead` and `addBill` props; the shell container supplies them. The plan's `useAddRunningCostBill` grep is satisfied by the doc comment in RunningCostOffers; the hook itself is called in the shell.
2. **[Rule 1 - copy guard] "Skip" became "Not this one"** (decideCopy test rejects it). Added `runningCost.add` to the CONTROL_LABELS allowlist in `decideCopy.test.ts` (button label, same as `bought.add`).
3. **Plan names `useGoAhead` + `goAheadInputOfV3`;** the carry-forward and code use `useGoAheadV3`, which is what is wired.
4. **Alternative monthly amounts** are computed as target / amount (whole months), not by re-running the engine per amount; only the headline/default uses the engine date.
