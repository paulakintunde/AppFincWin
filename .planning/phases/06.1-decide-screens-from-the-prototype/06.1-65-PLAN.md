from the checklist goes back to the checklist; from the answer, back to the answer; from home, back to home; a deep link with no history
lands on one checklist (plan 50's C-6 guarantee kept). Items must not push another item or the checklist on leave.

input) and dirty, "Save and close" shows the item's existing NeedLine and does not leave. Use `useIsDirty` from src/ui if it fits.
