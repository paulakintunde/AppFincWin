## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 3 - Blocking] The existing guards would have failed on UI-SPEC button and title text.** `decideCopy.test.ts` rejects strings that open with an order (Save, Try, Keep, Pay, Buy...) or an instruction (Add, Pick, Choose, Change the...) unless each key is listed. 35 keys were added to ACTION_LABELS and 38 to CONTROL_LABELS, each with its reason. They are buttons, sheet titles, rule names, "Pay and payday" and similar. (Task 1)

**2. [Rule 3] UI-SPEC draft strings reworded to pass the guards.**
- The disabled Check it hint "Add what it is and a price" became "What it is and a price come first", because hints are declarative under the 2026-10-23 rule.
- The D42 sub "Type the figures from your quote" became "The figures come from your quote".
- The pause choice "Not sure" became "Don’t know", because "sure" is banned (CMP-F12).
- "Free help with budgets" became "Money help at no cost" (D-122), and "Debt advice line" was dropped.
- "Put aside more and the goal comes sooner" became "A larger amount reaches the goal sooner".
- "How should Decide count it?" became "How does Decide count it?".
- Field errors are statements, e.g. "A name is needed." rather than "Add a name."

All of this is draft text that the owner reviews in plan 33.

**3. [Rule 3] Guards scoped for the new namespace.**
- `d106Wording.test.ts` (an older guard that rejects "left" and "short") now skips `decide.screens.`, the same way it skips `decide.v3.`.
- The "names no tab that does not exist (Grow)" test in decideCopy skips `decide.screens.`, because the screens ship with Grow (D-06).

**4. [Rule 2 - Forward fix] The wording page baseline loader.** `wordingPage.write.test.ts` `enAt()` threw on any import other than `../mandatedCopy`. Once this merges, a later baseline en.ts imports `./decideScreens`, so the loader now loads relative imports from the same git ref (`<path>.ts`, then `<path>/index.ts`).

**5. D21 copy placement.** D21 ("What you told Decide", "Edit steps") went in answer.ts, which owns D20 and D21, not stepper.ts. The first-answer safety-net question is only in safetyNet.ts. The plan table listed it under both setup.ts and safetyNet.ts.
