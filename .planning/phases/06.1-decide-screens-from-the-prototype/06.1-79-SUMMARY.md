# Phase 06.1 Plan 79: Figures that disagree between screens (H-16) Summary

Decide home and setup now show the same rules from one helper (`rulesShown`), with Grow's saving marked "Set in Grow". The safety-net page shows a loading line instead of a loading "$0". The months-of-records count on home and in Records is now the count the answer uses (`recordMonthsOf`). The answer's "Updating…" line keeps its space, so the answer no longer jumps.

## Each disagreement: cause and fix

### DS-8: home "Saving at least $800 · On" vs setup "No rules set yet"
- **Two sources:**
  - Home's card listed the debt share (`dsr_line`), the stored rules document (`custom_options.rules`) and Grow's regular saving and investing (`rulesFactsOf`, live `saving`/`investing` series).
  - The setup checklist counted only the engine's limit lines that are on (`inputs.limits.lines`). The Rules page's "No rules" line used `max(own rules on, engine lines on)`.
  - Neither of those counted Grow's saving, so the checklist said "No rules set yet" and the page said "No rules. Answers still keep your safety net.", while home showed the saving row as On.
- **Fix (OQ-79-1 A):**
  - New `screens/logic/rulesShown.ts`. `rulesShown({ debtPercent, rules, saving, investing })` gives the list in one order: debt share, Grow saving, Grow investing, then own rules.
  - `MoneyRulesCard` and `RulesSetup` both call it. Grow rows say "Set in Grow". On home a Grow row opens its read-only sheet ("Open in Grow"). In setup a Grow row has no switch and has an "Open in Grow" link.
  - The old "Saving at least … and investing at least …" note is gone. It also showed "$0" for investing when there was none.
  - `rulesOnOf(inputs)` (engine lines on, plus one per Grow rule) is the checklist's `rulesOn`. The page's "No rules" line shows only when `max(shownOnCount, rulesOnOf)` is 0.
  - `growRulesOf` now holds the series sum that `rulesFactsOf` had.

### DS-9: Safety net "$0 in it now", no account picked, while the checklist said Done; "$50" on the next visit
- **Cause:** NetSetup drew before its reads were in.
  - `saved` was `point === null ? 0 : …`. Grow's goals read (`useGoalsRead`) stays `loading` until the goal rows and goal lines are both in, and during that time it reports no net, so the page showed "$0 in it now".
  - Goal rows or accounts still loading also gave no radios, or the "no net yet" form.
  - The checklist reads `inputs.safetyNet`, which was already loaded, so it said Done.
- **Fix:**
  - While the goal rows, the accounts or Grow's read are loading, NetSetup shows only "Checking your safety net…" (`net-loading`), with Save off.
  - Once they are in, the saved account (`net.account_id`) is selected and the amount is Grow's figure.
  - With no figure at all (a failed read), there is no amount line rather than "$0".

### DS-10: answer "Based on 1 of the 3 months of records" vs home "Records … 3 of 3 months"
- **Two counting rules:**
  - The answer reads the engine's `confidence.basis`: the months complete enough to judge a usual month (`month.monthsUsed`). An under-logged month and months outside the window are left out.
  - Home (`footerFactsOf`) and Records setup ("N of 3 months so far") counted every month the inputs marked `full`.
  - Reproduced on the real engine in `recordMonths.test.ts`: three months are marked full, September is under-logged, and the basis is `partial, used 2 of 3`.
- **Fix (OQ-79-2 A):**
  - `screens/logic/recordMonths.ts`: `recordMonthsOf(inputs, evaluation)` reads the basis (`partial` → used/lookedFor, `records*` → months, `quick_estimates` → the month model's months). It counts spending entries in those months only.
  - Home's records row now reads "Records up to {date}", then "{n} of {total} months complete enough to use.", with the bar at n/total.
  - Home's "How this was worked out" note ("Answers use N months…") uses the same count.
  - Records setup's months line uses the same key. The answer side is unchanged, so no gap row for 06.1-61 was needed.

### DN-4 residual (routed from 06.1-67): "Updating…" shifts the answer
- **Cause:** the line was mounted only while `updating`, so the verdict moved down one line when an update started and back up when it landed.
- **Fix:**
  - One Text always sits in the slot above the verdict. Out of an update it holds a no-break space at opacity 0, and the slot is hidden from screen readers.
  - During an update the same Text reads "Updating…" through its polite live region.
  - No absolute layout, so the text-fit rule O allowlist is unchanged.

G-72-3 was already fixed by 06.1-75 and was not touched.
