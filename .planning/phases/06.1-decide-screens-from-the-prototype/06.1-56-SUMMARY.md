# Phase 06.1 Plan 56: Decide home to the prototype (G-8 part 1) Summary

Decide home now follows D01/D02. When? is one row of three compact chips with short dates. The price field leads with the currency
symbol, and a short tax note sits at the right. The + Add button is hidden on the Decide tab, and the first tap on Check it works with
the keyboard open. Try an example scrolls the card into view. The month card has one stacked bar in whole dollars. Open checks and
rules read like the prototype, and the setup banner can collapse. H-9: once a check's route opens, the card starts fresh, and Check
it waits for the engine inputs, so the same item routes the same way on a cold or a warm start.

## What changed

**Task 1: check card, keyboard, FAB, title (A-HOME-2, -3, -4, -5, -10, X-3, X-4, UX-11, DB-10, DS-21). Commit f89d471b.**
- `CheckCard`: When? is a radio group of three `WhenChip`s in one row (`when-row`; testIDs `when-today`, `when-payday`, `when-date`).
  Each chip shows a word over a short date: "Today / Sat, Oct 3", "Oct 25 / Next payday", "Pick a date / Any day". A picked date
  replaces "Pick a date" with its short date.
- `DecideHome`: short dates come from two `Intl.DateTimeFormat`s built once per locale (`shortDateFormatters`). Open-check rows use
  the same day-month format.
- Price: `MoneyInput prefix="symbol"`. The tax note is the label accessory, so it sits on the label row, right-aligned. It is short:
  "Tax is added for you" (rate known, no price yet), "About $14 tax added", or "Tax not included". `taxNoteFor` gained an `added` kind.
- FAB: a new `'decide'` FabHideReason. `useHideFab('decide', useIsFocused())` is on Decide home.
- `TabRootScreen` gets `keyboardShouldPersistTaps="handled"` (the prop from 06.1-62), `compactInFlow` (DS-21), `titleRole="display"`
  (X-3) and `scrollHandle`. `useTabRootChrome` and `TabHeader` gained the `titleRole` and `scrollHandle` options. `TabRootScreen.tsx`
  is unchanged.
- DB-10: an example chip sets the fields and then scrolls the content to the check card's offset, less 8.
- More details: Cash | Installments is a white pill on the soft (`fill6`) track. The quick-estimate tiles are a 2x2 grid of equal,
  text-only tiles. Need and want chips are kept (D-12).
- Maestro `decide-first-answer.yaml`: the keyboard-close tap on home is removed.

**Task 2: Your month card (A-HOME-6). Commit ba939974.**
- "Your month" title, then "‹ November ›" with the 44 pt arrows.
- One `StackedBar`: goes out + left (together, what comes in). A short month shows comes in + short (together, what goes out). The
  legend has two lines. The wrapper keeps the one accessibility label with all three figures, and the bar itself is hidden from
  screen readers.
- Whole currency units: a new `wholeUnit` prop (10^exponent from DecideHome) rounds every figure before formatting.
- Usual month / Low month is a local radio pair. Each shows a "$X left" (or "$X short") sub-line.
- Records row: the existing text, a thin n-of-3 bar (`month-records-fill`) and a Row-style chevron.

**Task 3: open checks, rules, setup banner (A-HOME-7, -8, -9, DS-12). Commit f08c96fc.**
- Open-check rows: a draft shows its typed price (and "how to pay not chosen"). Each row's date is the purchase date, or else the
  day the check was made (new `createdOn` from `created_at`), shown as "Nov 3"; today reads "today". The spoken label is built
  only from parts that have a value, so it never has ", ," and never ends in "leaves ". The newest 5 show, and "See all 7" opens the
  rest in place.
- Rule rows: the name over a sub-line, with the rule's own figure at the right ("30%", "$200"). On or Off is only in the spoken
  label. An Off rule draws its figure in faint ink. The debt share switched off has no figure.
- Setup banner (started state): a collapse chevron at the right. Collapsed, the banner is a slim pill with the bar and
  "Decide setup · 4 of 6 done", and a tap opens it again. The choice is kept for the app session only. The Past decisions count
  badge was already there from 06.1-21.
- Fit: the new chips and toggles do not clamp text (text-fit matrix rule).

**Task 4: home starts fresh and routes the same way (H-9: FQ3, CC-1, CS-6, CN-6). Commits 057c31a4 (+ the DecideHome part in f89d471b).**
- FQ3 / CC-1: when `newCheck.start` returns a route (`/decide/...`), DecideHome clears every check-card field: text, price, price
  text, when, pay way, plan, need/want, quick lines, and the open More details. A blocked or loading start keeps what was typed.
- CS-6: the collapsed More details row joins the choices that are set ("Installments · Need · 1 quick estimate"). With none set, it
  shows the usual sub-line.
- CN-6: Check it is disabled while `v3.inputs` is null, with 06.1-52's NeedLine "Your records are still loading." `useNewCheck`
  returns `'loading'` until the inputs load. `routeFor` and `isBigBuy` take `inputs.thresholds` (required), `inputs.region` and
  `inputs.regionSubdivision`. A loading null is never read as "large". `bigBuy.ts` stays at 100% coverage (the logic folder is at
  100/100).

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed / adjusted

1. **[Rule 3 - Blocking] No scroll ref on TabRootScreen.** The plan assumed one ("scrollTo on the TabRootScreen ref"). Without
   editing `TabRootScreen.tsx`, I added a `scrollHandle` option to `useTabRootChrome` (via `useImperativeHandle`). TabRootScreen
   already passes `...options` into the hook. Tested in `useTabRootChrome.test.tsx`.
2. **[Rule 3 - Blocking] The display role needs a shell option.** `TabHeader` always drew the title in the heading role. Added an
   optional `titleRole` to `TabHeader` and to the chrome options (default `'heading'`, so other tabs are unchanged).
3. **[Rule 1 - Bug] DS-21 cause.** Decide home never set `compactInFlow`, the 05.3-109 option made for it, so the compact header was
   an overlay with no status-bar backdrop. It now sets the option.
4. **"See all" opens in place.** No open-checks list route exists (`/decide/open` redirects to home), so See all expands the card.
5. **Missing Headphones / Used car rows (A-HOME-7).** I found no filter in the source. `selectOpenChecks` keeps every open, undeleted
   check, newest first, and `useCreateCheck` inserts the optimistic row at the start. The likely cause is the card's length on the
   device; See all now covers it. Not reproduced on a device.
6. **Chip wording.** `home.moreDetails.need` / `want` changed from "A need" / "A want" to "Need" / "Want", as the plan's key list
   says. The chips and the collapsed sub-line now read the same.
7. **Commit split.** Task 4's DecideHome changes went into the Task 1 commit (same file, written in one pass). Task 4's routing files
   have their own commit.
8. **Text-fit matrix.** `textFitMatrix.test.tsx` now mocks `useIsFocused` and `FabProvider`, because DecideHome imports
   `useHideFab`. Clamped text (`numberOfLines`) on the new chips and toggles was removed.
9. **Home tax note.** The note is short now. "Set your region to add it" no longer shows on home; the stepper's Price step still
   offers the region link.
10. **Safety-net row value.** The prototype shows "$1,300" on the safety-net row. The card has no net amount prop, so the row keeps
    its Locked / Unlocked sub-line. This is left for G-8 part 2 or the app-life phase.
