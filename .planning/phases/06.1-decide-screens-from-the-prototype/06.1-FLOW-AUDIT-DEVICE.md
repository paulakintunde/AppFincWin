# Decide device walk: taps, navigation, show and hide, CRUD

**Date:** 2026-11-12 (emulator clock 2026-11-13). **Device:** emulator-5554 (Pixel_8_API_36, density 420 = 2.625). **App:** dev build `com.fincwin.app` on Metro from `C:\dev\fincwin-ds` at `5c02582d`. **Account:** spare Google test account. The Pixel 6 and 21041FDF600BMN were not touched. No source was changed and nothing was committed.

**Evidence:** `C:\Users\harki\AppData\Local\Temp\claude\c2b776d808e07261\599577f3-705b-453c-95d6-57110dcf09a2\scratchpad\flow-audit\device\`. Each `NN-name.png` is the screen, and `NN-name.xml` is the uiautomator dump from the same moment. The helper scripts are in `..\tools\`. `tw.py` times a tap from the adb tap to the first pixel change below the status bar, with about ±0.7 s accuracy.

**Limits. Read these before you weigh the numbers.**
- **This is a dev build.** Every time is slower than a release build. The ratios between steps still matter, and so does the JS thread pinned at 60 to 100% while idle (DP-1).
- **The emulator has a hardware keyboard attached.** Gboard shows a small floating toolbar instead of the full keyboard. From about step 50 on, text went in through the hardware keyboard, so no on-screen keyboard was showing. DB-3 was first seen with the floating toolbar, and DB-4 then happened with no keyboard at all.
- **Many taps were slow, and a few are uncertain.** When the JS thread was busy, a tap could land after the layout had moved, or a second tap could queue behind the first. Each finding says whether it was reproduced or seen once.
- **Not reached:** "Save for it" (D18 flow), "I'm not buying it" from the answer, "Cash and installments side by side", "Every date", "Month by month", "How this was worked out", the Installments plan sheet, the How-often sheet, the quick-estimate sheets, and the first-answer safety-net gate (it never appeared on this account). Too much of the session went on 20 to 115 s waits.
