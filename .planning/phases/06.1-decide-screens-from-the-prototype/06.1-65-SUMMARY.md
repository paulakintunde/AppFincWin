# Phase 06.1 Plan 65: setup items leave to their caller, Save and close saves (H-4) Summary

Setup items now go back to the screen that opened them. "Save and close" runs the same save as the bottom button whenever something typed is unsaved. Back is the checklist's chevron with a 48 dp target. Quick estimates opened from Records save with Undo and come back to Records. Records says it is loading and lists the rough figures in use, with Clear. At the rule cap the chip is disabled and says why, and the rules toast has Undo.

## What changed

- **useLeaveItem** (replaces useLeaveToChecklist, which had no other importers) calls `router.back()` when there is history. Otherwise it calls `router.dismissTo('/decide/setup')`, so 06.1-50's C-6 one-checklist guarantee still holds. No item pushes another item or the checklist.
- **SetupItemFrame** props are now `onSave` (the item's save, without leaving; it returns false to stay open), `onLeave`, `dirty`, and an optional `onSaveAndClose` (used when nothing is dirty; Records uses it for its "still open" toast).
  - The primary runs onSave, then leaves.
  - Save and close does one of three things:
    - dirty: onSave, then leave
    - dirty while the primary is disabled: stay
    - not dirty: close
  - Async saves leave once they resolve. The 72 perf mark is kept.
- **Back** is the rotated-border chevron (the same as the checklist and StepFrame), `accessibilityLabel="Back"`, minHeight/minWidth 48 (`ITEM_BACK_TARGET`), with hitSlop 8. The text-fit GEOMETRY_HEIGHT allowlist entry was added.
- **Dirty per item:**
  - **Pay**: dirty from the fields the person touched (draft pay, typed amount or day, changed types, pattern, method, follow-ups, set-aside).
  - **Debts**: dirty from due-day edits, a bad day, or a changed "No other debts".
  - **Net**: dirty from a changed account, "Not set yet", or a typed amount. An other-currency outcome keeps the item open.
  - **Bills, Rules, Records**: never dirty.
  - The Pay ticks (54) and the Net NeedLine (53) are unchanged.
- **Rules**:
  - A rule with no value at the cap gets `Chip disabled` (accessibilityState.disabled, muted text, no press), with the cap line under its row (`rule-cap-<key>`).
  - The "N rules on" toast passes the step id of the `no_rules` clear (noUndo removed). It is null when the flag was already clear and nothing was written.
- **Chip** gained an optional `disabled` prop.
- **Records**:
  - While the facts load: the "Checking your records…" NeedLine sits under the disabled primary.
  - "From quick estimates" pushes `{ returnTo }` when Records got a safe returnTo. Otherwise it pushes `{ mode: 'save' }`.
  - "Quick estimates in use: X in, Y out" shows with Clear. Clear writes nulls to rough_income, rough_spending and rough_as_of through useSaveSetupStep and shows an Undo toast.
- **QuickEstimatesScreen**:
  - Writes through `useSaveSetupStep` (stepTitle "Quick estimates") and toasts "Quick estimates saved." with the step id. Undo puts back the old rough figures and currency.
  - In `mode=save` the button reads "Save estimates" and leaves via useLeaveItem. Otherwise it calls the route's onDone, as before (06.1-64's returnFromQuickEstimates).
  - With no signed-in user it writes nothing and still moves on.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

1. **[Rule 3 - Blocking] Rules and Records frame wiring went into the Task 1 commit.** The frame's props changed, so all six items had to move together for typecheck to pass. Task 2 and Task 3 then made their feature changes.
2. **[Rule 1 - Bug, plan premise] The quick-estimates write had no Undo step.** The route's `useUpdateDecideSettings.update` returns nothing and records no undo step, so no stepId could be passed. QuickEstimatesRoute.tsx belonged to 06.1-64 this wave and must not be touched. The screen now writes through `useSaveSetupStep` itself, which is depcruise-clean like PaySetup. The `onSave` prop is now optional and is no longer called. **Follow-up:** drop `onSave` (and its `useUpdateDecideSettings` binding) from QuickEstimatesRoute.tsx.
3. **[Copy guard] "Save" became "Save estimates".** The plan's bare "Save" fails the D-18 "never a bare Save or Add" guard and the DCU-09 imperative-lead guard. It is now "Save estimates" and is listed in ACTION_LABELS as a button.
4. **[Accuracy] The cap line copy changed.** The plan's text was "Five rules are on, the most Decide uses." The cap (`canAddRule`) counts saved own rules, on or off, so that text would be false when one of them is off. It now reads "Five of your own rules are saved, the most Decide keeps." The existing `rules.cap` ("Up to 5 of your own rules.") stays under "+ Add your own rule".
5. **[Rule 2] An extra toast key, roughCleared.** Clear needed its own toast ("Quick estimates cleared."). stepName was added for the History label.
6. **[Rule 3] Two shared test-file edits.** wordingScope.ts SAMPLE_VALUES gained `income` and `spending`. decideCopy.test.ts ACTION_LABELS gained `records.quickSave`.
7. **[Rule 3] Chip.tsx got the `disabled` prop.** It was not in the plan's file list, but the shared Chip had no way to say disabled. Chip.test.tsx gained a test for it.
