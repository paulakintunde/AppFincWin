# Phase 06.1 Plan 29: FIN-20 tax set-aside Summary

Freelance pay is now counted after the share the user types for tax. This is a user-typed figure, not a tax calculation, and no rate is ever suggested. A prepare() pre-pass applies it, and it is pinned by six owner-approved right-answer cases. The plain calculator and the engine agree on each of them.

## Owner decision (Task 2, D-26)
On 2026-11-12, in chat through a popup, the owner approved all six cases, TX-10 to TX-15, and answered the open questions:
1. Pay already received this month: keep holding its tax share back from what can be spent (as drafted).
2. and 3. Only a profile with freelance ticked gets the set-aside. "Mix" alone and commission-only do not (as drafted).
4. The share is rounded half up to the cent on each payment, and 60% is the cap. Both are fine.
5. The fact line shows only on answers the set-aside changed (as drafted).

## Tasks
1. Draft cases and plain support (commit 42f5118a). The case format gained `incomeTypes`, `taxSetAsideBp` and `pay.freelance` (freelance pay alongside a salary). The plain calculator restates the rule (`withTaxSetAside`, `plainSetAsideBp`, `plainSetAsideMonthly`).
2. Owner checkpoint: approved, as recorded above.
3. Build:
   - **Engine (`income/setAside.ts`):** `withTaxSetAside` runs in prepare() before anything reads the months. It cuts the freelance income lines, the steady figure, and the pay series and confirmed expected income when the profile has no salary part. It holds this month's received share back from the balance, the first everyday account, and an unspent figure confirmed on or after the pay arrived. It clears `taxSetAsideBp`, so the quick-line overlay's second prepare never cuts twice; the overlay carries the fact over instead.
   - **Fact:** `tax_set_aside { percent, amount }` appears on non-ask answers whenever the set-aside changed an input. It is registered in FACT_KEYS and FACT_VALUE_KEYS. The en.ts line is "{{amount}} a month is kept aside for tax, the {{percent}}% you set." The DECISION_MAP entry cites D-26 and the owner's words; the `amount` and `percent` sample values already existed.
   - **Builder:** `decide_settings.tax_set_aside_bp` → `V3SettingsSource.tax_set_aside_bp` → `V3Inputs.taxSetAsideBp`. Only whole basis points from 0 to 6000 are accepted; anything else reads as none.
   - **PaySetup:** a "Set aside for tax, % of freelance pay" row, shown only when the income types include freelance (own business maps to freelance). It is empty by default. It takes a whole percent from 0 to 60; a value above that shows the range note and holds Save. The share is saved through the existing setup save, only when it changed (an empty field saves none). The pure rules are in `screens/logic/taxSetAside.ts`.
   - **Rule list:** RULES/ruleList has FIN-20 (unit tests in `income/setAside.test.ts`, approved answer cases).
   - **Copy tests:** the copy-contract corpus now emits the fact through TX-10 and TX-12.
   - **Revision:** DECIDE_ENGINE_REVISION went from 06.3 to 06.4, and the lock was re-recorded.

## Deviations
- **[Rule 1] Fixture fix:** `toEngineInputs` now subtracts freelance pay already received from "money still to arrive". Before, the confirmed unspent figure in a salary + freelance case counted the received freelance pay twice. This only affects the new `pay.freelance` field.
- **[Rule 3] Copy tests updated:** `copyContract` and `verdictTable` list the new fact code.
- **Case ids:** see the note under the cases table.

## Not covered (left for later)
- Quick-estimate income (`typedEstimates.income`) is not reduced. A freelancer with no records yet gets no set-aside until their records exist. No approved case covers it.
- The set-aside applies to this month's received pay only. A tax share from earlier months is assumed to be already spent or moved.
