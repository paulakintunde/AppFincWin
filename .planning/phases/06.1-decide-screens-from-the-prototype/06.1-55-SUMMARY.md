# Phase 06.1 Plan 55: Right figures on the month card and the Running costs line Summary

The Your month headline now says "after this month’s plans" and its tiles say "A usual month" or "Your low month", in separate
accessibility stops. The Running costs line now compares a usual month with a usual month (the month picture's usual LEFT against
afterBuy.usualMonth). Tests run the real v3 engine to prove the gap equals the monthly costs, plus the plan payment for a plan check.

## What changed

**Task 1 (A-HOME-1, DS-11).** `YourMonthCard`:
- For this month, the headline shows `home.month.leadLabel` under its words. Its accessibility label now ends with ", after this
  month’s plans".
- The tiles show `tilesUsual` or `tilesLow`, following the usual/low switch, and the tiles' accessibility label starts with that
  label.
- A past month (back arrows) shows its own records, so neither label appears.
- Test: monthEnd -219095 over usual LEFT 89262. Both labels render, and $2,190.95 and $892.62 sit in different accessible stops.
  Neither stop contains the other figure.

**Task 2 (A-RC-1).** `CostsStep`:
- `before` = `useMonthPicture().picture.usual.left`.
- `after` = `evaluation.result.afterBuy.usualMonth`. The old fallback to `after.month` is gone.
- The line shows only when both figures exist. `share` uses the same `before`.
- `totalAfter` placeholder is `before` (was `left`).

The engine-checked test (`costsSummary.test.tsx`) uses the approved case RC-01 through `toEngineInputs`, `prepare`, `monthPicture`
and `judgeCheck`. Only the data hooks are mocked; `useCheckEngine` and `useMonthPicture` are real:
- Cash: before $250 (25000), after -$73 (-7300). before - after = 32300 = 150 + 120 + 30 + 276/12 a month. Exact, no other term.
- Plan (RC-01 on 12 payments at 0%, first payment 2026-12-15): before - after = 32300 + 50000, the running costs plus the plan
  payment. Exact.
- Heavy month so far (RC-01 with $1,500 of everyday already spent): homeFigure moves and the usual LEFT does not. The line still
  shows the usual figures and never the homeFigure.
- No Prepared: no line and no share.

For RC-01, afterBuy.usualMonth contains only the running costs (and the plan payment in the plan case). There is no sale of an old one
in this fixture.
