# Phase 06.1 Plan 59: Short line first on Decide home (G-10 part 1) Summary

This plan adds two shared controls, a "Why?" link (`WhyLine`) and a "More" row (`MoreRow`). Each opens hidden text in place, directly below. Decide home now keeps one short line per card, and every moved sentence is one tap away. A sentence-budget test checks both rules.

## What changed (section D home rows)

- **Header:** "Can I afford it?" stays. The subtitle ("Judged on what comes in…") opens under a Why? link.
- **Setup banner, started:** "Decide setup · N of 6 done" and the bar stay. The "Next: … · about 1 minute" line is no longer drawn. Tapping the row opens the next item, and the spoken label still names it.
  - New state: the title plus "6 short steps, about 4 minutes." The second sentence opens under Why?.
  - Done state: the title "Setup done" over "Answers use your records." (one key became two).
- **Price:** the short note now says "Tax not added", matching the stepper. When the region is unknown, a Why? link under the field opens "Tax is added once your region is set."
- **Your month:** the headline, its labels and the stacked bar stay. The usual/low switch, its explainer and the Records row open under More. The More row only appears when there is something behind it, so a past month with no records date has none.
- **Your money rules:** the title stays. The intro ("Every answer keeps to these rules. Tap one to change it.") opens under Why?.
- **Footer:**
  - The legal line "FincWin is a planning tool, not a lender." always shows and is never behind a disclosure.
  - A new "How this was worked out" row opens the answer's sheet. The sheet now shows the sources sentence ("Answers use N months of pay and M spending entries. Every answer updates when…") as an optional `note` line.
  - `workedSourcesOf(inputs)` in useHomeFacts reads the sheet's sources the same way the answer does.
- **oneLineBudget.test.tsx:** one `it` per card: header, banner (new and started), the banner's done note, price, Your month, Money rules, footer and Not ready. Each counts the sentences drawn before anything is opened. A sentence is text ending in ". ? !". Headers, control labels (button, link, radio), figures and the legal line do not count. Each card must show at most one. The test then opens each Why?/More and checks that every moved sentence appears.

The new key `home.check.taxWhy` says "Tax is added once your region is set." instead, and `home.check.taxNotIncluded` now reads "Tax not added". Plan 33 reviews both.
2. **[Rule 2] The banner's new and done states also follow the rule.** `setup.banner.newSub` was two sentences, so its second half became the new key `newWhy` behind Why?. `doneTitle` ("Setup done. Answers use your records.") became `doneTitle` "Setup done" plus `doneSub` "Answers use your records.". Both titles now carry `accessibilityRole="header"`. `setup.ts` was not in the plan's file list.
3. **[Rule 3] Files outside the list:**
   - `HowWorkedOutSheet.tsx`: new optional `note` prop. The answer screen does not pass it, so the answer is unchanged.
   - `useHomeFacts.ts`: new `workedSourcesOf`.
   - The existing home, month-card and banner tests now open More first and expect the new copy.

6. **The footer row only shows while the month picture exists.** The sheet needs the picture. Before the inputs load, the old footer sentence would have read "0 months … 0 entries", so nothing useful is lost.
7. **TDD gate:** Task 1's tests and components went into one commit (afdab06d) rather than a separate RED commit. The budget test was written after the card changes. Several of its checks would fail on the old home: the Money rules intro and the footer each drew two sentences.
