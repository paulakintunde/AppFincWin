Draft FIN-23 (owner confirms): UK and EU lenders quote an APR that already includes compounding (an annual effective rate). For region GB or an EU country, a typed APR is turned into the periodic rate r with (1 + r)^periods = 1 + APR (integer maths at the engine's rate scale, rounded so the computed payment is within 1 minor unit of the lender's for the reference quotes); US and CA keep today's nominal APR / periods. Fact `rate_convention_effective { apr }` "Read as the APR your lender quotes in {{country}}." on plan answers it changed.
Draft FIN-22 (owner confirms): for a plan with more than LONG_PLAN_MONTHS (12) payments, the payment is also checked against the low month (MonthModel.low.left); if it fits the usual month but the low month would be short, the answer keeps its code but adds the caution variant used for low months (`low_month`) or, when the engine's table has no such row for that code, a fact `long_plan_low_month { months, short }` "In a low month, this {{months}}-month plan leaves you {{short}} short." Short plans are unchanged.
</context>
