# Phase 06.1 Plan 35: Answer information sheets Summary

Four prop-driven information sheets an answer links to: About this answer, Give it more to go on, Money help at no cost, and How this was worked out, built on engine tables and the month picture.

## What was built
- **AboutSheet**: every `Result.caveats` sentence under "For this answer", then `ABOUT_KEYS` in order; "Money help at no cost" only when `freeHelpFor(country)` has a row (GB, US, CA). Opens without a result.
- **GiveMoreSheet (N03)**: at most one next step, readiness lines, each missing figure as a link (`onOpenMissing(code)`), optional "Remind me on {{date}}" when the caller passes a date. No "sure"/"surer".
- **FreeHelpSheet (D65)**: reads `FREE_HELP` only; `openHelpUrl` refuses anything not starting with `https://`.
- **HowWorkedOutSheet (D57)**: balance on date, after bills due before payday, expected at month end, unused overdraft on its own line (shown only when > 0), sources (Activity range, Grow size and Locked/Unlocked, rules count), records date, legal footer.
- Copy added under `decide.screens.answer.*`.
