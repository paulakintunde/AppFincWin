### Flow changes
- **decide-first-answer** and **common/decide-gate**: plan 44's Not ready card. "Answer from quick estimates" is tapped only when the card shows it (a brand-new account). Otherwise each named item is answered with its one-tap choice ("It didn't happen", "No pay that time", "Nothing more for …", "Records begin in …"), a goal's "Which account?" is set to the first account, and the new "First: What is <account> for?" item is answered "Everyday spending" (common/decide-account-use.yaml). - **Other fixes found on the run**: the plan sheet's "24 months" has a no-break space (`24.months`); "See it in Grow" now opens the goal (06.1-63), so decide-save-for-it follows it, checks the outcome line on the answer and undoes the goal through History; decide-bought prices a 2,500 car (a 9,000 cash car asks to confirm spare money first, G-61-2); sheets are closed with Back, because a "Close" text tap can match the full-screen backdrop (also labelled "Close") and land on the panel; common/open-sheet.yaml retries a sheet that opens with no panel (G-78-2, G-61-1).
