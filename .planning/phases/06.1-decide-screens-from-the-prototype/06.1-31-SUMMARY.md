## Deviation: the goal-target price limit was removed (owner decision, 2026-11-12)

Plan 10 (as its plan specified) made `record_decide_outcome('wait')` refuse a goal target above the check's stored
`inputs.price`. The app's Save for it target is the engine's `Purchase.whole` = price + sales tax + one-off fees (or a
plan's up-front part, which can include a setup fee), so the limit would have refused real saves (most US checks, any
check with a fee). The owner chose to remove the limit; the goal is the person's own record and the existing shape and
money bounds stay. The migration had not been pushed anywhere, so the file itself was changed (commit 4ead656b), and
pgTAP 97 now asserts that a target above the typed price is accepted.
