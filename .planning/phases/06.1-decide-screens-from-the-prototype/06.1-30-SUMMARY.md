# Phase 06.1 Plan 30: FIN-22 long loans and FIN-23 UK/EU rates Summary

In the UK and the EU a typed APR is now read the way lenders there quote it, so the payment matches the lender's own example to within a penny (Tesco Bank £195.23 → £195.24, Consorsbank €316.75 → €316.76). Long plans are pinned by six owner-approved cases on the existing low-month rule, and their savings line now reads correctly over the whole plan.

## Owner decisions (Task 2, D-26, 2026-11-12, chat popup)
1. **FIN-22:** approve LL-01..LL-06 as they are. No new maths. Drop the planned `long_plan_low_month` fact, because the low-month lines already show.
2. **FIN-22 wording:** reword the savings line for plans over 12 payments so "about $4,400 of your $4,000" no longer reads wrong. Suggested meaning: "Over the whole plan, low months would take about {{amount}} in total; usual months in between refill your savings."
3. **FIN-23:** approve AP-01..AP-06, with the UK/EU reading applied only when the region is set to the UK or an EU country. Pounds or euros with no region set stay as before, so BR-01 is unchanged.
4. **FIN-23:** rounding up (one penny over the lender's quote) is fine. Approved line: "This APR is counted the way lenders in {{country}} quote it." (see the deviation below).

## Deviations
- **[Rule 1/compliance] FIN-23 wording:** the owner approved "...the way lenders in {{country}} quote it". DCU-09 (`decideCopy.test`: "uses the word lend nowhere in the decide.* copy") fails on "lenders". That rule keeps the app clear of looking like a lender, so it was not loosened. The line reads "the way it is quoted in the UK / in the EU" instead, with the same meaning. The owner should confirm at the D-08 sign-off.
- **{{country}} becomes two lines:** the engine sends no display names (`country` is a discriminator, not a placeholder), so the place is fixed in two copy variants chosen by `area`.
- **Placeholder name:** the long-plan line uses `{{bridge}}`, the existing value, rather than the owner's suggested `{{amount}}`. The meaning is the same.
- **Not built:** the plan's `judge/longLoan.ts` / `longLoanLowMonth` and the `long_plan_low_month` fact. The owner chose no new maths and dropped the fact.
- **Schema:** new topics `long-loans` and `rate-convention`; `pageRates.test` now expects 13 pages.
- **Changed answer:** no earlier approved case changed. AP-05 is the one case where FIN-23 changes the answer itself: the weekly plan's interest drops below the 10% line.

## Not covered (left for later)
- A plan with only a quoted payment and no typed APR still shows its implied rate (`implied_rate`) as a nominal yearly rate. In the UK or the EU that reads a little below the lender's APR (for example 6.41% for 6.6%). This was not raised with the owner.
- The plain calculator's older plan helpers (dates, monthly figure) do not handle weekly plans. AP-05 is checked through the new FIN-23 helpers only; plans stay out of the whole-answer comparison under PLAIN-PLANS.
