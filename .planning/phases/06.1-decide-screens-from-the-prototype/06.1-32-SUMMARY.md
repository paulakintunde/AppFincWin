  estimates > 3000 / 1200 / 800 > See the answer now > checks that the basis line names quick estimates. The flow times
  the run with `evalScript` (Date.now) and counts every `tapOn`. It prints `UX-11 first answer = N ms, T taps` and asserts
  `<= 90000 ms` and `<= 12 taps`. **This path has 11 taps by design**, so it is under the 12-tap hard limit but over the
  8-tap target. The taps are the tab, the two fields, Check it, Got it, Not set yet, Answer from quick estimates, the
  three estimate fields and See the answer now. The target can only be reached if fields focus themselves or the flow
  changes.
- **decide-stepper-loan** (APP-13): "used car" 9000 > stepper step 2 > Installments > plan sheet 24 months, rate 9 >
  Use this plan > Next > step 3 > See the answer > the answer shows ".*in interest.*" and `answer-footer`.
- **decide-waiver** (APP-13, ask, don't block): a new check's answer shows first. Then "None" answers the debts ask, or a
  "Fix" missing-figure row sits under the facts. The verdict, the basis and the footer stay on screen.
- **decide-safety-net** (APP-13): Your money rules > Safety net (it uses "Set an amount" first if there is no net) >
  Unlock for answers > confirm Unlock > Undo puts the lock back on > unlock again > the lock switch shows

## Jest suites

- `textFitMatrix.test.tsx`: **32/32 pass.** It renders Decide home, the three stepper steps (kind, price, running
  costs), the answer frame (N02 headphones, N09 car plan), the setup checklist and safety-net settings, at 320 and
  402 dp, at 1.0x and 2.0x font. Every surface passes `expectFits`, which checks four things: no clamp on system copy,
  no fixed text height, no absolute or negative-margin wrapper, and 44 pt targets. The suite also checks that money
  figures carry no `numberOfLines` unless they shrink to fit, and that no View holding text has a fixed `height`. The
  verdict title size is checked exactly: heading (26) at 1.0x, sheetTitle x scale (32) at 2.0x, which shows the drop
  above `TITLE_DROP_SCALE` (1.3).
- `readingOrder.test.tsx`: **14/14 pass.** It walks the accessibility stops in tree order, the order TalkBack uses. On
  every LEDGER answer (N02, N04, N05, N09, N12, N26) the order is verdict, basis, caveat (if any), caveat line,
  can't-see link, footer, action bar. Each region is one unbroken run, and the action bar comes last. The verdict is
  one stop with a full label. The caveat line and footer read the legal words. The link has the link or button role.
  The order holds at 2.0x font.
