## Jest suites

- `textFitMatrix.test.tsx`: **32/32 pass.** It renders Decide home, the three stepper steps (kind, price, running
  costs), the answer frame (N02 headphones, N09 car plan), the setup checklist and safety-net settings, at 320 and
  402 dp, at 1.0x and 2.0x font. Every surface passes `expectFits`, which checks four things: no clamp on system copy,
  no fixed text height, no absolute or negative-margin wrapper, and 44 pt targets. The suite also checks that money
  figures carry no `numberOfLines` unless they shrink to fit, and that no View holding text has a fixed `height`. The
  verdict title size is checked exactly: heading (26) at 1.0x, sheetTitle x scale (32) at 2.0x, which shows the drop
  above `TITLE_DROP_SCALE` (1.3).
- `readingOrder.test.tsx`: **14/14 pass.** It walks the accessibility stops in tree order, the order TalkBack uses. On
  every LEDGER answer (N02, N04, N05, N09, N12, N26) the order is verdict, basis, caveat (if any), caveat line,
  can't-see link, footer, action bar. Each region is one unbroken run, and the action bar comes last. The verdict is
  one stop with a full label. The caveat line and footer read the legal words. The link has the link or button role.
  The order holds at 2.0x font.

### F-1 (real app bug, not fixed: app source not changed): "Answer from quick estimates" is a dead end on an account with records

Steps: the test account has records but is not ready. The two reasons shown are "Some envelopes or goals aren't linked
to an account yet." and "Last month isn't fully recorded yet". Start a check (concert tickets, $150) > **Not ready yet**
> **Answer from quick estimates** > enter 3000 / 1200 / 800 > **See the answer now**. Result: the same **Not ready yet**
card comes back, with the same reasons and the same link. The person can loop forever and never gets an answer.

Why, from the code:
- `NotReadyCard` (`src/features/decide/screens/answer/StatusCards.tsx`) always offers "Answer from quick estimates".
  `AnswerScreen` sends it to `/decide/setup/quick-estimates`, which saves `rough_income` / `rough_spending`.
- `buildSnapshot.ts` (around line 713) uses the rough figures **only when there are no full months**
  (`fullKeys.length === 0`). This account has full months, so the figures are ignored.
- `envelopes_unconfirmed` is "never waived" (`readiness.ts`, D-54), so quick estimates could not have cleared it anyway.

The link should be offered only when quick estimates can actually produce an answer. Otherwise it should say what
will, such as Finish setup or link the envelopes. Evidence:
`scratchpad\maestro-run\finding-quick-estimates-loop\not-ready-after-quick-estimates.png` and `decide-waiver-rerun.log`
(scratchpad = `C:\Users\harki\AppData\Local\Temp\claude\c2b776d808e07261\599577f3-705b-453c-95d6-57110dcf09a2\scratchpad`).
Suggested home: a 06.1 gap-closure plan. It hits exactly the "existing user, not ready" case, and the Decide tab's core
value depends on it.

### F-2 (environment, not shown to be an app bug): slow writes on the cold-booted emulator

After the restart, each write (toast Undo of the safety-net unlock, the harness's check create) took about 50 to 90 s to
reach the server. The persisted mutation count stayed at 1 that long. Online state did not change, and the network was
validated. The safety-net flow now waits up to 90 s after Undo; the record-as-bought harness has a fixed 30 s limit.
Recheck on a warm emulator or the Pixel before calling it a bug.
