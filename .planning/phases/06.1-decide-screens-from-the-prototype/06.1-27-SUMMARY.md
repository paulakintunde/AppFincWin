# Phase 06.1 Plan 27: Your decisions and decision detail Summary

Past decisions on the new design: a list with a look-back card that compares Decide's estimate with the user's records, and a detail screen that lists what Decide said on each date and reopens any of those answers read-only.

## Tasks

1. Your decisions, look-back, detail, as-checked answer, routes - commit d25ff28f.

## What was built

- **List**: rows with name, outcome (Bought {date} / Not bought, saved as a plan / Not buying), amount and a plain "Saved" text badge (no state colour). Empty state with "Back to Decide". Row tap opens the detail route.
- **Look-back card**: Expected / Your records show / Difference, "Did it cost what you expected?". The Yes / No question appears when an unlinked money-out line exists on the decision day; Yes attaches it (useAttachLine, toast with Undo), No writes nothing and names the amount left out (toast with Undo). After 3 look-backs one line says how close estimates were; hidden before.
- **Detail**: What Decide counted / What your records show so far (look back in {month} when no checkpoint is reached), history rows newest first with a StateBadge chip word, "See the check as it was", and "Remove from decisions" in danger text through the shared delete path (destructive toast with Undo).
- **AsCheckedAnswer**: VerdictCard, FactLines and LegalBlock from the stored Result under "As checked on {date}"; no action bar, no pay or date controls. A stored result with an unknown code or no facts array shows one plain line (T-06.1-27-02).
- **Fail soft**: `useCheckHistory` returning nothing (table not in production yet) lists nothing and shows no error.
