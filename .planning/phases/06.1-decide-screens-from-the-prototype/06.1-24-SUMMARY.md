# Phase 06.1 Plan 24: Setup items 1 and 2 (Pay and payday, Regular bills) Summary

Setup item frame plus D32 Pay and payday (found pay card, pay-changes question, four unchosen counting methods, other income with I spend it / It's kept) and D33 Regular bills (Regular / Not regular / Change per row, Regular-only total, add a missed bill), all writing to Activity series.

## Tasks

1. SetupItemFrame and Pay and payday - commit 5637803b
2. Regular bills, sheets and tests - commit 9f49f736

## Behaviour notes

- Pay: a live salary series, else the payer found in the records, else amount and day fields. Change on a saved series edits it at once (one Undo); on a found payer or empty fields, Save pay details creates the salary series. The save also writes the pattern (About the same / It changes / seasons) and, for the two changing patterns, the method plus its follow-up (months, steady amount, hold months, as in the old income steps). Primary stays off until the method is complete.
- Bills: rows are found outgoing payments plus live expense series with no found payment (a hand-added bill has no logged rows). Savings and income series are left out. Total uses the engine's monthlyEquivalent (one half-up rounding). "These are right" sets `bills_right` once through useSetDecideAnswered and returns to /decide/setup.
- "Add a bill / other income" needs a payable account; a short statement shows when there is none.

## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 3 - Blocking] depcruise fx-fetch-never-from-reads.** useEditSeriesFrom reaches db/fxResolve, which features/decide may not import. The screens take `editSeriesFrom` as a prop (type `EditSeriesFrom` in itemLogic.ts); `src/shell/decide/SetupItemRoutes.tsx` supplies it and the two route files render those containers. Plan 25 can follow the same pattern.

**2. Primary label on bills.** Plan text names both "Save bills" and "These are right". The spec (D33) says "These are right", so that is the visible label (new key `bills.right`); `bills.save` stays unused in copy.

**3. Pay copy: "the method is written to the income profile".** The plan's acceptance says never to write decide_settings income columns. The counting method has no other home than those columns (the existing MethodStep writes them the same way), so only that part goes there, through useSaveSetupStep. The pay amount, day and other income are series only, so nothing about pay is stored twice.

**4. Extra files.** itemFields.tsx (day field, frequency chips, radio row, money/ordinal words) and itemLogic.ts (pure rules) are shared pieces for plan 25; SetupItemRoutes.tsx in the shell.
