# Phase 06.1 Plan 75: Regular bills page — one bill set, a way off the list, an instant list (H-12) Summary

The Regular bills page and the setup checklist now read one helper over the Decide inputs (`decideBillSetOf`). They show the same bills and the same total, converted to the home currency. A bill can be stopped from the page with Undo. Every chip either works or reads as off. A Change shows on the row and in the total at once.

## Task 1: why the page and the checklist differed (DS-6, DS-7)

The rows come from the device dump `69-not-regular.xml` / `73-bills-bottom2.xml`. The checklist said "2 bills, $79.95" = $75 + CA$5 converted ($4.95).

| Bill on the page | Page state | Amount | v3 series role / status | Counted by the checklist? | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Extra on Design work | regular | $75 | bill / live | yes | an ordinary expense series |
| Emergency funds | regular | $800 | not a bill (goal-linked or Savings) | no | Grow contribution: Decide counts it once through the goal (ARC-15) or as a `saving` series |
| Laptop | regular | $180 | not in `series` (goal-linked) | no | Grow goal contribution, carried by the goal's monthly amount |
| Safety net | regular | $50 | not in `series` (goal-linked) | no | safety-net goal contribution |
| audit bill | regular | CA$5 (then CA$7) | bill / live, $4.95 home | yes | the page summed CA$5 as $5 (DS-7) |

**The rule that drops 3 of 5:** `buildV3Inputs` puts a series that feeds a live goal into `goalLinkedSeries` and never makes it a bill (06 ARC-15 / P5-CR-01). The goal's monthly amount carries it. A series in the Savings category becomes a `saving`/`investing` series. `buildDecideSnapshot` also leaves out a series covered by a debt. The old page summed every row marked Regular. It used the raw amounts (`regularMonthlyTotal`), and its series list filtered only `income_kind` and `saving_mode`, so these Grow-made series showed up as bills. Laptop, Emergency funds and Safety net are matched to this rule by their names and amounts. The exact goal link could not be read from the production rows without production access, so a device check should confirm it. The rule itself is shown on the real build path in `setupChecklist.test.tsx` ("Bills page and checklist agree"): a goal-linked series and a Savings series are left out and named elsewhere, and CA$5 counts as $3.60 at the test rates.

**OQ-75-2:** Decide does not leave these out. It counts them once in their own place. Answer A ("count them in Decide too") would count them twice, so nothing in `src/engine` or `src/features/decide/snapshot` changed. `check:right-answers` is unchanged. The page now lists only the bills Decide counts as bills. One line under the list names the others: "Counted with savings, goals or debts: Laptop, Emergency funds, Safety net." If the owner wants those payments to stay visible as rows instead, that is a small page-only change.

What changed:
- `itemLogic.ts`: `decideBillSetOf(inputs)` returns `{ monthly: Map<seriesId, homeMinor>, elsewhere, currency }`, plus `decideBillsTotal`, `billCountOf` and `billMonthlyMinor` (moved from useSetupProgress, so the figure is unchanged). `buildBillRows(found, series, set, billsRight)` lists every bill Decide counts and marks each saved row `bill | elsewhere | notCounted`. It marks `toConfirm` and sorts those rows first (DS-15). A row with a saved bill shows that bill's amount, day and frequency. `regularMonthlyTotal` is removed.
- `useSetupProgress.ts`: `setupFactsFrom` reads `decideBillSetOf`. The new `useDecideBills()` gives the page the same set and the bills-right flag.
- `BillsSetup.tsx`: the total is `decideBillsTotal(set)` in `set.currency`, labelled "Total each month, in USD". Rows keep their own currency. Rows counted elsewhere are hidden and named. A saved bill Decide does not count (for example, no stored rate) says "Not counted in Decide yet." There is no total until the inputs are read.

## Task 2: chips, a way off the list, an instant list (DB-2, DC-6, DC-5)

- **DB-2:** `MarkChip` takes `off` instead of `disabled`. The chosen chip is the current mark: it is inert, announced selected, and its hint says "Already marked.". A chip that cannot act (Regular with no account to save to) is drawn at the 0.5 disabled opacity, the same as Pill, announced disabled, and the row says "There is no account to save a bill to yet." Not regular on a Not regular row is the chosen chip. Regular on it works and makes the bill again.
- **DC-6 / OQ-75-1 A:** "Not regular" on a Regular bill and the new "Stopped" button in a saved bill's Change sheet both call `onStopBill`. That is `useSetSeriesStatus().stop`, bound in `SetupBillsRoute`, so depcruise `fx-fetch-never-from-reads` stays clean. The stop starts today, or on the bill's first date if it has not started yet (`stopFromFor`). It writes `end_date` = the day before and `end_reason` = 'stopped'. The toast says "{name} stopped." and offers Undo (the server-recorded `seriesStopped` step). Past entries are never touched. The sheet note says "Ends from today. Past entries stay in Activity."
- **DC-5:** `recurringSeries.ts` now lays the patch over the cached series row in `onMutate` for edit-from and for Stopped/Paused/Resume (`patchSeriesRow`). The Decide snapshot reads the same `recurringSeries` query, so the row and the v3 total change at once. The refetch after the write, or the conflict/reject handler's invalidate, puts back what the server holds. 06.1-73's summary did not cover this list, so this is new code, not a test-only change.

## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Rule 1 - Correctness] OQ-75-2 not applied as "count them".** The finding is that Decide already counts them, as goals or savings. Counting them as bills would double-count, so the page now follows Decide instead. No snapshot change. The owner should confirm (see above).
2. **[Rule 2] Copy beyond the plan's list.** `bills.notCounted`, `bills.elsewhere` and `bills.stoppedNote` were added so a hidden or uncounted bill and a stop are explained. `bills.totalIn` reads "Total each month, in {{currency}}" rather than "Total, in {{currency}}" because the figure is monthly. `bills.countedNote` (OQ-75-2 B only) was not added.
3. **[Rule 2] "Already marked." is the chosen chip's accessibility hint, not a visible line.** The only chips that cannot act are the current mark (selected look) and Regular with no account. The second shows its reason as visible text. A visible "Already marked." under every row would repeat on each card.
4. **[Rule 3] Files outside the plan's list:** `src/shell/decide/SetupChecklistRoute.tsx` (G-72-3 memo and stable handlers) and `src/i18n/__tests__/wordingScope.ts` (a `names` sample for the wording page test).
5. **[Rule 1] A found row with a saved bill now shows the bill's figures,** not the last logged payment. Without this, a Change never showed on that row (DC-5), and the row disagreed with the total.
