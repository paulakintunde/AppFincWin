# Phase 06.1 Plan 85: spare money confirm and the unexplained money ask (G-PD-2, G-PD-4) Summary

The "What is the $X for?" ask can now be answered on the answer itself. The spare money figure is read from the engine's own
prepared inputs. The empty price field is named "Price" again. The root cause of G-PD-2 is in the engine, so this plan did not
fix it there. Confirming spare money on an account with thin history still raises the ask once, but now it can be answered.

## Root cause (G-PD-2)

Found with temporary logging in the dev build on the spare test account. The logging was never committed. Figures on 2026-11-14:

| Reading | Everyday spending to come (erest) | Typical everyday (e) | Suggested spare money |
|---|---|---|---|
| As given (reading 1) | 421.39 | 501.39 (quick estimates) | 14,870.06 |
| Thin months read as full (reading 2, D-42) | 0 | 0 | 15,291.45 |

The account has thin months and quick estimates, so `prepare()` builds two readings and keeps the stricter one. The sheet showed,
and "That's right" saved, reading 1's figure ($14,870.06). This is correct: it is the reading readiness uses, and it holds back the
everyday spending still to come. But `buildFunds` works out unexplained money in each reading against that reading's own figure.
Reading 2 has no everyday spending to come, so it finds `15,291.45 − 14,870.06 = $421.39` unexplained. That is above the $200
drift threshold, so `needsInput` asks `unexplained_money_question`. An ask wins `stricterOf`, so every check showed Not ready.
Undo removed the anchor, so the ask went away.

No saved figure works in both readings without an engine change:
- Saving reading 1's figure makes reading 2 ask (what testers saw).
- Saving reading 2's figure ($15,291.45) stops the ask. But reading 1 then counts the $421.39 of everyday spending still to come
  as spare money, which is less cautious and contradicts the sheet's own words. It also flags `unspent_drifted`
  ("may be out of date") right after the confirm.

**Engine fix to decide (not made):** read unexplained money only from the as-given reading, the way readiness already does. One
option is to give reading 2 reading 1's `suggested` for the unexplained sum. Another is to drop `unexplained_money_question` from
the all-full reading. Either needs a DECIDE_ENGINE_REVISION bump and owner approval.

Reproduced in jest on fixtures (spareMoney.test, "thin history: the engine still asks after a confirm").
