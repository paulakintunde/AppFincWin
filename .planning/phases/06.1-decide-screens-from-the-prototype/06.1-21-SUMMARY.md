# Phase 06.1 Plan 21: Decide home Summary

The Decide tab now renders only the new home, and Check it creates the draft check and routes it to the stepper, the answer, or nowhere for a home.

## Tasks

1. useNewCheck: buildCheck, routeFor and the creating hook - commit b9b8f3a3
2. DecideHome, CheckCard, MoreDetails, YourMonthCard and the tab route - commit a1f80ec7

## What was built

- **Check it (APP-09, D-02, D-09).** The card is always rendered; the pill is dimmed with the hint "What it is and a price come first" until an item and price exist, and stays dimmed under the home note for home words. A paid-over-time word (loan, finance, on a plan) or Installments in More details counts as a plan for the big-buy rule. The pill is also disabled while the create is pending (a 900 ms guard plus a client uuid).
- **Stepper entry.** Plan 19's routes expect an existing draft row; `useNewCheck.start()` creates it with `useCreateCheck` (inputs = `toStoredV2(check)`, furthestStep 1) before pushing. The kind step opens first only when the words did not read a kind.
- **More details (D02, D-12, D-21).** Expands in place (260 ms; Reduce Motion: no animation). How you pay (Cash | Installments; Installments opens the plan sheet, and only a used plan switches the mode), Need or want (stores `purpose` necessity or nice_to_have; emergency is always false and never asked), quick-estimate tiles from `QUICK_LINE_KINDS` with the added lines listed (edit, remove), carried into the check as `quickLines`.
- **Your month.** Lead = `picture.monthEnd` (equal to `homeFigure`, asserted in a test), COMES IN / GOES OUT / LEFT bars for Usual or Low month, earlier months from `pastMonthBars`, the Records row to `/decide/setup/records`. A negative month grows its bar down and says "short" with the sDown glyph.
- **Mounted from other plans.** SetupBanner (17), OpenChecksCard + OpenCheckSheet, MoneyRulesCard + RuleEditSheet + AddRuleSheet, SomethingChanged (22), OfflineCard / ErrorCard (14), DatePickerSheet / InstallmentPlanSheet / QuickEstimateSheet (15; paydays and nextPayday passed from the month picture).
- **Tab route.** `app/(app)/(tabs)/decide.tsx` renders `<DecideHome />` (behind DECIDE_OPEN) and keeps the __DEV__ ShadowExportDevMenu require; ComingSoon, SetupPage and DECIDE_SETUP_VISIBLE are gone from it.
