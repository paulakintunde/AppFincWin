# Phase 06.1 Plan 25: Setup items 3 to 6 (Debts, Safety net, Your money rules, Records) Summary

Four setup item screens on plan 24's frame, each answer written to its home: due days to Grow debts, the net's account to the Grow goal, rules to the plan 22 rules document, and account uses to Accounts.

## Tasks

1. Debts (D34) and Safety net (D35) - commit 0e668556
2. Your money rules (D36), Records (D37/D37b), routes, tests - commit 6ce5f217

## Behaviour notes

- Debts: Grow's live debts that the engine counts (a card paid in full is left out), each with balance, rate, minimum, and a "Due day of the month" field. Save debts writes each changed day through the injected Grow writer (one Undo each; the toast carries the last), and sets or clears `no_debts` from the "No other debts" choice. "+ Add a debt" goes to `/grow?segment=debts`.
- Safety net: with a net, shows what is in it and its Grow size with a blue bar, "Which account holds it?" (saved on the goal), the lock word and the settings link, the starter-size line and "Change the size in Grow". Without a net, an amount field or "Not set yet" through the first-answer net write (the same one plan 18 uses). An amount in another currency shows a note and sends the person to Grow.
- Rules: one switch row per rule (debt share, keep at least, largest purchase, category rules) with a value chip. Chips and Keep/Largest switches without a value open plan 22's RuleEditSheet; the debt and stored-rule switches write at once through commitRule. All off at save sets `no_rules` and shows "No rules. Answers still keep your safety net."; any rule on clears it. Saving and investing at least are a note pointing to Grow.
- Records: Last month, This month and Your accounts, each with a word status. Done is active only when all three D-11 checks hold; otherwise the button is "Save and close". Open accounts show the four plain choices; the brand-new variant offers Import a statement, Add this month as you go, and quick estimates.

## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 3 - Blocking] depcruise fx-fetch-never-from-reads.** Grow's debt and goal writers reach the rate-fetch door, so DebtsSetup and NetSetup take `saveDueDay`, `setAccount` and `answer` as props; SetupItemRoutes.tsx binds them (same pattern as plan 24).

**2. [Rule 3] useSetupFacts extracted.** Records needs the three checks separately; `useSetupProgress.ts` now exports `useSetupFacts()` and `useSetupProgress` is built on it. Behaviour of the progress hook is unchanged and no rule changed.

**3. Debts read from Grow rows.** The plan said names from `useDecideV3().inputs.debts`; the engine debt has no name, so rows come from `useDebts` filtered to the ids the engine counts.

**4. Extra files.** `othersLogic.ts` holds the pure rules (due day edits, rules-on count, record checks, account-use choices). `itemFields.tsx` gains `locale` on `useSetupFormat`. New copy keys are in `setupOthers.ts` (no new placeholders; all already have sample values).
