## 1. What is going wrong, in plain words

Most of the trouble comes from four things. First, when you press a button, nothing on screen changes to show the press. The phone is often busy for 3 to 12 seconds after a tap, so the button looks dead even when the tap worked. A few buttons really do miss: the bottom bar on the answer page sits under the phone's own Back and Home buttons, and some text links are smaller than a thumb. Second, Back does not always take you to where you were. If you change something from the answer page, Back walks you through the other steps and ends on Decide home. If you open a setup item from the answer or from home, Back drops you on a setup list you never opened. Some buttons that say "Edit" or "Back to Decide" open a different page from the one they name. Third, the same item can look different from one time to the next. Whether you see your typed details, the steps, or a list of facts depends on how you got there and whether the app had finished loading. Choices from your last check can quietly carry into the next one. Fourth, when you answer a question or save something, the page often does not change until much later, so it looks as if nothing happened.

The fixes below fall into eleven groups. Each group is one plan. Groups H-1 and H-11 deal with taps and speed. Groups H-3 and H-4 make Back and Save do what they say. Groups H-5 and H-6 make the answer page show the same parts every time, with a short line first. The rest fix single screens and missing messages.

Technical root causes, for the plans:
1. **No pressed feedback while the JS thread is busy.** `Pill`, `Chip` and `SegmentedControl` have no pressed style or `android_ripple`. The Pixel 6 still needs 2.6 to 11.7 s per action (plan 51).
2. **Missing safe-area insets and keyboard tap handling on some frames.** The answer's action bar has no bottom inset. Bought, Save for it and Compare are bare Views. `DetailScreen` and `TabRootScreen` have no `keyboardShouldPersistTaps`.
3. **Return paths are hard-coded, not "back to the caller".** Steps `replace` to the step before them. Setup items `dismissTo('/decide/setup')`. "Go to Decide" pushes or replaces `/decide` from the outer stack.
4. **What shows depends on the path and the load state.** For example: "What you told" only after the stepper; everything hidden on Not ready; stepper or answer chosen from inputs that may still be loading; home fields never cleared.
5. **Writes give no instant sign.** Readiness answers, "None", the open-check actions and quick estimates wait for a slow engine rebuild, or give no toast at all.

## 5. Owner questions

Each one is a real product choice. The recommended option is what the plans assume if no answer comes.

**Q1. You open a setup item from the answer or from home. You finish or press Back. Where should you land?**
- A. Back where you were: the answer or home. The setup list shows only if you opened it from the setup list. **(Recommended)**
- B. Always on the setup list, so you see your progress.
- C. Back where you were, plus a short note such as "Pay done · 3 of 6" with a link to the setup list.

**Q2. From the answer you tap "Change" on something, such as running costs, and the step page opens. Where should Back and "See the answer" take you?**
- A. Straight back to the same answer, with your change applied. **(Recommended)**
- B. One step at a time (step 2, then step 1), then the answer.
- C. Open the change in a pop-up sheet over the answer, with no step pages.

**Q3. After you check one item and come back to Decide home, should home keep your last check's choices?**
- A. Start fresh: an empty name and price, and back to the usual pay way and date. **(Recommended)**
- B. Keep everything as it was, but show the kept choices on the folded "More details" row.
- C. Clear the name and price, but keep how you pay and when.

**Q4. On a setup page, what should "Save and close" do?**
- A. Save what you typed, then close. **(Recommended)**
- B. Rename it "Close". It saves nothing, and the main button is the only save.
- C. If you typed something and leave without saving, ask "Save your changes?"

**Q5. The first-answer intro, or the safety-net question, is swiped away instead of answered. Should it come back?**
- A. No. A swipe counts as seen. The safety-net question then shows as one line on the answer that you can tap later. **(Recommended)**
- B. Ask again, but at most once a day.
- C. Keep asking on every check until it is answered.

**Q6. When the answer says "Not ready yet", what else should that page offer?**
- A. Your typed details ("What you told Decide"), plus "I'm not buying it" and "Delete this check". **(Recommended)**
- B. Only your typed details.
- C. Keep it as it is: only the list of things to finish.

**Q7. Where can you see, and take back, answers you gave, such as "this income didn't happen"?**
- A. On the Records setup page, in a list called "Answers you gave", each with Remove. **(Recommended)**
- B. On each answer, under "What you told Decide".
- C. Both.

**Q8. You open the answer for an item you already bought or set a goal for. What should it show?**
- A. One line saying what you chose ("Bought on Nov 12"), with no buy or save buttons, and a link to that entry in Past decisions where you can remove it. **(Recommended)**
- B. Keep the buttons, but warn first: "You already marked this as bought."
- C. Hide the whole action area and show nothing about the outcome.

**Q9. The answer's "Give it more to go on" sheet has an unfinished "Remind me on …" button. It is wired up but never shows. What should happen to it?**
- A. Remove it for now and bring it back with the reminders work. **(Recommended: it is not part of today's complaint)**
- B. Finish it now: remind you on the date the answer becomes surer.

**Q10. The answer says October's records are missing and offers "Records for October". What should that open?**
- A. Activity, showing only October, with a banner naming what is missing. **(Recommended)**
- B. An "add an entry" form already set to October.
- C. Activity for October, with an "Add an entry" button in the banner.
