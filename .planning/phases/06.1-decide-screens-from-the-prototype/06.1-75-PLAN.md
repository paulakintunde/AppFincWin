## Owner questions

**OQ-75-1 (DB-2, DC-6). How should a bill come off the Regular bills list?**
- A. The Change sheet gets "Stopped": the bill ends from today, past entries stay in Activity, with Undo. "Not regular" on a bill marked Regular does the same thing. **(Recommended: it uses the existing Stopped choice, so life changes stay user-led)**
- B. "Not regular" only takes it out of Decide's count; the bill keeps running in Activity. No stop from Decide.
- C. Both: "Not regular" for Decide only, and "Stopped" in the Change sheet.

**OQ-75-2 (DS-6, only if Task 1 finds that Decide is not counting bills the page shows as Regular).** Decide's answers use only the
bills it counts. If some bills you marked Regular are not counted:
- A. Count them in Decide too. Answers that include those bills will change, and the change is listed for you first. **(Recommended)**
- B. Keep Decide's count as it is, and show on the bills page which bills Decide counts and why the others are left out.
Do not guess. If Task 1 finds the cause is in src/engine, nothing there is changed: the SUMMARY records it and the question is put to
the owner as an engine-revision item for after 06.6.
