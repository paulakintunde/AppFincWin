## What was built

- **Pick a date (D40):** radio-group month grid of 44 px days, past days disabled, today ringed by border, paydays carry a dot and "payday" in the day label. A settled polite preview line judges the check with that purchase date through `useCheckEngine` (state chip word plus the after-buy lead). Dates later than today + 90 days show a far-off note. "When it's saved up" appears when `readyDate` finds a day.
- **How often (D43):** Every month / 3 months / year, start and end chips, live "about N a month" line, "Save how often".
- **Quick estimate (D41):** Expense, Loan (payments 1..600), Savings (usable defaults to No), Income. Says "Counts in this check only." Edit mode has "Save quick estimate" and red "Remove" with a destructive toast whose Undo re-adds the line. Ids from `expo-crypto`.
- **Installment plan (D42):** cash down, read-only amount borrowed (price + tax + fees - cash down), length chips per item kind plus Other (1..120), cadence, rate, three fees, first-payment radios, pause-interest question, quoted payment, engine result card (`buildSchedule` + `planCard`), `fact.interest_is_a_cost`, `plan.feesVary`, `plan.estimatedRate`, footer `legal.decideAnswer.footer`. Writes `CheckV2.pay` mode 'plan'.

## Notes for the plans that wire these in

- Sheets are presentational: they take `formatMoney`, `formatDate`, `today`, `currency` and an `onPickDate(current, set)` callback. The caller owns opening `DatePickerSheet`.
- Rate: plan 11 `futures()` passes userRate null / years 1; nothing here changes that (the rate typed here is a loan rate, not a deposit rate).
- First payment mapping: "Next payday" and "Pick a date" write a `deferred` first payment (with the pause-interest answer, Don't know = null which the engine asks for); "One month after buying" writes `one_period`.
