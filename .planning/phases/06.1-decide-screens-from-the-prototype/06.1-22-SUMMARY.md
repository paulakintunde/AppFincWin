Open checks, Your money rules and Something changed? are built as self-contained components with their sheets; the rules a user sets now reach the engine.

## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 3 - Blocking] No store existed for own rules, keep-at-least or largest purchase.** The plan said to find a limits mutation; none exists (`settingsSourceOf` hard-coded `own_lines: []` and `tightLine` was unmapped). A new column would need a migration and would break reads before the push (plan 12's note). Rules are stored as a small versioned document under `custom_options.rules` (already a granted, owned column; the engine ignores unknown keys). Added `tight_line` to `V3SettingsSource`, mapped in `settingsSourceOf` / `buildV3Inputs` (own_lines and tightLine). Writes go through `useSaveSetupStep` with undo labels `ruleAdded` / `ruleEdited` / `ruleRemoved`. Plan 25 (rules setup) should reuse `readRulesDoc`, `upsertRule` and `useMoneyRules`.
**2. [Rule 3] Extra files** beyond the plan list: `homeCardsModel.ts` (pure model), `useHomeCards.ts` (data hooks), `commitRule.ts` (shared save with the crossing check).
**3. Own-rule cap.** Keep, largest and category rules together count toward the 5 own rules (matches the engine's own-line limit). Keep and largest can each be added once.
**4. Crossing check** names a check when a line it did not cross now crosses, or its answer moves from fits/extra to tight/short because of the new rule (covers keep-at-least, which is the tight line, not a line fact). The warning haptic and the "This changes N open check(s)" sentence arrive as one toast (tone warning) that also carries Undo.
**5. "Save for it in Grow" monthly default** is derived (target spread over months to the ready date, 6 if none, 1 to 24) because the home sheet has no amount field; the amount can be changed in Grow.
**6. D53 mapping** onto decide_changes: pay = income row with the difference from a usual month's pay; bill stopped/paused = spending row, negative by the bill's amount, open-ended; one-off = spending in that one month. The live "usual month would leave" line is shown for pay and bill only (a one-off is a single month, so no usual-month claim).
**7. Open-check names wrap** (no numberOfLines) so decideTextFit needed no allowlist change.
