# Phase 06.1 Plan 58: Setup, safety net and sheets to the prototype (G-9) Summary

Records now shows rows with word badges and reasons, and the accounts sit behind one row that opens a sheet. Quick estimates start from the saved rough figures and save on the first tap. The safety-net padlock now always shows the real state, and the amount appears once. Rule sheets use preset chips and say Close. The open-check sheet, Add rule and Something changed use grouped cards.

## What was built

### Task 1: Records and quick estimates (A-SU-5, A-SU-6, UX-11, DS-16), commit faaf053e
- **Records:** the three D-11 checks are rows in one grouped card. Each row has a title, a reason line and a StateBadge (glyph plus word: Complete, Not complete or To finish). An incomplete last month says "Not all of October is in your records yet." and links to "Records for October" (Activity). This month says "Up to date: entries to <date>", or how old the newest entry is, or "No entries yet this month."
- **Your accounts:** one chevron row, "3 accounts · 2 to finish" (or "N accounts say what they are for" once done). It opens a sheet with the per-account choices and the same write as before. "Savings or goals" closes this sheet and opens AccountUseSheet.
- **Check again:** a secondary pill shown while Records is not done. It refetches through `useSetupFacts().retry`.
- **Kept:** the "No records yet" view, the loading line, the readiness list (plan 45), the rough figures with Clear, and returnTo (plan 65).
- **Quick estimates:**
  - Fields start from `rough_income` and `rough_spending`. A null figure stays empty. If the settings arrive after the first render, they fill the fields as long as nothing has been typed yet.
  - Each field has the currency symbol.
  - The display-role headline is "A first answer from three estimates". The header title is now "Quick estimates".
  - A NeedLine under the disabled button names the first missing figure.
  - `keyboardShouldPersistTaps="handled"` lets the first tap on "See the answer now" save while the keyboard is open.

### Task 2: Safety net (A-SN-1, DN-11, T-06.1-58-01), commit 771df6fb
- **Padlock:** `LockGlyphAnimated` draws `CLOSED_SHACKLE` when locked and `OPEN_SHACKLE` when unlocked, with testIDs `net-lock-closed` and `net-lock-open`.
  - Root cause of the bug: the old glyph kept the state only in an animated rotation and lift on a `G`.
  - The motion now only fades in the new shackle. It does not run on the first draw, and Reduce Motion skips it.
- **One amount:** the amount is now said once, as a display figure, with a legend under it: "In it now" and "Goal $2,300, set in Grow". The old duplicate legend line ("$50 in it · Goal…") and its `legend` key are removed.
- **Lock row:** its sub-line now sits beside the switch, through the new optional `ToggleRow` `sub` prop. A screen reader reads it as the hint.
- **DN-11:** the screen title is "Safety net settings". The setup item stays "Safety net". The Back control is DetailScreen's, the same as on other Decide detail screens.
- **Unchanged:** the unlock flow (D52) and every text `decide-safety-net.yaml` looks for.

### Task 3: Sheets (A-SH-1, A-SH-2, A-SH-3, A-PD-1), commit 5afa9390
- **Rule sheet (D49):**
  - Percent rules use chips: 30%, 33%, 36%, 40% and Other. Other opens a whole-percent field with % after it. Its range is 5 to 60% for the debt share and 1 to 100% for a category share.
  - Money rules have the currency symbol in their field.
  - The switch row now comes after the value.
  - The dismiss button says Close. The rule's "today" figure sits in a soft box.
  - Remove stays on own (stored) rules only. The built-in debt share can only be switched off. Removal already existed through `commitRule` with one Undo, so `commitRule.ts` did not change.
- **"Today" lines (T-06.1-58-03):** these come only from values the parent already passes in. Nothing is worked out in the sheet.
  - Debt payments at most: `debtToday`, "Today your debt payments are 10% of pay ($312 of $3,200)."
  - Keep at least: `usualLeft`, "A usual month leaves $369 today."
  - Largest single purchase and the category rules: no today line, because the engine exposes no figure for them here.
- **Add rule and Something changed (D50, D53):** the options sit in a grouped card (new `OptionCard` / `OptionRow`). The chosen row gets the accent tint as well as the filled dot.
  - The Something changed row on Decide home now has a chevron.
  - Still user-led: nothing is pre-selected and nothing is written before Save.
- **Open-check sheet (D46):**
  - A StateBadge shows the glyph and the word. Under it come the price, date and what it leaves, then "Checked <date>" on its own line (answered checks only).
  - The "records changed" note sits in a notYet tint box.
  - The actions are one grouped card. Every row has a chevron except Delete.
  - Plan 53's NeedLines and the H-8 actions are unchanged.
- **Past decisions (A-PD-1):** the stack gets the screen's top padding, so the subtitle clears the header rule.

5. **Copy guards:**
   - The first link text, "Add what’s missing", failed the instruction-verb guard, so it became "Records for {{month}}". This matches FQ10's wording, and plan 71 (H-10) adds the month filter.
   - Placeholders `{{open}}` and `{{detail}}` had no wording-page sample. `{{open}}` became `{{n}}`. The accounts row's spoken label is now built in code, so `{{detail}}` is gone.

### Interpretation choices
- **Pre-fill:** only the sum is stored, so `rough_spending` starts in the bills field with everyday at 0, and the live line matches the saved figures. No migration was added for a split.
- **Chevrons:** radio option rows carry no chevron. The prototype's `opts()` has none, and a chevron on a row that chooses in place would read as navigation. Chevrons are on the navigating rows: open-check actions, the Records accounts row and the Something changed home row.
- **Close everywhere:** Add rule and Something changed also say Close (the plan named only the rule sheet). Close still acts like the old Cancel (D-27): it discards without asking.
- **No `sheetsHome.rule.today` key:** the plan's "Today: {{value}} of {{limit}}" was not added. The existing engine-fed lines (`rule.debt.today`, `rule.keep.today`) already say it in full sentences.
- **Add rule's category share keeps its Stepper.** Only the D49 edit sheet got the chips (A-SH-2).
