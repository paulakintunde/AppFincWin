# Phase 6.1 Plan 19: Big-buy stepper Summary

**Three-step big-buy stepper (kind, price and pay, running costs) on stored drafts, with records-first running-cost pre-fills from a dated "quick estimates" table.**

## Accomplishments
- `runningCostTemplates.ts`: US car figures from the spec (insurance 14000, fuel 12000 monthly; maintenance 60000, registration 15600 yearly; value loss off), GB/CA/EU tables marked `draft: true`. A matching recorded bill (category plus name words; car insurance must name a vehicle) wins and is marked `records`. 100% branch coverage.
- `StepFrame` (D10): back, item and price, "Step n of 3", 3-segment progressbar (done segments jump back, 44 px hit areas), focus moves to the title, "Why we ask:", primary, "See the answer now" (not on step 3), `legal.decideAnswer.footer`. Slides 220 ms in the direction of travel; Reduce Motion cross-fades 150 ms.
- `KindStep` (D11): radio grid, own kinds, dashed "Add your own"; Home shows the note and keeps Next disabled; skipped when the words already read as a non-Home kind (not when arriving by Back).
- `PriceStep` (D12): price with inline `missing.no_price` / `price_too_large`; "Price already includes tax" defaults on in UK/EU; US/CA state estimate shown as a placeholder and never stored; `notice.tax_not_included` box with "Set your region" when unknown; Cash/Installments with InstallmentPlanSheet; one-off cost rows; Up front WaterfallCard.
- `CostsStep` (D13): switch rows with how-often chip (HowOftenSheet), 96 px amount field, yearly/quarterly a11y labels; costs that stop (SaleSheet, AddYourOwnSheet stop); total card with StackedBar, legend, "A usual month leaves ... After this, about ..." from the engine.
- `useStepCheck`: reads the stored check (`readStoredCheck`), debounces 600 ms into `useSaveCheckDraft`, flushes on leaving; `finish()` writes `L0.skipped_steps` and pre-fills the car lines for steps never visited.

## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] `skipped_steps` key needs a layer prefix.** The plan says layerAnswers key `skipped_steps`; `parseCheckV2` drops any key without an L0..L7 prefix, so it would vanish on reload. Stored as `L0.skipped_steps`. D21 should read that key.

**2. [Rule 3 - Blocking] Added `ownAdds.ts`** (not in files_modified) to hold the Added + Undo writes the carry-forward assigns to the stepper.

**3. Own-kind and one-off persistence is per check** (layerAnswers `L0.own_kinds`, `L0.one_offs`), not account-wide. Carrying an own kind to later checks would need a settings write; not done here.

**4. Quick-estimate (D02) expense lines** are shown read-only in the running-cost list and counted in the monthly total; they have no switch (the lines belong to the quick estimate sheet, which has no on/off flag).

**5. Not built:** dedicated dealer-fee and trade-in rows in the one-off section (copy keys exist; the "+ Add a one-off cost" path covers fees). Date picks for the installment first payment and How-often dates open DatePickerSheet with no paydays list.

## Known Stubs
- `/decide/answer/{id}` is the target of "See the answer"; that route belongs to the answer-screen plans and does not exist in this worktree yet.
- Entry into the stepper from Home (create the draft row, call `isBigBuy`) is not wired here; the routes expect an existing draft with the `id` param. A missing row renders nothing (status `not_found`).
- GB, CA and EU table amounts are draft figures awaiting owner review (plan 33).
