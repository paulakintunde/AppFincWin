# Phase 06.1: Decide screens from the prototype - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-11-06
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Testers get the new Decide, built from the owner's clickable prototype (the design standard, as for Grow in 06-CONTEXT D-04). It runs on the v3 engine on main and reads Grow's safety net, goals and debts through their connection points. Every Decide screen in the prototype is built (D01-D53 and the N answer boards). It replaces today's tester Decide screens in one release, shipped together with Grow.

Planning starts now. Building starts after Grow wave 1 merges the safety net, goal and debt connection points (06-CONTEXT D-01).

Not in this phase: anything that needs shared household money (stays in 08.1, after Phase 8), the home-buying cost screens (own phase after 08.2), reading test round 2 and the public switch-on (stay in 08.1 / 08.2).

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Scope (what moves from 08.1)
- **D-01:** All prototype Decide screens are built in 06.1. That covers Decide home with More details (D01, D02), the big-buy stepper (D10-D13), the answer screens (D20, D21 and boards N02-N26 with their secondary screens N03, N14, N16-N19), Decide setup (banner D30, checklist D31, items D32-D37, done D38, quick-estimates first answer D39), every sheet (D40-D46, D49, D50, D53), Past decisions (D47, D48 / N20) and safety-net settings (D51, D52 / N07, N07U). No mix of old and new screens.
- **D-02:** Home buying stays hidden. Picking "Home" as the kind (or words that read as a home) shows a short plain note that home checks come later. The D13 Home variant, the mortgage variant of D42 and the closing-% sheet are not built. FIN-30 keeps its own phase after 08.2.
- **D-03:** Household is left out entirely. 06.1 shows no household rows, splits or the `shared_money` caveat. 08.1 adds the household parts to these same screens after Phase 8.
- **D-04:** 08.1 shrinks to household data in Decide, reading test round 2 and its fixes, and the public switch-on after 08.2 passes. The planner takes every 08.1 fix ID and every row of `08.1-CARRY-OVER-FROM-05.4.md` that does not need household data or reading-test round 2 into 06.1, and lists the split in the first plan. The ROADMAP 08.1 entry is amended to match.

### Release to testers
- **D-05:** Replace in one go. The new screens replace today's tester Decide screens for every tester in one release, and the old 05.x screens and their routes are deleted in this phase. Only one Decide remains in the routes (05.5 success criterion 5 and its "retired screens deleted" gate are met here for the screen side; 05.5 keeps the engine and plumbing side). No per-account switch between old and new.
- **D-06:** One release with Grow (06-CONTEXT D-03). The new Decide reads Grow's goals, safety net and debts, so both reach testers together in one preview APK or OTA.
- **D-07:** The reading test does not block building. The combined prototype sessions with testers run while 06.1 is built. Findings become fix plans inside 06.1 where they fit; the rest go to 08.1's round 2. There is no screen freeze before building.
- **D-08:** The Decide wording sign-off is a task inside 06.1, before release. It uses the 05.4-41 review page (about 35 worked answers plus every new or changed line) and the signed-copy file mechanism (`decideWordingSigned.json`). The 48 undo and History lines are reviewed at the same time, and so are the draft right-answer cases BR-16 and SN-16..SN-24 (05.4 D-180). The new screens ship with signed wording.

### Prototype open questions (spec "Open questions for the owner")
- **D-09 (Q1, decided earlier):** Big-buy rule as in the spec. The stepper opens when the kind has running costs (Car), it is paid over time, or the price with tax is over the engine's band-6 edge (US$5,000 converted to the home currency). Everything else goes straight to the answer.
- **D-10 (Q2, decided earlier):** The setup banner has no close or snooze. It shrinks to a one-line row once started and stays until all 6 items are done.
- **D-11 (Q3):** "Records" counts as done when last month is complete, this month has an entry within the last 10 days, and every account says what it is for. The "2 of 3 months" progress stays on the Your month card and never holds the banner open.
- **D-12 (Q4):** Keep only an optional "Need or want?" choice under More details. It feeds `check.purpose` and reorders the options only, never the answer (05.4 D-41 / D-110). Drop the "Is this an emergency?" question. The safety-net lock alone decides whether an answer may use the net.
- **D-13 (Q6):** Remove the "+ Investment" link from Open checks. Investment checks need their own design. Investments live in Grow (06-CONTEXT D-11).
- **D-14 (first answer):** The first time a user taps Check it, they see the one-time "How Decide answers" card (05.4 D-120, `legal.decideIntro.*`), then one safety-net question ("Do you have one? About how much?") (05.4 D-61). The answer is written to Grow's flagged safety-net goal (06-CONTEXT D-06) and ticks setup item 4. Every later check goes straight through. Unlocking stays only in safety-net settings (D51, D52).
- **D-15 (Q5, example data):** Prototype example figures (records up to Oct 21, $425 usual month) are design examples only. Screens use the engine's real numbers. The scenario table in `design/LEDGER.md` (run through the real v3 engine on 2026-11-06) is the reference for test fixtures.

### Look and motion
- **D-16:** Adopt the 7 state colours as named theme tokens, each with its tint: fits green #1B4D3E / #E4F0E8, tight amber #8A5A1B / #FBEFD6, safety net navy #1F3A5F / #E4ECF6, extra teal #1E6B5E / #DFF1EE, short red #B4472A (text #933720) / #F8E2DA, conditional plum #5B3A75 / #EEE6F4, not yet slate #3E5C6B / #E6ECEF, plus muted text on tints #5A564B. They appear only in badges, verdict cards and amounts, always with a symbol and a word (UX-09), and must pass the contrast tests in `src/theme/contrast.ts`. CLAUDE.md's "Design fidelity" rule is updated in this phase to name them. Grow may use the same tokens.
- **D-17:** No Lottie. The few one-time moments (padlock lock and unlock, the setup-done tick, the verdict card rising) use react-native-svg and Reanimated. They play once and are skipped under Reduce Motion. No new native dependency is added, so no new native build is forced by this choice.
- **D-18:** When the prototype and existing code disagree, layout, sizes and flow follow the prototype. Answer sentences come from the engine's copy keys (`decide.v3.*`, `legal.decide*`), so words and numbers always match the engine. Where the prototype's words differ from a copy key, the key wins; new screen-only text is added as new keys and goes through the D-08 sign-off.
- **D-19 (name, owner 2026-11-06):** The card and screen called "Lines you will not cross" in the prototype are called **"Your money rules"**, and each item in it is a **rule** ("Add your own rule", "Done · 3 rules", "under your $300 rule"). This covers D01 item 7, setup item 5 (D30, D31, D36), D49, D50, the D51 entry and answer N15. N15's sentence comes from the engine's copy keys (D-18), so its new wording goes through the D-08 sign-off. Grow G10 uses the same name (06 D-27). The live app keeps the old title until 05.5 deletes that screen.

### Owner answers to the research questions (2026-11-06, 06.1-RESEARCH "Questions for the Owner")
- **D-20 (Q1, unlock):** A new Decide-only flag on Grow's flagged safety-net goal, "answers may use it", default off, written only by Decide's unlock screens (D51, D52). Grow's lock rules and screens stay as they are. The old `decide_settings` safety-net lock column is not used.
- **D-21 (Q2, quick estimates):** All four D41 tiles ship: Expense, Loan, Savings and Income. Each is a temporary line for that one check only. Loan, Savings and Income are new maths: they get new right-answer cases the owner approves before release. Savings marked "answers can use it" counts as spare money for that check only, and the answer says so.
- **D-22 (Q3, Save for it):** One save makes a locked Grow goal linked to the check (for installments, only the up-front part) and moves the check to "waiting", with one Undo that reverses both.
- **D-23 (Q4, "nothing" answers):** Store four small "I answered this" flags on the Decide settings ("No other debts", "Not set yet", "No rules", "These bills are right") so those setup items stay ticked. The two first-run screens that still open the old Decide (first check, rough figures) are pointed at the new quick estimates (D39) and the new check.
- **D-24 (Q5, running-cost pre-fills):** Use the amount from a matching bill in the user's records when one exists (for example car insurance). Otherwise use a small in-app table of round figures by kind and country, labelled "quick estimates", dated, and reviewed by the owner on the wording page (D-08).
- **D-25 (Q6, intro card):** "How Decide answers" is remembered on the phone only (existing device-local hints pattern). A new phone shows it once more; a text-version change shows it again.
- **D-26 (Q7, new rules):** FIN-20 (self-employed tax set-aside), FIN-22 (long-loan check) and FIN-23 (UK/EU rates match lender quotes) are built in the last wave, each behind the owner's approval of its right-answer cases. A rule whose cases are not approved by release moves back to 08.1 and never blocks the release. FIN-12 stays in 08.1 (or v1.1).
- **D-27 (Q8, extra sheets):** Build every prototype sheet not named earlier: Buy later (D60), Cheaper one (D61), Full cost (D54), Every date for 90 days (D55), Month by month (D56), Free help (D65), Save for it amount (D64), and the pay and bill edit sheets. Skip D67 (investment check, D-13) and D43C (closing costs, D-02). Plan 1 inventories every `sheet('D..')` and `screen('..')` id in `design/decide-screens.js`.
- **D-28 (Q9, first safety net):** For a user with no Grow safety-net goal, typing an amount in the D-14 question creates the flagged goal in Grow, locked, with that amount. "Not set yet" creates nothing (and sets the D-23 flag); answers then say they hold nothing back.
- **D-29 (Q10, release):** Production migrations are pushed first (human-gated). Then publish to the preview channel as an OTA if the runtime fingerprint still matches the installed preview builds; otherwise build one new preview APK for both phones at the end.
- **D-30 (build start, from research):** Building starts after Grow's Decide feed (plan 06-10) and connection points (06-05) are merged, not only Grow wave 1, because the answers read Grow data through 06-10.

### Established Patterns
- Expo Router file routes under `app/(app)/decide/` (today: changes, check/[id], lines, open, past, pots, settings, setup, dev-harness). The new screens replace these (D-05).
- Engine is pure: screens call prepare once and read `Result`. No engine import of UI (CLAUDE.md).
- Copy guard: every on-screen Decide sentence is a typed key and passes the sentence rule (05.4-37).

### Integration Points
- Grow connection points (06 wave 1): safety net, goal, debt. Decide setup items 3 and 4 and the D-14 safety-net question write through them.
- Activity: pay series, bill series (setup items 1 and 2), quick add from the Records item, "I bought it" (N19) records the purchase and offers running costs as bills one by one.
- Grow: "Save for it" (N18) creates a goal linked to the decision (06-CONTEXT "Create from Decide").

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- The owner's rule from the prototype brief: calm and confident; one own two-layer icon set; spacing steps 4/8/12/16/22/28; rows 52 / 64 px; 44 px targets; cues use colour + shape + word; a normal day stays quiet; haptics by meaning (light tap for save, warning for a crossed line or unlocking the net, success only for a goal reached).
- Life changes are user-led: "Something changed?" (D53) suggests nothing and applies nothing on its own.
- "Ask, don't block": a new user can get a first answer from quick estimates (D39) before setup is done.


### Gap-closure decisions (owner popup, 2026-11-12, after 06.1-DEVICE-AUDIT.md)

- GQ1: Not ready with records shows a named list of missing items, each with a fix and a one-tap answer; the quick-estimates link shows only when estimates can count. No engine change.
- GQ2: setup "Records" is done only when the readiness list is empty.
- GQ3: Decide's pushed inner screens (answer, stepper, setup) have no tab bar, like Grow and Activity. Audit row X-1 is closed by this decision, not a gap.
- GQ4: Pay asks "How are you paid?" with six chips; "Salary or wages" is preselected only when steady pay is found in Activity.
- GQ5: the Your month card keeps both figures, labelled "after this month's plans" (headline) and "A usual month" (tiles).
- GQ6: Pixel 6 checks use a preview APK built from this branch, Pixel 6 only; no OTA.
- GQ7: plan 32 Task 2 is folded into plan 61.
- Out of 06.1: own icons, calm motion, theme-tinted background (new app-life phase after 06.1). Wording: short line first, "Why?"/"More" for details, facts never removed.
