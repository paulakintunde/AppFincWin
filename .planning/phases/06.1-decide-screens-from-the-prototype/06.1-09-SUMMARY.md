## Deviations from Plan (Task 1)

1. **[Rule 1]** A once-only expense is a one-time dated bill, not a running cost with start=end (that would add 1/12 to the usual month); it is left out of the usual month. Repeating expenses are running costs as planned.
2. **[Rule 3]** A loan is a recorded debt owed monthly x payments still to come, not a debt-payment series (the series missed this month's figure: QL-03 gave -$300 vs -$450). A past first date uses one payment per month. Limit: a first date more than a month away counts from the next occurrence of its day (cautious side).
3. Income is a pay-role series (other-income series never reach the cash path). Income dated today or earlier counts from tomorrow; income landing this month is added to the unspent-money anchor, matching pay still to arrive.
4. Usable savings = an unlocked goal in a synthetic untouched savings account, so it counts exactly like spareSavings (06 D-22). `unlocked_goals_counted` excludes the quick part, so only `quick_savings_counted` names it.
5. Open design points for later plans: share scaling of a repeating quick expense on a shared check is not handled; a once-only expense may raise `yearly_bill_ahead` (series name set from the quick line).
