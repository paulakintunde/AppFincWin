> **Added 2026-10-10 (IMP-05):** new plan for D-30, D-32 and D-34 (commit path).
> **Revision 2026-10-10 (checker warning 8):** the mark-paid fallback line carries `stmt_orig_*`. Fee lines whose purchase is a mark-paid are held back and inserted by the finalize step, pointing at the pending line or, if the mark-paid is set aside, re-pointed to the fallback line. The kept-mark-paid limitation is CONTEXT D-36 (user-accepted 2026-10-10). The old Task 2 is split into Task 2 (pipeline + finalize) and Task 3 (mapping roles).
> **Revision 2026-10-10 (checker round 2 blocker):** held fee lines are now part of the import's single undo step. Their ids go into `inserted` (the inverse of inserts) and into `n` before the step is built. They are inserted right after `applyPatches` succeeds AND on the `stepAlreadyRecorded` replay return, so Undo removes them and a retry inserts them exactly once.
