# Phase 10.2: Envelope rollover - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-11-24
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Unspent (or overspent) amounts in a category's monthly limit carry into the next month, so a category with a limit works like an envelope (was backlog 999.3; prototype You > Data row "Envelope rollover", `FincWin United.dc.html` L3258, L5275-5279, L6969). It changes **limits only**: it moves no money and creates no Decide envelope. Category parts from Phase 10.1 split lines count toward each part's category.

Out of scope: real money pots (Decide envelopes from 05.1 already exist and are unchanged), budgeting templates, yearly budgets.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Switch
- **D-01:** **One "Envelope rollover" switch** for all categories that have a monthly limit, under You (prototype L5359 wording: "Unspent cap carries into the next month"). **Off by default** for everyone, so nothing changes for current users.

### What carries
- **D-02:** Leftovers **and overspends** carry (true envelope style). Spend $550 of a $500 limit and next month's limit is $450, shown as "$500 − $50 over in October". A limit never goes below $0. This is stricter than the prototype's `rolloverFor`, which carried leftovers only.
- **D-03:** The carried amount is **capped at one month's limit**: a $500 Groceries limit can grow to $1,000 at most (and, per D-02, fall to $0 at least).
- **D-04:** Carrying is worked out per calendar month from the category's spend (spend minus refunds, 02.2 D-04), using Phase 10.1 category parts where a line is split. Integer minor units throughout.

### Turning it on and off
- **D-05:** Turned on mid-month, **this month's end** is the first to carry (November's result carries into December). Past months are never re-worked.
- **D-06:** Turned off, limits go **back to their plain amounts at once** and carried amounts are dropped; turning it on again starts fresh. A short note says so before switching off.

### Decide and display
- **D-07:** In Decide, **only the limit changes**: Decide's spare money is unchanged, but a purchase check in that category compares with the carried limit ("$580 limit this month"). Carried amounts are never held back like a 05.1 envelope and never counted as spare money. Approved Decide answers must not move when rollover is off.
- **D-08:** Carried amounts show on the **category and its limit bar, Activity's category view and Insights**, wherever a category's limit is shown, with the same wording: "$500 + $80 carried from October" / "$500 − $50 over in October".

### Claude's Discretion
- **Level:** list rollover in Features at **level 3** ("Planning"), as in the prototype (L3258). This amends 09-LEVEL-MAP §1 item 6 (owner said "No" on 2026-11-19 because it was not built); update `featureRegistry.ts` and the level map together.
- Whether a category's limit change mid-month affects the carried amount (planner defines, documented with right-answer cases).
- Storage: compute carried amounts from history versus storing a per-month carry; planner's choice, as long as turning off/on behaves per D-05/D-06 and undo works.
- Household: categories are per user; on shared lines, the member's own category (07 D-33 resolution) decides which limit a part counts toward.

</decisions>
