## Owner decision (2026-11-18): Decide stages by data, shown to the user

The owner asked whether Decide should stay hidden until 3 months of data exist, or launch as an estimates-only "mini" version first. Hiding was rejected: the 14-day store trial ends before a user could see Decide on their own money, finance apps keep only about 4-9% of users at day 30, it contradicts the "Can I Afford It?" listing, and it would undo Phase 9 D-28. Firm answers from estimates alone were also rejected (Decide is where a wrong answer causes real harm); exact calculators may stay firm.

**Chosen: visible stages.** Keep the data-based ladder already in the engine and make it visible:
- **First read** (no full month): cash-level answer from the user's figures (DEC-16 / DCU-10), labelled as resting on them.
- **Full answer** (1 full month logged or imported).
- **Deeper parts** (3+ full months): confidence (CONFIDENCE_MIN_MONTHS), income swing (INCOME_SWING_MIN_MONTHS), history-based pay.

The full design run (`/gsd-ui-phase 08.1`, Q8) must add: a plain line saying what the answer rests on and what unlocks the next stage; progress towards 1 and 3 months; and a setup prompt to import about 3 statements (CSV/OFX/PDF) for a full answer on day one. The planner checks which parts truly need 3 months and holds back only those.

Planning note (2026-11-18): built by plans 08.1-19 (engine stage read-model and the 3-month check), 08.1-20 (answer side: basis-window sentences, StageLine, StageTag, About rows) and 08.1-21 (StageCard, import prompt, Not ready actions, Records setup prompt); split from one plan after the plan check. Phase 9's sample-data answer before the trial (09-CONTEXT D-28) uses the same First read treatment ("First read" tag, basis "sample figures"); Phase 9 files are not edited by 08.1.

## Owner decision (2026-11-18): history windows stay app-chosen; add year views later

Users do **not** choose a 3/6/12-month window (switching until the answer says "fits" would undermine trust). Keep: everyday spending = median of last 6 full months; variable/seasonal income = up to 12 months (D-33); worst of next 12 months; no dates past 12 months (D-06). The answer names the window it used.

**Deferred to a small later phase (not 08.1):** a "same month last year" note shown beside the answer (information only, never changes the verdict), and spotting yearly costs from 12 months of records that the user never set up as yearly bills.
