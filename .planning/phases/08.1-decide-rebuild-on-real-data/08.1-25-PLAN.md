**Owner answers (2026-11-21): A for all five.** Q1: the trigger is a $0 share under the rule and nothing paid into a joint account this month; private accounts are never read. Q2: the rule plus equal-shares wording, with "See equal shares" and "Not now". Q3: every member sees the settings card, which names the person. Plain words first. Each has a recommended first option (A). The build waits for the answers; tasks say "per Q1" etc.

**Q1. How does the app tell that someone "brings no money"?**
- A. (recommended) Their share of the joint money comes to $0 under the group's rule, and they paid nothing into a joint account this month. Their own private accounts are not looked at, so nobody's private records are shown to the group. Under "What each person brings", money carried from before is shared equally, so anyone with carried joint money is never at $0.
- B. Same as A, and also no pay in their own accounts this month. The app can only check this for the person looking at their own answer, so the settings screen could not show it for other people.
- C. Only "their share is $0 under the rule", even if they paid something in.

**Q2. What should the suggestion say?** (`{{rule}}` is the rule's name, e.g. "What each person brings"; `{{name}}` is the member's name as the group sees it.)
- A. (recommended) On the answer: "Under {{rule}}, your share of the joint money is $0 this month. With equal shares, each person counts the same part." Buttons: "See equal shares" and "Not now". In settings: "Under this rule, {{name}}'s share of the joint money is $0 this month. With equal shares, each person counts the same part." Button: "See equal shares".
- B. Shorter. Answer: "Your share of the joint money is $0 this month." In settings: "{{name}}'s share of the joint money is $0 this month." Same buttons.
- C. Name the cause. "You paid nothing into {{account}} this month, so your share is $0." (Not recommended: it singles a person out by what they earn.)

**Q3. Who sees the suggestion on "How spending is shared"?**
- A. (recommended) Every member sees it, and it names the person whose share is $0. The whole group picks the rule, and the pay-in totals are already shared with the group.
- B. Every member sees it, but without a name ("One person's share of the joint money is $0 this month").
- C. Only the person whose share is $0 sees it.

**Q4. Can the line on the answer be put away?**
- A. (recommended) Yes. "Not now" hides it on this phone until next month. If it is still true next month, it shows again. The card on "How spending is shared" cannot be hidden. It goes away once the rule changes or the share is above $0.
- B. Yes. "Not now" hides it for good, until something changes (a pay-in, the rule, or the members).
- C. No. The line stays until the group changes the rule or the share is above $0.

**Q5. The new test cases and the engine version.** The new cases keep today's answers and only add the new line. Adding approved cases changes the engine's version lock, so the version must go up.
- A. (recommended) Approve the cases HH-35..HH-42 with this plan's answers, and bump the engine version to 07.2. The plan lists any existing case that now also carries the line. Its answer does not change.
- B. Keep the new cases as drafts (checked only for "runs without error") until you review them. The version stays 07.1 for now.
- C. Keep the line out of the engine (screens only). The right-answer cases then cannot check it, so screen tests stand in for them.
