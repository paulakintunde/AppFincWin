## Implementation Decisions

### Household in a check
- **D-01:** The scope choice "Who pays: Just me / Household" goes on the **price step**, next to the price. It shows only when the viewer's household has 2 or more members.
- **D-02:** The default is **Just me** on every check. A check uses shared money only when the person picks Household.
- **D-03:** The Household figure counts three things: the viewer's own money, the rounded total of each partner who has opted in, and the shared lines. If no partner has opted in, one plain line under the answer says only the viewer's money is counted and names who hasn't shared, for example "Only your money is counted. Sam hasn't shared theirs."
  - The locked rules from Phase 8 still apply: opt-in is off by default and counts as one rounded total, checks are never shared, two "Fits" answers can never spend the same shared money twice, and D-37's "still answers, with a named caveat".
- **D-04:** In a household, **Activity's month total counts the viewer's share**, matching Home's "Mine" figure. Shared rows still show their full amount, with a "your share" label. This resolves the 08-25 "known limit" about the two figures differing (03.1 D-47 kept them equal).

### Decide answer fixes
- **D-05:** **A ready-by date means "stays above $0".** The date is the first day a fresh check would keep the account above $0 for the next 90 days. Today a plan's date only means "when you can cover the first payment" (05.4-22 `planReadyBy`).
  - This is an engine change. Tests come first, then a revision bump, then a changed-answers review against the right-answer table.
  - SN-27's re-check table in `08.1-PROPOSED-SN-27.md` is the worked example: the real cash date is about 1 March 2027, not 1 December.
  - The existing rule still holds: no date when the usual month is short after the payment (engine revision 06.9, SN-27 option a).
- **D-06:** **No dates more than 12 months out.** If the D-05 date is over 12 months away, the answer says "Not within a year at your current pace" and points to the cheaper option and Save for it.
- **D-07:** **New "below $0 even without this" line.** When the account goes below $0 within the window even without the purchase, the answer shows one line near the top: "Your account goes below $0 by {date} even without this."
  - Example: SN-18, where Riley reaches -$250 by 31 January either way.
  - The line follows the UX-19 limits: at most 25 words and 3 numbers.
- **D-08:** **DS-4 wording.** Before buying, the answer card's date line reads "If you buy it today." After Record as bought it reads "Bought on {date}." The words need a wording sign-off (signed-copy file mechanism).

### Record as bought
- **D-09:** **Varying running costs** (fuel, repairs) become a new **"expected" monthly Activity line**, for example "Fuel, about $120". It counts in the month picture until real spending replaces it.
  - Fixed running costs stay as bills the user confirms one by one (ROADMAP success criterion 2).
  - This answers the 05.5-13 option B question and the second `test.todo`.
- **D-10:** **Old item in the same save.** If the check sells the old item or pays off its loan, the same save writes the sale money in and the payoff. Each is a row the user ticks first. One undo reverses the whole save. It reuses Grow's sale path (`20261106000300_grow_debt_plan_and_sale.sql`) and doesn't invent a second one.
- **D-11:** **Linked goal offered for closing.** If a Save-for-it goal is linked to the check, the save shows a ticked row to close it, which the user can untick. This follows the rule that the app suggests and never applies changes on its own.
- **D-12:** **Household purchases are shared.** A purchase checked with scope Household is written as a **shared line** with the household's normal split rule. The Phase 8 owing rules apply, so a purchase paid from a joint account creates no owing. Its running costs become shared bills.
- **Wiring (not a decision, must be built):** the screens must pass the check's `replaces.stops` and the recorded series to `record`. The server side (`20261116000100_decide_go_end_reason.sql`) already exists. Until the screens pass them, no bill ends.

### Reading test round 2 and testers
- **D-13:** About **5 readers** use the existing reading-test kit and score page.
  - Boards for tasks 2, 3 and 5 are updated, and tasks 15 and 16 get new boards (D-61..D-63).
  - The "$0 left" and "talk the app into a yes" tasks are included (ROADMAP success criterion 4).
- **D-14:** Round 2 runs **after** the household and answer changes are built and the boards are refreshed. Its findings get one fix pass, then the screens freeze (QA-01).
- **D-15:** **Named testers** are the owner's accounts plus people the owner names, with the round-2 readers as the natural pick. The names are asked for at that task and kept in the repo tester list.
- **D-16:** Apple enrolment isn't done. Round 2 and the screen-reader pass run on **Android with TalkBack**. The iPhone XR and VoiceOver checks are an open item for when enrolment lands, and they don't block 08.1.
