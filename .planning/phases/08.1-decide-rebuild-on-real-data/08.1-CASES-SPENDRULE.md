## What the code does today (findings a-e)

(a) **Cash path.** Yes, every member counts the joint account's balance in full, but it is not the true joint balance.
`buildInputs.ts:243-247` adds every live cash-like account's `source.balances[a.id]` (joint included) to cash on hand, and
`buildInputs.ts:423,437` puts the same figure on the `V3Account` with `joint: true`. The engine builds the spending money from
those account balances (`engine/decide/v3/funds/build.ts` `spendingOf`, line 43; `prepare.ts:257,264`). The number comes from the
`account_balances` RPC (`20260926000600_record_read_rpcs.sql:15-24`), which is `security invoker` over `transactions_active`.
Row security on transactions (`20261122000100_household_privacy_wave1.sql:64-65`) shows a member their own lines plus shared
lines only. Money going out of a joint account is shared at once (`hh_share_on_insert`, `20261122000600:423-424`); money going in
stays personal to whoever put it in (D-17). So a member sees: opening balance + their own pay-ins - every shared payment out. A
partner's pay-ins are invisible. The true joint balance exists only in `joint_account_balances` (`20261122000300:3127-3144`),
which Decide does not call.

(b) **Unspent.** The confirmed unspent anchor is a number the person typed (`buildInputs.ts:496-517`, `v3.unspent_anchor`). It is
not computed from accounts, so the builder cannot tell whether it includes joint money. If a person typed a total that includes the
joint account, it is counted a second time next to the joint balance. Plan 22 treats it as personal money and says so on the
sheet's question list (Q14 below).

(c) **Month flows.** A pay-in to a joint account is the creator's own line, counted in full for the creator only. A payment out of
it is a shared line, counted at the member's share (D-17: `viewerRows`, `householdScope.ts:37-42`). So month money from the joint
account is already "my pay-ins less my share of spending", not the account's own month figure.

(d) **Partner figure.** Yes, it carries joint money. `usePublishPartnerFigure.ts:43` publishes `monthPicture(prepared).leftAfterPlans`
= `homeFigure(prepared)` (`engine/decide/v3/afterBuy.ts:79-88`), the month figure built on the spending balance, which holds the
joint account's balance in full. So Ali's published "$ left" can include joint money, and in a Household check Noa then pools it
on top of her own joint balance.

(e) **Conclusion.** Today two partners **can** both be told Fits for the same joint money. Reading (a): money carried in the joint
account from before (the opening balance, or anything either of them paid in and that the other can see through shared spending)
shows in full to both members' cash path, and reading (d) lets the published figure repeat it in a Household pool. In JA-01
(HH-25) a $5,000 joint balance carried from before: both Ali and Noa count the whole $5,000, so each could be told Fits for a
$5,000 purchase: **$5,000 of joint money is counted twice, $10,000 in total** (plus any copy of it inside the published figure).
Pay-ins made this month are counted only for the person who made them, so they are not doubled, but a partner's pay-in is missing
from the other's view (HH-27: Ali sees $3,000 of pay-ins, Noa $2,000, neither sees $5,000). The planner's reading is confirmed;
one correction: pay-ins are not the double count, the carried money is. (No scratch run was needed: every line above is read
straight from the code.)
