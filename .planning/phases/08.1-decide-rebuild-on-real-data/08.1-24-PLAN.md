Plain words first. Each has a recommended first option (A). The build waits for the answers; the executor follows them where
tasks say "per Q1" etc. If an answer is not the recommended one, the task text names what changes.

**Q1. How should the sign sit next to the verdict word?**
- A. (recommended) One row: the caution icon, then the "Fits" badge, then "· dips below $0 on 14 Jun" in amber. It reads like your example: "⚠ Fits · dips below $0 on 14 Jun". On a narrow screen or with large text, the words wrap to the next line.
- B. An amber pill before the badge, holding the icon and the words: "[⚠ Dips below $0 on 14 Jun] [Fits]".
- C. The "Fits" badge first, then the amber pill after it. (This puts the icon after the verdict word, not before it.)

**Q2. What exact words should the sign use?**
- A. (recommended) "dips below $0 on 14 Jun". A screen reader says "Caution: dips below $0 on 14 June." Also ask plan 14 (the wording sign-off) to change the longer line under the answer from "by {{date}}" to "on {{date}}", so both name the same day the same way.
- B. The same sign words, but leave the longer line's "by {{date}}" for plan 14 to decide.
- C. "goes below $0 on 14 Jun", using the same verb as the longer line.

**Q3. Where does the sign show?**
- A. (recommended) On every answer that has the later dip: Just me and Household answers, and the saved "as checked" view of a past check. In the saved view, it stays still and does not breathe.
- B. Only on the live answer screen, for both Just me and Household. The saved view shows no sign.
- C. Only on Just me answers.

**Q4. How should it "breathe"?**
- A. (recommended) Two slow breaths. The icon grows about 12% and back, 0.6 s each way, so about 2.4 s in all. It starts once the card has risen in, then stays still.
- B. Three slow breaths, the same size and speed, about 3.6 s in all.
- C. Two quicker breaths, 0.4 s each way, about 1.6 s in all.

**Q5. How should the two new icons look? Both are drawn in FincWin's own style: a soft shape at 45% under a 2-pt line.**
- A. (recommended) Caution: a rounded triangle with "!" inside. Info: a circle with "i" inside. The triangle makes the caution easy to tell apart from the round "Fits" and "Tight" state shapes.
- B. Both round: a circle with "!" for caution, and a circle with "i" for info. This matches the other state shapes, but the caution looks a lot like the info icon.
- C. Do not draw them yet. Put a board round in front of the owner first. (This delays the plan.)

**Q6. Should the "What this means" tips from plan 23 use the new info icon?** (This is a small change: one shared component.)
- A. (recommended) Yes. Show the info icon before the words "What this means". The words stay, because every icon in FincWin has a word beside it.
- B. Show the icon only. The screen reader label stays. (This breaks the rule "an icon always has a word", 06.2 D-06.)
- C. No. Keep the text link as it is.
