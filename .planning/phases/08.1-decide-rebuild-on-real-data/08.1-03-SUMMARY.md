## Design notes
- `set_partner_optin` deletes the owner's published figure once no opt-in of theirs remains on (off is immediate).
- `partner_figures` and `safety_net_shares` have no client write grant; `partner_figures` has no select grant either. Reads go through RPCs.
- `safety_net_shares.rounded_saved` is seeded from `opening_saved` (rounded down) and refreshed by the owner's device through an extra RPC, `publish_safety_net_saved` (not in the plan: the saved amount is computed client-side, so the server needs a way to receive it). Size is the goal target rounded down to 1000.
- `partner_money_total` also returns `no_figure` when both sides are on but nothing is published yet.
- `src/engine/household/index.ts` did not exist; it was created with the single export.
