## Deviations

- **[Rule 1, bug] Blank stepper step (8c272c6c).** On big purchases, going to the next step often showed an empty page with no
  buttons. Maestro saw `step-frame` at x=1138 on a 1080 px screen: the frame mounted at `shown = 0` (one screen width off to the
  side, or opacity 0 under Reduce Motion), and when Android dropped the slide's updates it stayed there. Reproduced by hand on the
  first try. The frame now mounts at rest and only then moves to the start and animates back (the TabSlideIn / FadeSwap pattern);
  the worst case is a step with no slide. Tests: 90/90 in the stepper suites, plus two new ones for the resting position. A unit test
  cannot reproduce dropped frames, so the proof is the phone: after the OTA the flows walk all three steps.
- **Preview OTA `ee872d87`** (owner approved with the fix): published from fincwin-w1 at 8c272c6c with
  `APP_UPDATES_CHECK=ON_LOAD --environment preview`, after `eas fingerprint:compare` matched build 3c50fbb2.
- **Flow-script fixes** (f8bc0cf7, afe95db7): `When?` under the keyboard; `childOf` matches direct children only (Who pays pill, the
  Bought result's Undo); the stepper walked one step at a time with a wait for each heading; one short scroll before Next and
  See the answer, which can sit half under the Android navigation bar.
- **Test data on the owner's account.** A failed run recorded a Used car purchase and, on the next run, confirmed spare money. Both
  were rolled back from History in one step (the confirm listed only those two). The test checks this plan's runs left were deleted
  (undoable). Older test checks from 2026-11-14 were left for the owner.
