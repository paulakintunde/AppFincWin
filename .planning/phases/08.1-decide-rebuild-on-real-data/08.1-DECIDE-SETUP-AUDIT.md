# Decide setup gate: audit (2026-11-21)

Owner's ask: the Decide setup sometimes creates a log jam. It is aggressive and unclear about next steps; users want an effortless
flow and leave on frustration. Gently ask at the point of need, encourage the minimum to get a result, and use popups and other
components to show what is missing. An audit, not a fix.

## What a user meets after "Check it"

1. "How Decide answers" card (first time only).
2. "Do you have a safety net?" (first time only).
3. **Not ready yet**: replaces the whole answer when any of about 14 conditions trips. Two engine gates: readiness
   (`engine/decide/v3/readiness.ts`: last month fully recorded, this month current, every goal linked to an account) and
   needs-input (`judge/needsInput.ts`: eight question kinds plus missing purchase facts).
4. **Fix** usually leaves the answer for a setup screen or Activity.

## Findings, worst first

1. **"Did your pay arrive?" has no answer screen.** `pay_arrival` answers are stored and read (`decideAnswers`, `buildInputsParts`),
   the undo label "Pay arrival saved" exists, but no screen saves one. Fix routes to `/decide/setup/pay` (AnswerScreen FIX_ROUTE),
   which never asks about arrival. The only way out is to mark the pay paid in Activity. The offered choices are "No pay that time"
   and "Income comes in elsewhere"; the common truth, "Yes, it came", has no one-tap answer.
2. **All or nothing.** A pay six days late, one unlogged $11.99 bill or a quiet week of spending hides the verdict, even when the
   gap cannot change it. The engine already has a safe default for an unanswered pay (counted as neither received nor due).
3. **No end in sight.** The card shows "First: …" and "More may follow"; each answer reveals the next. Progress that starts slow
   raises drop-off (Villar, Callegaro & Yang 2013, meta-analysis of 32 experiments).
4. **Three setup stories.** App setup ("Answer 11 short questions" on Home), Decide setup ("5 of 6 done") and the Not ready items.
   5 of 6 does not mean answers work, and 6 of 6 is not needed. Median setup-checklist completion is about 10% (Userpilot 2024,
   188 companies).
5. **A cliff in month two.** Quick estimates count only while no earlier month exists (`readiness.ts`, D-57). On the first of the
   next month a user who was getting answers meets "<last month> isn't fully recorded yet", answered only by "Records begin in …".
6. **Fix leaves the answer.** Only spare money, unexplained money and cover are answered in place.
7. **Chore wording.** "…isn't in your records yet" plus "Records for November" reads as homework, not a yes/no question.
8. **Small friction.** On the stepper, Next and See the answer can sit half under the Android navigation bar until scrolled.

## Evidence (research, 2026-11-21)

- NN/g, modal and nonmodal dialogs: interrupt only when the information blocks the task; ask one easy question at a time; keep
  easy fixes next to where they apply.
- Affordability calculators answer from editable defaults (NerdWallet, Zillow, Fannie Mae); Credit Karma "Left After Bills" shows a
  labelled estimate. Google PAIR: show confidence to calibrate trust; prompt checks when stakes are high.
- TurboTax refund meter (forum report, unverified): a provisional number misleads unless its assumptions show.
- NN/g bottom sheets and Apple HIG: half-height sheets that keep context, easy to dismiss, never stacked.
- NN/g defaults: most people keep them, so a default must be the safe one.
- NN/g confirmations and user control: confirmations get ignored when overused; prefer undo.
- Nunes & Drèze 2006: a card with two free stamps was completed by 34% versus 19% for an empty card.

## Recommended direction: answer first, then ask to sharpen

A. Sort missing information three ways: blocks the answer (only no price, or no income and no estimates: ask in place, one
   question, half sheet); could change it (show the verdict on a cautious assumption with a one-line "Based on …" and one-tap
   answers that update in place, with undo); cannot change it (never interrupt; it goes to "make answers sharper").
B. Ask only when the answer could flip: the engine is pure, so run it both ways; if the verdict is the same, do not ask.
C. Batch the asks with a count ("2 quick questions to sharpen this"), one tap each, never "More may follow".
D. Err cautious: never count money that may not exist (unconfirmed pay not counted; unconfirmed bill counted). Say what the answer
   rests on; keeps the declarative compliance voice.
E. Remove the month-two cliff: estimates stay as a fallback until a full month exists, blended in as records grow.
F. One progress story, started part-filled, never a gate.
G. Measure: `decide_question_answered` exists; add "not ready shown" and "left without an answer".

Quick win: a Yes / Not yet sheet for the pay question using the existing `pay_arrival` save (finding 1).
