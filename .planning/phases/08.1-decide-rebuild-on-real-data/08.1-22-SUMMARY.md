## Task 1 findings (confirmed from the code)

Today every member counts a joint account's balance in full in the cash path (`buildInputs.ts:243-247, 423, 437`), but the number is only "opening balance + my own pay-ins - every shared payment out": a partner's pay-ins are invisible under row security (`20261122000100...sql:64-65`), and the true balance exists only in `joint_account_balances`, which Decide did not call. The typed unspent figure is personal input the builder cannot tell apart from joint money. The published partner figure carried the joint balance (spending money -> `homeFigure`). So two partners could both be told Fits for the same carried joint money (JA-01: $5,000 counted twice).
