# Phase 08.1 (Decide rebuild on real data) - UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for every screen-building plan in 08.1: 04 (Activity share), 05 (tester build line), 07 (household rows), 09 (Record as bought), 10 (Who pays, household answer lines, setup row), 11 (0% if paid in full plan fields), 12 (household purchase line), 14 (answer lines, DS-4) and one new plan this spec adds for the visible Decide stages (see "Plan changes needed").
>
> The base design is not redrawn. Decide screens are Variant C as built in `06.1-UI-SPEC.md`. Household rows follow `08-UI-SPEC.md`. This file adds the new parts and fixes the places where the earlier delta named the wrong component. Tokens, type scale, spacing, row heights, icons and the seven Decide state colours are exactly as in CLAUDE.md "Design fidelity": nothing new is added.
>
> Precedence: words and numbers come from the engine's copy keys and the signed-copy file (`src/i18n/decideWordingSigned.json`). Tokens come from this file and CLAUDE.md. Behaviour comes from `08.1-CONTEXT.md` and the owner answers of 2026-11-18 in `08.1-OWNER-QUESTIONS.md`.

Sources: 08.1-CONTEXT D-01..D-16, 08.1-OWNER-QUESTIONS (Q1-Q9, stages-by-data, history windows), 08.1-RESEARCH, plans 04/05/07/09/10/11/12/14, 06.1-UI-SPEC, 08-UI-SPEC, 06.2 kit (`StatusBadge`, `IconTile`, `Glyph`), `src/theme/tokens.ts`, `layout.ts`, `contrast.ts`, CLAUDE.md.

All copy below is draft. It goes to the wording sign-off (14 Task 3) before the freeze. Voice: declarative, never "you should"; UX-19 (one sentence per row, at most 25 words and 3 numbers); no "advice", "recommend", "sure", "certain", "accurate"; no Rough/Fair/Firm.

## Spacing Scale

Declared values come from `src/theme/layout.ts` `step`, six named steps. No new token and no 24 / 32 / 48 / 64 step is introduced.

| Token | Value | Usage in 08.1 |
|-------|-------|-------|
| `hair` | 4 | `ProgressBar` thin height; glyph to word inside a badge |
| `tight` (`gapSm`) | 8 | Between chips; between a row title and its sub-line group; tick-row gaps |
| `row` (`gapMd`) | 12 | Inside a card, icon tile to text; between a field and its helper |
| `card` (`cardPad`) | 16 | `StageCard`, `BelowZeroLine`, `PartnerOptInRow` padding |
| `edge` (`screenH`) | 22 | Horizontal screen padding (non-multiple of 4; the app's existing value) |
| `section` (`groupGap`) | 28 | Between cards and groups, including the group headings in `BoughtSaveRows` |

Row heights: 52 (one line), 64 (two lines); both are minimums and grow with text scale. Icon tiles 36 (rows) and 48 (not used here). Touch targets never below 44 pt (`touchMin`); text links reach 52 dp tall through `LINK_HIT_SLOP`. Exceptions: 22 and 28 are the owner's 06.2 steps, not 4-multiple template values.

## 1. Decide stages by data (owner decision 2026-11-18)

### Stage rule (the screens read it, the engine decides)

The stage is read from the one months-of-records count the answer uses (`recordMonthsOf`, 06.1-79: home, setup and the answer's basis line all agree).

| Stage | Words on screen | When | What it unlocks next |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **First read** | 0 full months used, or the answer rests on quick estimates only (DEC-16, DCU-10): a cash-level answer from the user's own figures | 1 full month logged or imported gives the full answer |
| 2 | **Full answer** | 1 or 2 full months used | 3 full months add the deeper parts |
| 3 | none shown (internal name: Deeper parts; owner answer 2) | 3 or more full months used (`CONFIDENCE_MIN_MONTHS`, `INCOME_SWING_MIN_MONTHS`, `HISTORY_PAY_MIN_MONTHS` = 3) | nothing further (a year view is deferred) |

The deeper parts are exactly the ones the engine holds back below 3 months: how much could move this answer, pay swings, and pay worked out from history. The planner checks which parts truly need 3 months (owner note) and holds back only those. Everything else is on from stage 2.

### StageCard (Decide home)

Placed directly under the "Your month" card. It is a quiet card (no `display` figure, no primary pill; the Check it card stays the visual anchor).

Top to bottom, inside one `surface` card, `cardPad` 16:

1. Title row: `sheetTitle` "What answers rest on" left; at stages 1 and 2 only, the stage chip right (`fill1` pill, `ink` `label` text: "First read" / "Full answer"). At stage 3 there is no chip (owner answer, planning 2026-11-18).
2. One `body` sentence (table below).
3. Progress (stages 1 and 2 only): `ProgressBar` thin, `percent` = min(months, 3) / 3 x 100, `tickPercent` 33 (the one-month mark). Under it, `meta` end labels: "1 month: full answer" at the tick and "3 months: deeper parts" at the right end. Under that, `label`: the existing `records.complete` text, "Months complete enough to use: {{monthsUsed}} of {{monthsTotal}}." (reused, not re-worded).
4. `ImportPromptRow` (stages 1 and 2 only).

At stage 3 the card shows only the title and the sentence, "Answers use your last {{months}} months of records, with every part on." (no chip, no bar, no prompt), so it takes little room.

| Stage | Sentence (draft) |
|---|---|
| First read | "Answers are a first read. They use the figures you typed, not your records." |
| Full answer | "Answers use your records. Three months of records add the deeper parts." |
| Deeper parts | "Answers use your last {{months}} months of records, with every part on." |

`ImportPromptRow` (64 pt, `IconTile` 36 with `import`, chevron, whole row is one button, opens `/import`; a safe `returnTo` brings the user back to Decide home):

| Months used | Title | Sub-line |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | "Import your last 3 statements" | "One month gives a full answer today. Three add the deeper parts. CSV, OFX or PDF." |
| 1 or 2 | "Add earlier months" | "Each month you add moves answers toward the deeper parts. CSV, OFX or PDF." |

Rules: not dismissible (as the setup banner, 06.1 D-10); never red; never a push; shown only on Decide home, the Not ready card and the Records setup item. It never says the answer is "wrong", and it never blocks Check it.

When months rise after an import or logging: the chip text cross-fades (150 ms, `DURATION.searchFade`; none under Reduce Motion), the bar fills (550 ms on view, as 06.1, final state at once under Reduce Motion), and one polite announcement reads "Decide now has {{monthsUsed}} months of records. {{stage}} is on." No haptic (import already ends with its own).

Offline: the card reads the cached count; no change to wording.

### Answer: where the stage shows

Visual order and screen-reader order (replaces 06.1 "Answer frame" items 1-9 only where marked, everything else unchanged):

1. `AnswerTopBar`. Sub-line under the item name: "{{stage}} · {{scope}}" for stages 1 and 2 ("First read · Just you"), the scope label alone at stage 3 ("Household · Flat 4B" or "Just you"). The stage word is also in the bar's accessibility label. At 320 dp or large text the sub-line wraps to two lines.
2. `notice.unsaved_changes` when sent.
3. `VerdictCard` (state glyph + chip, title, body, date line per section 3).
4. `BelowZeroLine` when the engine sends `below_zero_without` (section 3).
5. Fact lines, engine order.
6. Options chips (equal weight).
7. **Basis sentence** (names the window, below).
8. **`StageLine`** (stages 1 and 2 only), one `body` sentence and an optional text link:

| Stage | StageLine (draft) | Link |
|---|---|---|
| First read | "A first read. It uses the figures you typed. One full month of records unlocks the full answer." | "Import statements" (opens `/import`) |
| Full answer | "A full answer from {{monthsUsed}} of 3 months. Three months add pay swings and what could move this answer." | "Add earlier months" (opens `/import`) |
| Deeper parts | not shown | none |

9. The first caveat only, as before, except: while a `StageLine` is showing, the caveats `quick_estimates` and `few_months` are not chosen as "the first caveat" (the stage line already says the same thing). They stay in the About sheet's "For this answer" list, so nothing is hidden. The next caveat in the engine's order is the one shown. `large_for_month` still comes first when present (its figures list leads, 06.1 D-118).
10. `legal.decideAnswer.caveat` and the "What this can't see" link, then `legal.decideAnswer.footer`, then the tester build line (section 8), then the action bar. Order unchanged from 06.1.

Basis sentence names the history window used (D-33 windows are app-chosen, never a control):

| Case | Sentence (draft) |
|---|---|
| 2 or more months used from records | "Based on your last {{months}} months of records, up to {{date}}." |
| 1 month used | "Based on your one full month of records, up to {{date}}." |
| Income judged over a longer window (variable or seasonal pay, up to 12 months) | A second row directly after the basis: "Pay is judged over your last {{months}} months." |
| Quick estimates only | "Based on the figures you typed, not your records." (reads `basis.quickEstimates`, wording signed) |

`months` is the count the engine actually used inside the window, so a user with 4 months of records reads "last 4 months", never "last 6". The existing keys `basis.records` and `basis.partial` change to these sentences; their `recordsWithReason` variants keep their endings.

About sheet ("What this can't see"), two additions under "For this answer": a row "Stage: {{stage}}" with the next unlock in words (stages 1 and 2), and the held-back parts: "These need three months of records: pay swings, pay worked out from your history, and what could move this answer." When only some parts are held back, one of the two rare-mix rows (kept, owner answer, planning 2026-11-18): "These need three months of pay in your records: pay swings and pay worked out from your history." or "This needs three months of records: what could move this answer." Also one fixed row, "How far ahead": "Answers look up to 12 months ahead. Dates further out are not given."

### Not ready card (N24, 06.1) becomes the First read entry

## 5. Bought screen: Record as bought (D-09..D-12, plans 09 and 12)

This is a screen (`screens/actions/BoughtScreen.tsx`), not a sheet; the earlier delta called it a sheet. Layout, top to bottom, `Screen` wrapper with back chevron (44 x 44, label "Back"):

1. Title `heading` "Record as bought"; under it `body`: the item and amount. Household scope only: a `label` sub-line "Saved as a shared line, split {{rule}}." ({{rule}} in plain words, for example "evenly").

3. `BoughtSaveRows`, grouped, each group a `sheetTitle` heading over a `surface` card of rows (rows 52, or 64 with a sub-line). Empty groups are omitted (no empty card). Every row is a `ToggleRow`, one row per item, never merged.

| Group heading | Row title | Sub-line | Default |

| Costs that start | "{{name}}, {{amount}} a month" (fixed: becomes a bill) | none | unticked |
| Costs that start | "{{name}}, about {{amount}} a month" (varying) | "Counts until real spending replaces it" | unticked |
| Money in and out | "Money in from selling {{item}}, {{amount}}" | none | unticked |
| Money in and out | "Pay off {{debt}}, {{amount}}" | none | unticked |
| Costs that stop | "{{bill}} stops from {{date}}" | none | unticked |
