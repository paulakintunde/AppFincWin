# Phase 08.1 — UI design contract (delta)

**Status:** draft, written at planning (2026-11-18). **[OWNER-Q8 default]** this delta stands in for a full `/gsd-ui-phase 08.1` run. 08.1 adds parts to screens 06.1 already built. The base contract is
`.planning/phases/06.1-decide-screens-from-the-prototype/06.1-UI-SPEC.md` (Variant C, the clickable prototype) and, for household
rows, `.planning/phases/08-household/08-UI-SPEC.md` Screen 13. This file lists only what changes. Tokens, type scale, spacing,
row heights, icons and the seven Decide state colours are as in CLAUDE.md "Design fidelity": nothing new.

All copy below is draft and goes to the wording sign-off (`src/i18n/decideWordingSigned.json`) before the freeze. Voice:
declarative, never "you should"; UX-19 (one sentence, at most 25 words and 3 numbers).

## 1. Price step: "Who pays" (D-01, D-02)

- Where: `src/features/decide/screens/check/PriceStep.tsx`, directly under the price field.
- Shown only when the viewer's household has 2 or more members. Hidden for a household of one (no empty control).
- Control: the existing `SegmentBar` with two equal segments, "Just me" | "Household". Default "Just me" on every new check.
- Helper under it (Household picked): "Uses shared money and anything a partner has agreed to share."
- Accessibility: radio group, label "Who pays", 44pt targets.

## 2. Answer: scope label and caveats (D-03, APP-22, UX-21)

- Scope label in the answer's top bar sub-line: "Just you" (household of one or Just me) or "Household" with the household name.
- D-03 caveat, first caveat under the basis sentence when no partner has opted in:
  "Only your money is counted. {{name}} hasn’t shared theirs." (two partners not shared: "{{name}} and {{name2}} haven’t shared theirs.")
- Shared-bills caveat (08 D-37): "{{name}} hasn’t logged shared bills since {{date}}, so shared costs may be higher". Never blocks.
- UX-21 couples note: "Money in {{account}} may be planned by someone else too." when the engine emits `shared_money`.
- Names always through the member-name resolver; a hidden name shows its colour name.

## 3. Answer: new lines (D-05..D-08)

- D-07, one line directly under the verdict card's headline, before the facts, warn tone with symbol and word (never colour alone):
  "Your account goes below $0 by {{date}} even without this."
- D-06, replaces the ready-by line on a Not yet answer whose date would be over 12 months away:
  "Not within a year at your current pace." Followed by the two actions already on the action bar, in this order: the cheaper
  option, Save for it.
- D-08 / DS-4, the verdict card's date line: "If you buy it today." before buying; "Bought on {{date}}." after Record as bought.
  The card never says "Bought today." on an unbought check.

## 4. Record as bought sheet (D-09..D-12)

- The existing Record as bought confirm gains a list of tick rows (the `ToggleRow` pattern, 52pt rows), each its own row:
  - each fixed running cost: "{{name}}, {{amount}} a month" (becomes a bill, ticked by default; the user confirms one by one as now);
  - each varying running cost: "{{name}}, about {{amount}} a month" with the sub-line "Counts until real spending replaces it";
  - sale of the old item (when the check sells it): "Money in from selling {{item}}, {{amount}}";
  - payoff of its loan: "Pay off {{debt}}, {{amount}}";
  - linked Save-for-it goal: "Close the goal {{goal}}" (ticked; can be unticked, D-11);
  - a cost that stops (from the check's `replaces.stops`): "{{bill}} stops from {{date}}".
- Household scope purchase: a sub-line under the sheet title, "Saved as a shared line, split {{rule}}."
- One Save; one undo toast reverses everything saved ("Undo" restores every row, bill and goal).

## 5. Setup checklist: Household row (08-29 carry-over)

- One extra row at the end of `SetupChecklist`, "Who do you share money with?", opening `/household`. It does not count toward
  setup progress and has no done tick.

## 6. Household screens (08-28 carry-over)

- As 08-UI-SPEC Screen 13: PartnerOptInRow in the member sheet, SharedSafetyNetRow on Household home (exactly two members),
  copy as listed there.

## 7. Activity (D-04)

- Month total and category subtotals count the viewer's share in a household of 2+.
- A shared row keeps its full amount and gains the sub-line "Your share {{amount}}" (`inkMuted`, never `inkFaint` on the page tone).

## 8. Tester footer (ROADMAP SC8)

- Under the legal footer, for accounts on the tester list only: "Build {{build}}" in the caption style.
