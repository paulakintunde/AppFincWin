<objective>
Give every household its own SPENDING rule (owner decision 2026-11-20). Plan 08 made a Household check's money "pool and split"
using the BILL split weights, which lets a member who brings 20% of the spare money spend 60% of it (HH-08: Ali $1,000 left,
Noa $4,000, 60/40 bills, Ali's slice $3,000 uses $2,000 of Noa's money). Groups differ (couples, roommates, parent and child,
friends), so the group picks one of three rules in household settings:

1. **What each person brings** — my slice is my own money left (partners: their rounded figure). Nobody spends another's money.
2. **A set spending split** — the group sets spending shares (50/50, or a percent per member for 3+), separate from the bill split.
3. **Same as the bill split** — today's plan 08 behaviour: the bill split also sets spending.

Proposed default: **What each person brings** (owner confirms in Task 2). Invariant kept: slices always add up to the pool, so
two Fits answers never spend the same money twice. Only members with both opt-ins and a published figure count. Shared lines
still count my share in both scopes; joint-account and "hasn't shared" lines unchanged. This refines D-03 a second time
(plan 08 owner A refined it to pool and split; this makes the split the household's own choice).

Order: this plan is wave 9 and runs after 08.1-13 (wave 8, revision 07.0). Plan 08.1-14 (wording sign-off) and 08.1-15 (the
production push) must follow THIS plan: 14 signs the new copy keys listed in Task 5 and 15 must push the new migration

migration list). Nothing here runs against production (`--remote` is never used).

Context budget: five tasks, two of them heavy. Execute Tasks 3, 4 and 5 each with a fresh look at only the files they list.
