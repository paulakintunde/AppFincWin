<objective>
Household money, modelled per account, with a spending rule the group chooses (owner decisions 2026-11-20, three rounds).

1. **Spending rule.** Plan 08's pool-and-split used the BILL split, so a member who brings 20% of the spare money could spend
   60% of it (HH-08). Each household now picks: **What each person brings** / **A set spending split** (custom shares) /
   **Same as the bill split**.
2. **Joint money counted once, split by the rule.** A joint account (accounts.is_shared) is shared money: counted once for the
   household and split; personal accounts (any number per person) stay each person's own, summed as today. In Just me a
   member counts only their slice of joint money. The published partner figure excludes joint money.
3. **Joint account purpose.** Each joint account gets a purpose the group sets: joint bills, joint savings or joint everyday.
   The purpose decides how the account counts.
4. **Household setup type** ("Separate accounts" / "One joint account" / "Mixed") is a summary chosen alongside the accounts;
   it only picks the DEFAULT spending rule. The group can still change the rule.

Invariants kept: slices add up to the money split, so two Fits answers never spend the same money twice; opt-in rules
unchanged for personal money; D-17 shared-line shares unchanged; shared_money and household_not_shared lines unchanged.
