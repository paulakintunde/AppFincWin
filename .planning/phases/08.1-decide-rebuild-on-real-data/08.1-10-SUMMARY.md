## What was built
- **Migration 20261125000400 + pgTAP 113.** Both `record_decide_go` overloads take a check that is `open` or `waiting` (the database status is `waiting`, not "waited"). The goal rule is unchanged (the goal's `check_id` must be the check); a "Save for it" goal is made by that same check, so it now closes. Undo restores the prior `status` and `decided_at` (a waiting check returns to waiting, not open). **Not run:** no local Postgres in this worktree; plan 15 runs pgTAP 113 with the 08.1 migrations and pushes. 17 assertions: record from waiting, goal closes, replay is already-applied, undo back to waiting with the same `decided_at`/`wait_monthly`, foreign goal refused (goal_not_linked), row-array overload, bought/passed/another member's check refused.
- **Who pays.** `WhoPays.tsx`: `SegmentedControl` (new optional `itemRole="radio"`: radiogroup/radio, default stays tablist/tab) labelled "Who pays", "Just me" | "Household"; at fontScale above 1.3 and width 320 dp or less two stacked `Pill`s (new optional `accessibilityRole` and `tall` props, 52 minHeight, `selected` state). Helper "Uses shared money and anything a partner has agreed to share." as a polite live region only on Household. Renders nothing for a household of fewer than 2. Picking Household saves `check.share` as `{household, memberCount, myIndex, rule}` using the household default split row (even when none; weights read as even when all zero; `mine` kept); Just me saves personal. A new check is personal (stored default), so Household never carries over (T-08.1-30). The choice is in the draft, so Back keeps it.
- **Household scope into the engine.** `scopeSource.ts` (pure): `shareScopeOf`, `partnersOf`, `settleUpOf`, `scopeSourceOf`. `useHouseholdScopeSource(on)` reads members and weights, each other member's `partner_money_total` (new `usePartnerMoneyTotals`), settlements (lapsed as payer in the last 30 days) and balances (what the viewer owes). `useCheckEngine` builds `prepare(buildInputs({...source, household}, v3))` once per data change for a Household check and overlays the live unsaved count; Just me keeps the shared Prepared untouched. While the figures are on their way the engine reports `loading` with no evaluation (no flash of "Sam hasn't shared"). A partner with no ok answer is handed over as not sharing, so the engine's caveat names them.
- **Answer.** `AnswerTopBar` sub-line (label role, inkMuted, no line limit): "First read · Just you" / "Full answer · Household · Flat 4B" at the two stages, the scope alone at deeper or no stage; the sub-line is also read in the bar's accessibility label. `useHouseholdScopeLabel` ("Just you" / "Household · {name}"). `sayResult` says `household_not_shared` through `deps.names.member` (the member resolver, D-32): one name "hasn’t", two or more "haven’t" with "A and B" / "A, B and C"; a missing name or no resolver drops the line, never a key. `firstShownCaveat` (plan 20) already puts the household caveat first while a StageLine shows; tested with [few_months, household_not_shared] at stage full. `HouseholdAnswerNote` shows the shared-bills caveat on a Household answer.
- **Setup row.** "Who do you share money with?" as the last row of the checklist (IconTile `household` 36, chevron, 52 minHeight), opens `/household`, no done tick, count and bar unchanged.
- **Publisher.** `AppPartnerFigurePublisher` is mounted in `app/(app)/_layout.tsx` (and removed from Household home so only one publisher runs). `usePublishPartnerFigure` also calls `publish_safety_net_saved` (new `publishSafetyNetSaved`) for each of the viewer's enabled net shares with `inputs.safetyNet.saved` rounded down to 1000, on the same hourly throttle; the publisher mounts when an opt-in or a shared net is on.
