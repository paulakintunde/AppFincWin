# Phase 08.1 Plan 08: Household money in a Decide check Summary

A Household check pools the viewer's money left with each opted-in partner's one rounded figure and counts only the viewer's agreed share of the pool, names the partner who has not shared, says when joint-account money is counted, and widens the free-help pointer to a late or oversized settle-up. A partner's items never reach the inputs.

## Owner decisions (2026-11-20, popup)
- Cases HH-07 to HH-13 approved. HH-07 already passed on the base; HH-08 to HH-13 were red until Task 3, so knownRed ends empty.
- **A, pool and split.** Household figure = (own left + partner rounded_left) x my agreed share, by `sharedMoneyFor`. **This refines D-03's literal "own + partner figure" wording on the owner's choice** (the literal reading lets two partners both be told Fits on the same money).
- **B, shared lines count my share in both scopes.** **Deviation from the plan text**, which says Household adds shared lines in full: a partner's rounded figure is already after their half of a shared bill, so adding the whole bill would count their half twice. No code adds shared lines in full.
- **C**, the joint-account line shows whenever the money counted sits in an account in both names, in either scope, naming the account.
- **D**, "spendable" for the settle-up trigger is money left this month, the same figure the answer uses (`homeFigure`).
