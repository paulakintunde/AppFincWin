# Phase 08.1 Plan 04: Activity counts the viewer's share Summary

In a household of 2+, Activity's month total, narrowed totals and over-cap badges count the viewer's share through the same helper Home's Mine figure uses; shared rows keep their full amount with a "Your share" sub-line.

## Tasks

1. Engine `shareOf` on `monthTotals` (default identity, existing callers unchanged), property tests (identity equality; household share sum equals `myShareOfLine` sum; null share counts as unconverted). View model reads household members and shared splits, builds Mine rows, and passes shares to the position, the narrowed totals and over-cap rows.
2. Row `yourShare` prop (label role, inkMuted, min height `rowHeight.two` = 64, spoken label ends with "your share {amount}"); summary card `shareNote` prop (line under the month figure and appended to the accessibility label); en strings `activity.row.yourShare`, `activity.row.yourShareA11y`, `activity.summary.shareNote`; screen test.

## Deviations from Plan

- The equality test proves Activity = Home Mine at the shared-helper level (same `mineMonthRows` / `figureTotalsOf` / `monthPositionOf` chain, plus the narrowed `shareOf` path agreeing with it) instead of mounting the full Activity and Home screens, which need dozens of data hooks. Row, label and note behaviour are tested by rendering the real components.
- Commits were not made by task-level tdd RED/GREEN split: tests and code were committed together per task.
