# 08.1 draft cases: a "fits" answer whose account goes below $0 later in the year (SN-32..SN-34)

Status: DRAFT, for the owner's decision. Nothing here is approved and no engine change is made. Written 2026-11-20 (plan 08.1-13).

## The question
A plan (or any buy) can be judged "Fits" on the 90-day path while the account goes below $0 later in the 12-month stretched path (the one D-05 builds). Example: the Quebec case, where the $13,418 three-monthly bill on 3 Feb takes the account to -$2,523 on 28 Feb 2027, even with no purchase.

Two different situations:
- (i) the account goes below $0 later even WITHOUT the purchase (a quarterly shortfall that is already there);
- (ii) the account goes below $0 later ONLY because of the purchase (for a plan, its payments add up).

## Options (plain English, recommended first)
1. **Say it, do not change the answer (recommended).** The verdict stays "Fits" because the 90-day path is what D-05 and the verdict promise. Two new lines:
   - (i) "Even without this, your account is below $0 on 28 Feb 2027." (the D-07 idea, looking out 12 months; new fact `below_zero_later_without { date }`, only when the dip starts after day 90, since D-07 already covers the first 90 days);
   - (ii) "With this purchase, your account is below $0 on 31 Aug 2027." (new fact `below_zero_later_with { date }`).
   Code, variant and ready-by date never change. Fits D-05 and D-06 (dates untouched), D-07 (fact only, same rule: never changes code, variant or date), D-80 ("soon" is untouched) and the usual-month rules (plan_makes_short and "usual month short" still use the usual month; these lines are about calendar dips, not the usual month). New copy: two lines for the wording sign-off.
2. **Same lines for (i); for (ii) the plan no longer "Fits".** A purchase that alone pushes the account below $0 within 12 months turns into Not yet, with the date found by the same D-05 search (the first day a fresh check stays at or above $0 for 90 days, extended to the 12-month path). Stricter and consistent with "stays above $0", but it widens D-05 from 90 days to a year for the Fits test, and a monthly plan that pays for 48 months can only pass if the whole year is clean. Needs new wording for Not yet on a plan.
3. **Leave it.** The answer promises the next 90 days and the "how far we look" note says so. Cheapest, but the Quebec buyer reads "Fits" with no hint of February.

## Draft cases (revision 07.0 today answers all three "Fits", no safety net; the fact lines are the proposal)
Common setup: pay $10,000 on the 1st monthly; usual month bills $2,000 (paid the day before pay) and everyday $4,000, so $4,000 left; a $15,000 bill every three months (3 Nov, 3 Feb, 3 May, 3 Aug); today 15 Oct 2026 with $2,000 of everyday spending and the $2,000 of bills still to come this month; the item is $2,000 on a 0% plan, $200 now and ten payments of $180 from 15 Nov; no safety net, no overdraft. Month-end = the day before pay, when the low falls. The engine spreads spending by day and shows about $65 less each month, so its lows differ from the hand table by under $70.

### SN-32 (Quebec simplified): dip even without the buy -> option 1 adds `below_zero_later_without`, not `below_zero_later_with`
Checking $20,000. No purchase, month-end: Oct 16,000; Nov 5,000; Dec 9,000; Jan 13,000; Feb 2,000; Mar 6,000; Apr 10,000; **May -1,000**; Jun 3,000; Jul 7,000; **Aug -4,000**. Each quarter loses $3,000 ($12,000 left against a $15,000 bill). With the plan each month-end is lower by $200 + $180 per payment so far (31 May: -2,460). The first 90 days (to 12 Jan) stay at or above $0 (lowest 4,620 on 30 Nov), so "Fits". Dip starts 31 May 2027 (engine: day 228, lowest -$4,065 on 31 Aug).

### SN-33: only the plan causes the later dip -> option 1 adds `below_zero_later_with`; option 2 turns it into Not yet
Checking $25,000. No purchase, month-end: Oct 21,000; Nov 10,000; Dec 14,000; Jan 18,000; Feb 7,000; Mar 11,000; Apr 15,000; May 4,000; Jun 8,000; Jul 12,000; **Aug 1,000**; Sep 5,000. Never below $0 (engine lowest $935). The plan has taken $200 + 10 x $180 = $2,000 by August, so **31 Aug 2027 is -1,000** (engine -$1,065, first below $0 on day 320). First 90 days fine, so "Fits".

### SN-34 (control): 12-month path stays above $0 -> neither line
Checking $40,000. No purchase, month-end: Oct 36,000; Nov 25,000; Dec 29,000; Jan 33,000; Feb 22,000; Mar 26,000; Apr 30,000; May 19,000; Jun 23,000; Jul 27,000; Aug 16,000; Sep 20,000. With the plan the lowest is August at 14,000 (engine $13,935). Neither line shows.
