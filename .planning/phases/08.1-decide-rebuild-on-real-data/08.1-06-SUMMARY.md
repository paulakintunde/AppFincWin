## How it works

- `judge/readyBy.ts` `firstSafeStart({asOf, paydays, notBefore, maxMonths, floor, lowestFrom})`: candidates are the check day, every payday, the 1st of each of the next 12 months, sorted; returns `{date}`, `{beyond:true}` or `null` (no window could be read: the date as judged stays). Never earlier than the judged date (`notBefore`).
- `cash/horizon.ts` `buildHorizon`: the no-purchase path to 12 months plus a 90-day window. Series flows are built one calendar month at a time (so a pay cap is never given twice), saving transfers and debt minimums are dated once over the whole span, spread is the same cumulative daily spread. `lowestFrom(start, purchaseFlows)` = lowest of (no-purchase balance on each window day minus what the purchase has taken by then). A window on the check day equals the ordinary 90-day path (property test).
- `prepare.ts` `safeStartOf` builds the closure lazily and passes it to the judge as `JudgeInput.safeStart`. Left out (judged date stays, never looser) when: no purchase, the purchase replaces something (sale and stopped-cost flows are dated from the purchase day), pay comes from history, or a pay cap applies (variable or seasonal pay is only known 90 days out).
- `judge.ts`: after the SN-27 guard, if a date is left, the month is not already short (D-63) and a search exists: later first-safe day gives `{kind:'month', month}` (shown as a month, as today); `beyond` gives `readyBy = null` and `not_within_year` as the very first fact. Floor is $0 on the money the check already counts (the locked net is outside it).
- `below_zero_without`: first day in the 90-day no-purchase path below $0, first fact before the depletion lines, any code except needs_input; carries `{ date, zero: 0 }`; never changes code, variant or date (judge unit test).
- `instead/futures.ts` `orderOptions(present, purpose, notWithinYear)`: with `not_within_year`, `cheaper` then `keep` (Save for it) lead; the rest keep their order.
- Copy (drafts, go to the wording sign-off in plan 15): `decide.v3.fact.not_within_year` "Not within a year at your current pace."; `decide.v3.fact.below_zero_without` "Your account goes below {{zero}} by {{date}} even without this." (the `{{zero}}` placeholder follows the existing overdraft lines' convention for the currency's zero).
