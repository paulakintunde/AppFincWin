# 08.1-23 Owner questions (before the build)

Plan 23 builds the screens for plan 22's household money rules. These are the open choices that change what gets built.
Already decided (not asked again, see 08.1-FOLLOWUPS.md): any member may change the rule, setup and purposes, named in Notices
with Undo (22 Q2); set shares that are out of date count "what each person brings" until updated (22 Q10); a member who leaves
is removed from the set shares by the server; a joint account with no purpose counts as everyday (22 Q7); joint savings is kept
out unless marked "can be spent" (22 Q6); one settings screen holds both the setup and the rule (plan 23 text).

All draft words go to plan 14 for wording sign-off as usual.

---

## Q1. Where the new setting sits on Household home

The rule changes every Household check, so people need to find it, but it should not crowd the bill-split settings.

- **A (Recommended):** a fourth row in the existing settings card, right after "How new shared lines are split", named "How spending is shared", showing the current rule as its value (for example "What each person brings").
- **B:** the first row of that card, above the bill-split row, because it changes Decide answers.
- **C:** its own card under the people list, with a one-line summary of the rule and setup.

## Q2. Names for the three spending rules

These are the words people see in settings, on Who pays and on the answer.

- **A (Recommended):** "What each person brings" / "A set spending split" / "Same as the bill split" (the plan's drafts).
- **B:** "Each person's own money" / "Set shares" / "Follow the bill split".
- **C:** "Own money first" / "Fixed percentages" / "Same as bills".

Helper lines under A (draft):
- What each person brings: "Each person's checks count their own money and their part of any joint account. Nobody's check uses someone else's money."
- A set spending split: "The group sets each person's share of the money."
- Same as the bill split: "Each person's share follows the bill split ({{rule}})."

## Q3. Names for the three setups, and groups bigger than two

The plan's drafts say "an account in both names", which is wrong for a group of three or more (roommates, families).

- **A (Recommended):** "Separate accounts" / "One joint account" / "Mixed", with helpers that do not count people:
  - Separate accounts: "Each person keeps their own accounts."
  - One joint account: "Everything goes in and out of one shared account."
  - Mixed: "A shared account for shared costs, and each person's own accounts for the rest."
- **B:** the same names, but "Mixed" becomes "Some shared, some separate".
- **C:** keep "in both names" for groups of two and switch to "shared by everyone" for three or more (two copies of each helper).

The same fix applies to the joint purpose refusal line: "This account is no longer in both names." becomes "This account is no longer a joint account." under A.

## Q4. When someone changes the setup, does the rule change too?

Each setup has a default rule (Separate = What each person brings, One joint account = Same as the bill split). If the group already picked a rule, changing the setup could quietly change everyone's answers.

- **A (Recommended):** if nobody ever picked a rule, the rule follows the new setup's default; if the group picked one, it stays, and the new setup's default is only marked "Default for this setup" in the list.
- **B:** changing the setup always switches the rule to the new default (shown in the toast, with Undo).
- **C:** changing the setup asks "Also use the default rule for this setup?" with Yes / Keep current rule.

## Q5. Starting numbers when someone first picks "A set spending split"

The editor must always add up to 100%, so it needs starting numbers.

- **A (Recommended):** start from the bill split's percentages (the weights people already see on Household home); 100% from the start, so Save works straight away.
- **B:** start equal (50/50, 34/33/33 and so on).
- **C:** start from what each person brings this month, rounded to whole percentages.

If set shares were saved before, the editor opens with those numbers (the server keeps them when the group switches away and back).

## Q6. The "set shares need updating" line, and telling people

When someone joins (or leaves) a group on set shares, checks fall back to "what each person brings" until the shares are fixed. Nobody is told today unless they open settings or read an answer.

Draft (settings and answer, same words): "Spending shares need updating: someone joined or left. Until then each check counts what each person brings."

- **A (Recommended):** the draft line in settings (with an "Update shares" button that opens the editor) and on the answer; the Household home row shows "Needs updating" as its value. No extra notice.
- **B:** as A, plus a notice in Notices for every member when shares go out of date.
- **C:** as A, but shorter words: "Spending shares are out of date, so each check counts what each person brings."

## Q7. The "don't include joint accounts" line on the spare money sheet

People who type their own spare money figure may include joint money, which then counts twice (plan 22 Q14). The line belongs on "Your spare money" (the sheet with "Use my own figure"), a file the plan does not list yet.

Draft: "Count only your own accounts. Joint accounts are counted separately."

- **A (Recommended):** show the draft under the "Your spare money now" field, only when the person has a joint account; add the sheet to plan 23's files.
- **B:** show it always, for everyone in a household of two or more.
- **C:** leave it out of 08.1; log it as a follow-up.

## Q8. Names for the three joint account purposes

Shown in the sheet that opens after an account is marked Joint, and as the value on Shared accounts.

- **A (Recommended):** "Joint household bills" / "Joint savings" / "Joint everyday spending" (the plan's drafts), with the switch "Can be spent" under Joint savings.
- **B:** shorter: "Bills" / "Savings" / "Everyday spending" (the sheet title already says it is joint: "What is {{account}} for?").
- **C:** "Shared bills" / "Shared savings" / "Shared spending".

Helper drafts (under A):
- Bills: "Money kept for shared bills. Only money above the bills due before the next pay-in counts as spare."
- Savings: "Money saved together. It is kept out of checks unless the group marks it as money that can be spent."
- Everyday: "Money for anything. Each person's checks count their part of it."

## Q9. Joint accounts made before this release (no purpose yet)

Existing joint accounts have no purpose, so they count as everyday spending. A joint bills or savings account counted as everyday makes answers too generous until someone sets it.

- **A (Recommended):** one card on Household home, "{{count}} joint accounts have no purpose set. Until then they count as everyday spending.", with a button to Shared accounts; it goes away once each has a purpose. Shared accounts shows "Purpose not set" on each.
- **B:** only "Purpose not set" on Shared accounts; nothing on Household home.
- **C:** as A, plus a meta line on any answer that counted a joint account with no purpose.

## Q10. Marking a joint savings account as the shared safety net (plan 22 gap)

The engine can treat a joint savings account as the couple's shared safety net (counted once, never spendable, HH-34), but nothing in the app marks which account it is.

- **A (Recommended):** add a switch "This is our safety net" under Joint savings in the purpose sheet (turning it on turns "Can be spent" off). Needs one new column and a small change to `set_joint_account_purpose`, an eighth migration added to plan 15's push list.
- **B:** no new switch: a joint savings account counts as the shared net when its creator's account use already says "safety net" (existing column, no migration). Less clear, and only the creator can set it.
- **C:** leave it out of 08.1; log it as a follow-up. Until then a joint savings account is simply kept out of checks.

## Q11. Home and overview figures with joint money (plan 22 gap)

Checks now count joint money once, but the "left this month" figures on home and overview still count the whole joint balance for each person, so the two can disagree.

- **A (Recommended):** add a fourth task to plan 23 that hands the household source to the shared home/overview build (data layer only, no engine change), with a test that home and a Just me check agree.
- **B:** a separate small plan before plan 15 pushes.
- **C:** after 08.1 (home and checks disagree for households with joint accounts until then).

## Q12. The rule lines on Who pays and on the answer

Every Household check should say which rule it counted. The plan has lines for "bring" and set shares; "Same as the bill split" needs one too.

Drafts:
- Who pays, bring: "Counts your own money and your part of any joint account."
- Who pays, set shares or bill split: "Counts your {{percent}}% of the money everyone has shared."
- Who pays, Just me with a joint account: "Counts your part of {{account}}." (more than one joint account: "Counts your part of your joint accounts.")
- Answer, bring: "Counted: what you bring."
- Answer, set shares: "Counted: your {{percent}}% of the shared money."
- Answer, bill split: "Counted: your {{percent}}% of the shared money, the same as the bill split."

- **A (Recommended):** the drafts above.
- **B:** name the rule outright: "Counted by: What each person brings" / "Counted by: A set spending split ({{percent}}%)" / "Counted by: Same as the bill split ({{percent}}%)".
- **C:** no line on Who pays (answer line only), to keep the price step short.
