## SN-29: a payment plan that does not make the month short (D-05)

**Situation.** Jo's usual month ends $400 over. Her account holds $900 today (about $600 at the end of November). She looks at a
$2,150 item on a 12-payment plan at 0%: $950 down today, then $100 a month from 15 December.
**Answer.** Not yet. Ready by January 2027.
**Why.** The plan takes her month from $400 over to $300 over, so it is not short and a date is fair. Her month-end balances without the plan: $600 (30 Nov), $1,000 (31 Dec), $1,400 (31 Jan), $1,800 (28 Feb), $2,200 (31 Mar).

| Start on | Month-end low | Working | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Dec | 31 Dec | $1,000 - $950 down - $100 payment | **-$50, no** |
| 1 Jan | 31 Jan | $1,400 - $950 - $100 | $350 |
| 1 Jan | 28 Feb | $1,800 - $950 - $200 | $650 |
| 1 Jan | 31 Mar | $2,200 - $950 - $300 | $950, **yes** |

On 1 December the first payment is covered ($1,600 in the account on the day), so a date that only meant "the first payment is
covered" would say 1 December. A date that means "stays above $0 for 90 days" says 1 January 2027. **What the app does today:**
already gives 1 January 2027. This case keeps it that way.
