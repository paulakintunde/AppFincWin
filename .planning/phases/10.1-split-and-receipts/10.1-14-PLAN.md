RPC contracts (exact):
```sql
public.set_split_by_item(
  p_id uuid, p_expected_version int,
  p_items jsonb,        -- [{"name": text, "price": int>0, "discount": int>=0, "c": uuid|null, "people": [uuid,...]}], 1..200 items
  p_extras jsonb,       -- {"tax": int>=0, "tip": int>=0, "discounts": int>=0}
  p_parts jsonb,        -- category parts from itemsToParts, or null when one category
  p_category uuid default null,   -- the single category when p_parts is null
  p_store text default null, p_date date default null)
returns jsonb
  -- ok: {"ok": true, "before": {"split": <hh_split_json>, "rule": text, "manual": bool, "parts": jsonb, "category_id": uuid, "item_split": jsonb}, "version": int, "shares": {"<member>": int, ...}}
  -- refusals: not_found | not_shared | version_conflict (+ current_version, updated_by) | line_locked | items_invalid (+ detail: empty_item|unknown_member|bad_price|too_many) | items_not_total (+ diff) | no_items | parts_invalid (+ detail) | category_not_owned
public.restore_line_split(p_id uuid, p_expected_version int, p_before jsonb) returns jsonb
  -- puts back rule, manual flag, participants/shares (via hh_split_plan + hh_store_splits), category_parts, category_id, item_split; same refusals as set_line_split; writes hh_event 'split_changed' with params {"undo": true, "by_item": true}
```
Server share algorithm (must equal itemShares): per item net = price - discount; split net over its people with hh_allocate(net, array of 1s) in the given people order; per-member item totals in household member join order (the order hh_split_plan uses); E = tax + tip - discounts; extras = hh_allocate(E, item totals) when E <> 0 (all item totals 0 and E <> 0 -> no_items); member totals in the line's original currency; check sum(net) + E = abs(original_amount) else items_not_total; household-currency shares = hh_allocate(abs(household_amount), member totals) (equal to member totals when the currencies match); participants = members with a total > 0 (a zero-item person is left out of the split, UI-SPEC owner-to-confirm 11).
