# Phase 10.1: Split and receipts - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-11-24
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Three capabilities, all before launch (owner 2026-11-24):
1. **Split one purchase across categories** (was backlog 999.1). One transaction, several category parts.
2. **Receipt on a transaction** (was backlog 999.2). Photo, PDF or multi-page scan attached to a line.
3. **Receipt scan with a split by item** (08.12 round 4: "before launch"). Read the receipt's items and assign each to categories and, on a shared line, to household members.

Category totals, category limits, Activity, Insights and Decide read the parts; money is counted once. This phase does not add envelope rollover (Phase 10.2), joint spending agreements (08.12) or rooms (08.13).

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### How a split is kept
- **D-01:** One transaction stays **one line** (one movement on the bank balance). Its category parts are stored inside it. This replaces the prototype's behaviour, which turned one purchase into N separate transactions (`FincWin United.dc.html` L6514-6545). Reason: the line keeps matching the bank statement, so import de-duplication, undo and the household split stay right.
- **D-02:** Parts are integer minor units and always add up exactly to the line's total. The split sheet follows the prototype: rows of category + amount, an "add part" control, and the balance label "Adds up" / "X left to place" / "X over"; save stays disabled until it adds up.
- **D-03:** Editing the total of a split line never guesses: the split shows the gap ("$5 left to place") and asks the user to place it.
- **D-04:** A part may use **any** category, including Savings and Transfer. Each part counts the way its category counts today (a Savings part counts as saving in Decide and Insights; the builtin-key checks in `buildInputs.ts` apply per part).
- **D-05:** Activity shows a split line as **one row** with the full amount and an "N categories" tag; tapping it shows and edits the parts. Filtering Activity by a category shows only that part's amount. Category totals, caps (`engine/categorize/usage.ts`), Insights (`engine/insights/categories.ts`, month model) and Decide (`buildInputs.ts`, `judge/lines.ts` caps) read parts wherever they read a line's category today.

### Receipts
- **D-06:** A receipt can be a **photo** (camera or gallery), a **file** (image or PDF) or a **multi-page scan** that is straightened automatically, as in the prototype receipt sheet (L2747-2760, L5994-6003). Several files per line are allowed; "Remove all" leaves the line without any.
- **D-07:** On a **private** line only the owner can see its receipts. On a **shared** household line, everyone the line is shared with can open them; nobody outside the line ever can (storage access rules, not just app hiding).
- **D-08:** A receipt is kept **as long as the line**: until removed, until the line is deleted, or until account deletion, then deleted for good (storage objects removed, not just rows). Receipts are included in the data export (Phase 10 export needs a way to deliver files, since CSV cannot hold images).
- **D-09:** **Offline:** attaching a receipt saves it on the phone and uploads it when back online; the line shows "Receipt waiting to upload". Nothing is lost. (Receipt uploads are not ordinary row writes; the planner decides how they relate to the Phase 10 offline queue limit D-27.)

### Reading the receipt
- **D-10:** **Phone first, server if needed.** The receipt is read on the phone (private, free, works offline). If that fails or reads badly, the user can choose "Read it online"; this shows a one-line consent note (02.1 D-05 pattern), the image is processed in us-west-2 (02.1 D-04) and deleted from the server right after reading, and the server keeps no copy (02.1 D-10..D-13). Never sent online without that choice.
- **D-11:** It reads **store, date, total and each item with its price**, plus tax, tip and discounts as their own rows.
- **D-12:** Unsure rows are **marked "Check this"** with the photo alongside; if the items don't add up to the total, it says by how much. **Nothing is saved until the user confirms.**
- **D-13:** Scanning is available **from Add** (scan first; the new line is filled in) **and from an existing line** (attach and split).

### Splitting items between people and categories
- **D-14:** On a shared line, every item **starts split the line's usual way** (its current rule, e.g. even); tapping an item gives it to one person or some people. Each person's share of the line becomes the total of their items (stored through the existing household split as the per-member amounts).
- **D-15:** Tax, tip and discounts are shared **in proportion to each person's items** (Splitwise Itemize style). Odd cents use the existing largest-remainder rule (`hh_allocate` / `src/engine/split/allocate.ts`), so shares always add up.
- **D-16:** Each item gets a **suggested category** (from the user's past lines and the store) that the user can change; the line's category parts are built from the items. This works on private lines too, not only shared ones.
- **D-17:** **Whoever can edit the shared line today** can split it by item. Everyone on the line sees the result, the receipt and who changed it, with the existing before-and-after notice and Undo.

### Owner answers after research (2026-11-24)
- **D-18 (amends D-10):** **Phone-only reading at launch.** No online reading service, no "Read it online" button and no consent step in this phase. If the phone cannot read a receipt, the review screen opens with the photo and empty rows to type in. Online reading (AWS Textract AnalyzeExpense in us-west-2 was the research pick) is deferred to a later phase.
- **D-19:** When a member deletes their account, receipts they attached to **shared** lines **stay with the household** (as the line stays as "Former member", Phase 10 D-20); receipts on their **private** lines are deleted.
- **D-20 (amends D-15 for category parts):** After a scan, tax and tip become their **own "Tax and tip" part** in the category parts (its category is chosen by the user, default the line's category). Between **people**, tax, tip and discounts are still shared in proportion to each person's items (D-15 unchanged). Discounts reduce the item or part they apply to.
- **D-21:** Limits: up to **5 files per line**, **5 MB per file**; photos are shrunk to about 2,000 px on the long side before upload.
- **D-22:** The owner will collect **20-30 real receipt photos** (names and card numbers covered; grocery, restaurant with tip, pharmacy, hardware, long, faded) as the on-device reader's test set. Planner: add an owner task early (before the parser plan) with where to drop them; keep the images out of the public repo unless the owner agrees.
- **D-23:** Receipt image files are **not** in the daily backup (Phase 10 D-19); backups hold records only. The privacy copy (Phase 11) states that a restore brings back lines without their receipt files.

### Owner answers after planning (2026-11-24)
- **D-24 (D-21 reading):** a multi-page scan is **one receipt** of up to 10 pages; the 5-per-line limit counts receipts, not pages.
- **D-25:** on a split shared line, each part shows the **partner's own matching category** (mapped by built-in type); a partner's own pick for the whole line applies only to unsplit lines (07 D-33), and those override rows are kept.
- **D-26:** a person who gets **no items** in a split by item is left out of that split (owes nothing for the line); saving is not blocked; everyone sees who was left out.
- **D-27:** masked OCR **text** from the owner's receipts (long numbers, emails and phone numbers masked) is committed to the repo as the parser test set so CI runs it; the **images** stay private on the owner's PC (D-22).
- Planner choice kept (technical): a `transactions.item_split` column stores the confirmed items so "Split by item" can reopen and a hand edit can end the item link (D-11 UI answer).

### Owner answers after plan check (2026-11-24)
- **D-28:** copies keep the split. Clone last month and repeat bills (recurring series) keep a line's category parts, scaled to the copy's amount when it differs (largest remainder, odd cents to the largest part). Plan 10.1-30.
- **D-29:** a statement import that matches an existing split line never removes or rewrites its parts; a changed amount shows as the D-03 gap. Plan 10.1-30.

### Claude's Discretion (owner chose "ready"; recommended defaults, owner to check)
- Receipts and scanning sit at **level 2** (prototype feature flag L3242, "Receipts / Attach photos and files to a line / Recording"); add the feature row to `featureRegistry.ts` and the signed level map in lockstep (Phase 9 pattern).
- ~~Online reading in every paid plan~~ superseded by D-18 (no online reading at launch).
- File size limit, image compression and the number of files per line: planner's choice, documented.
- On-device reading library and the online service: researcher's choice (Windows + EAS build constraints, Expo SDK 57, New Architecture); a new native module means a new dev and preview build.
- Household default rule per category (`hh_resolve_default_rule` keys off one category): with category parts, the planner defines how a split line's default rule is resolved.

</decisions>

## Existing Code Insights

### Reusable Assets
- `src/engine/split/allocate.ts` and SQL `hh_allocate`: largest-remainder integer allocation, for parts and for tax/tip shares.
- `transaction_member_categories` (per-member category override on a shared line): closest precedent for per-part categories.
- `src/ui/Sheet.tsx`: the app's sheet for the split and receipt sheets.
- `expo-document-picker`, `expo-file-system`: already installed (file pick, temp files). No `expo-image-picker`, `expo-camera` or text-recognition package yet.
- `read-pdf-statement` Edge Function: pattern for a caller-checked, read-then-delete server function.
- `src/data/exportManifest.ts` + its guard test: every new table must be listed.

### Established Patterns
- Shared-line writes go through RPCs only (RLS read-only to clients); new split-by-item writes need RPCs that keep `transaction_splits` equal to the sum of item shares.
- Undo is server-side (`undo_log`, `apply_patches`); every new write must be undoable.
- Cache shape changes need a `CACHE_SCHEMA_VERSION` bump (`src/data/cache/persister.ts`, `scripts/cache-shape.lock.json`).
- Feature rows change in `featureRegistry.ts` and `09-LEVEL-MAP.md` together.

### Integration Points
- Category readers to switch to parts: `src/engine/insights/categories.ts`, `src/engine/month/model/*`, `src/engine/categorize/usage.ts`, `src/engine/activity/filters.ts` and `calendar.ts`, `src/features/decide/snapshot/v3/buildInputs.ts` (L119, L190, L218, L235-254, L321, L561), `src/engine/decide/v3/judge/lines.ts` L74-103, `fullCost/replaces.ts`, `householdMoney.ts`.
- Transaction detail: `src/features/record/activity/detailRows.ts` (Receipt row is missing today).
- Account deletion: `src/services/account/deleteAccount.ts`, `supabase/functions/delete-account/` must also remove receipt files.
