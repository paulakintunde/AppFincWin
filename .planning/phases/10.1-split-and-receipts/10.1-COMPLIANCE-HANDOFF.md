## 1. Apple App Privacy (App Store Connect)

Add one data type. Financial Info is already declared.

| Data type | Collected | Linked to the user | Used for tracking | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Photos or Videos | Yes (receipt photos, scans and PDF files the user attaches to a line) | Yes | No | App Functionality |

Notes:
- Receipts are only collected when the user picks or takes one for a line. Nothing is read from the photo library in the background.
- The receipt image is read on the phone only (D-18). No receipt image or receipt text is sent to any reading service. There is no third-party processor for receipts to declare.

## 2. Google Play Data safety

| Data type | Collected | Shared | Optional | Purpose | Encrypted in transit | User can ask for deletion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Photos and videos | Yes | No | Yes (a line works without a receipt) | App functionality | Yes (HTTPS to Supabase Storage) | Yes (remove the receipt, delete the line, or delete the account) |

Play photo and video permission policy (RESEARCH A8, to re-check at Phase 11): the app uses the Android system photo picker, which needs no storage permission. `app.config.ts` blocks `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`, `READ_MEDIA_VIDEO` and `RECORD_AUDIO` (`android.blockedPermissions`). `CAMERA` is the only new permission. Confirm in the Play Console that the release's merged manifest has no `READ_MEDIA_*` permission, so the Photo and Video Permissions declaration form is not required.

## 4. Privacy policy lines to add (receipts)

Plain-English points for the policy writer. Wording is Phase 11's; the facts are these:

1. **What:** receipt photos, scans and PDF files a person attaches to a line, and the items, store, date and total read from them on the phone.
2. **Where stored:** in the app's private file storage (Supabase Storage, a private bucket) in the project region (AWS us-west-2, United States). Files are reached only through short-lived signed links.
3. **Who can see them:** the person who attached the receipt, and on a shared household line, the people the line is shared with. No one else.
4. **Reading:** receipts are read on the phone only. No receipt image or text is sent to an outside service to be read (D-18).
5. **How long kept:** as long as the line. A receipt is deleted for good (the file, not only its record) when it is removed, when its line is deleted, or when the account is deleted (D-08).
6. **Shared lines:** when a member deletes their account, receipts they attached to shared household lines stay with the household, as the line itself does (shown as a former member). Receipts on their private lines are deleted (D-19).
7. **Export:** receipt files are included in the data export (a ZIP with the files and a list).
8. **Backups:** receipt files are not in the daily backups. Backups hold records only. If data is ever restored from a backup, lines come back without their receipt files (D-23). The existing line "Backup copies are kept for 30 days, then they are gone too." still applies to the records.
9. **Limits (for the help page, not the policy):** up to 5 files per line, 5 MB each; photos are made smaller (about 2,000 px on the long side) before upload (D-21).

## 5. Restore note (D-23)

`docs/ops/backup-restore.md` production-restore step 5 deletes the `receipts` storage rows that come back without files and runs `public.prune_receipt_rows_without_objects()`, so no line points at a missing file. The app shows "This file did not open." for any file missing before the prune. The privacy policy line in 4.8 is the user-facing side of this.
