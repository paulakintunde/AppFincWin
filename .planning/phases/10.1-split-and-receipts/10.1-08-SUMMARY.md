## Deviations
- **The corpus is synthetic.** The owner had no photo set ready and asked Claude to generate the receipts.
  - `gen_receipts.py` made 26 receipt photos with made-up store names:
    - 9 grocery, 5 restaurant (3 with tips after the total, 2 on the bill), 4 pharmacy, 4 hardware, 4 café;
    - 3 long, 5 faded and 1 blurred;
    - USD, CAD GST+PST and HST, GBP with VAT included, EUR with comma decimals;
    - coupons, a member discount, "n x price" lines, wrapped names, cash with change, and AMOUNT DUE / BALANCE DUE totals;
    - dates 03/04/26 and 04/03/26 plus ISO, long and dotted formats.
  - Each photo is printed text with a tilt of up to 2.5°, noise, fade bands and uneven lighting.
  - The real on-device reader read them, so the OCR output is real.
  - Ground truth for each receipt is in `C:/dev/fincwin-private/receipts-corpus/answers.json` (outside git). 10.1-15 should label `expected` from it, not by eye.
  - Not covered: creased or curled real paper. Add owner photos as r27+ later.
- **OCR line order is by reading block, not by row.** Typically all names come first, then all prices. The 10.1-15 parser must rebuild rows from `frame` y-positions; line order cannot be trusted.
- **Scrub gap:** a phone number with an OCR-inserted space (`752 -555-8427`) is not masked. Harmless for this synthetic set, but tighten the phone pattern before real receipts are added.
