# Phase 10.1 Plan 20: Splits in Activity, detail and the entry sheet Summary

A split line is one Activity row with a "3 categories" tag and the categories tile; under a category filter it shows that part with "Part of {full amount}"; a line whose parts fall short carries the amber "Parts need placing" badge. The detail sheet gains a Categories row that expands into the parts (with Total and "Adds up" or the gap badge) and "Edit split"; a gap shows the info card with "Place it", which opens the split sheet with the gap outlined. The entry sheet offers "Split across categories" in edit state at level 3.

## Deviations from Plan
1. **[Rule 3] Existing suites mock the feature seam.** The view model, detail sheet and entry sheet now call `useFeatureAccess('splitLine')`, which needs the auth/plan providers. Twenty existing activity and entry suites got a one-line `jest.mock('@/features/levels/useFeatureAccess', ...)` (splits off) so their behaviour is unchanged; TransactionSheet.test.tsx drives it with `mockSplitOn` and adds the "action shown at level 3 / hidden below" tests.
2. **[Rule 2] New copy key `split.action` ("Split across categories")**; none existed in 10.1-07.
3. **The filtered part amount is in the line's own currency**, computed in the view model with `amountInCategories(line, original_amount, filterIds)` (parts are stored in the line's currency), not in home currency, so it sits beside the row's own-currency figure and "Part of" reads in the same currency.
4. **PartsView shows magnitudes** (parts share the line's sign and the sheet header already shows it): "Total $50.00".
5. **"Place it" card is shown only when the parts fall short** (left to place > 0). Parts that overshoot the total cannot be stored (server guard); the parts view still shows the gap badge if they do.
6. **Entry sheet:** the split sheet saves through its own version-checked mutation, so after saving the open edit sheet holds a stale version; a later save from it would raise the normal version-conflict path. The split sheet closes on its own; the entry sheet is not closed (cancel and save are indistinguishable from `onClose`).
7. **Shared lines:** parts show the viewer's own categories (`categories.byId`); an id the viewer does not have reads "Uncategorised" in the parts view. The shared-category resolution of 10.1-16 covers the Decide/Insights readers, not this display (T-10.1-20-02 is therefore met by the existing viewer-lookup rule, not by a new resolver).
8. **Level-2 split row:** with splits off the row renders as an unsplit row under the line's stored `category_id` (the largest part by 10.1-04's rule).
