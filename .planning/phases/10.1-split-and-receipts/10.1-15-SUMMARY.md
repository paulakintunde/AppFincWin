# Phase 10.1 Plan 15: Receipt parser Summary

Pure receipt parser: OCR lines are regrouped into rows by frame position (tilt-aware), then read into store, date, total, items with prices and per-item discounts, and tax, tip and receipt discounts as their own rows, with "Check this" flags wherever the reading is a guess. `reconcile` reports whether the parts add up to the total and by how much.

## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Rule 1 - Bug] Rows need a tilt search, not a fixed band.** A 2.5 degree tilt moves a price ~40 px against its name across the receipt, more than half a line height. groupRows picks the slope that puts the most side-by-side line pairs on one baseline, then groups on half the median line height as specified.
2. **[Rule 2 - Correctness] OCR slip repairs.** A dash read for the decimal point (`$40-00`, `5-25`), a letter O for zero, and a decimal split by a space are repaired and marked ambiguous; l/I/O in a numeric date are repaired and the date marked unsure. Without these, two corpus totals were lost.
3. **[Rule 2 - Correctness] Total fallbacks after the total row.** If the printed total cannot be read, the first card payment amount after it is used, flagged (cash tendered never counts). If a tip follows the total with no "total with tip" line, the total becomes printed total + tip, flagged. The plan said only "rows after the total are ignored except tip rows on restaurant receipts"; a tip row after the total is taken as the restaurant signal.
4. **Labels from answers.json, not photos** (per the orchestrator's 10.1-08 facts): the corpus is synthetic and its ground truth file is outside git. No photo or answers file was copied into the repo.
5. **Subtotal cross-check:** when items do not match the subtotal and no item is already flagged, the total is flagged (as specified); when an item is flagged, it carries the doubt.
