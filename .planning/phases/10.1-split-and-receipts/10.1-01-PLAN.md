SPL-01 Split one line into 2 or more category parts that add up exactly to its total (sheet, RPC, undo).
SPL-02 Editing a split line's total shows the gap ("X left to place" / "X over"); the gap counts as Uncategorised until placed.
SPL-03 Category totals, caps, Insights, the month model, Decide and Activity filters count each part exactly once.
SPL-04 Activity shows a split line as one row with an "N categories" tag; the detail shows and edits the parts; "Remove the split".
SPL-05 Savings and Transfer parts count as their category does today; a split line's household default rule resolves from its largest part.
SPL-06 "Split a line" is level 3; parts are in the export, the cache version is bumped, undo and deletion cover them.
RCP-01 Attach a photo, a file (image or PDF) or a multi-page scan to a line; up to 5 receipts per line, 5 MB per file; remove one or all.
RCP-02 Receipts live in a private bucket; only the owner (private line) or the people the line is shared with can read them.
RCP-03 Open a receipt: zoom viewer for images, the system viewer for PDFs.
RCP-04 Attaching offline keeps the file on the phone and uploads it later; the line shows "Receipt waiting to upload".
RCP-05 Removing a receipt deletes the stored file; deleting the line or the account purges files (shared-line receipts stay with the household, D-19).
RCP-06 The data export delivers receipt files with a mapping to their lines.
RCP-07 Receipts are level 2; camera/photo permission strings, store disclosures and the receipt/file/scan icons are in place.
SCN-01 Scan a receipt from Add (creates one line) and from an existing line (fills its parts).
SCN-02 Read a receipt on the phone into store, date, total, items, tax, tip and discounts (phone only at launch, D-18).
SCN-03 Unsure rows show "Check this"; a mismatch shows the amount; nothing saves until the user confirms; a failed read opens the review with empty rows.
SCN-04 DROPPED by D-18 (online reading deferred to a later phase).
SCN-05 Each item gets a likely category from past lines and the store.
SCN-06 On a shared line, assign items to people; tax, tip and discounts shared in proportion with largest remainder; tax and tip become their own "Tax and tip" part (D-20).
SCN-07 Everyone on the line sees who split it, before and after, with Undo; two editors cannot silently overwrite each other.
