# Phase 10.1 (Split and receipts) - UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for splitting one line across categories, attaching receipts, reading a receipt, and splitting a receipt by item. Built only from the app's own tokens (`src/theme/*`) and primitives (`src/ui/*`). Follows the 07-UI-SPEC format. Sources: 10.1-CONTEXT D-01..D-17 (locked), CLAUDE.md design fidelity and compliance, prototype `FincWin United.dc.html` (split sheet L2572-2596, L6514-6545; receipt sheet L2747-2759, L5990-6002; feature flag L3242), the Phase 8 household `SplitSheet` (`src/features/household/sharing/SplitSheet.tsx`). Where this file and the prototype differ, this file wins on tokens; CONTEXT wins on behaviour. The prototype's "split into N lines" behaviour is NOT used (D-01): one line, many parts. Choices CONTEXT did not settle are listed in "Owner to confirm" at the end.

| Mark | Token | Rule |
|------|-------|------|

| "X left to place" | `stateColors.tight` pair (#8A5A1B / #FBEFD6) | Badge with `stateTight` glyph + word. Left to place is an unfinished state, not an error, so amber, not the prototype's red |

## Layout and Screen Contracts

All sheets use `Sheet` (own gesture root, drag-to-dismiss, backdrop, `dirty` / `onDiscard` for unsaved changes) with `SheetHeader` (title left, "Cancel" right as in `SplitSheet`) and `SheetScroll`. The sheet is `radii.sheetTop` 28. The primary button sits at the bottom of the scroll, full width, pill radius, 52 tall, `space.sheetPadBottom` below it.

### 1. Split across categories sheet (D-01..D-04)

Opened from: transaction detail row "Categories" (a row, see section 2), the entry sheet's overflow ("Split across categories"), and the Add-by-scan result (section 6). Level 3 feature (prototype L3244 "Split a line", level 3).

Order, top to bottom:
1. `SheetHeader`: title "Split {name}", cancel.
2. Note (`label`, `inkMuted`): "One line, several categories. The parts have to add up to {total}." The line stays one movement on the account.
3. **Parts card** (`fill1`, `radii.card`): one `SplitPartRow` per part, separated by a 1 px `line1` rule (no rule above the first).
   - `SplitPartRow` (min 64): `CategoryGlyph` tile 36; category name (`bodyStrong`, wraps to two lines, never truncated); chevron; amount field (`MoneyInput compact`, right aligned, 120 wide, grows with font scale); remove button (44 x 44, `close` glyph, accessibility label "Remove part N"; hidden when only 2 parts remain, because a split needs at least 2).
   - Tapping the category area opens the existing category picker sheet over this one, title "Category for part N". **Every category is offered, including Savings and Transfer (D-04).** A part in Savings or Transfer shows its own word under the name in `label` / `inkMuted` ("Counts as saving" / "Counts as a move between your accounts") so the user sees how it counts.
   - Starts with 2 parts: the line's current category with the full total, and an empty second part. The first part's amount re-derives as "the rest" while the user types the second, until the user edits the first by hand.
4. Row: dashed pill "Add another part" (44 tall, `step.card` side padding, `line2` dashed border, accent on press) and `BalanceLine` right aligned (wraps under the pill at font scale > 1.3).
5. Primary button. Enabled only when the parts add up exactly and there are at least 2 parts; disabled style `fill2` with `inkDim` text. Label enabled: "Save split". Label disabled: "Make the parts add up". Disabled tap: `hapticWarn`, the balance line is announced again; no toast.

`BalanceLine`: left to place and over show the amount in words and numbers; it is a polite live region, spoken once the figures settle (`useSettledLabel`, 600 ms, same as `SplitSheet`). Integer minor units throughout; a part never goes negative; an amount of zero part is allowed while typing but blocks save with the line "Every part needs an amount." under that row (`label`, short text colour, `stateShort` glyph).

**Edit-total gap (D-03):** when the user edits the total of a split line (entry or detail edit), the line is NOT re-spread. Saving the new total is allowed; the line then shows a `WarningCard`-style info card at the top of its detail ("The parts add up to $45, the total is $50. $5 left to place.") with a text button "Place it" that opens this sheet with the gap highlighted in the balance line. Until placed, Activity shows the row with the `stateTight` badge "Parts need placing" and the whole line counts under its first part's category, and the Insights and Decide readers treat the unplaced remainder as "Uncategorised" (planner to confirm which bucket exists today).

**Editing an existing split:** same sheet, prefilled; a text button "Remove the split" (destructive text, below Save) turns the line back into one category, the line's original category chosen by a picker row "Keep as" (default: the largest part's category). Confirmation: see Destructive actions.

Motion: the sheet slides in with the app default; rows added fade in (`FadeIn` 0.15 s); removed rows fade out; balance badge cross-fades (`FadeSwap` 0.15 s). Reduce Motion: no slide of rows, instant, cross-fade only. Haptic: `hapticTap` on a successful save, `hapticWarn` on a blocked save.

### 2. Activity row and the parts view (D-05)

- **Activity row** for a split line: unchanged height (64 two-line). Name left, **full amount** right (`bodyStrong`). Category slot shows a `CategoryTag` pill: `categories` glyph + "3 categories" (`label`, `inkMuted` on `fill1`, 44 tall hit area through slop). The row never shows one category glyph tile for a split; it shows the `categories` glyph tile instead (36).
- **Filtered by a category:** the row shows only that part's amount, with the line's full amount as the sublabel "Part of {full amount}" (`label`, `inkMuted`). The tag reads "3 categories" still.
- **Tapping the row** opens the existing transaction detail sheet. Its row list gains a **Categories** row (detailRows key `parts`): value "3 categories", chevron; it expands in place into the parts view: one `Row` (64) per part, `CategoryGlyph` tile 36, category name (`bodyStrong`), amount right (`bodyStrong`, `AmountDisplay`), and under the list a total line "Total {amount}" (`bodyStrong`) with the "Adds up" badge. Button "Edit split" (text button, 44) opens section 1 prefilled.
- A non-split line's detail shows a **Categories** row only in the entry/detail edit state as the action "Split across categories".
- **Calendar and week views** show the line once, at the full amount; the `CategoryTag` is shown only where the row template already shows a category.
- **Household:** a shared split line's parts view also shows who paid and each member's share (existing rows). Partner category overrides (`transaction_member_categories`) show on the member's own view per part; the planner defines the per-part override display, but the visible rule is: the parts card always adds up to the line.

### 3. Receipt sheet (D-06..D-09)

Opened from: the detail row **Receipt** (new row, level 2, value "None" / "1 file" / "3 files" / "Waiting to upload"), the entry sheet's "Add a receipt" row (prototype `entryReceipt`, L2274), and swipe-free: no gesture opens it.

Structure (`Sheet`, title "Receipt"):
1. **Thumbnails strip** (only when there is at least one file): horizontal `ReceiptStrip` of 64 x 64 thumbnails (radius `radii.glyphTile`), each with a 44 x 44 hit area. Images show the picture; PDFs show a `file` glyph tile with "PDF" and page count in `meta`; a scan shows its first page with a "3 pages" caption. Caption under each: file kind and date (`meta`, `inkMuted`). A file still waiting to upload carries the `savedOffline` glyph badge in its corner and the word "Waiting" in its caption. Tap a thumbnail to open the viewer (section 3a). Long-press or the viewer's remove control deletes one file.
2. **Action list**, copy and order from the prototype (`shotActions`), each a `Row` 64 with a 36 icon tile, name (`bodyStrong`) and sublabel (`label`, `inkMuted`):
   - "Take a photo" / "Opens the camera" (`camera`)
   - "Choose from files" / "Image or PDF" (`file`; the system picker also offers photos)
   - "Scan a document" / "Multi-page, straightened automatically" (`scan`)
   - "Read this receipt" / "Fills in the items and the total. Nothing is saved until you confirm." (`checkIt`; shown only when at least one image file exists and the line is not yet read; this is the entry to section 5)
   - "Remove all" / "Leaves the line without any" (`delete`, destructive text; shown only when files exist)
3. **Shared cue (D-07):** on a shared household line, a note card under the title: `household` glyph + "Everyone this line is shared with can open these receipts. No one else can." On a private line: `privacy` glyph + "Only you can see these receipts." (`label`, `inkMuted` on `fill1`.) Shown always, not dismissible, so the rule is visible where files are added.
4. **Retention note** (`label`, `inkMuted`): "Receipts stay with the line until you remove them or delete the line." Plus on first use only: "They are included when you export your data."
5. **Limits line**, when relevant: "Up to {max} files per line, {size} MB each." The numbers are the planner's (D-discretion) and come from config, not copy.

Multiple files: each action adds one more file; "Add another" is the same action list, so no second control exists.

**3a. Viewer** (`ReceiptViewer`): full-screen modal over a `ink` 94% scrim is NOT used (no new colours); the viewer is a full-screen `canvas` page with a header: close (`close`, 44), page/file counter ("2 of 3", `meta`), and "Remove" (destructive text, 44). Body: the image with pinch zoom and double-tap zoom (Reanimated worklets); PDFs open in the system viewer through `expo-sharing`/the OS share target when no in-app renderer is chosen (planner's choice). Swipe left/right moves between files; the swipe also has Previous/Next 44 px buttons for screen-reader users. Reduce Motion: no zoom animation, instant. A file that failed to load shows the info card "This file did not open." with "Try again".

**3b. States**
- **Offline attach (D-09):** the file saves to the phone; the thumbnail shows the "Waiting" badge; the line's detail row value reads "Waiting to upload"; the Activity row carries a small `savedOffline` glyph with the words "Receipt waiting to upload" as its accessibility hint and, in the row, the sublabel under the name. When back online the badge fades out (0.15 s), a quiet toast is not shown (nothing to announce). If an upload fails after retries: the thumbnail shows `caution` glyph + "Didn't upload" and tapping opens a small menu "Try again" / "Remove".
- **Camera or photos permission denied:** replaces the action with an info card: `camera` tile 48, "Camera access is off", body "FincWin needs the camera to take a receipt photo. You can still choose a file.", buttons "Open settings" (primary) and "Choose from files". Photos: "Photo access is off" with the matching body. Never a dead end; the file picker needs no permission on iOS or Android.
- **Too big or wrong type:** inline line under the action list, short text colour with `stateShort` glyph: "That file is too big. Up to {size} MB." / "That file type isn't supported. Use an image or a PDF."
- **Empty (no files):** no strip; just the action list. No empty-state illustration; the list is the empty state.
- **Loading:** a 64 x 64 `fill1` block with a small progress ring while a photo is being processed or compressed.

### 4. Scan entry points (D-13)

- **From Add:** the Add sheet gains a row "Scan a receipt" (`scan`, 36 tile; 64 row; level 2) beside the existing ways to start an entry. Tap runs capture (section 3 camera or scan), then reading (section 5), then review (section 5), then on confirm creates ONE new line prefilled with store as name, date, total, category from the item parts (section 1 parts if more than one category), and the receipt attached. Nothing is created before confirm. Backing out of review discards the capture with the sheet's discard prompt (see Destructive actions).
- **From an existing line:** the Receipt sheet's "Read this receipt" row (section 3). Confirm updates the existing line: sets its parts and, on a shared line, the member shares; it does not change the line's total unless the user accepts "Use the receipt total" (see 5, difference rule).

### 5. Reading and review (D-10..D-12)

**5a. Reading.** Full-sheet progress state, title "Reading your receipt": a determinate-looking indeterminate bar is NOT used; use a `ProgressBar` showing page count progress when the scan has pages ("Page 2 of 3"), otherwise a calm pulsing `scan` tile (one slow 1.2 s fade between 0.6 and 1 opacity, no spinner loop beyond 8 s; after 8 s the line "Still reading. This can take a moment." appears). A "Cancel" text button (44) is always visible. Reduce Motion: static tile, no pulse. Reading happens on the phone first (D-10); the screen says "Reading on your phone" (`label`, `inkMuted`, `privacy` glyph).

**5b. Reading failed or read badly.** Info card in the sheet (`caution` glyph, `fill1`): title "That receipt didn't read clearly" and body "You can try again with a flatter photo, enter the items yourself, or read it online." Buttons stacked, full width, 52: "Try another photo", "Read it online" (outline pill), and a text button "Enter it myself" (skips to an empty review list with manual rows). Online reading is **never** started without this choice.

**5c. Read it online consent.** Pressing "Read it online" shows, inline above a primary button, the one-line consent note (02.1 D-05 pattern): "This receipt is sent to a server in the US West, read, and deleted straight away. FincWin keeps no copy." (`label`, `inkMuted`, `privacy` glyph.) Buttons: "Send and read" (primary) and "Cancel". Consent is per use; no remembered tick. Offline: "Read it online" is disabled with the sublabel "Needs a connection". Server errors use the generic error card in Error states.

**5d. Review list** (`ScanReview`, title "Check the receipt"). Nothing is saved until the primary button at the bottom, "Use this receipt" (enabled when the list is complete; section "Does not add up" below).
- Header card (`fill1`): **Store** (editable `Row` 52, value, chevron), **Date** (editable `Row` 52), **Total** (editable `Row` 52, `bodyStrong`). Each editable by tap with the app's own input sheets or inline `MoneyInput`.
- **Items section** (section title "Items", `sheetTitle`): one `ItemRow` per item: item name (`bodyStrong`, up to 3 lines, wraps), price right (`bodyStrong`, `AmountDisplay`), the usual category as a tappable chip under the name (`CategoryGlyph` 24 inside a `Pill`, `label`; "Choose a category" accent text link when none). A `Pill` "Check this" (`caution` glyph + words) sits under the name on every unsure item and the receipt photo crop appears beside it (96 x 96 thumbnail of the receipt region; tap opens the viewer at that spot). Tap the name or price to edit; tap the trash (`delete`, 44) to remove an item; "Add an item" dashed pill at the end.
- **Extras section** (title "Tax, tip and discounts"): tax, tip and each discount as their own `Row` (52) with name, amount (discounts shown as a minus: "-$3.00" with the word "Discount"). Edit and remove as for items. These rows are not items: they are shared in proportion (section 6).
- **Does-not-add-up message (D-12):** when items + tax + tip − discounts ≠ total, a `WarningCard` `soft` at the top of the list and again above the button: title "Doesn't add up", value "{amount} {over|short}", body "The items come to {itemsTotal} and the receipt total is {total}. Check the marked rows or change the total." Two text buttons: "Use the receipt total" (keeps the total, leaves the gap as an unplaced "Other" item the user can name) and "Use the items total" (changes the total). The primary button stays disabled until one is chosen or the numbers match. The gap amount is in integer minor units; the message never says "wrong".
- **Footer note** (`label`, `inkMuted`): "FincWin reads the printed words. Check each figure before you use it."
- Primary button "Use this receipt" (from Add: "Add this line"). Secondary text "Discard" (destructive text). Leaving review with edits asks to keep or discard (`Sheet dirty`).

### 6. Split by item (D-14..D-17)

Entry: on the review list after reading (when the line is shared, a toggle row "Split items between people" appears, `household` glyph, `SegmentedToggle` off/on, default on for a shared line, hidden for a private line), and from a shared line with a receipt via the detail row "Split by item". Private lines get the item-to-category part only (no people controls).

Layout (added to `ScanReview`, below the items):
- **Who-chips on each item** (shared line): a row of `MemberAvatar` 28 + first name chips, 44 tall hit area, wrap onto lines at large font scale. By default every item is on every member the line's usual rule includes ("starts split the line's usual way", D-14), shown as all chips selected plus the sublabel "Split {even|by weight|by amount} as usual" on the first item only. Tapping a chip toggles that person; at least one person must stay on. Selected chip: accent border + `checkIt` glyph + name (not colour alone); unselected: `line2` border. A long-press on an item is not needed; the chips are the only control.
- **Rule change:** the line's whole-line rule (even, by weight, by amount, mine) is not editable here; changing an item's people overrides it for that item. A text button "Back to the usual split" resets all items.
- **Per-item category (D-16):** the chip under the name carries the likely category from the user's past lines and the store; label "Likely category" appears once as `label` / `inkMuted` above the first item only, not on every item. Tap to change.
- **Proportional extras (D-15), shown not editable:** under Items, a read-only card "Tax, tip and discounts are shared in proportion to each person's items." (`label`, `inkMuted`, `info` glyph). The per-person card shows the extras share line per person; there are no input fields for extras per person. Odd cents follow `hh_allocate`'s largest-remainder rule; the UI never shows a rounding remainder.
- **Result card** (`PersonTotals`, `fill1`, `radii.card`), titled "Each person's share": one `Row` (64) per member: `MemberAvatar` 36, name (`bodyStrong`), total right (`bodyStrong`), sublabel "{items} items · {extras} tax, tip and discounts" (`label`, `inkMuted`). Bottom line "Total {amount}" with the "Adds up" badge. Updates live as chips change; a polite live region speaks "Alex 24.50 dollars, Sam 18.00 dollars. Adds up." once settled (600 ms).
- **Category parts card** (`fill1`), titled "Where it goes": the parts built from the items by category, one `Row` (52) per category with `CategoryGlyph` 36, name, amount; the user can see but not edit amounts here (edit an item's category instead); a text button "Edit parts" opens section 1 prefilled, which breaks the item link with the note "Editing the parts here stops the item split."
- **A person with zero items:** shown with "Nothing" (not a number) and their row gets the sublabel "No items". Allowed (the line may not involve everyone) unless the line's rule requires them; then `BalanceLine` blocks save with "{name} has no items." in short text colour with `stateShort` glyph.
- **Save:** "Save split by item", enabled when person totals and category parts both add up to the line total. Saves through the RPC that keeps `transaction_splits` equal to the item sums and writes the parts (planner). On success: sheet closes, `hapticTap`, toast "Split saved" with action "Undo" (existing `ToastView` undo, server-side, 6 s).
- **Who-changed notice (D-17):** everyone on the line sees, in the line's detail, a notice card (existing before-and-after pattern, `somethingChanged` glyph): "{name} split this line by item" with a before/after pair of share amounts per person ("Alex $30.00 → $24.50") and a button "Undo". Undo is available to whoever can edit the line today; after undo the card reads "{name} undid the split". The receipt thumbnail strip is visible in the same detail (D-07 rules).

### 7. Level and flag

Receipts, scanning and Receipt sheet: level 2 (D-discretion, prototype L3242). Split a line across categories: level 3 (prototype L3244). Split by item on a shared line: appears only when the household feature is on AND the receipt feature is on. Hidden rows leave no gap. Both feature rows are added to `featureRegistry.ts` and `09-LEVEL-MAP.md` together.

## Copywriting Contract

Declarative, never "advice", "recommend", "you should", "we suggest" (general set, `src/i18n/copyGuard/rules.json`; the "decide" set's stricter words are also avoided: no "free", "better", "best", "sure", "accurate", "should"; "safe" appears only in the existing error phrase "Your records are safe.", which already ships in `en.ts` outside the decide set). FincWin holds no money and moves none; the receipt reading says what it did, never that it is right ("Check each figure"). Params are named `{amount}`, `{total}`, `{name}`, `{count}`, `{size}`, `{max}`, `{category}`; none is a bare number key outside `rules.json`'s `notANumber` list.

## Interaction and Motion

- One motion at a time (06.2 kit): sheet slide, row fade in/out 0.15 s, balance badge cross-fade 0.15 s, reading pulse (5a). Nothing loops after 8 s.
- Reduce Motion (`useReduceMotion` / `useMotion`): no slide-in of rows, no viewer zoom animation, no pulse; cross-fade only, instant otherwise.
- Haptics (`expo-haptics` async API only): `hapticTap` on Save, on adding a file, and on a who-chip toggle (selection); `hapticWarn` on a blocked Save and on the does-not-add-up message appearing. None while reading.
- Keyboard: amount fields use the decimal pad; the sheet scrolls the focused part row above the keyboard (SDK 57 fix already in `Sheet`); "Next" moves to the next part's amount.
- Scroll position and an unfinished split survive a dismissed keyboard but not a dismissed sheet (the `dirty` prompt guards it).

## Accessibility

- Every control has an `accessibilityLabel`; roles: parts rows are `button` (category) plus `adjustable` text input (amount); who-chips are `checkbox` with state; thumbnails are `button` "Receipt 2 of 3, photo, waiting to upload"; viewer image `image` with a label; Previous/Next buttons exist for non-gesture navigation.
- Labels (keys `split.*.a11y`, `receipt.*.a11y`, `scan.*.a11y`): part row "Part 1, Groceries, 30 dollars. Opens category list."; remove "Remove part 2"; "Check this" badge "Check this, the reading is unsure"; item row "Oat milk, 4 dollars 50, Groceries, Check this"; person row "Alex, 24 dollars 50, 3 items"; Activity row "Lunch at Pret, 50 dollars, 3 categories, receipt waiting to upload".
- Live regions: `BalanceLine` and the person-totals card are `accessibilityLiveRegion="polite"` with `useSettledLabel` (600 ms), speaking "Adds up" or "{amount} left to place" once. The reading screen announces once "Reading your receipt" on mount and "Receipt read, {count} items" or "That receipt didn't read clearly" on finish via `AccessibilityInfo.announceForAccessibility`. Offline attach announces "Receipt saved on your phone. It will upload when you are back online." once.
- Focus order: sheet title, note, parts top to bottom, add-part, balance, save. Returning from the category picker restores focus to the part row.
- Targets >= 44 x 44 via hit slop; chips and thumbnails never rely on a smaller visible box.
- Colour never the only signal: badges carry glyph + word; selected chips carry `checkIt` + border; "Check this" carries `caution` + words; waiting uploads carry `savedOffline` + words.
- **Text fit (owner standard): 320 dp and 200% font scale.** Part rows grow from 64 to as tall as needed: category name wraps to 2 lines then the amount field drops below the name at font scale > 1.3 (stacked layout). Amounts never truncate. Item names wrap to 3 lines. Who-chips wrap onto extra lines. Action row sublabels wrap. `BalanceLine` wraps under the "Add another part" pill. The sheet footer button stays visible through `SheetScroll`'s sticky footer. The Activity row's `CategoryTag` drops under the name at large scale and the amount stays on the right. Verified on the Pixel text-fit matrix (wm density 430/540 + font_scale for 402/320 dp) at plan end.
- Contrast: all text tokens already tested in `src/theme/contrast.ts`; badges use the tested state pairs; `inkFaint` never on the page tone.

## Owner to confirm

Design choices the CONTEXT, prototype and CLAUDE.md did not settle. Each takes the option most consistent with the prototype and existing screens; none blocks planning.

1. **Three new glyphs** (`receipt`, `file`, `scan`) added to FincWin's own icon set via the 06.2 board process; stand-ins `camera` / `import` / `view` until then.
2. **"Left to place" colour:** amber (`stateTight`) with glyph and words, not the prototype's red. "Over" is red (`stateShort`). "Adds up" is fits green.
3. **Minimum of 2 parts**, starting with the line's category holding the whole total plus an empty second part; the prototype split it in half. The first part re-derives as the rest until hand-edited.
4. **Edit-total gap (D-03) display:** the line stays saved and shows "Parts need placing" in Activity and a "Place it" card in detail; where the unplaced remainder counts in Insights and Decide (suggested: an "Uncategorised" remainder) needs the planner and owner to check.
5. **"Remove the split"** returns the line to one category, "Keep as" defaulting to the largest part.
6. **Split a line** is level 3 (prototype L3244); **Receipts and scanning** level 2 (CONTEXT).
7. **Reading screen wording** and a one-time "Reading on your phone" line; the 8 s "Still reading" line.
8. **"Read it online" consent** is per use, not remembered; wording "a server in the US West" (plain-English form of us-west-2).
9. **Mismatch actions:** "Use the receipt total" keeps the total and adds an editable "Other" item for the gap; "Use the items total" changes the total.
10. **Who-chips are the only item-assignment control** (no long-press, no swipe); the whole-line rule is not changeable inside item mode; "Back to the usual split" resets.
11. **Zero-item person allowed** unless the line's rule requires them.
12. **Editing the parts after a by-item split** breaks the item link with a notice (single-direction: items to parts).
13. **PDF viewing** is handed to the OS viewer or an in-app renderer, planner's choice; the spec only fixes the thumbnail and the open action.
14. **Maximum files, size and compression** are config-driven and the numbers in the limits line come from config (CONTEXT leaves them to the planner).
15. **Offline "Waiting to upload"** uses no state colour (neutral chip), not amber.
