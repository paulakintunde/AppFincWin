# Phase 10.1 Plan 21: Receipt sheet, strip and viewer Summary

The Receipt sheet offers Take a photo, Choose from files and Scan a document (the approved `receipt`, `file`, `scan` glyphs), always states who can see the receipts, how long they stay and the limits from config, shows waiting and failed uploads with Try again / Remove, replaces a denied camera or photo permission with an info card (Open settings, Choose from files), and confirms before removing one or all. The viewer zooms pictures (pinch, double tap), moves between files by swipe or Previous / Next, hands PDFs to the system viewer and shows "This file did not open." with Try again. Wiring into the detail, entry sheet and Activity is plan 10.1-23.

## Deviations from Plan
1. **[Rule 2] New copy key `receipt.addFailed`** ("That receipt could not be added. Try again.") for add failures that are not size, type or permission (outbox full, unavailable, failed); 10.1-07 had no generic line.
2. **Viewer navigates files, not groups.** The counter ("2 of 3") counts every page of every receipt, so a 3-page scan is 3 stops; Remove removes the whole receipt (all its pages) after the confirmation. Thumbnail labels count receipts ("Receipt 2 of 3, photo, ...").
3. **PDF in the viewer** is a tappable row ("PDF", file tile, arrow) that calls `openPdf`; it is not auto-opened on swipe, so moving through files never throws the system share sheet. A PDF still waiting to upload is shown but not openable until stored.
4. **The Try again / Remove menu for a failed upload** is an inline card in the sheet (two pills) rather than a popup menu; Remove goes through the same "deleted for good" confirmation.
5. **Add flow after enqueue** fires `drainOutbox()` and invalidates the receipts query on completion, so an online add shows as stored without waiting for the host's next trigger.
6. **Loading block** (64 x 64 fill1 with a 36 ProgressRing) shows while a capture is being prepared; the ring is fixed at 30 % (no indeterminate ring exists in the kit).
7. The `permission_denied` result maps camera and scan to "Camera access is off" and library to "Photo access is off"; the file picker needs no permission and maps to the generic add error.
8. The strip's caption date uses the receipt's created date (`formatDate(createdAt.slice(0, 10))`) as the UI-SPEC "kind and date".
