# Phase 10.1 Plan 17: Receipt outbox and signed links Summary

Receipts are saved on the phone first, uploaded when online and the line exists on the server (upload, then `add_receipt`; a 409 or `already` counts as done), retried with backoff (2^n minutes, max 60) and marked "Didn't upload" after 5 retries or an immediate rejection. Viewing uses a 60-second signed link made on demand and never stored; PDFs download to a temp file and open in the system viewer.
