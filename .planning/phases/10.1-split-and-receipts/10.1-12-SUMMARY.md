# Phase 10.1 Plan 12: Receipt purge and account deletion Summary

`purge-receipts` (a copy of purge-statement-uploads) drains `pending_receipt_purges` in batches of 100 (max 10 batches), removes the files through the Storage API, and calls `ack_receipt_purges` for a batch only after its removal succeeded; it answers 405 to non-POST, 403 without the `x-receipt-purge-secret` header matching `RECEIPT_PURGE_SECRET`, and logs counts only. `delete-account` now also removes receipt files on the leaver's private lines, listed from `transaction_receipts` rows joined to `transactions` (created_by = user, is_shared = false), never by listing a storage folder; shared-line receipts stay with the household.
