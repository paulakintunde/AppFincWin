## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] hh_allocate cannot take part-sized weights.** The plan says line_category_amounts uses hh_allocate; its weights are `int[]`, which overflows for parts above about 21 million minor units. Added `public.parts_allocate(bigint, numeric[])` (same largest-remainder rule, ties to the lowest position) and used it. Parity cases in 131 cover it.

**2. [Rule 2 - Correctness] Item link unlink trigger** does not clear item_split when the same UPDATE writes item_split itself, so an Undo patch carrying both category_parts and item_split restores both. The flag path (`fincwin.item_split_rpc = 'on'`) is as specified.

**3. household_rpc flag.** set_category_parts sets `fincwin.household_rpc = 'on'` around its update (as set_line_split does); without it guard_shared_line_edit refuses a partner's category_id change.

## Known gaps (by plan scope)

- `transactions_active` and the client read path do not expose category_parts or item_split yet; a later plan must add them to the view.
- The settled-line (line_locked) test replaces `hh_line_locked` inside the test transaction (rolled back) instead of building a settlement.
