# Quick 261025-f46: Decide coming-soon switch Summary

One build switch, `DECIDE_OPEN = false`, in `src/features/decide/availability.ts`. While it is false the Decide tab stays in the bar but shows only "A better Decide is on its way". "New check" is gone from the FAB quick menu and from You > Add, because DecideShellHost is not mounted.
