# Quick Task 261101-fmv: add four safeguards to the Decide fix plan

## Context

The owner asked why the app, and Decide most of all, has needed so many fixes, and what would cut that down sharply. Findings from the repo history on origin/main (2026-11-01):

- 459 of 3,025 commits are fixes; 199 are in the Decide phases (05, 05.1, 05.2). 85 of those came from code-review rounds, about 3 from device checks.
- The Decide screen spec went through 18 revision rounds (R1 to R18); the engine was rewritten (05.1, v3) after the screens were built on v2. Decide has used 270 plans so far.
- The first golden answer tests were generated by running the design prototype (`scripts/decide-golden/generate-golden.mjs`). They prove parity with the prototype, not correctness, so the audit's too-generous "Fits" rules passed with 100% branch coverage.
- Decide runs before Grow and Household exist, so it guesses debts, goals and household money.
- The same figures are worked out twice (Theme K), and there are no robot flows yet (Theme J).

The owner approved four safeguards by popup on 2026-11-01: freeze the design first, a right-answer table, a cross-check calculator, and testing the tests.
