## 1. Why Kint was dropped

- The Kint first pass (`.planning/phases/08.25-kint-rebrand/08.25-TRADEMARK-FIRST-PASS.md` on branch `phase/08.25-kint-rebrand`) found three conflicts:
  - **SKINT**, UK00004263764, registered, classes 9/35/36/42/45. Its goods are almost the same as ours. **High risk in the UK.**
  - **KINT**, Germany, DE 3020261178762, pending, class 9 among others. The goods look like building or technical products.
  - **KINT**, US, Benza LLC, 99783574, pending, class 3 (cosmetics). Low risk.
- Case research on SKINT and KINT:
  - In our favour: Mülhens (SIR v ZIRH), Picasso v Picaro and YESSICA (UK IPO O/092/11). In each, the earlier mark had a clear meaning ("skint" means broke) and that meaning set the two marks apart.
  - Against us: identical goods, and later rulings that a difference in meaning does not automatically win (UK IPO O/566/19; INKO NITO v INCOGNITO, BLO/078/22).
  - Read: arguable, not safe.
- On 2026-11-20 the owner chose to drop Kint and find a name with no encumbrance.
- **FincWin was also checked and is worse:**
  - FINWIN, US 98078309, FinWin.AI, Inc., registered 2025, class 36.
  - FINWIN, UK00004345731, filed 2026, software, financial services and SaaS.
  - Finwin, a live app in the UAE.
  - Both FincWin and FINWIN are made-up words, so neither has a meaning that could set them apart.

## 2. What makes app names work (research summary)

Sources: Monzo's rename blog posts, Venmo founder interviews, Alter & Oppenheimer (PNAS 2006), Argo/Popa/Smith (J. Marketing 2010), Klink 2000, Yorkston & Menon 2004, INTA and USPTO guidance on trademark strength.

- Most top apps have **2 syllables and 4 to 7 letters**. Many end in a vowel (Monzo, Venmo, Klarna, Cleo).
- **Radio test:** anyone who hears the name once can spell it.
- **Made-up names** and **real words used in an unrelated sense** are the strongest trademarks. **Descriptive names** (spend, budget, fund, sum, tally) are the weakest. That is why word-plus-word mashups such as Spendkin and Sumhome felt weak.
- For a calm tone, use soft sounds (m, l, n, v) and an open vowel ending. Avoid z and x.
- The name should work as a verb ("Venmo me").
- Avoid regulated words: bank, pay, credit, loan, invest, advice, wealth.
- **Monzo's lesson:** when it renamed from Mondo, it went from about 4,500 suggestions to 10 checked names, and **legal clearance chose the winner**. Clear first, like second.
