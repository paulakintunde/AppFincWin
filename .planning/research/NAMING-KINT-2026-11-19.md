# Naming research: Kint (2026-11-19)

**Methods:**
- **Google Play search suggestions:** a real browser typed 20 seed phrases into Play's search box (US English) and recorded the suggestions.
- **App Store top-10 results** for 6 terms.
- **iTunes Search API** for name clashes.
- **Verisign and Google Registry RDAP** for .com and .app registration status.

**Limits:**
- Apple's autocomplete wasn't captured. The App Store website shows no suggestions, and extracting its hidden access token was declined as unauthorized access.
- Competitor subtitles weren't captured.
- Search volumes are unknown.
- Trademarks weren't checked.

## 5. FincWin vs Kint

| | FincWin | Kint |
|---|---|---|
| Recall | 7 letters, two parts | 4 letters, one syllable |
| Spoken | Heard as "fin win", which is an existing brand | "kint" may be heard as "kind" or "kent"; spell it out early |
| Search today | Fuzzy "fin…" results; "finwin" is a UAE broker app and FinWin Technologies | Magician tool and business app (0 ratings); Play suggests Kinto and Kintone |
| Meaning | finance + win: sounds like investing or trading | kin = family and household: fits shared money |
| Domains | fincwin.com and .app registered | kint.ca owned; kint.com and kint.app taken; getkint.app, kintapp.app and kinthome.com free |
| Trademark | Unchecked | Unchecked; check Kintone (Cybozu) closely |

**Verdict:** Kint, subject to a clean trademark search.
