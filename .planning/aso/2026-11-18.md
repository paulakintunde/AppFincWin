## 1. What changed since September

### 1.1 "Can I afford it" now has new apps

In September, every app ranking for the phrase had 0–2 ratings. That's still true, but these have launched since:

| App | First released | Subtitle | Price | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Can I Afford It Today? | 2026-09-28 | Find the spot they won't share | Free | Type a few numbers, get yes or no |
| OK2Buy: Can I Afford This? | 2026-10-31 | Budget check before you buy | Free | Earn, spend and saved; three answers |
| Verdict Money: Can I Afford It | 2026-11-10 | Bill Organizer & Money Manager | Premium $49.99 | Forecast, plus decisions typed in your own words ("buy a $40,000 car next June") |
| CIBI: Can I Buy It? Budget | 2026-11-16 | Spending check before you shop | Pro $4.99 / $34.99 | Scan a price tag; Safe, Careful or Over against this month's budget |

**Verdict Money is the closest.** It uses the word "verdict" and promises to "test a decision before you make it". It seems to be a rebrand of an older app called Visor, because it still sells an in-app purchase named "Visor Premium".

**What none of them do:**
- use months the person has actually logged;
- cover a household or a couple;
- show what a purchase breaks;
- offer cheaper or later versions of the purchase;
- protect a safety net.

That list is now the differentiator. "We answer can I afford it" no longer is.

**Google Play** is still quiet for the phrase: mostly loan and home-affordability calculators, plus Wallet.

**Household and couples terms** haven't changed. The leaders are still Honeydue (10.3K ratings), Monarch (119K), YNAB (62K), EveryDollar (84K) and Our Expenses (492). None of the purchase-check apps uses "household".

### 1.2 The hard paywall (Phase 9 D-01, D-15, D-17b, D-22)

- **LAUNCH-POSITIONING §4 "The quick check is free" is no longer true.** There's no free tier. Decide on your own money runs only during the trial or a paid plan. Before the trial starts, a look-around with sample data shows a sample Decide answer.
- **The planned 2.3.2 line "Some features need FincWin Pro" is wrong now.** Replace it with a disclosure of the trial and the subscription. The trial needs a card, renews into the chosen plan, and is on Solo or Duo only.
- **Rating risk.** The store name promises an answer, and the first answer is a sample. Expect early "it's just a paywall" reviews. Ratings carry 20% of the score.

## 5. Features FincWin can compete on

These are the built features from the Phase 9 level map and the completed phases. Store claims must match what ships at launch.

### 5.1 Lead with these (no app checked shows them)

| Feature | What it does | Store angle |
|---|---|---|
| Decide on your own months | The answer is worked out from the person's logged months: recurring bills, money in vs out, unspent money rolled forward | "Your months answer", the main difference from every calculator |
| What it breaks | Shows which bills, goals or rules a purchase would push out | Screenshot 2 |
| Cheaper, later, or skip | Alternatives at a lower price or a later date, plus payment plans and financing | Screenshot 3 |
| Safety net kept out | Emergency savings never count as money you can spend; checks for overdraft, debt minimums and tax | Trust; the "careful" answer, not just yes or no |
| Plain confidence sentence | Says how sure the answer is, given how much history there is | Honesty, versus "instant yes/no" apps |
| Your money rules | Limits the person sets that Decide respects | Personal, not generic |
| Free help when it's tight | Free debt-advice and money-help links, never behind the paywall | Responsible, store-review friendly |
| Household Decide | One answer for a shared budget | No purchase-check app does households |

### 5.2 Strong (only some leaders have them)

| Feature | Notes |
|---|---|
| Household sharing | Invites, shared accounts, splits by weight or exact amount, category split rules, settle up with locked lines, live sync. Honeydue and Our Expenses compete here; Monarch and YNAB partly |
| Plans by household size | Solo, Duo, Household (up to 6) and Extended (up to 12); the price won't go up while the plan stays active. A family of 12 is rare in this category |
| No bank login needed | Import CSV, OFX/QFX, PDF statements and pasted lists, then reconcile against the statement. Bank sync is the #1 complaint in competitor reviews (180 of 2,280 in Sept) |
| Reconcile | Tick off lines against a statement and find the gap |
| Any currency | Enter in any currency and convert to the home currency with stored rates; custom currencies |
| Investments with cost basis | Holdings, every buy, sale, dividend and fee, with gain and loss. Rare in budgeting apps |
| Debt payoff planner | Pick an order, compare scenarios |
| Simple to start, deep later | Four levels from setup questions; 5 of 32 features at Basics, all 32 at Everything; any feature can be switched on in You > Features |
| Undo history | Roll back recent changes |
| Privacy and deletion | GDPR standard everywhere, in-app deletion that truly purges, export always open |

### 5.3 Table stakes (have them, don't lead with them)

- Money in and out
- Categories and auto-categorise rules
- Category caps
- Repeating bills, detected from history
- Coming up
- Transfers
- Refunds
- Several accounts
- Balances and net worth
- Savings goals with monthly contributions
- Calculators (loan, savings, payoff, growth)
- Search, including across every month
- Clone a month
- Select several
- Works offline (changes save and send when back online)
- Apple and Google sign-in
