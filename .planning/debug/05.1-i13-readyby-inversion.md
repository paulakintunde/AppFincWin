# 05.1 I13: a dearer price was told it was ready sooner

## Symptom

`monotone.test.ts` I13 failed deterministically with seed -1306121219 (numRuns 60). Counterexample: person `priya`,
tweak `{ everydayPct: 50, balancePct: 91, unspentMode: 'keep' }`, plan yearly (`month` x 12), 18 payments,
APR `30000000000n` (that is `percent('3')`, 3%: RATE_SCALE is 10 digits, not 9), first payment today.

| price | dayOne | cash check | answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3,727,926 | 263,158 | `payday` until 2026-11-01 (short 13,108) | not_yet / payday, readyBy 2026-11-01 |
| 3,541,529 | 250,001 | `ok` (low 49 on 2026-10-31) | not_yet / not_covered, readyBy 2026-12 |

asOf 2026-10-03, monthLeft 150,000, typicalLeft 150,000, unspent 100,000.

## Root cause

`judge/judge.ts` `oneOffPart` returned `not_yet` / `payday` with `readyBy = cash.until` the moment the cash path
dipped, without running the ladder. "Ready on payday" is only true when the ladder would pass by then. Here the
ladder fails at both prices (day one 263,158 against month 150,000 and unspent 100,000: gap 163,158 over 150,000 a
month = 2 months, December). The cheaper price passes the cash check, so it reached the ladder and got December; the
dearer one stopped at the cash check and got November 1. The cash path, purchase flows, today-payment removal and
schedule were all correct; the inversion is purely in how the judge combined the two checks.

## Fix

`oneOffPart` now runs the ladder (`ladder()`, unchanged logic) after a passing or payday cash check:

- cash `not_covered`: unchanged (not_covered, ladder month).
- cash `ok`: the ladder's answer, unchanged.
- cash `payday` and the ladder passes (or cannot tell, unspent unknown): payday, as before (Tia #12 unchanged).
- cash `payday` and the ladder fails: the later of the two. The ladder month (variant not_covered), or payday when
  the pay date falls after the first of that month. `null` (never) when the typical month leaves nothing.

Code is still `not_yet` in every payday case, so I18 and the cash-first order are untouched; only variant/readyBy change.

## Evidence

- Reproduced through `fc.check` with the seed and as a plain unit test (`invariants/readyByRegression.test.ts`).
- The regression test and five judge-level cases fail before the fix and pass after.
- I13 sweep after the fix: seeds -1306121219, 1, 2, 3, 42, 7, 123456, -99 at 500 runs, and 11, 22, 33, 44, 55 at
  2000 runs: no counterexamples.

## Residual (not observed)

Cash `not_covered` still answers with the ladder month. In principle a dearer price whose cash check is not covered
could get month M (key M-01) while a cheaper price with a payday dip later inside month M gets that pay date. No sweep
found it with the golden persons; it needs a pay date late in the month after asOf.
