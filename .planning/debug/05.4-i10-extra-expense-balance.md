# 05.4 I10: a $1 spending line turned Not yet into Monthly short

## Symptom

`src/engine/decide/v3/__tests__/invariants/monotone.test.ts` I10 "an extra expense never helps" failed in the `checks` job of
PR #94 (GitHub run 42216986391, `npm run test:coverage`), after 150 runs and 74 shrinks:

- seed -1072770010, path `253:15:25:17:...:27:27:27:27:27`
- Expected `codeRank(after) <= 1`, received 2: before `not_yet` (rank 1), after `monthly_short` (rank 2). Rank 2 is the looser code.

Shrunk case (golden person `kim` with tweak `{ everydayPct: 131, balancePct: 103, unspentMode: 'none', emergency: null, extras: [] }`):
steady pay $3,000 a month, rent $1,200, saving $200, everyday $917 in each of six full months (April to September); October
partial (asOf 2026-10-03) with the $3,000 pay, rent, an $800 `other_in` refund and $65.50 spending; balance $6,334.50, one
everyday account. Check: price $98,960.18 on a plan every 11 weeks x 47 payments, APR `300000000000n`, no deposit, first
payment one period out. The extra line: $1.00 spending dated 2026-10-01, with the balance left at $6,334.50.

## Hypotheses

1. The APR is absurd and the generator should be bounded. **Rejected.** `Percent` uses `RATE_SCALE` = 10 digits, so
   `300000000000n` is `percent('30')`, 30%. `planPayArb` already caps APR at 0..30%. The plan ($6,649.01 every 11 weeks,
   $2,619.31 a month) is large but a legal input.
2. The engine treats recorded spending wrongly (a real bug). **Rejected,** see evidence.
3. The test's "after" input is not one extra expense: the spending line is added but the money never leaves the account.
   **Confirmed.**

## Evidence

Instrumented `judge()` (temporary, reverted) on both sides:

| | one-off part | cash check | monthly part | answer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| before | `not_yet` / `not_covered` | `not_covered`, short by 1 (path ends 2026-12-31 at -$0.01) | `monthly_short` | `not_yet` (stricter of the two) |
| after ($1 line, same balance) | `fits_month` | `ok`, lowest $0.99 on 2026-12-31 | `monthly_short` | `monthly_short` |

Every point on the after path is exactly $1.00 higher (2026-10-31: 528,300 to 528,400; 2026-12-31: -1 to 99). The monthly
part is unchanged (typicalLeft 68,300 against 261,931 a month).

The cash path (`cash/path.ts`) spreads `Erest`, the rest of this month's everyday spending. `month/thisMonth.ts` `erestOf` sets
it to the larger of the day's pace (E x days left / days in month = $828.26 here) and the usual everyday less what is already
logged this month (E - logged = $917.00 - $65.50 = $851.50 here, so this side binds). The balance is the money in the account now,
so it already shows everything spent so far. Adding a $1 paid spending line lowers `Erest` by $1 (the month's everyday is
$1 further along), which is right only if the balance is also $1 lower. The test left the balance as it was, so its "after"
is an account that opened $1 higher and spent it: $1 more cash on the path. On this knife-edge case (-$0.01) that flips the
cash check, and the one-off part goes from Not yet to fits; the monthly part keeps the answer at Monthly short.

## Root cause

Test model, not engine. I10 "an extra expense never helps" added a paid spending line without taking the money out of the
balance. That is the mirror of the I11 fix in 05.4 (D-86, FIN-09b: "a pay line the balance does not show means the account
opened lower", `withPaidIn`). The engine's reading (recorded spending counts toward this month's usual everyday, the balance
shows what is left) is the intended design and is not changed.

## Fix

`monotone.test.ts`:

- New `withPaidOut(inputs, amount)` (the mirror of `withPaidIn`): the balance and the first everyday (or mixed, or unstated)
  account fall by the amount. I10 "an extra expense never helps" now adds the line and takes the money out.
- New pinned test "I10 pinned: a $1 expense that leaves the account does not lift a Not yet on a -$0.01 cash path (seed
  -1072770010)": rebuilds the case from `inputsOfPerson('kim', ...)` and `checkOf(9_896_018, ...)`, and asserts before
  `not_yet`, after with the money out `not_yet`, and documents the impossible input (line without the money out) as
  `monthly_short`.

No engine file changes; no answer strings or engine behaviour change. Engine coverage is unaffected.

## Verification

- The pinned test passes; I10 passes at the default 200 runs and replays the CI seed and path without failing.
- Engine tests, `npm run test:coverage`, lint and typecheck: see the commit.
