# Decision Research

Everything produced while researching how to rebuild the Decide ("Can I afford it?") feature, 2026-10-24 and 2026-10-25.

## Start here
00. `panel/00-MAJOR-CHANGES.md`: **the 16 major changes in plain words**, today vs next.
0. `panel/00-UPGRADE-PLAN.md`: **how today's Decide becomes v3**: discussion, add/edit/delete/update for every piece of data, the UI plan, the engine plan and the execution plan.
1. `panel/00-REBUILD-PLAN-v3.md`: **the current plan** (owner's third round): what changes, how, what is discarded, and how today's Decide changes.
2. `panel/00-OWNER-DECISIONS.md`: every decision, newest round at the top. v2 and v1 plans are kept for comparison.
3. `panel/00-SCENARIOS.md`: 22 common money situations and what the engine does in each.

## Folders
| Folder | What's in it |
|---|---|
| `audits/` | The first two expert audits: finance (`FINANCE-AUDIT.md`) and language (`LANGUAGE-AUDIT.md`) |
| `panel/` | The six-expert panel: 01 behavioural economist, 02 statistician, 03 financial planner, 04 architect, 05 product designer, 06 risk and compliance. The `00-` files are the combined plans (v1 is kept for comparison), the decisions and the scenarios |
| `screenshots/` | Pixel 9 device screenshots cited by the audits: `fin-*` (finance walk-through), `lang-*` (language walk-through), and earlier walk-through shots |
| `working-files/` | Scripts and drafts the language audit used (reading-level counter, tables, draft body) |

No code was changed as part of this research.
