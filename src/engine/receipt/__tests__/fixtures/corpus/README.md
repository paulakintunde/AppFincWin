# Receipt corpus fixtures

Each `rNN.json` is the on-device OCR text of one receipt photo: `{ name, module, ocr, expected }`.

- Made on the Pixel by the dev reader (10.1-02) from the photos in `C:/dev/fincwin-private/receipts-corpus`. The photos are never committed.
- **The 2026-11-25 set (r01-r26) is synthetic.** At the owner's request, the receipts were generated by `gen_receipts.py` (kept beside the photos, outside git). They use made-up stores, a slight tilt, noise, fading and one blurred image, then were read by the real on-device reader. The true values for each receipt are in `answers.json` in the same private folder, for labelling `expected` in 10.1-15. Real paper (creases, curl, smudged thermal print) is not covered; add owner photos as r27+ when available.
- Scrubbed by `scripts/receipts/scrub-ocr-dump.mjs` (long digit runs, emails and phone numbers masked; full dates kept). Fixed 2026-11-25 after 10.1-15: dates such as 2026-10-28 are no longer masked, and phone numbers with a stray OCR space (`752 -555-8427`) are masked. All 26 fixtures were re-scrubbed from the phone's dump with their labels kept.
- `expected` is labelled (10.1-15, 2026-11-25) from `answers.json`, not by eye: `{ store, date (ISO), total, itemCount, tax, tip, currency }`, amounts in minor units. The scrub script never overwrites a labelled fixture.
  - `total` is the **final amount paid**. On a restaurant bill where the tip was written after the printed TOTAL (r03, r13, r24), it is the "TOTAL WITH TIP" line, not the printed TOTAL. This matches the parser rule (after the total, a tip row is still read, and a later total row replaces the total) and the reconcile formula (items + tax + tip - discounts = total).
  - `tax` is 0 on GBP and EUR receipts: VAT is included in the prices and printed after the total as information.
  - `itemCount` counts purchase lines; a "2 x 11.84" row is one item, and a coupon under an item is that item's discount, not an item.
  - The receipt-level discount in `answers.json` (member discount) is not part of `expected`.
  - `corpus.test.ts` is the gate: a wrong total must be flagged or show a reconcile mismatch, and at least 80% of totals must be exact.
- OCR module used: `@react-native-ml-kit/text-recognition@2.0.0`, with line frames (2026-11-25). Lines come back in reading blocks, often all item names and then all prices, so rows must be rebuilt from `frame` positions, not from line order.
