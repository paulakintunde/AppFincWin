# A quiet check on your phone (for FincWin testers)

Kept for 08.1. The first round (05.4) used only the owner's accounts (D-160). How testers are asked, and what happens when someone says stop, are decided in 08.1 (D-161, D-162). Fill in the "If you want to stop" line before sending.

---

Hi,

FincWin has a part called Decide that answers "can I afford this?". It is still hidden while we test it. We would like to check its answers against real money records, and yours would help.

**What would run.** Once after each app update, your phone quietly checks 6 made-up purchases against your own FincWin records:

- a 60 item
- a 500 phone
- a 2,000 laptop
- a 9,000 car paid in cash
- the same car paid over 48 months
- a 1,200 item paid over 12 months at 0%

The amounts are in your own currency. Nothing shows on your screen, and nothing is bought or changed.

**What is sent.** For each made-up purchase, the phone sends three things:

- the answer it would give, such as "Fits this month" or "Not yet"
- a few short codes saying why
- some numbers rounded to 2 significant figures, so 1,234 is sent as 1,200

**What is not sent.** Your name, your transactions, your account names, your notes and your exact balances are never sent.

**Who sees it.** Only the owner of FincWin sees the results, on a summary page. On that page you appear only as "Tester 1", "Tester 2" and so on. The stored results carry your account number so that they are deleted when your account is deleted. Unusual results may become test examples about made-up people, with made-up names and changed amounts.

**Decide stays hidden.** Saying yes does not turn Decide on for you, and the app looks the same as before.

**If you say no.** Nothing runs, and nothing changes for you.

**If you want to stop later.** Tell the owner at any time. [08.1, D-162: what happens to results already sent.]

Can FincWin run this quiet check on your phone? Yes or no.
