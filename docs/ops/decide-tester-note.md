# Decide: note for testers

This build turns on the new Decide screens for testers on the preview channel. Thank you for trying them.

**Please don't rely on Decide for real purchases yet.** Use it to try things out and tell us what looks wrong. A safety patch (phase 05.4) is planned next. It fixes the issues below.

## Known issues until the 05.4 safety patch

- Decide can say "Fits" even when nothing would be left over afterwards. There is no buffer yet.
- An overdrawn account is counted as zero, not as a negative balance.
- Minimum payments on cards and loans are not yet part of the 90-day cash check.
- A borrowing option can look better than paying cash, even when it costs more.
- The big number shows the money left before the purchase, not after it.
- Decide can say your safety net is "untouched" even when you don't have one.
- The "Rough / Fair / Firm" label is being replaced by a plain sentence.

## What to send us

If something looks wrong, send a screenshot and one line about what you expected to see. Please cover or crop out any real balances before you send it.
