## Differences kept, not changed (for information)

- Activity's pay screen asks how often and the next pay date. Decide's pay form asks the day of the month and the account. Those are different questions on different screens, so each host keeps its own. The shared form holds the amount, the day grid and the account chips; Activity adds its two fields in the middle.
- Activity has no stand-alone "regular bill" form with a name, amount, day and how often. Bills there are edited in the entry sheet, which also carries category, account and notes. The shared bill form is therefore used by Decide's setup step only for now; the entry sheet is not changed.
