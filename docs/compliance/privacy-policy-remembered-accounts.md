# Privacy policy paragraph: remembered accounts (ACC-14)

Status: Draft - publish on fincwin.com/privacy with the Phase 02.3 rollout (plan 02.3-20).

To make signing back in quicker, the app keeps the name and email of up to three accounts that signed in on this phone, even after you sign out. It keeps no password, token or financial information. Tap "Not you? Remove" on the sign-in screen to forget an account. Uninstalling the app removes the list.
