# Data map (CMP-F20)

**Date:** 2026-11-13
**Status:** working draft. Built from `src/data/exportManifest.ts` (every exported table) plus the stores the export does not cover. The privacy text and the Apple and Google store forms are drafted from this file only (`privacy-text-draft.md`). When a table or field family is added, add its row here first.
**Standard:** GDPR everywhere. Legal basis defaults to Article 6(1)(b), performing the service the person asked for, unless stated. Lawyer sign-off is Phase 11.
**Not a bank:** FincWin holds no money and keeps no bank login. Every figure is something the person typed, imported from a file they chose, or a number worked out from those.

Columns: `Deleted on account deletion` is what the database does today. Every household-owned table cascades from the user's `auth.users` row (proved by pgTAP `supabase/tests/database/36_account_deletion.test.sql`). The in-app "delete my account" action and the full export download are Phase 10 (CMP-F06, DAT-02); until they ship, deletion is through the database cascade.
