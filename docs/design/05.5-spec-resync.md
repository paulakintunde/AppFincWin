## Gaps (owner items)

1. `volatile_cover_question` (asked when income swings and the low months are not covered): the engine asks and the Answer shows the "your answer about low months" line, and the "Answer changed" toggle exists once an answer is stored. No screen writes the first answer (the save exists in `src/data/mutations/decideAnswers.ts`, but nothing in `src/features` calls it). The fixing link falls through to the setup checklist, which has no such field. Needs a decision: add a yes/no question on the Answer screen, or remove the code.
2. `legacy_invest_check`: only raised for checks saved under the old "invest" kind. The Answer says "this check again" but offers no input; the user has to start a new check. Fine as a hand-off, but it is not an input. Needs a decision: accept as a restart message, or retire it.
3. `share_invalid` (a share outside the allowed range): the Answer says "your share" and the link goes to the setup checklist, not to the share field. Needs a decision: point the link at the share field on the Price step.

Every other code has an input; every input feeds the Answer and at least one Verdict.
